Sean Bean has voiced strong opposition to a controversial housing development plan that threatens green belt land near his hometown of Sheffield. The esteemed actor, known for his work in films and television, described the proposal as one that would 'practically obliterate the character and nature of our area.' In an email of objection shared with the BBC, Bean lamented, 'It seems the council sees us as second-class citizens who will just have to put up with it.'nnThe planned development has spurred significant backlash from the local community, and notable voices have emerged in opposition. Sadie Charlton, a member of the grassroots campaign Save S13 Green Belt - The Sapphire McCarthy Campaign, expressed gratitude for Bean’s support, stating, 'It's really boosted our morale and everybody's so thankful for him to take the time to put his voice forward because what he says in his objection is how we all feel.' Indeed, the campaign was founded in memory of Sapphire McCarthy, a passionate activist who passed away, and Charlton emphasized that her legacy continues to inspire the group's efforts.nnBean, who submitted his objection just before the consultation period ended on July 11, characterized the council's approach as targeting working-class communities while leaving wealthier areas 'to escape lightly.' He noted a growing awareness in the Handsworth area, with residents like Charlton indicating a newfound political engagement in local planning issues.
