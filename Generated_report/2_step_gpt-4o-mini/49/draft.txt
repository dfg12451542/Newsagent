Liverpool Football Club has confirmed the signing of 20-year-old Scotland midfielder Kirsty Maclean from Rangers. Maclean, who is a product of the Rangers academy, has made over 100 appearances for the Glasgow club before embarking on this new chapter in her career. In an official statement, she expressed her thoughts on the transfer, stating it was the 'right time for a new challenge and to take the next step.'nnThe young midfielder further emphasized her pride in joining Liverpool, saying, 'I think the club speaks for itself. It’s obviously a massive club with a lot of history and to be able to represent the badge is a really proud moment for me.' Maclean, known for her ball control and playmaking abilities, is hopeful to make an immediate impact, stating, 'I’m a midfielder who likes to be on the ball and tries to make things happen, so hopefully I can link up with the players in front of me and we can try to score lots of goals this season.'nnMaclean's move follows a notable trend of talent flowing from Scottish clubs to larger leagues, with many players seeking to develop their skills in more competitive environments. Her signing signifies Liverpool’s continued commitment to enhancing their squad for the upcoming season, aiming for success both domestically and in European competitions. This transfer not only highlights Maclean's individual talent but also underscores the growing reputation of women's football in Scotland, where players like her have begun to gain international recognition.
