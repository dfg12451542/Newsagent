In an impressive display of dominance, Chelsea FC secured the Club World Cup title with a 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain in New Jersey on Sunday. The team's captain, Reece James, along with goalkeeper Robert Sanchez, lifted the trophy amidst a surprise appearance by former President Donald Trump, who had initially been expected to leave the stage after the presentation. 'They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage, and I thought that he was going to exit the stage, but he wanted to stay,' James recounted.nnAfter Chelsea's victory, Trump remained on stage, applauding the players as James raised the trophy high, a gesture that delighted fans and players alike. 'It was a bit confusing,' said teammate Cole Palmer, reflecting on the unexpected presence of the former president during such a monumental moment for the club. Trump, who watched the match alongside FIFA president Gianni Infantino, expressed his enjoyment of the day, stating, 'I'm having a really great time.'nnIn a post-match interview, Trump praised Infantino's efforts in leading the league and indicated a humorous willingness to change soccer’s nomenclature in the U.S. from 'soccer' to 'football.' 'Gianni is a friend of mine, he's done such a great job with the league and with soccer - or as they would call it 'football',' he elaborated, before adding, 'I think we could do that. I think I could do that.'nnThis victory marks a significant achievement for Chelsea, reigniting memories of their previous successes, including their triumph in the 2021 Club World Cup. Fans celebrated not only the team's skill and determination on the pitch but also the unexpected and memorable moments that these events generate. As the evening concluded, the presence of Trump on stage further highlighted the blending of sports and politics, a phenomenon that has become increasingly prevalent in recent years.
