Golf fans flocked to Royal Portrush on Monday morning to witness Rory McIlroy's highly anticipated practice round for The Open 2025, set to commence later this week. The excitement among the large crowd was palpable as fans gathered to support the local hero and world number two, who previously clinched his Open title at Hoylake in 2014.nnDuring his practice session, McIlroy captivated the numerous spectators by sinking a birdie putt on the 18th green. After completing his round, he graciously took time to engage with the community, signing autographs for 15 minutes, much to the delight of those present. The atmosphere at the course reflected the profound connection McIlroy maintains with his fans, especially in his home country of Northern Ireland.nnAs the day progressed, McIlroy is scheduled to hold a media conference at 14:00 BST, followed by 2019 champion Shane Lowry at 15:00 BST. The anticipation for the upcoming tournament is further heightened by the local interest in McIlroy's performance on his home turf, where he has always felt a significant sense of pride and responsibility to excel.nnIn 2014, McIlroy's previous Open victory established him as a premier figure in the sport, and his return to Northern Ireland in this prestigious tournament only intensifies local enthusiasm. With the eyes of the golfing world upon him, McIlroy aims not just to compete but to clinch a major title amid the passionate support of his home crowd.
