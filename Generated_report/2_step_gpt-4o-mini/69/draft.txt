Firefighters are currently battling a significant blaze at a commercial site in Lancashire, sending plumes of thick smoke visible from miles away. Eight fire engines responded to the emergency as crews work diligently to contain and extinguish the flames. 'We are working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely,' a spokesperson from the Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) stated.nnGroup Manager Mark Warwick added that the fire is 'giving off large volumes of smoke,' posing a potential risk to nearby residents. LFRS has advised locals to 'keep windows and doors closed' if they notice any smoke in their vicinity to minimize inhalation hazards.nnFortunately, there have been no reports of injuries related to the incident, though nearby roads, including parts of the Bay Gateway, have been closed as a precaution. The smoke is billowing heavily above the site, leading to increased concerns amongst local residents over air quality and safety.nnThis incident is reminiscent of previous industrial fires in the region, such as a significant blaze at an industrial estate in Leyton, London, which highlighted the risks associated with such facilities.
