A petition to restore the historic Victoria Marine Lake in St Helier has gained significant momentum, reaching 1,000 signatures in a demonstration of public support for the 128-year-old sea pool. Petition organiser Nina Zaech emphasized the importance of the pool, describing it as a 'vital piece of Jersey's heritage' that could contribute to health, leisure, and tourism on the island.nnThe growing interest in preserving the Victoria Marine Lake has prompted a response from Jersey's government, particularly after last month's inspection revealed that the pool required 'comprehensive work' to restore it. This urgent call for action has been amplified by the newly-formed Save Our Pool Working Group, which is advocating for immediate restoration efforts. Zaech highlighted that not only would the restoration preserve a significant historical site, but it would also support the local economy through increased tourism.nnHowever, challenges remain. Infrastructure Minister Andy Jehan noted that repairs to the pool may be 'uneconomical' due to planned sea defense upgrades along the waterfront, complicating efforts to secure funding for the project. Local campaigners are now calling for a reevaluation of priorities to ensure that this longstanding community resource is not lost to neglect.nnHistorical records indicate that the Victoria Marine Lake has long been a cherished locale for both residents and tourists alike. Its preservation is viewed not just as a maintenance issue but as a commitment to protecting Jersey's unique cultural identity. With the petition reaching a significant milestone, the campaigners are hopeful that their efforts will lead to renewed attention and action from the authorities.
