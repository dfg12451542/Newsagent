Motherwell manager Jens Berthel Askou has indicated a need for the team to 'increase tempo' following a challenging start in the Premier Sports Cup. After a draw with Clyde, Askou remarked on the unpredictability of football, stressing that draws can showcase just how 'tricky' the sport can be. He noted, 'It just shows how tricky football is in general,' highlighting that surprises are common, especially in cup tournaments.nnWhile the team did not achieve the desired result against Clyde, Askou observed effort and structure on the pitch, stating, 'I think in terms of effort and structure, we were good.' He added that the intensity during their pressing moments was commendable, but acknowledged areas for improvement, particularly dealing with direct play from opponents: 'Every time we tried to press, they put the ball in behind.' nnLooking ahead, Askou is optimistic about the potential for improvement as the team finds its rhythm. 'That’s something to improve on, definitely, and then it’s about getting the tempo up,' he said, expressing confidence in the support they will receive from fans at Fir Park, which he believes will aid in elevating their performance.nnThis comes in the wake of a pre-season match against Eredivisie side Twente, where Askou praised his team's 'brave and dynamic' display despite the outcome. Fans will be hoping the team can build on this foundation as they continue in their cup campaign.
