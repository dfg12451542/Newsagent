A memorial bench dedicated to Sophie Lancaster, a young woman tragically murdered in a hate crime over a decade ago, has been temporarily relocated to accommodate the installation of a giant sightseeing wheel in Whitby. The bench, situated on West Cliff, honors Lancaster, who was a member of the Goth community and murdered by two teenagers in 2007.nnAccording to a spokesperson for the Sophie Lancaster Foundation, the organization was 'shocked' to discover that the bench had been moved without their consultation. They emphasized the bench's significance, noting that it was a 'tangible connection' for Sylvia, Sophie’s mother, as well as for the many visitors who come to reflect on her life and legacy.nnNorth Yorkshire Council, which oversees local attractions, described the wheel as a 'respectful, exciting addition to the town.' They reassured that the memorial bench will be restored to its original position by August 4. 'This was a serious oversight,' remarked Whitby mayor, councillor Sandra Turner. She confirmed that local authorities have raised the issue with the council, which has apologized and assured that this will not happen again.nnCouncillor Mark Crane further explained that the wheel aims to boost tourism by offering stunning views over the harbor and Whitby Abbey, with initial feedback from local businesses indicating strong support. 'The decision to install the wheel was made in good faith to avoid delays,' he added, indicating that council officers will attend forthcoming town meetings to improve local engagement.nnMeanwhile, the Sophie Lancaster Foundation has announced plans for a small ceremony to rededicate the bench at the Tomorrow's Ghosts Festival on November 1st. Foundation representatives expressed joy over the bench's upcoming return, acknowledging the heartfelt responses from the alternative community supporting Sophie’s memory. 'Whitby is such a special place for us,' they shared, highlighting the love and affection shown to the foundation established in her honor.
