Phyll Babb, a vibrant centenarian from Salisbury, completed an impressive 200-mile walking challenge on her 100th birthday, raising over £11,000 for the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless (STFH). Babb initially aimed for a target of 100 miles, but after realizing she was well ahead of schedule early in the year, she decided to double her goal. 'It's hard to believe I've done 200 walks. It's gone like a dream,' she expressed after completing her final lap around Salisbury Cathedral, cheered by her family and friends.nnReflecting on her lifelong passion for walking, Babb said, 'Walking is good for you. I was brought up to walk, so as children we walked everywhere, and I still like walking.' Her positive spirit has inspired many, with local accountant David Evans noting, 'To reach the age of 100 and to do it so fit, looking so well, being so articulate, that’s the really amazing thing she’s done.' Liz Huntley, a family member, also echoed her sentiments, saying, 'She is inspirational, definitely. She’s been inspirational my whole life, to be honest.'nnBabb recorded her walks meticulously with the help of Gordon Pardy, another supporter of the charity, who said, 'To do this in her 100th year was so impressive.' The large turnout of Babb's family and supporters during the challenge further underscored the community's admiration for her resilience and dedication. With her charm and vitality, Babb has truly showcased a remarkable milestone that celebrates not only personal achievement but also compassion for those in need.nnHistorically, Babb's challenge stands as a testament to the increasing trend of charity walks that mobilize communities for worthy causes. The local charity scene has seen a surge of participation in events, including previous sponsored walks that brought together over 2,000 individuals in celebration and fundraising efforts. In light of Babb’s achievement, local residents are reminded of the impact one person's commitment can have on community well-being, paving the way for future charitable endeavors and inspirational stories like hers.
