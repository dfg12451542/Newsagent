Wythall PE teacher Joe Maidment is celebrating the impressive achievement of former student Lloyd Glasspool, who made history by becoming part of the first all-British pair to win the men's doubles title at Wimbledon in 89 years. Glasspool and his partner Julian Cash triumphed at the prestigious tournament, solidifying their status in tennis history. 'To think a student from Woodrush High School in Wythall can win at Wimbledon - it's just amazing,' Maidment expressed, emphasizing the significance of the victory for the local community.nnDuring the tournament, Maidment traveled to Wimbledon to witness Glasspool's stellar performance, which he lauded as 'inspirational.' The PE teacher reinforced Glasspool's success as not only a personal victory but also a beacon of hope and motivation for aspiring athletes in the West Midlands, saying, 'It should be a message to everyone to follow their dreams and see what they can achieve.' nnReflecting on their time together, Glasspool playfully recalled their past encounters: 'I've come a long way since when we played in year seven or eight and I gave you that little thrashing,' he told Maidment, further expressing gratitude for the support he received from his former school.nnGlasspool's recent triumph at Wimbledon adds to his impressive accolades, including titles at Queens and Eastbourne, leading Maidment to refer to it as 'the icing on the cake.' The 31-year-old's continued success serves as a testament to his hard work and dedication in the sport. 'I appreciate the support from you and the whole of the Woodrush School,' Glasspool added, acknowledging the foundation laid during his formative years at the institution.
