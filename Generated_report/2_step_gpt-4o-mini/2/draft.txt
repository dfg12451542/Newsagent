Six abandoned kittens, now affectionately known as the 'Smelly Cats', have found new homes after being discovered in a rubbish dump in southern Scotland. The kittens were rescued from Eyemouth Recycling Centre, where they were found in a 'heartbreaking scene' on May 27, suffering from severe malnutrition, flea and worm infestations, and painful eye infections. Centre manager Kim McNeill described their plight, stating, 'All were suffering from severe malnutrition... They were frightened, weak, and understandably wary of human contact.'nnThe team at Borders Pet Rescue undertook the challenging task of nursing the four-week-old kittens back to health. McNeill explained that the kittens required more than just medical treatment; they needed time, patience, and plenty of love. Fortunately, one of their dedicated foster carers provided them with a nurturing environment, allowing the kittens to regain their confidence and experience warmth for the first time.nnIn a whimsical twist, the kittens were named after characters from the beloved television show *Friends*, inspired by the 'strong bond and the laughter they brought to us.' McNeill noted, 'They slowly gained confidence, learned how to play, and after about a month in foster care, they were healthy and full of personality.' The fostering process not only aided in their recovery but also captured hearts on social media, leading to a fundraising effort that raised thousands of pounds for their veterinary care.nnIn an uplifting conclusion to their story, all six kittens have now been adopted into caring homes. Kim McNeill expressed her delight, stating, 'We're delighted to share that all six kittens have now been adopted into wonderful homes, each beginning a new chapter filled with love and joy.' Their journey from despair to happiness serves as a reminder of the impact compassion and care can have on the lives of vulnerable animals.
