Historically, Portrush hosted The Open Championship in 2019, marking a significant occasion for Irish golf fans, and Shane Lowry's victory during that event swelled national pride. With expectations high for this year, McIlroy is eager to build on his recent form and make an impression at a venue that carries personal and national significance. 'When I was looking at the calendar for 2025, this was the tournament that was probably circled even more so than the Masters, for different reasons,' he noted, signaling his hopes for an unforgettable week.
