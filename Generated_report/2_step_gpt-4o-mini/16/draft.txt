A biker is in hospital with serious injuries following a single-vehicle crash on a rural road near Hawick on Sunday, July 14. Emergency services were alerted to the incident on the B6399 around noon. The accident involved a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike and occurred just south of the access road leading to the Pines Burn wind farm.nnA 57-year-old man was transported to the hospital to receive treatment for his serious injuries, and authorities closed the road for approximately eight hours to facilitate investigation and clean-up efforts. Police are currently urging anyone who may have witnessed the crash to come forward with information, as their inquiries remain ongoing.nnSgt David Farr commented on the situation, stating, 'Our inquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances of this crash and I would appeal to anyone who may have witnessed the incident to please contact us.' Such incidents on rural roads can often evoke longstanding concerns about road safety in these areas, particularly given the mix of increasing traffic and sometimes limited signage or visibility that can occur in more remote regions.
