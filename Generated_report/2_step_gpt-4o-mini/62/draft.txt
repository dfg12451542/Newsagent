MasterChef presenter John Torode has confirmed he is the subject of an allegation involving the use of racist language, stemming from an inquiry into separate complaints against co-host Gregg Wallace. In a statement posted on Instagram, Torode disclosed that the allegation dates back to 2018 or 2019, asserting that he had apologized immediately after the incident. 'For the sake of transparency, I confirm that I am the individual who is alleged to have used racial language on one occasion,' Torode stated.nnDespite acknowledging the allegation, Torode expressed his disbelief about the incident, firmly stating, 'I have absolutely no recollection of any of this, and I do not believe that it happened.' He emphasized the importance of maintaining a respectful discourse, adding, 'I want to be clear that I've always had the view that any racial language is wholly unacceptable in any environment. I'm shocked and saddened by the allegation as I would never wish to cause anyone any offense.' nnThis situation comes in the wake of another inquiry that confirmed two separate allegations involving other individuals, one of which also pertained to the use of racist language. The controversy surrounding the MasterChef franchise highlights ongoing concerns about racial insensitivity in entertainment, raising questions about accountability and the ramifications of such allegations in the industry.
