In a thrilling finale at the Evian Championship, Australia's Grace Kim clinched her first major title with a dramatic eagle putt on the 18th hole during a playoff, edging out her compatriot Atthaya Thitikul. The two were neck-and-neck, with Kim's incredible performance capped off by four birdies in her final four holes, securing her place in golf history. 'I was just focused on doing my best out there,' said Kim after her victory.nnEnglish golfer Lottie Woad, who had captured the Irish Open title just a week prior, came remarkably close to joining the playoff. After a stunning seven-under 64, Woad set the clubhouse target at 13 under par. However, her opportunity slipped away when she missed a crucial 10-foot birdie putt. Reflecting on the moment with her caddie, she remarked, 'Is there even any point, she's not making six,' expressing her frustration as she watched Thitikul birdie the 17th and put pressure on the final hole.nnDespite not qualifying for the playoff, Woad's finish was significant, earning her critical points on the LPGA's Elite Amateur Pathway. With 20 points now in her tally, she has the chance to join the LPGA Tour, a prospect she regards as a dream. 'It has always been in my dreams to play on the LPGA Tour,' Woad stated, signaling her ambition for the future. As she continues to develop her game, she is already looking towards the next steps with her family and coaches.nnThe Evian Championship has proven to be a vital stage for emerging talent in women's golf, and Kim's victory adds to the excitement of the sport.
