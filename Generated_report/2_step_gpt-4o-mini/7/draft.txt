In Leicester, police have arrested four more individuals in connection with a stabbing that occurred on Sturdee Road in Eyres Monsell. The incident took place shortly before 9.15pm on Friday night, prompting a swift response from authorities. 'It was still light at that time and with the weather being very warm there were probably quite a few people who were in the area or in their gardens,' noted Det Sgt Adrian Davies of Leicestershire Police, emphasizing the community's potential role in gathering information about the event.nnOfficers responded to calls for help after a man in his 20s was attacked. He managed to make his way to nearby Lawford Road, where he was taken to the hospital and remains in stable condition. A 36-year-old woman was initially arrested on suspicion of attempted murder, along with two men aged 18 and 29 who have also been implicated in the case.nnAdditionally, a 38-year-old woman was apprehended for conspiracy to commit murder, and a 25-year-old woman arrested for assisting an offender. While the 18-year-old suspect and the 25-year-old woman are still in custody, the other three detainees have been released on police bail as investigations progress.nnThe police have confirmed that the wounded man and the suspects were known to each other, adding a layer of complexity to the investigation. Davies urged anyone who might have witnessed the incident or has relevant information to come forward: 'If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us.' As inquiries continue, officers are undertaking house-to-house visits and have conducted a thorough examination of the scene.
