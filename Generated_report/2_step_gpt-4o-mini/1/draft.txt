Colchester is gearing up for a memorial running event dedicated to the life of Arthur Whiston, a beloved athlete who tragically passed away in a road accident last year. The first anniversary race, organized by the Colchester Harriers Athletic Club, will take place on Sunday at the garrison athletics track, commencing at 09:30 BST. 'Arthur was incredibly loved by the community,' said Lydia Cunningham, a close friend and fellow runner. 'He was a very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular,' she added, highlighting the profound impact Whiston had on those around him.nnParticipants will gather to celebrate Whiston's legacy and share in the community spirit he fostered through his passion for running. The funds raised from this event will be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity that Mr. Whiston was known to actively support. ‘It’s important for us to honor his memory and the causes he cared about,’ Cunningham stated, emphasizing the significance of the event.nnThose interested in participating can still register on the day of the race. The event not only symbolizes the community’s love for Whiston but also aims to inspire new and seasoned runners alike to carry forward his legacy of kindness and dedication.nnThis gathering echoes the sentiment seen in other communities mourning a member lost too soon, reminiscent of the impact made by figures like Bob Emmerson, famously known as 'Mr Northampton Parkrun,' who notably participated in 537 weekly running events. The shared experiences born out of running unite these stories and remind participants of the enduring bonds forged through sport, even in times of sorrow.
