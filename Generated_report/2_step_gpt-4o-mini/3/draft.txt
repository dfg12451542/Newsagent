In their opening match of the 2025 Island Games, Jersey's women football team faced a tough challenge, losing 3-1 to Bermuda, the reigning champions. The game took place on July 13, with Jersey initially taking the lead early on, thanks to a left-footed shot by Anita Tavares that found the corner of the net just six minutes in. However, the Bermudans quickly responded, showcasing their championship pedigree throughout the match.nnBermuda almost equalized shortly before halftime, striking the Jersey bar amid heated first-half penalty appeals from both teams. The equalizer came in the second half when Jaden Masters tapped in from close range after an hour. The momentum shifted further in favor of Bermuda as Khyla Brangman converted a penalty kick, giving them a 2-1 lead. The final nail in Jersey's hopes was driven home in stoppage time, as Jya Rattaray Smith capitalized on a rebound after a shot hit the bar, securing Bermuda’s victory and placing them at the top of Group B.nnThis defeat leaves Jersey at the bottom of their group, especially after Shetland and Isle of Wight played to a draw earlier on Sunday. The match follows a similar pattern to the opening game between these two teams at the previous Island Games in 2023, where Bermuda also emerged victorious with a scoreline of 4-0. Jersey will aim to bounce back in their next match against Shetland Islands on Monday, followed by a critical clash with Isle of Wight on Tuesday, as they seek to improve their standing in the tournament.
