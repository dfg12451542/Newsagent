The M11 has reopened near Stansted Airport following a significant vehicle fire that caused substantial delays on the busy motorway. As of July 13, motorists experienced congestion extending approximately 1.5 miles, with delays reaching up to 60 minutes near the affected area, according to a spokesperson for National Highways. 'There is approximately 1.5 miles congestion following this incident so please allow time for these delays to clear,' the spokesperson advised, stressing the impact of the fire on traffic flow.nnFire crews swiftly attended the scene to manage the situation, ensuring the safety of drivers affected by the unexpected incident. Eyewitness accounts revealed chaos as drivers were left stranded, with many exiting their vehicles and walking along the motorway while waiting for traffic to resume. Images captured during the incident show hundreds of cars halted, with some occupants leaving their doors open as they looked for alternatives to pass the time.nnThis disruption occurred on a Sunday morning when traffic is typically busy, particularly on routes leading to London Stansted Airport, a crucial gateway for travelers. The delays highlight ongoing challenges on major roadways, especially during peak travel times, as authorities continue to improve response strategies to minimize commuter inconveniences in the future.
