Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) has confirmed that a fire at a GAA club in west Belfast was started deliberately, causing extensive damage to the facility. The incident occurred around 18:20 BST on Saturday, as officials reported significant destruction to the club's amenities, which are vital for the local community.nnInsp Dalton of the PSNI condemned the act, stating, 'It's absolutely unacceptable that a community organisation should be targeted in this way.' The fire, described as a blatant act of vandalism by Belfast city councillor Arder Carson, sparked outrage among local residents and officials alike, particularly as the club is instrumental in providing opportunities for young people through various programs and summer camps.nnCarson expressed his anger, noting, 'It definitely fills you with despair and some anger that a club that does so much for the local community...has been subject to such an attack.' He emphasized the irony of the timing, as the club was in the midst of preparations for a championship season. Furthermore, Carson criticized the recklessness of the act, particularly given the recent dry weather conditions in the area, stating, 'Setting fire outdoors at any time is reckless but this level is just beyond reckless.'nnAuthorities are currently appealing for anyone with information about the incident to come forward, as the community grapples with the repercussions of this destructive act aimed at a cornerstone of local spirit and support.
