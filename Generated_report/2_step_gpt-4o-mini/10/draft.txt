St Mirren manager Stephen Robinson expressed his shock following his team's disappointing performance in the Premier Sports Cup opener against Arbroath, which ended in a goalless draw and a subsequent defeat on penalties. 'Shocked' Robinson apologized to the St Mirren fans who travelled to Gayfield Park to support the team, but left the ground unsatisfied with their outing. 'I'm in a little bit of shock,' he acknowledged in an interview with club media, highlighting his surprise at the players’ performance that deviated from their strong pre-season form.nnRobinson noted that the decision-making and simple passes exhibited by his squad were uncharacteristic of their pre-season training. 'The performance of maybe 13 of them is something I've not seen all pre-season. We've had a fantastic pre-season,' he remarked. The manager emphasized the team's collective failure to execute plays they had practiced rigorously, indicating a need for immediate improvement. He reflected on the disappointment felt not only by himself but also among the fans, many of whom had travelled considerable distances: 'It’s disappointing for us, disappointing for the people that travelled a long way on a Saturday when they could have been sitting in the garden – I bet they wish they had done.'nnLooking forward, Robinson remains optimistic that his team will bounce back. 'We have to react to it, we will be better. We have to do simple things which we didn't do,' he affirmed as St Mirren prepares for their next challenge against League 2’s Forfar Athletic on Tuesday. The St Mirren manager has faith in his players, stating, 'It’s a group of boys that have never let me down over a longer period of time.' Robinson's focus will be on renewing their competitive spirit as they face a challenging schedule ahead.
