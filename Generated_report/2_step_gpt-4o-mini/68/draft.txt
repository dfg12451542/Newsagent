In a remarkable achievement, amateur runner Zoe Tucker from Sherborne, Dorset, has won gold at the duathlon world championships, just months after taking up the sport. Competing in the sprint category, Tucker's victory came during a thrilling event held in Spain, where she not only emerged victorious in her category but also claimed the title of the fastest British woman across all age groups.nnDescribing the experience as 'an absolute honour,' Tucker reflected on the scale of the event, stating, 'The scale of the event, the opening ceremony, a parade of nations, walking with GB kit and flag - it was an incredible experience.' As she approached the final lap of the race, her sense of accomplishment surged, noting, 'Just coming round the track for the last lap, knowing I was probably going to get over that line first was incredible.' nnTucker, who admitted she 'absolutely never in my wildest dreams' expected to compete at the world championships, now aims to develop a training plan for the next competition in Abu Dhabi scheduled for next year. She expressed a desire to secure an indoor training bike, saying, 'I'm hopefully inspiring them a bit, but it’s been hard.'nnIn her journey, Tucker emphasized the importance of seizing opportunities: 'There are so many activities and opportunities and events - I just think if an opportunity comes your way, just grab it and enjoy it along the way.' Her inspiring performance has not only earned her a world championship title but also highlights the potential of newcomers entering the sport.
