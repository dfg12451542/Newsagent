Wales fly-half Dan Edwards celebrated a crucial victory against Japan, delivering a defiant message about the resilience of his team after their 22-31 win in Kobe. 'It was about getting a win, I'm absolutely chuffed,' Edwards stated, reflecting on the pressure his side faced following previous criticisms. He emphasized that this win served as a clear statement, showcasing the character of the Welsh team: 'We’ve come back and shown everyone why we’re here. It shows the character we’ve got. That was a real statement that when it gets tough, we can pull through.'
