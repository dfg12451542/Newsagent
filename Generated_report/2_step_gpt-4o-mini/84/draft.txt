A family from Hereford has expressed immense joy after traveling 800 miles (1,280 km) in their campervan to support England in the UEFA Women's Euro 2025. With a resounding victory over Wales, securing a 6-1 win at the St Gallen stadium in Switzerland, the Morris family witnessed history as they cheered on the Lionesses.nnKevin Morris, who attended the match with his wife, Kelly, and their children, Amelia, 16, and Ethan, 13, described the atmosphere as 'great.' 'As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,' said Morris. However, the journey back home wasn't without its challenges. After the match, he shared, 'It was just busy coming out of the stadium. We got on to the one train station; we couldn’t get on to the train. The next one came in, and we just stopped in the middle of nowhere.' The family eventually found a bus to return to their temporary lodging near Lake Constance, but it took them on a convoluted route before reaching their destination.nnThe Morris family’s adventure is part of a broader enthusiasm surrounding Euro 2025, marking Wales' first appearance at the Women's Euros, having never qualified for a major tournament before. As the Morris family prepares for their next stop, they have high hopes for England's continued success in the tournament. 'I think we can do it again,' Morris stated, expressing optimism for their chance to see the semi-finals, particularly as the family plans to begin their journey back to Hereford on July 24.nnTheir journey has been extensive, having already traveled to Paris, Courgenay, and Zurich, where they previously watched England play against the Netherlands. The family is set to watch England's quarter-final match against Sweden in Zurich on Thursday, reminiscent of the Euro 2022 semi-final clash. As they relive their memories from the night before, Morris concluded, 'It was worth it,' highlighting the family's commitment to supporting their national team.
