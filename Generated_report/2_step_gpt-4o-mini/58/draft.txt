Stars flocked to the Wimbledon finals on July 13, 2025, where Princess Kate and acclaimed actor Paul Mescal were among the high-profile attendees. The prestigious event showcased thrilling matches that concluded with the presentation of the coveted men's and women's trophies for the 2025 Wimbledon Championships. As the tournament's rich history continues to be celebrated, this year's finals brought together an elite audience, reiterating Wimbledon’s status as a pinnacle of both sports and social engagement.
