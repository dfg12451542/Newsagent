Great Britain's Oliver Rowland has clinched the Formula E championship title for the first time following a solid fourth-place finish at the Berlin E-Prix on July 13, 2025. Rowland, who drives for Nissan, secured the championship with two races remaining in the season, thereby becoming the 10th different driver to win the title in just 11 seasons of the all-electric racing series. 'Honestly, I was just thinking before the race not to have too much damage going into [the final round in] London,' Rowland confessed after the race, highlighting his cautious yet strategic approach to the finale.nnRowland's triumph is marked by impressive performances throughout the season, including wins in Mexico, Jeddah, Monaco, and Tokyo. He finished the Berlin race with a total of 184 points, leaving the championship contender, Pascal Wehrlein of Porsche, trailing significantly with only 125 points after a disappointing 16th-place finish despite starting from pole position at the historic Tempelhof airport venue.nnReflecting on his victory, Rowland expressed disbelief, stating, 'I can’t believe it, I have no words. It’s incredible. I was trying to play it safe but everybody was so aggressive, so at some point I was all in.' He also acknowledged his fortune in avoiding any trouble during the race, which was essential for maintaining his lead in the standings as the season approaches its conclusion. 'Thankfully I stayed out of trouble,' added Rowland.nnThe Berlin race concluded with Jaguar’s Nick Cassidy taking the victory, and Jake Dennis from Andretti finishing in second place, followed by Jean-Eric Vergne of DS Penske in third. With the season finale scheduled for London on July 26-27, Rowland's achievement not only highlights his talent but also serves as a testament to the competitive and evolving landscape of Formula E racing.
