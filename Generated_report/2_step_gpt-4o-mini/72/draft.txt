A car fire on the A30 in Cornwall has ignited a significant blaze on the nearby moorland, alarming local authorities and raising concerns about fire safety. Fire crews from Launceston and Bodmin responded promptly to tackle the flames, which quickly spread from the vehicle to the surrounding vegetation, underscoring the risks associated with fires in dry conditions prevalent in the region.nnThe incident occurred on the eastbound lane of the A30, near Temple Tor on Bodmin Moor, as reported by the Cornwall Fire & Rescue Service. Eyewitness accounts noted thick smoke rising from the scene as firefighters worked diligently to control both the car fire and the encroaching moorland blaze. nnFirefighters faced challenges due to the unpredictable nature of the fire as it spread into the dry foliage of the moorland, which has been experiencing dry weather conditions. Authorities emphasized the importance of fire safety, urging motorists and outdoor enthusiasts to exercise caution to prevent future outbreaks.nnThis incident highlights ongoing concerns surrounding fire management in Cornwall, especially in areas prone to wildfires. The region has faced similar challenges in the past, reflecting a growing need to improve preventive measures and educate the public on fire risks associated with outdoor activities.
