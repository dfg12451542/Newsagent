The Australian batsmen were initially in a promising position at 157 for 3, but they faltered dramatically, losing their final seven wickets for just 68 runs. Steve Smith led the way for Australia with 48 runs, while Cameron Green contributed 46, but their dismissals around the dinner break triggered the team's downfall. 'Every pitch has been tough.
