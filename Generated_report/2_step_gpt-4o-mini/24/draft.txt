A significant fire has erupted in fields near Piccards Farm House, close to Guildford, prompting a swift response from local fire services. At approximately 16:20 BST on Monday, the Surrey Fire & Rescue Service dispatched ten crews to tackle the large blaze in the Sandy Lane area. A spokesperson confirmed that 'the fire broke out in fields adjacent to Piccards Farm House,' indicating the urgency of the situation as teams worked to extinguish the flames before they could spread further.nnFortunately, local authorities have reported that no properties have been damaged in the blaze. However, due to the significant amount of smoke generated, residents in the vicinity have been advised to keep their windows and doors closed to mitigate health risks.nnThis incident comes amid ongoing concerns regarding fire safety in rural and suburban areas. Historical incidents of wildfires in Surrey, including a major fire near Roxeth Green Avenue in Harrow last year which required over 100 firefighters to control, highlight the growing need for vigilance during the warmer months when conditions can heighten fire risks. Community resilience and preparedness continue to be crucial as local governments address these environmental challenges.
