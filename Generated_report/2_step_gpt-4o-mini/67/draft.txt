Hibernian FC has announced the acquisition of Togo international striker Thibault Klidje from Luzern, breaking their transfer record in the process. The deal, described as undisclosed, is a significant investment for the Scottish club. Klidje, aged 24, has penned a three-year contract with an option for an additional year at Easter Road, signaling Hibs' intent to bolster their attacking lineup for the upcoming season.nnDavid Gray, Hibernian's manager, expressed enthusiasm about the new signing, stating that Klidje will add 'extra pace and trickery' to the team's front line. He praised the young striker's performance during his time at Luzern, highlighting his potential for development. 'He performed well for Luzern last season, and at 24 has a lot of room for development,' said Gray. He added, 'We want to help him progress, and look forward to working with him moving forward.'nnKlidje's move follows a pattern of strategic player acquisitions by Hibernian, which aim to enhance their competitiveness in Scottish football. As clubs increasingly invest in emerging talent, records are continuously being shattered, reflecting a growing emphasis on dynamism and skill in the squad.
