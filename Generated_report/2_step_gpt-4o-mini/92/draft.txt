Wycombe Wanderers have announced the signing of midfielder Ewan Henderson from Belgian club Beerschot, marking the club's eighth acquisition this summer. The 25-year-old Scotland youth international joins the ranks at Adams Park for an undisclosed fee, as the team bolsters its lineup ahead of the new season.nnWycombe head coach Mike Dodds expressed his enthusiasm for Henderson, describing him as 'a really energetic midfielder who's had a great start to his career in the top flights in Scotland and Belgium.' Dodds noted Henderson's experience and stated, 'He's played a lot of football in the past three years which stands him in good stead to come here and hopefully hit the ground running as he integrates into the squad in pre-season.'nnHenderson's addition comes as part of a larger strategy for Wycombe as they prepare for the coming season. The club has already added seven new players, including Armando Junior Quitirna, Taylor Allen, and Mikki van Sas among others. This concerted effort to rejuvenate the squad reflects Wycombe's ambition to build a competitive team capable of making a significant impact in the league.nnThe move from Beerschot, where Henderson has been playing in the Pro League, reflects his continued development in European football and adds depth to Wycombe's midfield, which is crucial for the upcoming challenges of the season ahead.
