Swansea made history on July 13, 2025, by hosting the UK's inaugural IRONMAN Pro Series edition. This groundbreaking event saw elite triathletes competing under scorching conditions, with air temperatures soaring above 28°C, necessitating a non-wetsuit swim as water temperatures exceeded the wetsuit-legal limit. Defending champion Harry Palmer triumphed with a time of 3:51:18, while overall Ironman Pro Series 2024 winner Kat Matthews claimed victory in the women’s category with a time of 4:20:37.nn“I feel like a completely different person, and it felt like a different event this time around,” Matthews reflected, reminiscing about her previous victory in Swansea three years prior. The competition was fierce, with Palmer noting that the age-group competitors faced particularly challenging conditions. “It was hot, and I do feel for all the age-groupers who are out there today,” Palmer told BBC Sport Wales.nnThousands of supporters lined the 70-mile course, providing encouragement and welfare support throughout the day, making it a memorable experience for both competitors and their families. The atmosphere was electric, with Race Director Rebecca Sutherland exclaiming, “It was another incredible race with thousands of athletes crossing the famous red carpet finish line to rapturous spectator support.”nnThis watershed moment for Swansea comes as the city continues to solidify its reputation in the triathlon community, with plans already in place for the return of the race next summer as the event enters its fourth edition. The successful execution of the IRONMAN Pro Series highlights not just the city's capacity to host significant sporting events but also the unwavering spirit and support of the local community.
