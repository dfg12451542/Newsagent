In a highly anticipated return, boxing legend Manny Pacquiao is set to face off against challenger Barrios this Saturday, rekindling the excitement of fans and sparking a debate around veteran fighters making comebacks. However, trainer Dave Coldwell has made it clear he will not tune in to watch the fight, expressing concerns about the implications of such a matchup. 'I won't be watching Manny Pacquiao's comeback fight on Saturday,' said Coldwell, who emphasized that his decision reflects deeper issues within the sport. 'It feels like a shortcut. A nod to nostalgia rather than merit, and that's unfair to fighters who are working their way up properly.'nnColdwell, who acknowledges Pacquiao's significant accomplishments as an 'eight-division world champion' and 'global icon,' raises questions about the precedent set when established legends return to the ring. He insists that this trend undermines emerging fighters who are diligently earning their place through rigorous competition. 'I had a brief exchange with Mauricio Sulaiman, the WBC president, on social media about this. He wasn't pleased with my doubt but, in my view, it sets a strange precedent,' Coldwell added.nnThe nature of Pacquiao's return has also drawn attention because he is skipping the customary preliminaries that often accompany such comebacks by battling at a high level right away. Coldwell noted, 'That's the part that really shocked me when I heard of Pacquiao's return. Usually, a fighter coming back from retirement eases into the sport with tune-up fights.' This raises alarms regarding the management of boxers who undertake such ambitious comebacks at an older age.nnAs the fight approaches, fans and analysts alike are reminded that while Pacquiao's legacy is etched in the history of boxing, every comeback carries inherent risks not just for the veteran but for the integrity of the sport itself. The excitement around his return is palpable, but it comes hand-in-hand with a critical dialogue about the future of boxing and the treatment of its athletes.
