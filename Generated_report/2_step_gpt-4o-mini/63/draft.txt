A significant fire broke out at the Worm's Head Hotel in Rhossili early Monday morning, causing devastating damage to the historic establishment. The Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service received the alarm at 00:51 BST and quickly responded to the blaze, which affected the accommodation block and spread throughout the entire roof space. The firefighters managed to bring the situation under control, extinguishing the fire by 05:30, but not before a large section of the roof collapsed, exposing blackened beams and an empty chimney stack.nnHotel owners described the incident as 'particularly heartbreaking and soul destroying,' expressing sorrow over the loss of 25 years of hard work that vanished in less than half an hour. In a Facebook statement, they conveyed their uncertain future, saying, 'Not sure how we come back from this at all let alone in the near term future but we will strive to come back stronger.' All hotel occupants were safely evacuated, and there were no reported casualties, although the emotional toll of the event has resonated deeply within the community.nnLocal patrons and annual visitors expressed their shock at the hotel's destruction. Long-time guest Sian Nichol, who cherished summer visits to the hotel, remarked, 'It’s absolutely shocking to see it like this.' Beverly, another frequent visitor, shared her emotions as she drove past the site: 'I felt quite emotional when we turned the corner.' Gareth lamented a future outing he had envisioned, stating, 'The vision I had today was to sit outside there with a nice meal and lager together.'nnIn response to the fire, the hotel management released a statement thanking the community for their support and affirming that the bar and hotel will remain closed pending further updates. This incident occurs amidst a backdrop of historical challenges for hotels in Gwynedd, where emergency services have often coordinated investigations into unfortunate fire incidents in recent years. The local community and former guests now await updates on the hotel’s future as they reflect on the beloved venue that has been a central part of their memories and celebrations.
