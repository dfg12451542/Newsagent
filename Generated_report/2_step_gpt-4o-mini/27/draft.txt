Tragedy struck in County Antrim on Saturday evening when a man lost his life in a single-vehicle crash on Ballyhill Road. According to police, the victim was driving a white Volkswagen Golf at approximately 17:50 BST when the incident occurred. Despite the swift response from the ambulance service, the driver was pronounced dead at the scene. Police reported that a male passenger was taken to the hospital; fortunately, his injuries are not believed to be life-threatening.nnAn investigation into the circumstances surrounding the crash is ongoing, with local authorities working to piece together the events leading up to the unfortunate incident. In light of the collision, Ballyhill Road was temporarily closed to facilitate emergency services but has since reopened to traffic.nnThe news of the tragedy has resonated deeply within the community, with prominent Sinn Féin politician Pádraig Mac Lochlainn expressing condolences. He described the news as 'heartbreaking,' highlighting the emotional impact such accidents have on families and friends as well as the greater community.nnThis incident is a stark reminder of road safety concerns prevalent in Northern Ireland, drawing attention to the need for awareness and caution among drivers. As residents come to terms with this loss, the community remains united in support for the victim's family during this difficult time.
