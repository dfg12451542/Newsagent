Channel Islands athletes had the opportunity of a lifetime as they met Princess Anne during the opening ceremony of the 20th Island Games in Orkney on July 13, 2025. Attendees described the encounter as a unique experience that underscored the significance of sporting camaraderie across small islands. 'The encounter was amazing,' remarked Ms. Longden, a coach who discussed her team’s journey with the Princess. She added, 'I have met royalty before but because of her love of sport, it was so good to talk to her.'nnThe opening ceremony, hosted by broadcaster Lorraine Kelly, was filled with memorable moments. Kelly expressed her emotional connection to the Orkney islands and praised the organization of the event. 'I don’t know that the others [Channel Islands] need me, I think because they’re so good,' she said, acknowledging the talented athletes present. Tickets to the ceremony were highly sought after, evidenced by Mr. Le Boutillier’s two-and-a-half-hour wait before gaining entry.nnDoreen Manson, who performed with her exercise group, highlighted a palpable excitement among the participants and the community, stating, 'It’s just something that we’ve never seen before, and all the different athletes coming in from different countries, it’s just great.' Her enthusiasm mirrored the overall sentiment as athletes from various Channel Islands entered the arena.nnThe Island Games originated with the vision of uniting athletes from small islands around the globe for a festive sports event. Fast forward to today, and the games continue to thrive, reinforcing connections and showcasing talent in a spirit of friendly competition. As the games kick off this year, it looks set to be an unforgettable celebration of sport and island identity.
