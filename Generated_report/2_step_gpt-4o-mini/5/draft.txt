Trust in the police within the Millfield area of Peterborough has seen a significant boost due to the Alliance project, which focuses on combating organized crime in the community. According to Peterborough City Council, confidence in policing has markedly increased alongside a remarkable '700% increase in community intelligence reporting.' This initiative is designed to foster stronger relationships between local residents and law enforcement, with results already showing promise. 'It's encouraging to see the community becoming more involved in reporting issues to the police,' a council spokesperson stated.nnThe successes of the Alliance project have not been limited to community engagement; substantial tangible results have also been achieved. The council reported the seizure of over 700,000 illegal cigarettes, 20,000 vapes, and £115,000 in cash from the area as part of the ongoing enforcement efforts. New interventions, such as the installation of drug needle bins, have contributed to the design of a safer and more secure environment for local residents.nnNevertheless, the project has faced challenges, particularly in forging connections with local groups. A recent report submitted to the council’s scrutiny committee indicated that while the project has made strides, it still struggles to engage effectively with some community members. Insp. Sam Tucker from Cambridgeshire Police acknowledged these hurdles, stating, 'We can definitely use some of the concepts in there, but to use this initiative in its entirety is unlikely.'nnIn terms of future endeavors, police officials indicate that agencies involved in the Alliance project will continue to pursue funding to sustain and possibly expand their activities in Millfield, but any rollout to other city areas will take time. 'This is an intense project focusing on longevity and sustainability,' said a police spokesperson. nnHistorically, the relationship between police and the communities they serve has been fraught with challenges. Det Supt Jon Capps previously noted the importance of fostering trust, emphasizing, 'We recognise that coming forward to the police in itself is a very big step and we need to do more in terms of trust and confidence.' The progress seen in Millfield serves as a potential blueprint for enhancing public trust and engagement in policing across the region.
