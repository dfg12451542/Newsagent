Crystal Palace has unveiled their new home kit for the 2025-26 season amid excitement surrounding their participation in European competitions. The announcement comes just days after the club's successful victory over Millwall, where they debuted the kit with a narrow 1-0 win in their pre-season kickoff. This season’s design reflects the club's continuing evolution and ambition as they strive for success both domestically and in Europe.nnFeatured in the launch event were several prominent players, including Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, and new signing Walter Benitez, who took the spotlight modeling the kit alongside fellow teammates like Eddie Nketiah and Chloe Arthur from the women’s side. The eye-catching light green goalkeeper jersey worn by Benitez marked a new chapter in Palace's effort to enhance their athletic apparel appeal.nnAs Crystal Palace prepares for a season filled with hopes and aspirations post their significant international engagements, their new kit symbolizes not just a fresh start but also the loyalty and passion of their fans. The Eagles aim to build on their impressive performance last season, looking forward to making a mark in both league play and on the European stage.
