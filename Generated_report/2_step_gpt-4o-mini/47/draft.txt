The operator of a waste recycling facility in South Elmsall, known as Mineral Processing Ltd., is appealing a decision that stripped the site of its operational license due to health hazard concerns. Carly Chambers, area manager for the Environment Agency (EA) in Yorkshire, confirmed that the appeal has been lodged and highlighted that the environmental permit will remain in effect until the Planning Inspectorate provides an outcome. 'The suspension notice, however, remains in place during this time,' she explained, adding that the order prevents any additional waste from being brought onto the site and mandates the removal of waste that contravenes permit conditions.nnAccording to Chambers, failure to comply with the suspension notice would constitute an offence. The EA has recognized the significant impact the site has on the local community, with Chambers stating, 'Our increased regulatory response continues.' Meanwhile, a spokesperson for Minore asserted that the company is currently excavating and processing materials in compliance with the notice, maintaining that routine water sampling has shown no detrimental effects on nearby Frickley Beck.nnThe situation has raised concerns among employees at the site, with the spokesperson indicating that the workforce, many of whom have been at the site for over a decade, now fear for their jobs due to the heightened scrutiny of operations. As the appeal process unfolds, the community and stakeholders remain anxious about the potential implications for public health and environmental safety.
