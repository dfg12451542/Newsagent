A West Yorkshire Police officer has been charged with sexual assault, raising serious concerns about conduct within the force. PC Jack Blagbrough, 31, who is currently assigned to the Kirklees District, is scheduled to appear at Barnsley Magistrates' Court on 16 July, following the allegations which stem from an incident involving a woman while he was off-duty.
