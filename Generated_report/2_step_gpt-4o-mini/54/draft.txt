A tragic accident near Loch Lomond claimed the life of a 71-year-old motorcyclist during a six-vehicle crash on Saturday, July 13, 2025. The man, who was riding a Harley Davidson equipped with a sidecar, died at the scene, leaving behind a community in mourning. 'Sadly, the male motorcyclist died and our thoughts are with his family and friends,' stated Sgt. Kenny Malaney of Police Scotland, emphasizing the profound impact of such an event on loved ones.nnThe collision involved multiple vehicles, but fortunately, the drivers of the cars were not injured. Eyewitnesses are being encouraged to assist with the ongoing investigation. 'Our inquiries are continuing to establish the full circumstances of what happened,' Sergeant Malaney added. The road was closed for more than six hours as police undertook investigations to piece together the events leading up to the crash, heightening concerns about road safety in the area, which was particularly busy at the time.nnThis incident marks a somber reminder of the dangers that motorcyclists face on the roads, particularly in areas like Loch Lomond, known for its picturesque landscape and popular tourist routes. Despite the beauty of the surroundings, the collision underscores the importance of vigilance and caution while driving. Authorities are appealing to anyone with dashcam footage or who witnessed the crash to come forward to assist in the investigation.
