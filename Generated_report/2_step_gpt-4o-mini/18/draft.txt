Rory McIlroy displayed a positive outlook following his performance at the 2025 Scottish Open, where he finished 13-under par with a tally of 19 birdies throughout the tournament. Despite not clinching the trophy, McIlroy expressed contentment with his game during the week, stating, 'No frustration; it’s been a great week.' He highlighted his effective shot control and reported that the windy conditions proved challenging for placement on the greens.nn'I’m really happy with where my game is; the way I played over the weekend,' McIlroy elaborated, noting that his focus was on performance rather than the end result. Reflection on the scoring conditions led him to mention, 'I feel like the greens got a little bumpy. I had some good putts that just didn't go in, whether they were mis-reads or poor speed.'
