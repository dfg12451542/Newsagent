Toprak Razgatlioglu has seized the lead in the World Superbike Championship after an impressive triple victory at Donington Park. The BMW rider clinched the second feature race on Sunday, propelling him to the top of the leaderboard, ahead of title rival Nicolo Bulega by four points.nnRazgatlioglu's weekend dominance became evident when he closed the gap to just one point with his win in the Superpole sprint race, outpacing Ducati's Bulega. His Sunday triumph in the feature race not only secured him a pivotal championship lead but also marked his third victory of the weekend, following his wins in Saturday's opening feature race and the Superpole event.nnReflecting on the dramatic shifts in the standings, reigning champion Razgatlioglu's victory reshaped the competitive landscape of the 2025 season. Behind him, Alvaro Bautista maintained a steady pace, finishing third, while Danilo Petrucci and Andrea Locatelli rounded out the top five. Jonathan Rea, Northern Ireland's esteemed six-time champion, despite his promising fifth-place finish on Saturday, could only manage 15th on Sunday.nnThe triple-win milestone at Donington Park is significant for Razgatlioglu, setting a precedent as he strives for yet another title. The Turkish racer, known for his resolute track performance, has once again demonstrated his prowess and emerged as a formidable contender in this year's championship race.
