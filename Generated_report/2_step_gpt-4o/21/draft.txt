Businesses in Jersey and Guernsey are increasingly calling for enhanced collaboration across the Channel Islands, according to the latest economic confidence survey conducted by the Institute of Directors (IoD) Jersey. Released yesterday, the survey gathered insights from 70 members and highlighted a notable demand for strategic unity to tackle mutual economic challenges shared by both islands.nnThe survey findings revealed that while 56% of participants expressed optimism regarding their own organizations, only 17% felt optimistic about Jersey's broader economic outlook. As Alex Ruddy, IoD Jersey Chair, emphasized, the survey provides a 'clear mandate for action.' He highlighted the need for 'bold, strategic investment and policy clarity' as essential steps to restore economic confidence. Ruddy further underscored the necessity for collaboration across the Channel Islands, along with support for innovation, infrastructure, and housing as key contributors to Jersey's future prosperity.nnReflecting similar sentiments, Richard Hemans, IoD Guernsey's lead on economics, noted the business community's recognition that closer cooperation between the islands is crucial. 'Given the same challenges apply to both islands and our economic, geographical, demographic, constitutional, and fiscal profiles are so similar, the best way to confront our mutual challenges is to work together more closely,' said Hemans. The survey's responses echoed Guernsey IoD's own survey results published recently in June, which similarly urged political leaders to foster closer ties.nnIn terms of actionable steps, Jersey respondents expressed interest in leveraging Guernsey's strategies for addressing fiscal deficits, particularly through reducing public expenditure and promoting economic growth. Survey results also suggested that entrepreneurship and innovation could benefit significantly from tax incentives, youth talent attraction, and skills development. Furthermore, the survey found that while AI adoption is still in its nascent stages, 70% of companies are offering training in AI, and 33% have strategies in development.nnAs the Channel Islands navigate these complex economic landscapes, the call for joint efforts reflects a shared commitment to safeguarding their economic futures. This collaboration not only aims to enhance the current economic environment but also paves the way for sustainable growth and development across the islands.
