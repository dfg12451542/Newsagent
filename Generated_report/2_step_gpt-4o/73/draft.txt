F1: The Movie's Callie Cooke stunned fans by performing a real pit stop during the British Grand Prix at Silverstone, in 2024. Cooke, who stars in the film, described the experience as 'mad' and a highlight of her career. Speaking about her unique experience, Cooke enthused about the special connection to Silverstone, located in Northamptonshire, which is not far from her own upbringing. 'It feels so special and so real, it's just been the coolest thing,' she remarked.nnThe film, which was released globally on June 25, 2025, revolves around a fictional Formula 1 team, APXGP. As part of the production, the filmmakers were granted access to an operational garage within a real F1 pit lane at Silverstone during the 2023 and 2024 Grand Prix events. Callie Cooke trained alongside professional mechanics and stunt performers, making her the sole actor embedded in the pit crew amid seasoned professionals. 'I was the only actor in the pit crew,' she recalled, highlighting how she learned to change tires, oil, and the wing of the car.nnReflecting on the intensity of the experience, Cooke shared, 'We did a real pit stop in between Mercedes and Ferrari, and we did it as quick as the other teams.' The experience was not only challenging but also rewarding, as it pushed her to represent a woman in a typically male-dominated industry. Her experience was summarized as 'once-in-a-lifetime' both for her and the entire cast and crew who had the opportunity to work in and around real F1 teams. The experience at the Silverstone Circuit stands out as one of the most thrilling points of her career. Furthermore, the film's authenticity is cemented by such real-world experiences, adding a layer of genuine excitement and realism to F1: The Movie's portrayal of the high-adrenaline world of Formula 1 racing.
