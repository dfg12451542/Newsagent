More arrests have been made following a stabbing incident in Eyres Monsell, Leicester, that took place late Friday evening. According to police reports, four additional individuals have been arrested, raising the number of suspects involved in the incident. A 36-year-old woman was previously detained on suspicion of attempted murder, and police have now apprehended two men, aged 18 and 29, under the same charges.nnIn addition to the initial arrests, a 38-year-old woman has been taken into custody on suspicion of conspiracy to commit murder, and a 25-year-old woman is being held on suspicion of assisting an offender. Of those detained, the 18-year-old man and the 25-year-old woman are currently in police custody, while the other suspects have been released on police bail pending further investigation.nnThe incident occurred shortly after 9:15 PM BST in the vicinity of Sturdee Road, where a man in his 20s sustained serious injuries following an altercation. Despite the severity of his injuries, the victim managed to reach Lawford Road, from where he was subsequently taken to the hospital. He is reported to be in stable condition.nnDet Sgt Adrian Davies has appealed to the public for any information that might aid in the ongoing investigation. 'We believe the stabbing happened shortly before 9:15 pm,' he stated, urging residents who might have been outdoors given the warm weather to come forward if they witnessed anything suspicious. He emphasized, 'If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us.'nnAcknowledging the connections between the suspects and the victim, as noted in the police statement, authorities have assured the public of the continuation of thorough house-to-house inquiries and an exhaustive examination of the crime scene to gather further evidence in this complex case.
