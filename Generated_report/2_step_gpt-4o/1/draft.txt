Colchester is set to host a memorial running race in honor of Arthur Whiston, a cherished community member and athlete, who tragically passed away in a road accident last year. The event, organized by Colchester Harriers AC, Mr. Whiston's running club, will take place on Sunday at the garrison athletics track in the city, commencing at 09:30 BST.nnArthur Whiston, who passed away on July 5, 2024, at the age of 73, was a beloved figure in Colchester. He met his untimely demise while cycling home along Argents Lane near West Bergholt, Essex. Lydia Cunningham, a fellow club member, recalled, 'Arthur was incredibly loved by the community.' She further described him as 'a very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular.' In memory of his contributions and the joy he spread, the club has initiated this annual event.nnProceeds from the memorial race will be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity that Mr. Whiston ardently supported. The event is expected to draw large crowds, as runners are encouraged to participate by registering on the day of the race. This gesture not only honors Mr. Whiston's memory but also continues his legacy of supporting a cause close to his heart.nnParticipants and spectators alike are invited to celebrate the life of a man who made a significant impact on those around him. The race serves as a testament to Arthur Whiston's enduring spirit and the profound connection he shared with the community.
