Kilmarnock fans have expressed their elation following an invigorating victory over Brora Rangers, marking an auspicious start to the 'Kettlewell era'. The clash, which ended with a win for Kilmarnock, was not just an ordinary game; it represented the first competitive outing under new manager Stuart Kettlewell, and showcased his nine new recruits. 'We asked you for your views as Kilmarnock kicked off the Kettlewell era with victory over Brora Rangers,' remarked a source close to the team.nnSupporters were keen to voice their opinions after the hard-fought encounter. Gordon, a long-time supporter, commented, 'A very good, competitive game really enjoyed it. Not as easy as I thought it would be but the three points are the most important thing.' He further lauded the team’s performance saying, 'Well done Killie and Kettlewell. Bring on Livingston on Wednesday hopefully another three points.'nnThe match, played on Saturday, saw Kilmarnock not only test their new strategies but also set the tone for the rest of the season. Amy, another enthusiastic fan, highlighted the promising performance of young Ben Brannan, likening him to former player Aaron Hickey due to his versatility and stamina. 'A long day but an enjoyable one. Young Ben Brannan reminds me of Aaron Hickey - can play RWB or LWB, is comfortable coming inside and taking men on and has a great engine. Hope to see more of him this season,' she said.nnLooking forward, Kilmarnock fans are hopeful and excited about the team's trajectory under Kettlewell’s guidance. George, a jubilant fan, asserted the importance of improving the team's shape and tactics, praising the recent solid signings. 'Solid start, keep on improving the team's shape and tactics will come together. Good solid signings. Come on the Killie,' he concluded, capturing the anticipation and optimism that surrounds the club’s upcoming fixtures, starting with Livingston.
