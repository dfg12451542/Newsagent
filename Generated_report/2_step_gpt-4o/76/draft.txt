M11 Reopened Near Stansted Airport After Vehicle FirennThe M11 motorway, a key route leading to Stansted Airport, has been reopened following a vehicle fire that caused significant disruptions on Sunday. Emergency services rushed to the scene, and the fire crews tackled the blaze, which resulted in massive traffic jams and commuter frustration.nnAccording to a spokesperson from National Highways, motorists faced delays of up to 60 minutes on approach as the fire was being extinguished and the debris cleared from the roadway. 'There is approximately 1.5 miles congestion following this incident, so please allow time for these delays to clear,' the spokesperson advised travelers.nnImages from the scene showed a significant number of vehicles halted on the motorway, with some passengers having exited their cars and were seen walking on the highway. This unprecedented congestion highlighted the vulnerability of transportation arteries to unexpected incidents, especially those leading to a major airport.nnWhile the motorway has now been reopened, the ripple effects of the morning's events are expected to continue impacting traffic flow in the surrounding areas. Authorities encourage drivers to remain patient and to plan for possible delays as the aftermath is fully resolved.
