A biker is currently in hospital with serious injuries following a crash on the rural B6399 road near Hawick in the Borders. The accident took place at approximately 12:00 on Sunday and involved a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike. Emergency services were promptly dispatched to the scene just south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm.nnThe rider, a 57-year-old man, was swiftly taken to the hospital for treatment of his serious injuries. In response, the road was closed for about eight hours, as authorities worked to investigate the incident and secure the area. The local police have issued an appeal for any witnesses who might provide further insights into the circumstances leading to the crash.nnThis accident adds to a troubling history of vehicular incidents in the region. According to data from crashmap.co.uk, the area has witnessed several accidents up to 2023, notably around the intersection of Eye Road and Eastfield Road. While unrelated, another recent collision in Scotland involved a black Audi A4 and a blue BMW M2 on the A89 Clark Street, highlighting ongoing road safety concerns.nnAuthorities continue to work diligently to improve road safety across Scotland, especially on rural roads known for their challenging terrain and limited visibility. The police urge all motorists to exercise caution, particularly on less-traveled roads where emergency response times may be longer. Witnesses or anyone with information about the recent Hawick incident are encouraged to contact Police Scotland.
