The Open 2025 saw an early surge of enthusiasm as massive crowds flocked to Royal Portrush on Monday morning to witness Rory McIlroy's first practice round. The world number two, revered both locally and globally, was a major draw at the storied course, marking the start of a week that promises competitive thrills and high drama as The Open unfolds from July 13 to July 20.nnFans gathered in throngs to see McIlroy, whose star has only continued to rise since his memorable victory at Hoylake in 2014. His performance did not disappoint. In an impressive display of skill, McIlroy delighted the gallery by sinking a birdie putt on the 18th green. Following his round, in a gesture connecting with the enthusiasm of his supporters, he took the time to sign autographs, spreading cheer among the excited onlookers.nnThe spotlight shines brightly on McIlroy, a native of Northern Ireland, especially as the event returns to Royal Portrush—a course with deep historical roots in The Open's celebrated history. The last time The Open took place here was in 2019, a tournament remembered for Shane Lowry's triumphant finish. Both McIlroy and Lowry are scheduled to speak to the media today, with McIlroy at 14:00 BST and Lowry at 15:00 BST, setting the stage for what promises to be a much-anticipated week of golf at the challenging links course.nnMcIlroy's popularity and his roots in Northern Ireland add a layer of emotional resonance to the event, with many locals hoping to see their favorite claim the Claret Jug on home soil. As the crowds continue to gather, the palpable excitement and anticipation are indicative of The Open's enduring appeal and the passionate following it commands globally.
