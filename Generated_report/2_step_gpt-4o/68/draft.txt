Duathlon: Sherborne's Zoe Tucker wins world championship goldnnSherborne amateur running enthusiast Zoe Tucker has captured a gold medal at the duathlon world championships, just months into her journey with the sport. Tucker described her victory as an 'incredible experience,' after emerging triumphant in the sprint category at the recent competition held in Spain.nnIn an outstanding feat, Tucker ascended to the position of world champion in the sprint distance, which involved a demanding course consisting of a 5km run, followed by a 19.7km bike ride, and concluding with a 2.5km run. She not only led in her category but was also the fastest British woman overall across all age groups. 'Just coming round the track for the last lap, knowing I was probably going to get over that line first was incredible,' she exclaimed.nnTucker reflected on her journey with awe, admitting that she had 'absolutely never in my wildest dreams' expected to compete, let alone win, at the world championships. She detailed the grandeur of the event, noting the emotional impact of the 'opening ceremony, a parade of nations, walking with GB kit and flag.' The experience, she noted, was 'an absolute honour.'nnWith an eye towards the future, Tucker expressed her aspirations for continued excellence in the sport. She is aiming to develop a robust training plan ahead of the next world championships scheduled for Abu Dhabi next year. Her sights are set on acquiring an indoor training bike to help sustain her competitive edge at the highest level. Reflecting on her remarkable achievement, she encouraged others to 'grab [opportunities] and enjoy [them] along the way,' hoping to inspire others while acknowledging the challenges that lie ahead.
