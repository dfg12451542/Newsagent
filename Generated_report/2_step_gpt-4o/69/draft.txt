Crews from the Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) are currently battling a significant fire at the White Lund Industrial Estate in Morecambe. The incident, which began around 09:30 BST, has resulted in massive plumes of smoke visible from miles away. Firefighters on the scene are working tirelessly to control the blaze, ensuring no injuries have occurred so far, although the smoke continues to billow above the site.nnA spokesperson for LFRS confirmed that eight fire engines have been dispatched, emphasizing that crews are 'working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely.' Group manager Mark Warwick highlighted the challenges posed by the large volumes of smoke, advising local residents to 'keep windows and doors closed if you see or smell any smoke in the area.' Amidst the ongoing efforts, nearby roads have been closed, particularly parts of the Bay Gateway, to allow emergency services unhindered access to tackle the fire.nnThe incident is reminiscent of similar emergencies in industrial areas, highlighting the importance of swift and coordinated responses. Historical records show previous instances of industrial fires, such as one at Leyton, London, which required extensive resources to manage similar situations.nnAuthorities are urging the public to remain vigilant and cooperate with road closures and other safety measures in place. As firefighting efforts continue, residents are advised to stay away from the affected area and remain informed through official updates.
