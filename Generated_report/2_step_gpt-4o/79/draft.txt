In a remarkable display of vitality and generosity, Phyll Babb from Salisbury, Wiltshire celebrated her 100th birthday by completing a 200-mile walking challenge. What began as an ambitious goal of walking 100 miles soon doubled when Ms. Babb realized she was ahead of schedule in January. By the time she crossed the finish line on Sunday, her efforts had raised over £11,000 for the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless (STFH).nnAs Ms. Babb completed her final mile around the iconic Salisbury Cathedral, she was met with cheers from family members and supporters. 'It's hard to believe I've done 200 walks. It's gone like a dream,' she exclaimed. Despite the challenging nature of her endeavor, Ms. Babb maintained a cheerful outlook throughout, affirming, 'It's been delightful. I've had a ball actually.' Her commitment to walking stems from her upbringing, where walking was a natural part of life. 'Walking is good for you,' she noted, mentioning how it still brings her joy.nnPraising Ms. Babb, David Evans, a family friend, remarked, 'To reach the age of 100 and to do it so fit, looking so well, being so articulate, that's the really amazing thing she's done.' Liz Huntley, another family member, echoed these sentiments, describing Ms. Babb as an inspiration. The 200-mile journey was meticulously logged with the help of a local accountant, ensuring each step was counted. Ms. Babb expressed her gratitude for the support, saying, 'People had been extremely generous, thank you all very, very much.'nnGordon Pardy, head of fundraising for the STFH, shared his enthusiasm over the significant contribution Ms. Babb's efforts will provide for the charity. The funds will assist in supporting single homeless individuals, aligning with the mission of the Salisbury Trust. He added, 'We bought a house recently, and so the money that Phyll has raised will go towards furnishing the house,' highlighting the tangible impact of her efforts.nnPhyll Babb's incredible journey exemplifies not only her own vitality and perseverance but also the indomitable spirit of community support and goodwill. Her story serves as a remarkable testament to the power of dedication at any age.
