The Evian Championship concluded with a dramatic finish in France, where Australia's Grace Kim captured her first major title in a gripping play-off against Atthaya Thitikul. The triumph unfolded after Kim holed a decisive 12-foot eagle putt on the second extra playing of the 18th hole, ensuring her victory over good friend Thitikul, who narrowly missed an eight-foot birdie putt that could have granted her a maiden major win.nnEngland's Lottie Woad, despite a valiant effort, saw her hopes dashed as she missed what could have been a play-off-qualifying 10-foot birdie putt, eventually finishing at 13-under par with a superb seven-under 64. Her steely performance in the finale was not enough as Thitikul's birdie on the 17th hole and Kim's four-shot rally in the closing stages both bested her score. Before this climax, Woad humorously remarked to her caddie on the improbability of her immediate win, aware of Thitikul's strong position, 'Is there even any point, she's not making six.'nnKim's victory was solidified in a series of tense moments, with both she and Thitikul securing birdies on the first replay of the 18th. It was Kim's strategic finesse and consistent scoring across the extra holes that delivered her the coveted trophy. Among the spectators at the driving range observing the nail-biting showdown was Lottie Woad, whose completion of the tournament granted her key points toward the LPGA's Elite Amateur Pathway.nnWoad expressed her intentions moving forward, mentioning discussions with family and coaches about pursuing opportunities on the LPGA Tour. 'It has always been in my dreams to play on the LPGA Tour and I'm grateful for the opportunities of the programme,' she stated in relation to achieving a spot on the prestigious circuit. With this event in France, she accrued sufficient points, poising her for her next competitive chapter as she transitions to compete against the sport's luminaries stateside.
