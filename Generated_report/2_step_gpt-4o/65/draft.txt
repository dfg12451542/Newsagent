Chelsea claimed a stunning 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain to secure the Club World Cup in New Jersey on Sunday. The spotlight, however, was unexpectedly shared with former U.S. President Donald Trump, who remained on stage with the players during the trophy presentation, alongside Fifa president Gianni Infantino.nnReece James, Chelsea's captain, was taken by surprise at Trump's prolonged presence. 'They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage,' James said. 'He just congratulated me and the team for lifting the trophy and told us to enjoy the moment.' Cole Palmer echoed James’s bewilderment, admitting he was unaware Trump would remain on the stand. 'I was a bit confused, yes,' Palmer commented.nnTrump, who was enjoying the match with Infantino, expressed his pleasure during a half-time interview with Dazn, stating he was 'having a really great time.' Known for his increasing engagement with sports during his second term, Trump's presence at the final is part of a broader pattern, having attended major sporting events including the Super Bowl and supporting future events like the NFL Draft in Washington DC scheduled for 2027.nnReflecting on the event and his enduring friendship with Infantino, Trump remarked humorously on the longstanding debate over the sport's name in the U.S. 'Gianni is a friend of mine, he's done such a great job with the league,' he said, whimsically pondering if an executive order would be necessary to change the term 'soccer' to 'football' in American discourse. As Chelsea celebrated their triumph, with Trump in the midst of the jubilant team, the match will be remembered not only for the Blues' victory but also for the unexpected presence that lingered during the winning moment.
