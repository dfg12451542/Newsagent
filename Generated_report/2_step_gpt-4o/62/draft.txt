MasterChef presenter John Torode finds himself at the center of a controversy, following allegations of using racist language. The claims, which stem from remarks allegedly made in 2018 or 2019, emerged as part of an inquiry into separate allegations against co-host Gregg Wallace. In an Instagram post on Monday, Torode addressed the situation, acknowledging the allegation but maintaining he has 'no recollection' of the incident.nnTorode confirmed his involvement in the alleged incident in a statement, emphasizing transparency. 'For the sake of transparency, I confirm that I am the individual who is alleged to have used racial language on one occasion,' he stated. The chef also expressed that the recipient of the comment did not perceive it to be malicious and that he allegedly offered an immediate apology.nnThe allegations against Torode have been maintained as part of a broader investigation into complaints surrounding the show. Historical context provides that two separate allegations involving other individuals—one specifically relating to the use of racist language—were substantiated. Despite his adamant denial of the recollection and occurrence of the alleged incident, Torode clearly articulated his stance against racial language, declaring, 'I want to be clear that I've always had the view that any racial language is wholly unacceptable in any environment. I'm shocked and saddened by the allegation as I would never wish to cause anyone any offence.'nnAs the host of a beloved culinary program, Torode's reputation is significantly in the public eye. The situation further underscores ongoing challenges within the entertainment industry to address issues of racial conduct and accountability effectively.
