A young child in Oxfordshire is struggling to fit into the school environment, according to accounts from their mother, Lauren, who described the situation as 'harrowing.' Lauren, which is not her real name, recounted how severe her child's anxiety around attending school had become. Her child once told her they 'didn't want to live any more,' illustrating the extreme distress he faces over attending school. The child's anxiety has resulted in them spending only about half an hour in school each day, with Lauren often waiting with them for up to two hours.nnThe emotional and logistical toll on Lauren is immense. 'You question all those things when, deep down, you know your child and you know they're unhappy,' she said, expressing the emotional conflict faced by parents in such situations. Her comments highlight the frustration many parents feel when their children do not fit into the traditional education system, a scenario she describes as 'harrowing.'nnOn the brighter side, Lauren mentioned that her child finds solace and engagement in an online version of school sessions. 'Even if they're having a really bad week, they'll go to that session, and they'll come out really buoyant and happy,' she noted.
