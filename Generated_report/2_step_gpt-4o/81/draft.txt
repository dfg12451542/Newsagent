Channel Islanders had a rare opportunity to meet Princess Anne at the opening ceremony of the 20th Island Games in Orkney on July 13, 2025. The event was described by several athletes as a 'once in a lifetime' experience, highlighting the significance of the occasion for both participants and spectators alike. Notably, Alderney runner Benjamin Mutua and his coach, Julie Longden, were among those who spoke with Princess Anne, discussing their journey to Orkney and the games themselves.nnJulie Longden expressed her enthusiasm about the encounter, stating it was 'amazing' and particularly special due to Princess Anne’s passion for sports. 'I have met royalty before, but because of her love of sport, it was so good to talk to her,' Longden shared. Meanwhile, Lorraine Kelly, a television presenter, felt 'very emotional' during the ceremony, applauding the event organizers and expressing a fondness for the Orkney islands, applauding the vibrant support from the Channel Islands athletes.nnSpectators from Jersey, including Lucy and Nick Le Boutillier, traveled specifically to support their daughter Tilly, a gymnast in the competition. Their journey involved flying to Glasgow and then driving a motorhome to Orkney, meticulously planned since Tilly secured her place in the squad. Mrs. Le Boutillier praised the 'glorious' weather and the opening ceremony's vibrant atmosphere, while Mr. Le Boutillier highlighted the logistical challenges they encountered, such as a lengthy queue to attend the event, owing to a cap of approximately 3,000 attendees.nnAdding to the day's excitement, Doreen Manson, a lifelong resident of Orkney, performed with her exercise group to promote fitness among older people. Describing the games as a 'great buzz', Manson noted the unique atmosphere created by the diverse assembly of athletes from various countries. The Island Games, hosted by Orkney this year, will continue until July 18, offering a stage for athletes from across the globe to compete in a unique spirit of camaraderie and sportsmanship.
