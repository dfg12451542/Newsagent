This week in Tyneside, several notable local stories have emerged, capturing both the public's imagination and concern. A new permanent memorial to the iconic Sycamore Gap tree, famously felled illegally two years ago, was unveiled at the Sill National Landscape Discovery Centre. The memorial allows the public to touch part of the tree's trunk, bringing the historical wonder back to life in a tangible way. Artist Charlie Winney expressed amazement at the memorial, remarking that they were 'blown away by how huggable it is.'
