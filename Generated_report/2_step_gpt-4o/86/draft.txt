Powys toddler, Caitlyn, becomes one of the youngest competitors to qualify for Crufts, the world-renowned dog show which is set to take place next year. Just three years old, Caitlyn and her eight-year-old Miniature Schnauzer, Zeeva, have captured hearts already, taking a winning spot at the Three Counties Championship Show last month, which secured their place at Crufts.nnThe little girl, hailing from Groesffordd near Brecon, Powys, has displayed exceptional skill and enthusiasm in the show ring. Her mother, Lydia Mellor, expressed immense pride, noting how Caitlyn's love for the dogs and her inherent talent have brought them such success. 'She is so confident generally and she works so well with the dogs. She loves the dogs,' Lydia commented, adding that Caitlyn has also shown excellent sportsmanship, a trait she highly values.nnCaitlyn's success story is not only heartwarming but also reflects a passion and dedication evident even at such a young age. 'I like running around with her', Caitlyn shared, expressing a clear and simple joy in her accomplishment. According to Helen Kerfoot, a spokesperson for Crufts, Caitlyn's achievement is a testament to hard work and dedication, and demonstrates the accessibility of dog shows to participants of all ages.nnAs anticipation builds for next year's Crufts, the entry of such a young competitor has infused fresh enthusiasm into the event. Lydia Mellor underscores the importance of children like Caitlyn in keeping the spirit of these competitions alive. 'I am massively proud of the sportsmanship she is showing at this age,' she remarked, emphasizing that dog shows are not mere beauty pageants but celebrations of companionship and care for pets. Caitlyn's journey continues to inspire, promising a delightful showcase at the upcoming event.
