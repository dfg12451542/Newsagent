Tragedy struck Lexington, Kentucky, on Sunday when a gunman opened fire at a church, killing two women and injuring two men. The incident began with a routine traffic stop around 11:36 a.m. local time near Blue Grass Airport, where the suspect, later identified as 47-year-old Guy House, shot and injured a state trooper. Non-life-threatening injuries were reported for the trooper, according to Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers.nnIn a deadly sequence of events, House carjacked a vehicle and drove approximately 16 miles to Richmond Road Baptist Church. Upon arrival, he opened fire on parishioners gathering for service. The church, described by a county coroner official as a 'tight-knit group,' was the scene of heartbreak as 72-year-old Beverly Gum and 32-year-old Christina Combs lost their lives during the attack. Two male parishioners, whose names have not been released, were also shot—one critically injured, the other stabilizing at a local hospital.nnAuthorities were quick to respond, and House was shot dead by law enforcement at the scene. Police suspect that House had some connection to individuals within the church community, though this remains under investigation by the Kentucky State Police and the department's Public Integrity Unit. Chief Weathers and other officials are urging the community to remain calm as they piece together the suspect's motives and connections. Meanwhile, churchgoers and the community are left reeling from the violence inflicted during what should have been a sanctuary of peace and worship.
