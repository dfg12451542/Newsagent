Hereford campervan family cheers Lionesses to victory. A family from Hereford embarked on an 800-mile journey in their campervan to support the Lionesses against Wales in the UEFA Women's Euros. Their efforts were rewarded as they celebrated England's emphatic 6-1 victory that secured a place in the quarter-finals. Kevin Morris, joined by his wife Kelly, daughter Amelia, and son Ethan, was overjoyed as England delivered another dazzling performance at the St Gallen stadium in Switzerland. 'As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,' said Kevin.The Morris family had quite the adventure, leaving Hereford and travelling through several European cities, including Paris and Zurich, before arriving at their current location in Arbon, near Lake Constance, where Germany, Austria, and Switzerland meet. Despite the late-night challenges of returning from the stadium, including extended waits for public transport, Kevin Morris shared with BBC Radio Hereford & Worcester that 'it was worth it.' After resting, the family looks forward to the upcoming quarter-final, where England will face Sweden in Zurich this Thursday.Historically, this tournament marks significant progress for women's football in Wales, as the Welsh team qualified for Euro 2025, although they faced tough fixtures against top teams like the Netherlands and France. The Lionesses, building upon their Euro 2022 success, are setting their sights on the semi-finals. 'I think we can do it again,' expressed Kevin, hopeful his family will witness further triumphs in Switzerland before heading back to Hereford on July 24.
