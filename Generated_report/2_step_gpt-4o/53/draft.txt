ArtHouse Jersey awards local artists development grants, offering a boost to creative projects on the island. The Jersey-based arts charity has announced the recipients of its second round of development grants, providing crucial funding to support burgeoning talent. Among those selected are actor and writer Jack Evans-Rentsch, who is crafting a new comedy about the unique aspects of remote island politics.nnOne significant project to benefit from the grants is a short film by Tiny Speck Productions, led by Evans-Rentsch. 'There are plans to shoot the short film on location in Jersey with a local cast and crew,' confirmed Evans-Rentsch, underscoring the local-first approach of the initiative. The project embodies ArtHouse Jersey's objective to bolster creative work within the island’s community.nnThe support from ArtHouse Jersey extends beyond film. FlashMob band member Henry Matson revealed that the band has received funding for a second EP, which they plan to record by the year's end. 'The support that ArtHouse Jersey provides to local artists is invaluable,' Matson said, emphasizing the opportunities the grants create for grassroots artists.nnHanni El Dardiry Osman, another recipient, stated the grant will facilitate his training to safely operate a chainsaw for processing timber into guitars, highlighting the diverse range of projects supported by ArtHouse Jersey. Meanwhile, filmmaker Sean Frost expressed gratitude for the funding, which will enable him to work on an immersive short film titled Memoriam. 'ArtHouse Jersey's generous grant will provide the majority of the budget for the film,' he noted, with plans to shoot it in Jersey come autumn.nnArtHouse Jersey’s quarterly funding initiative has long been a cornerstone of its mission to nurture the local arts scene. By providing financial assistance to foster new projects and help artists build their careers, the charity continues to be a vital catalyst for creative growth on the island.
