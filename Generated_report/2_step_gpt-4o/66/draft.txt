Warwick's maritime past is set to receive a breath of new life, as the historic leper hospital site in Saltisford is poised for an ambitious restoration. The National Lottery Heritage Fund has pledged over £2.8 million to transform two noteworthy 15th-century structures—the Master's House and St Michael's Chapel, once crucial to the town's history—into affordable homes. This initiative is a collaborative effort, spearheaded by West Midlands Heritage and Warwick District Council, with the aim to not only rescue these venerable buildings but to bestow upon them a sustainable future.nnThe site, which features the remnants of an 800-year-old leper hospital, has long been considered a staple in Warwick's rich historical tapestry. Designated as Grade II* listed buildings, the restoration of these structures is a significant undertaking, one that has been overlooked for more than half a century. Councillor Helen Adkins emphasized the importance of this project, stating, 'We will be able to preserve the legacy of the site and rescue these long-neglected historic buildings, offering them a sustainable future.'nnCorinne Brazier, chair of West Midlands Heritage, acknowledged the invaluable support from colleagues and the local community, marking this milestone as an 'important delivery stage.' She commented, 'We are now able to ensure that St Michael's Place will have a very homely future.' Not only will these restored buildings offer affordable housing, but the initiative will also engage the community further through education. A heritage community assistant role will be created alongside various local events, and opportunities focused on heritage craft skills, helping to instill a deeper appreciation for the local heritage.nnAs the restoration project launches, the future of St Michael's Place promises to offer both new beginnings for residents and renewed ties to Warwick's storied past. The hope is that these efforts will contextualize and preserve this historical legacy, making the past a vital part of the community's future. This transformation honors the former leper hospital's presence as a significant heritage site, a testament to Warwick’s enduring historical legacy.
