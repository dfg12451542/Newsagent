In a dramatic rescue at Port Grat beach, Guernsey, a quick-thinking woman, Jacky Meinke, saved her 77-year-old husband from drowning after he collapsed during a swim on Friday afternoon. The 67-year-old Mrs. Meinke, who is trained in first aid, reacted swiftly when she realized her husband, Chris Meinke, was face down in the water, appearing lifeless. nn'I heard a splash and carried on swimming, thinking he was behind me,' recalled Mrs. Meinke. However, she turned around to find her husband in distress. 'I started screaming his name and grabbed his head out of the water. He was completely white,' she said, describing the terrifying moment. With her fast actions, she managed to put his head on her shoulder and 'walloped his back,' which caused him to splutter and regain consciousness. She was joined by fellow swimmer Rosie Mclauchlan, who assisted in bringing Mr. Meinke back to the beach.nnThe rescue unfolded at approximately 16:30 BST at the well-known bathing location, where the couple had gone for a leisurely swim. Mr. Meinke was immediately attended to by paramedics upon reaching the shore and was transported to the hospital for overnight observation. 'The hospital thinks I had vertigo; I just didn't know which way was up,' Mr. Meinke explained after the incident, noting that the exact cause of his collapse remains unclear pending further medical evaluation.nnExpressing gratitude, Mrs. Meinke praised her fellow rescuer and others who assisted during the emergency. 'I am so grateful to them all,' she mentioned, highlighting how bystanders contributed by running for a defibrillator, contacting emergency services, and providing warmth while they awaited further help. This heart-stopping incident emphasized how quickly situations can escalate during a swim, with the Meinkes grateful for the assistance of Good Samaritans and the immediate response of medical personnel.
