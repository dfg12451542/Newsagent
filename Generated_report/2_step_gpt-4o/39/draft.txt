The Open 2025 at Royal Portrush is set to be a significant event for golf, particularly for Rory McIlroy. Having achieved his career-defining Masters victory, McIlroy is now looking to replicate that success on home soil. Speaking to BBC Sport NI, the Northern Irishman expressed his anticipation and emotional investment in aiming for victory at The Open. 'It would be amazing,' the 36-year-old said, emphasizing that a win at Portrush could be just as emotional, if not more so, than his triumph at Augusta. 'Everyone saw the mess I was after that,' he recounted, referring to the emotional aftermath of clinching his Masters victory.This year, The Open Championship returns to Royal Portrush from July 13 to July 20, 2025, a venue that holds personal significance for McIlroy. His past experiences, particularly the disappointment from the 2019 Open Championship at the same venue, have fueled his determination to succeed this time around. 'I'm so happy it's back so soon because I want to have another crack at it,' McIlroy reflected. He candidly expressed his regrets about his performance six years ago, acknowledging, 'I didn't have the week I wanted in 2019. I'm a better player now, manage my game better, and even if I have an off day, it's not going to be a 79 like it was on the first day in 2019.'As McIlroy gears up for The Open, he feels invigorated by recent performances, particularly in Scotland, and the respite he took after the Travelers Championship. 'The two weeks off to reset, get over here, a bit of a change of scenery, has been really nice,' he said. With a major win already under his belt this year, McIlroy is excited about facing the challenges at Portrush, noting this was one of the tournaments he had been most looking forward to in 2025. 'It's lovely to be coming in here already with a major,' he noted, excited about the position his game is in heading into the prestigious tournament. The support from local fans and the prospect of embracing the attention rather than shying away seems to be motivating McIlroy further, as he prepares to contend for the historic title on familiar terrain.
