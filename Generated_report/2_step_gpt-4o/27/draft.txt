A tragic single-vehicle crash in County Antrim has claimed the life of a man driving a white Volkswagen Golf on Ballyhill Road, Nutts Corner, late Saturday afternoon. The incident occurred around 17:50 BST, as confirmed by local police sources. The emergency services, including the ambulance service, swiftly attended the scene, but, unfortunately, the driver succumbed to his injuries and was pronounced dead on site.nnIn the aftermath of the crash, authorities also reported that a male passenger was involved in the accident. He was transported to a hospital for treatment, and his injuries are currently assessed as non-life-threatening. The crash scene was so severe that Ballyhill Road had to be temporarily closed, but it has since reopened to traffic.nnWitnesses at the scene described a somber setting, marked by mementos left at a nearby shed, including flowers tied to a gate, honoring the life lost. The local community is coming to terms with another tragedy on their roads, prompting renewed calls for increased safety measures in the region. Police are likely to continue their investigations to determine the cause of the crash as efforts to ensure road safety intensify in light of this latest incident.
