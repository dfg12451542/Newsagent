The Jersey women's football team opened their campaign at the 2025 Island Games with a challenging match, succumbing to a 3-1 defeat against the defending champions, Bermuda. Under the blazing sun of the island event, the encounter was a reflection of their 2023 Island Games clash, where Bermuda had similarly dominated with a 4-0 victory in Guernsey.nnAn earlier momentum appeared promising for Jersey as Anita Tavares scored just six minutes into the game, delivering a precise left-footed shot into the corner of the net. The performance showcased her agility and precision, as seen in past fixtures. However, as the match progressed, Bermuda amped up their attack, hitting Jersey's bar shortly before halftime. Both sides also saw their penalty appeals dismissed during an active first half, indicating a closely contested battle.nnThe momentum shifted in Bermuda's favor after the break, with Jaden Masters equalizing from close range around the hour mark. It only took another 14 minutes for Bermuda to edge ahead when Khyla Brangman confidently converted a penalty. Jya Rattaray Smith cemented Bermuda's victory in stoppage time with a swift reaction following a rebound off the bar, sending the reigning champions to the top of Group B.nnJersey, who missed out on the semi-finals in 2023 due to an administrative error, now find themselves at the bottom of the group after Shetland Islands and Isle of Wight drew 2-2 earlier that Sunday. The team face a crucial upcoming fixture against Shetland Islands on Monday as they strive to advance, before concluding their group stage against Isle of Wight on Tuesday.
