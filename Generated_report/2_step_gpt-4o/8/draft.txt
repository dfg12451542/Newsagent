In a miraculous rescue, a dog named Jimmy was saved after being trapped at the bottom of a well in Corby for 11 days. This heartwarming event occurred when a crew from the Northamptonshire Fire and Rescue Service, responding to a nearby fire on Stephenson Way, heard the dog's barking and discovered him lodged at the bottom of the well. Speaking about the ordeal, Jimmy's owner, Dorota Gruszczynska, recounted the emotional rollercoaster her family experienced during the search. 'We thought he had been hit by a car and we would never see him again,' she said.nnThe search for Jimmy was intense, with Ms. Gruszczynska and her family posting flyers and reaching out to the local community. According to Gruszczynska, 'We searched the area so many times and nothing, we created posters, put them online and sent them around the local area.' After receiving a crucial message from a firefighter on July 2nd, the Gruszczynska family was informed that their dog might have been found. Firefighters confirmed Jimmy's identity by spotting his green harness, and the rescue operation was swiftly set into motion.nnUsing a reach pole and line, the firefighters managed to lift the metal cylinders trapping Jimmy, eventually bringing him to safety. The dramatic rescue was aided by the overwhelming support of the local community and the diligent work of a drone company that volunteered their services. 'We're so grateful for everyone who helped, including the drone company. They did a huge amount of work, purely out of their heart,' added Gruszczynska.nnFollowing his rescue, Jimmy was taken to a veterinary clinic to be checked over. Despite being very hungry, veterinarians found him otherwise in good health. Gruszczynska expressed amazement at her dog's resilience, stating, 'He's back on his feet, he's recovered surprisingly well, we don't know how he survived, there must have been water in the well.' This story of survival and community spirit has not only brought relief to the Gruszczynska family but has also served as a reminder of the power of shared efforts in times of need.
