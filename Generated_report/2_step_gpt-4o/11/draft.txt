Motherwell's manager Jens Berthel Askou has emphasized the need for his team to increase their tempo following a challenging draw with Clyde in the Premier Sports Cup. Askou, speaking with the club media after the match, described the game as a testament to the unpredictable nature of football. 'It just shows how tricky football is in general,' he explained.nnAskou acknowledged that the team did not achieve the desired result, a sentiment shared with other clubs facing unexpected outcomes. 'There'll always be surprises in football, especially in the cup tournaments; there will be things that don't go as expected. We didn't get the result we wanted, obviously. A few other clubs, as far as I've heard, didn't do that either,' he said, reflecting on the broader competitive landscape.nnDespite the setback, Askou remains optimistic about the team's overall effort and structure. 'I think in terms of effort and structure, we were good,' he added. He noted the intensity of their pressing but admitted that the team should have better handled certain aspects of Clyde's direct play. 'Every time we tried to press, they put the ball in behind. That's something to improve on, definitely, and then it's about getting the tempo up.'nnLooking ahead, Askou believes Fir Park will provide Motherwell with the opportunity to enhance their pace and performance. 'I'm sure that Fir Park will give us the possibility to play at a much higher tempo,' he concluded. Motherwell will hope to implement these improvements and build on this experience as they progress in their Premier Sports Cup campaign.
