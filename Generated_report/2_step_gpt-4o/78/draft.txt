More than £8,000 has been raised to support the family of Daniel Drewitt, a teenager whose life was tragically cut short when he went missing in a lake at Sutton Park, Sutton Coldfield. Within just 48 hours, the online fundraising page garnered tremendous community support, surpassing its initial target of £1,600 and setting a new goal of £11,000.nnDaniel's body was recovered after emergency services responded to reports of a missing person at the park just after 18:00 BST on Thursday. Despite prompt actions by rescuers, Daniel was already deceased by the time his body was retrieved, leaving family and friends in mourning.nnThe GoFundMe page, created with the consent of Daniel's parents, aims to provide financial relief during these challenging times. Organiser Helena Swallow expressed gratitude on behalf of the family: 'The family are so very grateful, thank you so much.' An update revealed that Daniel's mother is 'overwhelmed' by the outpouring of support.nnThis incident echoes past tragedies and serves as a grim reminder of the dangers associated with swimming in open waters, particularly during warmer months. Community members continue to extend their condolences and financial aid to the Drewitt family, reinforcing the collective effort to support those facing unexpected hardships.
