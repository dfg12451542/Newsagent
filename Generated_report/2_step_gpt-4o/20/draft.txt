The prestigious 153rd Open Championship is underway at Royal Portrush in Northern Ireland, attracting the world's top golfers to the picturesque town of Portrush. Spanning from July 13 to July 20, 2025, this esteemed tournament promises an exhilarating week of world-class golfing competition, where athletes will vie for the coveted title of 'Champion Golfer of the Year.' nnBBC Sport has announced comprehensive coverage of the tournament, ensuring golf enthusiasts are kept well-informed with live radio and text updates. Golf fans can look forward to insights and expert analyses from the likes of Iain Carter, BBC's renowned golf correspondent, and on-the-ground reports from other seasoned sports journalists. As part of the extensive coverage, press conferences featuring Rory McIlroy, a local favorite, and defending champion Xander Schauffele will be highlighted throughout the week.nnTo further engage its audience, BBC Sport encourages followers to utilize their online platforms, offering an interactive experience by simply hitting 'follow' on their website or app to receive updates directly to their 'MySport' feed. With the Open Championship having been previously staged near Edinburgh, Scotland, this event at Royal Portrush marks an exciting transition to Northern Ireland, bringing a global spotlight to a course renowned for its challenging links and stunning coastal views. As the tournament unfolds, golf enthusiasts worldwide will eagerly watch to see who will prevail in this historic contest.
