Gower's historic Worm's Head Hotel in Rhossili suffered severe damage following a devastating fire in the early hours of Monday, leaving a significant void in the local community's heart. Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service were alerted to the blaze at 00:51 BST, with flames quickly engulfing the accommodation block's roof space, a total span of 25 meters. Although the cause of the fire remains under investigation, officials have confirmed that there were no casualties and all occupants have been safely accounted for.n  nIn the wake of the destruction, the ownership took to social media, expressing their sorrow and devastation over the tragic incident. 'It is particularly heartbreaking and soul-destroying for me and my family to see 25 years of hard work destroyed in less than 30 minutes,' they shared, highlighting the emotional toll of the inferno. Despite the overwhelming damage, the owners have vowed resilience, stating their intent to 'come back stronger.'n  nResidents and regular guests shared emotional recollections of the beloved establishment. 'We used to come every summer. It's absolutely shocking to see it like this,' said Sian Nichol, while another frequent visitor, Beverly, lamented the extensive damage, reminiscing about memorable new year celebrations spent there. The sight of blackened beams and an exposed chimney stack, coupled with the closure of the bar and hotel, left visitors stunned.n  nThe emergency response was swift, with fire crews tirelessly extinguishing the blaze by 05:30 and maintaining a presence on the scene until 09:32 to ensure safety. South Wales Police were also present to assist with the investigation. Hotel management conveyed gratitude for the community's support, ensuring that everyone's safety was paramount. As the town grapples with the loss, the investigation into the fire's cause continues, with both locals and visitors hopeful for the hotel's restoration and the revival of its vibrant role in the community.
