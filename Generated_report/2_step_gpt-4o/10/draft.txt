Stephen Robinson left 'shocked' after St Mirren draw with ArbroathnnSt Mirren's manager Stephen Robinson expressed his surprise and disappointment following his team's unexpected goalless draw and subsequent penalty defeat to Arbroath in their Premier Sports Cup opener at Gayfield Park. Describing himself as being 'in a little bit of shock,' Robinson apologized to the traveling fans who expected more from the team that had previously showcased strong performances during their pre-season preparations.nn'I didn't recognize some of the decision-making, some of the passing, some of the simple things we've continually done through the pre-season,' Robinson remarked, referencing the uncharacteristic performance from his squad. Despite his team having had a 'fantastic pre-season,' they seemed unable to translate that into success on the pitch against the newly promoted League 1 champions, Arbroath. The weekend marred what was supposed to be a confident start for St Mirren as they look ahead in the competition.nnAs the team prepares to meet League 2's Forfar Athletic next Tuesday, Robinson is adamant on a better response, demanding improvements across the board. Assuring supporters that the performance was an anomaly, he stated, 'We have to be and will be better than that.' Historically, Arbroath's recent promotion to League 1 and past performances suggested a challenging fixture for St Mirren, yet the match's outcome was more lackluster than anticipated. Despite the setback, Robinson remains optimistic about the squad’s ability to bounce back in their upcoming fixtures.
