A significant fire erupted in fields close to Guildford, Surrey, prompting an urgent response from emergency services on Monday afternoon. According to a spokesperson, the blaze began in fields adjacent to Piccards Farm House, necessitating the dispatch of around 10 fire crews to manage the situation. Surrey Fire & Rescue Service reported being called to the fire in the Sandy Lane area at 16:20 BST.nnFortunately, despite the intensity of the fire, no properties sustained damage, as confirmed by the Surrey Fire & Rescue Service. Residents, however, faced disruption, as those living nearby were advised to keep their windows and doors closed to guard against the encroaching smoke.nnThis incident near Guildford underlines the persistent risk of field fires during dry spells, which have been a growing concern in many rural areas. Response teams remain vigilant as efforts to fully extinguish the blaze continue, ensuring both residents and properties remain safe.
