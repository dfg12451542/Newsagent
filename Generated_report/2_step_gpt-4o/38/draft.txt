A memorial bench commemorating Sophie Lancaster, a young woman tragically murdered in 2007, has been temporarily relocated in Whitby to accommodate a new Ferris wheel attraction. The bench, a cherished tribute situated on West Cliff, serves as a somber remembrance of Lancaster, who lost her life following a brutal attack by two teenagers in Bacup, Lancashire. nnThe Sophie Lancaster Foundation expressed its disappointment at the unannounced move, highlighting a significant lack of consultation. 'It was a shock to learn that the bench has been removed to locate a giant sightseeing wheel. Unfortunately, we weren't informed or consulted about this decision,' said a foundation spokesperson. 'For Sylvia, Sophie's mother, the bench was a tangible connection to Sophie.'nnNorth Yorkshire Council defended the execution of the big wheel project as part of a broader tourism strategy intended to invigorate the local economy. 'The wheel in Whitby was chosen to provide stunning views across the harbor and towards the town's famous abbey,' explained North Yorkshire councillor Mark Crane. He acknowledged a lapse in local consultation and assured that the memorial would be returned to its original site by August 4. nnAcknowledging the oversight, Whitby mayor Sandra Turner called it a 'serious hiccup.' She confirmed receiving an apology from the council and ensured that measures would be taken to prevent future oversights. Meanwhile, a rededication ceremony for the bench is scheduled during the Tomorrow's Ghosts Festival on November 1. 'Whitby is such a special place for us, and we will be overjoyed when the bench returns to its home on West Cliff,' expressed a foundation spokesperson, adding that the community's support remains invaluable.
