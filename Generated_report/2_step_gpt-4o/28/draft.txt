Wales emerged victorious against Japan, defeating them 31-22 in a thrilling encounter in Kobe. The win was a significant morale booster for the Welsh side, who have been grappling with an 18-match international losing streak and the tumult off the field. This victory not only quenched the drought of wins for Welsh rugby but also showcased the indomitable spirit and character of the team, as highlighted by fly-half Dan Edwards.nn'It was about getting a win, I'm absolutely chuffed,' said Edwards. 'Especially with everyone saying we didn't deserve to win last week and were writing us off. We've come back and shown everyone why we're here. It shows the character we've got. That was a real statement that when it gets tough, we can pull through. It's also just that little bit of a boost that Welsh rugby needed.' Edwards' crucial late try, facilitated by an audacious reverse pass from replacement flanker Taine Plumtree, was instrumental in securing the elusive win.nnThe team executed a bold strategy under the guidance of interim head coach Matt Sherratt, who encouraged them to be brave and expressive. 'That last play summarises it for me,' said Sherratt. 'It would have been easy to go to a safe lineout at the front. We have thrown to the back, run a more risky play. And Taine Plumtree throws a reverse pass. I didn't intend for him to be that brave, but it worked!' Plumtree, who replaced Cardiff flanker Alex Mann, injected much-needed energy into the game and was pivotal in the second half.nnFor Keelan Giles, who came on as a half-time replacement for the injured Josh Adams, the win marked a significant personal triumph. 'It's a massive day for me and my family,' shared Giles. 'They made the trip over, which made it extra special. It's been a long time coming. It's almost like a full-circle moment, playing against Japan and being on the bench nine years ago against Japan and not getting on. A win has been a long time coming for fans of Wales. This group has worked extremely hard over the last couple of years.' The victory was not just a win but also a stepping stone for the team as they look forward to the autumn and the future, hoping to build on the momentum they gained in Kobe.
