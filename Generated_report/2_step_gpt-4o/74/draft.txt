Newcastle United have set their sights on acquiring Eintracht Frankfurt's rising star striker, Hugo Ekitike, as the club looks to bolster its offensive lineup ahead of the new season. This ambitious move, according to inside sources, could potentially turn into a record deal for the Magpies, with the cost expected to soar beyond £70 million. Such a figure underscores Newcastle's intent to compete decisively in the Premier League and Europe's elite competitions.nnThe pursuit of Ekitike comes amid swirling transfer market activity, with the 23-year-old French striker drawing attention from several big names across the continent. Liverpool, for instance, have also expressed interest in Ekitike; however, their involvement hinges on the approval of outgoing deals, notably involving Uruguay forward Darwin Nunez. As competition intensifies, Newcastle's financial commitment could play a pivotal role in securing the prolific striker.nnDespite their active exploration of Ekitike's transfer, Newcastle has clarified its commitment to holding onto Alexander Isak. Last season's top scorer for the club has been a target for multiple European teams, including Liverpool. However, Newcastle's management appears resolute, asserting no intention to sell the Swedish striker, aiming instead to pair him with Ekitike for a formidable striking partnership capable of challenging for top honors on multiple fronts.
