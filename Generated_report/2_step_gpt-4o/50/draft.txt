A climber suffered a head injury following a fall at Hoffman Quarry, Harper Hill, Buxton on Friday. Emergency services, including the Buxton Mountain Rescue Team, responded to the scene around 11:22 BST after the climber, whose identity remains undisclosed, fell approximately 3 meters (9.8 feet) while climbing at the quarry site.nnAccording to a spokesperson, the climber briefly lost consciousness due to a head injury sustained in the fall. An air ambulance was dispatched promptly and airlifted the victim to a location where an ambulance was on standby to transport him to the hospital for further medical care.nnHoffman Quarry, part of the historical limestone quarries in Derbyshire, is a popular yet perilous location for climbing, drawing enthusiasts despite the risks. The incident echoes previous emergency responses in the region, such as the multi-casualty event in Newgale, Pembrokeshire, in August 2023, which saw several individuals injured and hospitalized following a crash.nnFortunately, the climber is now receiving hospital treatment, and authorities have reminded the public of the inherent dangers associated with climbing activities, urging climbers to take all necessary precautions to ensure their safety.
