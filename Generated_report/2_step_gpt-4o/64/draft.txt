Crystal Palace have unveiled their new home kit for the 2025-26 season, marking an exciting moment for the club as they prepare to compete in European competitions. The release of the kit comes on the heels of the Eagles' buzzworthy participation in Europe, setting a vibrant tone for the upcoming season.nnThe kit launch was celebrated in style with several key players from the team, including Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, and the fresh names like Maxence Lacroix, Eddie Nketiah, Adam Wharton, Justin Devenny, and Romain Esse donning the new attire. The showcasing was not limited to the men's squad; Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur from Palace Women also proudly modeled the new gear, highlighting the inclusion and expansive reach of the club.nnAdding to the excitement, new signing Walter Benitez made his debut appearance in the club's distinctive light green goalkeeper home jersey. The exhibition of the kit was further endorsed by the Eagles' successful 1-0 win over Millwall in their pre-season opener, where the team sported the new design to great applause.nnThe release not only showcases the club's modern and dynamic approach to design but also reflects their ambitions and readiness for the challenges that come with the promise of European football. Fans eagerly anticipate seeing the new kit in action as Crystal Palace looks forward to a competitive season.
