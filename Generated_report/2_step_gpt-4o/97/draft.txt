The Wurzels were forced to cut their performance short at the Party at the Palace event in Wells, Somerset, as band members grappled with the effects of the ongoing heatwave on Saturday. Temperatures soared to a blistering 31C, causing both the band's frontman Pete Budd, who is set to celebrate his 85th birthday this coming July 18, and accordionist Tommy Banner to suffer from heat exhaustion on stage.nnTommy Banner, speaking about the incident, remarked, 'Both Pete and I appear to have fully recovered from sun and heat exhaustion. Up on stage there was no air moving, and we were facing straight into the direct, unobstructed rays of a very strong sun.' This led to a sudden halt in their performance as Budd, who was in 'a bad way,' needed immediate medical attention. Banner himself experienced dizziness and signs of sunstroke after leaving the stage.nnHistorical data from Imperial College London highlights the severity of the heatwave gripping the region, reporting 263 excess deaths linked to heatwaves in the 10 days following June 23. This alarming report further underscores the extreme conditions that led to the abrupt end of The Wurzels' performance. Medical staff were promptly on hand to assist Banner and Budd, with a nurse advising Banner on exercises to aid recovery and cautioning against driving after the incident.nnAs the sweltering temperatures persist in Somerset, the health and safety of outdoor performers and attendees become increasingly paramount. The Wurzels’ experience is a stark reminder of the dangerous impact heatwaves can have, particularly on outdoor events, highlighting the need for adequate precautions and medical support at public gatherings.
