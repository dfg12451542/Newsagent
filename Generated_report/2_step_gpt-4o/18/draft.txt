Rory McIlroy remains optimistic and focused following a strong performance at the Scottish Open, despite narrowly missing out on the victory. The Northern Irish golfer finished his tournament at The Renaissance Club in North Berwick with a commendable 13-under par, showcasing his mastery on the course with 19 birdies over four rounds.nnReflecting on his performance, McIlroy expressed satisfaction with his overall game. 'No frustration; it's been a great week,' he remarked, noting the quality of his shots and control over his ball flight. Although he did not clinch the trophy, he expressed a positive outlook. 'I'm really happy with where my game is; the way I played over the weekend; the shots that I hit, how I controlled my ball flight. Missing the trophy, that’s about it.'nnMcIlroy acknowledged the challenging conditions, describing how the windy weather made precision difficult. 'It was pretty windy and was hard to get the ball super close, but I gave myself plenty of chances coming down the stretch,' he explained. The greens posed additional challenges, with some putts going awry due to bumps and misreads. Nevertheless, McIlroy feels he achieved his goals for the week and is eager to focus on the future, stating, 'I feel like I've gotten out of this week what I wanted. I'm looking forward to getting to Portrush tonight and getting out on to the golf course early tomorrow.'nnAs McIlroy prepares for his next stop at Royal Portrush Golf Club, his contentment with his current form and optimism for the upcoming events highlight his determination. The 2025 Scottish Open adds to a rich historical tapestry of the sport in the region, where McIlroy has once again demonstrated his skill and sportsmanship.
