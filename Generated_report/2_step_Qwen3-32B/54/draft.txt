A 71-year-old motorcyclist has died following a six-vehicle collision near Loch Lomond on Sunday, July 13, 2025. Police Scotland confirmed the man, who was riding a Harley Davidson equipped with a sidecar, was killed at the scene. The crash involved multiple cars, though drivers were reported unharmed. Road closures lasting over six hours were implemented to facilitate police investigations. Sgt Kenny Malaney of Police Scotland stated, 'Our inquiries are continuing to establish the full circumstances of what happened,' adding, 'Sadly, the male motorcyclist died and our thoughts are with his family and friends.' Authorities are urging witnesses or those with dashcam footage to come forward, noting the road was 'busy at the time.' The incident echoes similar tragedies in the region, where complex multi-vehicle collisions often raise concerns about road safety in tourist-heavy areas.
