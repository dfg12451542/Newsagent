A Hereford family who traveled 800 miles in their campervan to support England’s Lionesses at the UEFA Women’s Euro 2025 celebrated a decisive 6-1 victory over Wales on Sunday. Kevin Morris, who attended the match in St Gallen, Switzerland, with his wife, Kelly, and children Amelia, 16, and Ethan, 13, described the team’s performance as 'another great one,' praising their dominance from the outset. The family, staying near Lake Constance at Arbon—a hub where Germany, Austria, and Switzerland converge—joined thousands of fans in securing England’s place in the quarter-finals.      The Morris family’s journey was as eventful as the match itself. After the game, Kevin recounted chaotic travel delays, including missed trains and a bus that 'went everywhere' before returning to their campervan. 'We were told to get off and wait for a bus, which eventually turned up,' he said, noting the 20-minute wait added to their Swiss adventure. Despite the logistical challenges, the family, who have traveled across Paris, Courgenay, and Zurich to follow the tournament, called the experience 'worth it.'      England’s triumph builds on their legacy as reigning Euro 2022 champions and 2023 World Cup finalists. With the Lionesses set to face Sweden in the quarter-finals—a rematch of the 2022 semi-final—Kevin expressed optimism. 'I think we can do it again,' he said, hopeful for a repeat of their historic 2022 title win. The Morris family plans to return to Zurich for the next match on Thursday, continuing their cross-Europe pilgrimage as England’s quest for another major trophy enters its critical phase.
