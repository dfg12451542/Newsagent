A large fire erupted in fields near Guildford, Surrey, on Monday as the UK endures its third heatwave of the year. Surrey Fire & Rescue Service responded to the blaze in the Sandy Lane area at 16:20 BST, deploying 10 fire crews to contain the flames. A spokesperson confirmed the fire originated in fields adjacent to Piccards Farm House, though no properties were damaged. Residents were advised to keep windows and doors shut due to lingering smoke. 'The priority is ensuring public safety and preventing the fire from spreading,' the spokesperson added.  The incident follows a surge in fire-related emergencies across Surrey this summer. Historical records show a nature reserve fire in the region during the current heatwave, as well as multiple suspected arson incidents at properties in Provost Rust Drive, Cummings Park Crescent, and Birkhall Parade between late last week. In South Harrow, a similar blaze near Roxeth Green Avenue required 100 firefighters to quell within two hours earlier this month.  Firefighters emphasized the heightened risk of wildfires during prolonged dry spells, with Surrey’s hot, dry conditions exacerbating vulnerabilities. While the cause of Monday’s fire remains under investigation, authorities have noted an uptick in deliberate and accidental ignitions amid record temperatures.  This latest incident underscores growing concerns over fire safety in the region. With heatwaves expected to become more frequent, local officials are urging residents to report suspicious activity and exercise caution near open fields. The Surrey Fire & Rescue Service has extended its public awareness campaign, stressing the importance of community vigilance during critical fire weather conditions.
