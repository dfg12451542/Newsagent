Callie Cooke, star of the Formula 1-inspired film *F1: The Movie*, has revealed she performed a real pit stop during last year’s British Grand Prix at Silverstone—a surreal experience she called “mad” and “one of the best experiences of my life.” The actress, who plays a key role in the fictional APXGP team depicted in the film (released globally on June 25, 2025), described how her connection to Northamptonshire, near the iconic racetrack, made the experience feel “so special and so real.”     Cooke trained extensively to join the pit crew, working alongside real mechanics, engineers, and stunt professionals during filming. “I was changing tyres, oil, and the car’s wing—just trying to exist in the world of a garage,” she said, emphasizing her determination to authentically portray a woman in a male-dominated industry. “I knew I had to hold my own and get stuck in, and maybe that’s how I got the job.” Her efforts paid off when she was invited to participate in a real pit stop between Mercedes and Ferrari, executing the task as quickly as the professional teams. “I don’t know how they ever let us get away with it,” she added, recalling the high-stakes maneuver.    The British Grand Prix, a cornerstone of Formula 1’s calendar, has long been celebrated for its economic and cultural impact, with officials noting its “great benefits” for the region. For Cooke and the *F1: The Movie* crew, the event offered a rare opportunity to blend fiction with reality. “Working in and around real F1 teams was a once-in-a-lifetime experience,” she said, highlighting the collaboration between the production and motorsport professionals. As the film bridges the gap between Hollywood and the high-octane world of Formula 1, Cooke’s hands-on involvement underscores the industry’s growing efforts to diversify and inspire new audiences.
