Liverpool FC has secured the signing of 20-year-old Scotland international midfielder Kirsty Maclean from Rangers, marking a significant step in the player's career. The move sees the young talent transition to Anfield after making over 100 appearances for the Glasgow club, where she emerged as a key figure in their academy and senior team. Maclean described the transfer as 'the right time for a new challenge and to take the next step' in a statement released by the clubs.      Maclean, who has been a consistent presence for the Scotland national team, expressed her excitement about joining one of English football's most storied institutions. 'I think the club speaks for itself. It's obviously a massive club with a lot of history and to be able to represent the badge is a really proud moment for me,' she said. The midfielder, known for her technical ability and vision on the pitch, added: 'I'm a midfielder who likes to be on the ball and tries to make things happen so hopefully I can link up with the players in front of me and we can try to score lots of goals this season.'      The transfer underscores Liverpool's focus on nurturing and acquiring dynamic young talent to strengthen their squad for future campaigns. Maclean's move follows a pattern of high-profile signings aimed at blending experience with emerging stars. Her track record with Rangers, coupled with her international experience, positions her as a promising addition to the Reds' ambitious plans.
