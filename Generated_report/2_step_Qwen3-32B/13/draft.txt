The Open Championship at Royal Portrush is approaching, and defending champion Xander Schauffele is already on the course gaining familiarity. Schauffele, who faces challenges from a rib injury sustained early in the season, took to the links on Monday to assess conditions. Despite the injury impacting his performance this year—though he managed to tie with Scottie Scheffler at nine-under par in last week's Scottish Open—the two-time major champion is using the practice sessions to fine-tune his game ahead of the tournament.  Joining Schauffele on the course was Shane Lowry, the 2019 Open champion known for his expertise on this type of terrain. Lowry, who has a proven track record at challenging links courses, partnered with Denmark's Rasmus Hojgaard during Monday's round. His presence highlights the competitive depth of the field, as players look to capitalize on Royal Portrush's demanding layout.  Schauffele's participation underscores the stakes for the defending titleholder, who must overcome lingering physical setbacks to repeat his success. With historical context of his injury affecting his form earlier this season, the Open presents a critical opportunity to showcase resilience. Meanwhile, Lowry's strategic practice sessions and experience with links golf add intrigue to the race for the Claret Jug this week.
