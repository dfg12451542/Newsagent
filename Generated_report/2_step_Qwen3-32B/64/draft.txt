Crystal Palace unveiled their 2025-26 home kit amid their recent European campaign, marking a fresh chapter as the club prepares for the upcoming season. The release coincided with the Eagles' participation in continental fixtures, with the new design set to become a staple for fans and players alike. The kit launch event featured prominent figures from the Palace squad, including Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, Maxence Lacroix, Eddie Nketiah, Adam Wharton, Justin Devenny, and Romain Esse, alongside representatives from the women's team, Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur. New signing Walter Benitez made his first appearance in the club's light green goalkeeper home jersey, adding a fresh face to the presentation.     Palace wasted no time putting the kit to the test, wearing it during their pre-season opener—a 1-0 victory over Millwall on Saturday. The match served as both a celebration of the new design and a strategic move to gauge readiness for the season ahead. The kit's unveiling aligns with a broader trend in English football, as clubs like Leeds United have also introduced 2025-26 kits inspired by iconic club landmarks, such as the Lowfields Tunnel. For Crystal Palace, the home kit represents not only a visual identity but a symbol of ambition as the club continues to build momentum under its current trajectory.
