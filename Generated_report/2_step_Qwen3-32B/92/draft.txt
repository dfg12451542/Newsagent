Wycombe Wanderers have confirmed the signing of 25-year-old midfielder Ewan Henderson from Belgian side Beerschot, marking their eighth summer addition ahead of the 2025/26 season. The Scotland youth international joins on an undisclosed fee, bringing experience from top-flight campaigns in both Scotland and Belgium. 'Ewan's a really energetic midfielder who's had a great start to his career in the top flights in Scotland and Belgium, and gives us another dimension in the middle of the park,' said Wycombe head coach Mike Dodds. 'He's played a lot of football in the past three years which stands him in good stead to come here and hopefully hit the ground running as he integrates into the squad in pre-season.'      Henderson, who began his career at Celtic before spells at Kilmarnock and Beerschot, adds depth to a Wycombe midfield reshaped this summer. Alongside new signings Armando Junior Quitirna, Taylor Allen, and Dan Casey, the midfielder will vie for a starting spot during pre-season preparations. Dodds emphasized Henderson's readiness to contribute immediately, citing his physicality and adaptability as key assets.      The move underscores Wycombe's ambitious recruitment strategy, with eight players secured since the transfer window opened. Henderson's arrival follows a pattern of targeting experienced attackers and midfielders, as the club aims to build on its recent Championship campaigns. While the fee remains undisclosed, historical records indicate the transfer aligns with Wycombe's approach of acquiring proven performers on competitive terms.
