Hull City fans remain divided over owner Acun Ilicali amid growing financial concerns and a turbulent season, with some calling for his exit while others defend his commitment to the club. Recent reports revealed long-standing worries within the club about cashflow and the ability to meet supplier payments, compounded by a £1.5m shortfall from an unpaid player transfer. Ilicali addressed the issue in an open letter, vowing to cover the deficit and denying conflicts with other clubs, but fans like Daniel Mawer argue the owner’s strategy has led to 'wholesale changes' rather than stability. 'This one feels more like we’re on the steady decline of the rollercoaster,' Mawer said, reflecting on four years of fluctuating fortunes.  Joe Collins, another fan, acknowledged Ilicali’s initial popularity—citing cheaper tickets—but highlighted the disillusionment following a 'disastrous' season and financial instability. 'You’ve got some fans who are “Acun out,” and there is definitely merit to that point of view,' Collins noted, while also recognizing a faction that believes in the owner’s 'feelgood factor.' Charlotte Berry offered a more optimistic perspective, calling the recent turmoil 'a blip in the road' and expressing confidence in Ilicali’s leadership.  The club now faces calls to overhaul its financial approach amid transfer restrictions. Collins emphasized the urgency: 'We can’t carry on spending as we were.' As Hull City navigates this crisis, fan sentiment remains split between frustration and cautious hope, with many awaiting concrete action to determine Ilicali’s future in the eyes of the Tigers’ supporters.
