A 14-year-old boy has been charged following a stabbing incident at Sunnydale Park in Derbyshire, authorities confirmed on July 14. Police were called to the park at approximately 16:20 BST on Saturday after reports of the altercation. A 15-year-old boy sustained non-life-threatening injuries in the attack and was hospitalized, according to Derbyshire Police. The accused, who cannot be named for legal reasons, faces charges of wounding with intent and possession of a bladed article in a public place. He has been remanded in custody and is scheduled to appear at Southern Derbyshire Magistrates' Court on Monday.  Four other teenagers arrested in connection with the stabbing have been released on bail as investigations continue, police added. The incident has sparked concern among local residents, with the park’s entrance near a nature reserve now under heightened scrutiny. Authorities have not disclosed the motive or further details about the altercation.  This marks the latest in a series of youth-related incidents prompting calls for increased community safety measures in the area. Derbyshire Police urged anyone with information to come forward as they work to piece together the circumstances surrounding the attack.
