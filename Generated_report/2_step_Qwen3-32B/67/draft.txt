Hibernian Football Club has broken their transfer record to sign Togo international striker Thibault Klidje from Swiss side FC Luzern, marking a significant step in their summer recruitment drive. The 24-year-old forward has penned a three-year contract at Easter Road, with an optional fourth year, though the undisclosed fee is understood to surpass the club's previous record of £700,000 paid for Ecuadorian defender Ulises de La Cruz in 2001. Manager David Gray highlighted Klidje's potential, stating, 'Klidje will add extra pace and trickery to Hibs' front line,' while acknowledging his development trajectory: 'At 24, he has a lot of room for growth, and we want to help him progress.'      Klidje's move to Scotland follows a strong 2024-25 campaign with Luzern, where he showcased his technical abilities and goal threat. Gray emphasized the striker's adaptability, noting his 'performances last season' as a key factor in the transfer. The Hibs boss added, 'We look forward to working with him moving forward,' signaling the player's anticipated role in bolstering the team's attacking options.      The acquisition underscores Hibernian's ambition ahead of the new season, as they aim to strengthen their squad following a mixed 2024 Scottish Championship campaign. While the exact cost of Klidje's transfer remains confidential, sources indicate it reflects a substantial investment in talent, positioning him as a pivotal addition to Gray's plans for competitive progression.
