Six abandoned kittens discovered in a rubbish dump in southern Scotland have been rehomed after being named after characters from the iconic TV show *Friends*. The four-week-old litter, found in a 'heartbreaking scene' at the Eyemouth Recycling Centre on 27 May, was suffering from severe malnutrition, flea and worm infestations, and painful eye infections, according to Borders Pet Rescue centre manager Kim McNeill. 'They were frightened, weak, and understandably wary of human contact,' McNeill said, describing the kittens' initial condition.      The rescue effort saw the kittens nursed back to health through medical treatment, foster care, and months of patience. 'These kittens needed more than medicine—they needed time, patience, and love,' McNeill explained. A dedicated foster carer provided a safe environment where the kittens gradually gained confidence, learned to play, and 'experienced the warmth of a home for the first time.' By July, the six had recovered fully, with McNeill noting they were 'healthy and full of personality.'      Inspired by their strong bond and the joy they brought to staff, the rescue team named the three male and three female kittens after *Friends* characters: Joey, Ross, Chandler, Phoebe, Monica, and Rachel. The social media campaign 'Kitten Friends' helped raise thousands of pounds for their care and attracted attention to their adoption search. 'We’re delighted to share that all six kittens have now been adopted into wonderful homes,' McNeill concluded, marking a heartwarming end to their journey from a rubbish tip to loving families.
