Colchester is set to host a memorial running event this Sunday to honor the life of Arthur Whiston, a beloved local athlete who tragically died in a road crash. Organized by Colchester Harriers AC, the race will take place at the garrison athletics track starting at 09:30 BST. The event marks the first anniversary of his passing and aims to celebrate his legacy while supporting the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity Whiston championed. 'Arthur was incredibly loved by the community,' said Lydia Cunningham, a friend, noting his enduring impact.      Whiston, described as a 'very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular,' was a dedicated member of Colchester Harriers AC. The race will draw participants from across the region, echoing historical attendance patterns seen in past local athletic events, including crews from Coggeshall, Tiptree, and Clacton. Funds raised will directly benefit the Essex Wildlife Trust, reflecting Whiston’s commitment to environmental causes.      The event remains open for last-minute registrations, inviting runners to join in tribute. As the community gathers, the race serves as both a celebration of Whiston’s life and a testament to his lasting connections. 'It’s about keeping Arthur’s spirit alive through movement and kindness,' Cunningham added, encapsulating the event’s dual purpose of remembrance and action.
