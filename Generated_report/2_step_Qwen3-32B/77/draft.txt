Arsenal are preparing to formally offer teenage forward Ethan Nwaneri a new contract, as negotiations enter an advanced stage between the club and the 18-year-old England Under-21 attacking midfielder. According to BBC Sport, the proposed deal would span five years or include a four-year term with an option for a fifth, reflecting the club's commitment to securing his long-term future. Talks are expected to accelerate in the coming days, with both parties demonstrating a shared willingness to finalize an agreement.      The move comes amid reported interest from Chelsea and German clubs, though Arsenal remain confident in Nwaneri's decision to stay. The young star, who has emerged as a key figure in the Gunners' academy setup, has been a focal point of transfer speculation despite his tender age. Historical reports highlight the club's assurance in retaining his services, even as external suitors reportedly monitor his progress.      While no definitive breakthrough has been announced, sources indicate growing optimism that a resolution is near. Nwaneri's contract situation has taken on added significance as he continues to develop into one of English football's most promising talents, with Arsenal keen to reward his progress and deter potential bids from competitors.
