Leicestershire Police have arrested four additional suspects in connection with a stabbing incident in Eyres Monsell, bringing the total number of arrests to six. Officers responded to reports on Friday evening after a man in his 20s was stabbed on Sturdee Road and treated at a local hospital for stab wounds. The victim, who remains in stable condition, was known to the suspects, according to authorities.      Det Sgt Adrian Davies provided key details about the incident, stating the attack occurred shortly before 9:15 pm—a time when the area was still lit and warm, potentially increasing the number of witnesses. 'If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us,' Davies urged, emphasizing the importance of public cooperation. Police have conducted house-to-house inquiries and a thorough examination of the scene as part of their ongoing probe.      Among those arrested are two men aged 18 and 29, a 38-year-old woman charged with conspiracy to commit murder, and a 25-year-old woman suspected of assisting an offender. The 18-year-old and 25-year-old remain in custody, while others have been released on bail. This follows the initial arrest of a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted murder earlier in the week. The case marks the latest in a series of violent incidents in the region, though local officials have not yet linked it to broader trends in knife crime.      Historical records show a similar attempted murder arrest in Leicester earlier this year, but police have not confirmed connections between past and present cases. Investigations continue to determine the full context of the stabbing and the roles of those detained.
