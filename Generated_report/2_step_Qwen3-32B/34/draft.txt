The Open 2025 opened in spectacular fashion as Royal Portrush Golf Club welcomed massive crowds for Rory McIlroy’s first practice round on Monday, with fans eager to catch a glimpse of the Northern Irish star ahead of the championship. Thousands gathered along the course as McIlroy, the world number two, navigated the iconic links, drawing cheers at every turn. The 35-year-old, who claimed his Open title at Hoylake in 2014, treated spectators to a standout moment on the 18th green, sinking a celebratory birdie putt before capping the session with a 15-minute autograph signing that delighted fans.      McIlroy’s presence has reignited excitement at Royal Portrush, a venue with deep Open history that previously hosted the tournament in 2019. His performance on the practice round underscored his readiness for the challenge, though the focus remains on his quest to add another Claret Jug to his collection. Later on Monday, McIlroy is scheduled to address the media at 14:00 BST, followed by 2019 Open champion Shane Lowry at 15:00 BST, as the build-up to the tournament reaches a fever pitch.      The event marks a significant moment for Northern Ireland, with Royal Portrush providing a global stage for local talent and fans. McIlroy’s popularity, combined with the course’s storied reputation, has set the tone for a week of high-stakes golf and electric atmosphere, promising to be one of the most memorable editions of The Open in recent years.
