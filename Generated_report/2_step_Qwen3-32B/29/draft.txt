A petition to preserve Jersey's 128-year-old Victoria Marine Lake has surpassed 1,000 signatures, triggering a formal government response. The campaign, organized by local advocate Nina Zaech and the newly formed Save Our Pool Working Group, highlights the historic sea pool's cultural and economic significance. 'It’s a vital piece of Jersey’s heritage that could support health, leisure, and tourism,' Zaech emphasized, underscoring the site’s potential as a community asset.      The marine lake, a horseshoe-shaped pool in St Helier, has faced an uncertain future since a 2024 inspection revealed it requires 'comprehensive work' to address structural and maintenance issues. Last month, Infrastructure Minister Andy Jehan stated repairs would be 'uneconomical' due to ongoing sea defense upgrades along the waterfront, sparking concern among campaigners. The petition’s success demonstrates public demand for a solution that balances preservation with infrastructure priorities.      With government officials now obligated to address the petition, advocates are urging a collaborative approach to restore the landmark while integrating it into broader coastal plans. The outcome will test Jersey’s commitment to safeguarding its historical legacy amid modern development challenges.
