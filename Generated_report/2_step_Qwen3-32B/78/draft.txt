More than £8,000 has been raised within 48 hours for the family of a boy who tragically died after going missing in a lake in Sutton Coldfield. A GoFundMe page, established with the permission of the boy’s parents, initially aimed to raise £1,600 but has since surpassed its target, with organisers setting a revised goal of £11,000 to support the family during this 'difficult time.' Helena Swallow, the fundraiser’s organiser, expressed gratitude on behalf of the family, stating, 'The family are so very grateful, thank you so much,' while noting the teenager’s mother was 'overwhelmed' by the outpouring of support.  The fundraiser gained momentum rapidly, reflecting the community’s response to the tragedy. Local residents and donors have rallied to assist the family, echoing Sutton Coldfield’s history of charitable efforts. For instance, earlier this year, a community member raised over £800 for a charity by participating in the town’s 8.5-mile fun run, highlighting the area’s tradition of collective support during crises.  This incident has once again underscored the power of grassroots fundraising in times of loss. As the GoFundMe page continues to grow, organisers emphasise that the donations will provide critical financial relief to the family while they navigate their grief. The community’s solidarity, both new and rooted in past efforts, remains a focal point of this ongoing response.
