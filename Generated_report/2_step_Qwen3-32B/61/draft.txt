West Lindsey's popular 'big bin hire' scheme is set to potentially become a permanent service after receiving overwhelmingly positive feedback from residents. The initiative, which allows residents to rent large bins for waste disposal, has been praised for its convenience, with a recent report revealing that hundreds of bins were hired out during the trial period and 95% of customers left four or five-star reviews. The success of the program has prompted local authorities to consider making it a long-term offering.     The report highlighted the scheme's effectiveness in managing bulky waste, particularly for items that do not fit into standard household bins. Large black bins on wheels—similar to those used by commercial establishments—were deployed, featuring stickers that clearly outlined prohibited items. This approach has been lauded for reducing illegal dumping and improving waste management efficiency in the area.    A decision on the scheme's permanent implementation is expected on Tuesday, following a recommendation from the authority to extend the trial. Councillors will review the data, including customer satisfaction rates and operational feasibility, before finalizing the move. If approved, the service will continue to provide residents with a practical solution for waste disposal while maintaining high environmental standards.
