Jersey’s women’s football team began their 2025 Island Games campaign with a 3-1 defeat to reigning champions Bermuda in a tightly contested opening match. The loss echoes their 4-0 defeat to Bermuda in the 2023 Island Games, as the Bermudan side once again proved dominant. Jersey took an early lead in the sixth minute when Anita Tavares scored with a left-footed shot into the corner, but Bermuda equalized in the 61st minute through Jaden Masters before Khyla Brangman converted a penalty to put them ahead 14 minutes later. A stoppage-time goal by Jya Rattaray Smith sealed Bermuda’s 3-1 victory, propelling them to the top of Group B.      The match saw tense moments in the first half, including a Bermuda shot hitting the Jersey bar and both teams having penalty appeals rejected. Despite Jersey’s initial success, Bermuda’s resilience in the second half highlighted their status as title contenders. Jersey, who missed out on a semi-final spot in 2023 due to an administrative error, now face a challenging path to qualification after the loss left them bottom of the group.      Jersey’s next fixtures will test their resolve: they face the Shetland Islands on Monday, followed by a crucial match against the Isle of Wight on Tuesday. With Shetland and the Isle of Wight drawing 2-2 earlier in the day, Jersey must regroup quickly to avoid a repeat of their 2023 struggles. For Bermuda, the victory sets a strong tone for their defense of the gold medal, as they continue to dominate island football with precision and experience.
