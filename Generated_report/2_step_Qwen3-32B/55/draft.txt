St Johnstone defender Andre Raymond is set to complete a £50,000 transfer to Finnish club Ilves, marking the Trinidad and Tobago international's latest career move. The deal, poised to finalize in the coming days, signals the end of Raymond's tenure at the Scottish Championship side after four years with the club. While neither St Johnstone nor Ilves have officially confirmed the transfer, multiple sources indicate the financial terms have been agreed upon, with administrative details nearing completion.  Raymond, 30, joined St Johnstone in 2021 and became a key figure in the team's defense, contributing to their promotion to the Scottish Premiership in 2022. His departure follows a season in which he made 28 league appearances, maintaining a consistent presence in the side despite competition for places. The move to Ilves represents a return to international football for Raymond, who previously played in Finland during a loan spell with the club in 2023.  The transfer aligns with St Johnstone's ongoing efforts to reshape their squad ahead of the new season, while Ilves seek to strengthen their defensive options for the 2025 Veikkausliiga campaign. Both clubs were approached for comment, but no statements have been released as of Monday evening.
