A 57-year-old motorcyclist is in hospital with serious injuries following a crash on a rural road near Hawick in the Borders. Emergency services responded to the single-vehicle accident on the B6399 at approximately 12:00 on Sunday, just south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm. The incident involved a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike, with witnesses reporting the road was closed for around eight hours afterward as investigations took place. Police have appealed for anyone with information to come forward. The crash site, a remote stretch of the B6399, has raised concerns among local riders about road safety in the area. While no other vehicles were involved, the severity of the injuries has prompted calls for increased safety measures on the route, which is known for its winding terrain. Separately, historical records note a similar motorcycle crash in Bournemouth in 2024, where a collision between a BMW and a Honda motorcycle occurred on the A347. However, no direct connection has been established between that incident and the recent Hawick crash.
