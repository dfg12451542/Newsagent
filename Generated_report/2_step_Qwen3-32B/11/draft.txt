Motherwell manager Jens Berthel Askou has emphasized the need for his team to 'increase tempo' following a 'tricky' draw with Clyde in the Premier Sports Cup. Speaking to club media, Askou acknowledged the unpredictability of football, stating, 'It just shows how tricky football is in general. There’ll always be surprises... You have to be there on the day.' Despite praise for his side's 'effort and structure,' the manager highlighted defensive lapses, noting, 'Every time we tried to press, they put the ball in behind. I think we should have dealt better with some of their direct play.'  The result marks a challenging start to Motherwell's campaign, with Askou observing that 'a few other clubs... didn’t do that either,' suggesting broader unpredictability in cup tournaments. However, he remains optimistic about harnessing Fir Park's atmosphere to elevate performance, asserting, 'I’m sure that Fir Park will give us the possibility to play at a much higher tempo.'  Historically, Askou has shown a pattern of balancing critique with encouragement. Earlier this pre-season, he lauded the team's 'brave and dynamic' display during a defeat to Eredivisie side Twente, reflecting his focus on progress amid adversity. As Motherwell aim to regroup, the manager's emphasis on tempo and adaptability will be critical in navigating their upcoming fixtures.
