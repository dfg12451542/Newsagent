Leicester City's 21-year-old Moroccan midfielder Bilal El Khannouss has emerged as a transfer target for multiple Premier League clubs ahead of the 2025-26 season. According to recent reports, Sunderland are leading the interest in the playmaker, with West Ham United also monitoring the situation. French Ligue 1 side Monaco have additionally entered the race for the former Genk star, who has established himself as a key figure in Leicester's midfield under manager Enzo Maresca.  El Khannouss, who joined the Foxes from Belgian club Genk in 2022 for an undisclosed fee, has earned praise for his creativity and technical ability. Having represented Morocco at the 2022 FIFA World Cup, the young midfielder's performances in the Premier League have attracted attention beyond the East Midlands. Sources indicate that while Leicester remain open to offers, they are unlikely to sell unless approached with a bid reflecting his growing value.  The move would mark a significant career step for El Khannouss, who has yet to sign a professional contract beyond his current Leicester deal, set to expire in 2027. With the January transfer window approaching, speculation is expected to intensify as clubs weigh their options. For now, the player appears focused on Leicester's domestic and European campaigns, with no official statements regarding his future.
