A man has died following a single-vehicle crash on Ballyhill Road in Nutts Corner, County Antrim, on Saturday, 13 July 2025. Police confirmed the victim was the driver of a white Volkswagen Golf involved in the incident at approximately 17:50 BST. Ambulance services responded to the scene, but the driver was pronounced dead on arrival. A male passenger sustained non-life-threatening injuries and was transported to hospital for treatment, according to authorities. Road closures were implemented temporarily, though Ballyhill Road has since reopened to traffic.  The crash occurred near a location marked by an old shed and iron gate, with emergency personnel deploying orange arrow markings at the site. While details surrounding the cause of the collision remain under investigation, police have not indicated any immediate suspects or factors contributing to the accident.  Historical records reveal a similar incident on Ballymagowan Road in June 2025, where a Range Rover was involved in a crash at 16:20 BST. Though the roads and vehicles differ, the proximity in time and nature of the incidents have sparked local discussions about road safety in the area. Authorities have not yet commented on potential links between the two events. Investigations into Saturday’s fatal crash are ongoing.
