Newcastle United have launched a bid to sign Eintracht Frankfurt striker Hugo Ekitike, with sources indicating the club is exploring a potential record deal for the 23-year-old French forward. The move, reported to exceed £70 million, would mark a significant step in Newcastle's summer transfer strategy as they aim to bolster their attacking options ahead of the new Premier League season. Despite growing interest in Ekitike, BBC Sport has confirmed that Newcastle remain committed to retaining last season's top scorer Alexander Isak, who has been linked with moves to clubs including Liverpool.  The striker, who has emerged as one of Europe's most coveted talents, has drawn attention from multiple elite clubs. Notably, historical reports suggest Liverpool are also considering a pursuit of Ekitike but must first secure approval for the transfer of Darwin Núñez to free up financial and squad space. However, Newcastle's approach appears to be progressing independently, with no indication of overlap in these negotiations.  Newcastle's focus on Ekitike underscores their ambition to compete at the highest level following recent successes. The potential signing would not only set a new club transfer record but also signal a bold statement of intent as the Magpies seek to maintain momentum in an increasingly competitive league landscape.
