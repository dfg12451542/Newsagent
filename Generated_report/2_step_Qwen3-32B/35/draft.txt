Kilmarnock fans celebrated a hard-fought 3-0 victory over Brora Rangers on Saturday, marking the start of manager Steve Kettlewell's tenure and the debut of nine new signings. The match, played at Rugby Park, served as a crucial test for the revamped squad ahead of the new season. Fans praised the team's resilience, with many highlighting standout performances and optimism for the upcoming campaign.      'A very good, competitive game—really enjoyed it,' said fan Gordon, who lauded the team's effort. However, he acknowledged the challenges of the Kettlewell era's opening match: 'Not as easy as I thought it would be, but the three points are the most important thing.' The win sets a positive tone ahead of Wednesday's next fixture against Livingston, a match Gordon eagerly anticipates: 'Well done Killie and Kettlewell. Bring on Livingston… hopefully another three points.'      Young players also drew praise, particularly 16-year-old Ben Brannan. 'He reminds me of Aaron Hickey—can play RWB or LWB, is comfortable coming inside and taking men on and has a great engine,' noted fan Amy, expressing hope for his continued development. Meanwhile, George emphasized the team's tactical progress: 'Solid start, keep on improving. The team's shape and tactics will come together. Good solid signings. Come on the Killie.'      The match, part of Kilmarnock's pre-season preparations, showcased early cohesion under Kettlewell. With the full squad now integrated, supporters remain cautiously optimistic as the team eyes a strong campaign in the Scottish Championship.
