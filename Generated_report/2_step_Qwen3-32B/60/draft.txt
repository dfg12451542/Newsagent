Two women were killed and two others injured in a shooting at a church in Lexington, Kentucky, after a suspect opened fire following a confrontation with law enforcement, officials confirmed. The incident began around 11:36 a.m. local time on Sunday when a state trooper was shot during a traffic stop near Blue Grass Airport. The suspect, identified as 47-year-old Guy House, carjacked a vehicle and fled toward Richmond Road Baptist Church, where he fatally shot two women—72-year-old Beverly Gum and 32-year-old Christina Combs—before being killed by responding officers. 'The suspect fired his weapon at individuals on church property,' said Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers, adding that the gunman was pronounced dead at the scene.  According to authorities, the trooper initially stopped House after a license plate alert, but the suspect shot the officer, inflicting non-life-threatening injuries, and stole the vehicle. Law enforcement pursued House as he drove approximately 16 miles to the church, where he opened fire. Two male parishioners were wounded in the attack, with one in critical condition and the other stable. Investigators believe House had a connection to individuals at the church, though motives remain under review. 'Preliminary information indicates that the suspect may have had a connection to the individuals at the church,' Weathers stated.  The small congregation described as 'a very tight-knit group of people' by a county coroner’s official was left reeling by the violence. The Kentucky State Police and Public Integrity Unit are leading the investigation. Historical records from Kent Police note that firearms have been involved in similar incidents, though this shooting was confirmed 'not related to terrorism.' The community mourns the loss of Gum and Combs, whose deaths mark a tragic end to a day that began with a routine traffic stop and spiraled into violence.
