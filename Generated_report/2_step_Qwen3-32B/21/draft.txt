A new survey by the Institute of Directors (IoD) Jersey reveals strong demand for closer collaboration between the Channel Islands to address shared economic challenges, with 70 members contributing to the findings. The report highlights that while 56% of respondents are optimistic about their own businesses, only 17% share confidence in Jersey’s broader economy. Alex Ruddy, IoD Jersey Chair, emphasized the results as a 'clear mandate for action,' calling for strategic investments in infrastructure, housing, and innovation, alongside policy clarity to restore economic confidence. 'Collaboration across the Channel Islands... is essential to Jersey's future prosperity,' Ruddy stated, noting the need for joint efforts to attract industries and tackle affordability crises.  The survey aligns with recent findings from Guernsey’s IoD, which identified parallel challenges in economic growth, demographics, and fiscal management. Richard Hemans, IoD Guernsey’s economics lead, underscored the islands’ 'similar economic, geographical, and fiscal profiles,' urging political leaders to prioritize unity. Respondents suggested learning from Guernsey’s fiscal deficit strategies, such as reducing public spending and boosting growth. Additionally, the report highlights opportunities in entrepreneurship, AI adoption (with 70% of companies offering AI training), and youth talent development through tax incentives.  Historical data reinforces the urgency of these calls, citing a prior report that estimated over £150 million in annual tax revenue could be generated by improving workforce participation and addressing aging populations across the islands. As businesses advocate for cross-island cooperation, the IoD surveys present a unified plea for coordinated action to secure long-term prosperity.
