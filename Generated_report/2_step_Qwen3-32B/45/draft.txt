Chelsea secured a dominant 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain in the Club World Cup final on July 13, 2025, in New Jersey, with goals from Cole Palmer and a brace from Joao Pedro. The Blues claimed their second major trophy of the season, following their triumph in the Conference League in May, to cement a memorable campaign under manager Enzo Maresca. The win marked a decisive statement against a PSG side that had entered the match with formidable form, having won their previous five knockout-stage encounters since the Coupe de France final by a combined score of 18-0.      Despite PSG's recent dominance in high-stakes matches, Chelsea's tactical discipline and clinical finishing proved insurmountable. Palmer opened the scoring in the first half, while Pedro added two second-half goals to seal the result. The performance underscored Chelsea's resilience ahead of a critical summer transfer window, as the club aims to strengthen its squad for the 2025/26 UEFA Champions League campaign.      The Club World Cup victory adds to Chelsea's storied history in international competitions, joining their 2012 FIFA Club World Cup triumph. With the Blues now holding two major titles this season, fans and analysts alike are speculating on their potential to challenge for domestic and European honors next term. The result also marks a historic moment for English football, as the Premier League side continues to assert its global dominance against continental powerhouses.
