A three-year-old girl from Powys is set to make history as one of the youngest competitors ever to qualify for Crufts, the world's largest dog show. Caitlyn Mellor, accompanied by her eight-year-old Miniature Schnauzer Zeeva, secured their spot after placing at the Three Counties Championship Show last month. The duo, from Groesffordd near Brecon, will compete in next year’s event, defying the current record for the youngest competitor, which stands at 14 years old.      Caitlyn, who began showing Zeeva in the ring at age three, has already demonstrated remarkable poise. 'She is so confident generally and she works so well with the dogs,' said her mother, Lydia Mellor. 'She will shake hands with the judges, she shakes hands with the winners, she always congratulates everyone.' Lydia expressed pride in Caitlyn’s sportsmanship, noting, 'I am massively proud of the sportsmanship she is showing at this age.' Caitlyn herself simply stated, 'I like running around with her,' during a recent competition.      Crufts organizers highlighted Caitlyn’s achievement as both extraordinary and emblematic of the sport’s accessibility. Helen Kerfoot, a spokesperson, remarked, 'We are delighted to see a young competitor doing so well... Taking part in Crufts is a real achievement but it's also something that's accessible to all.' Lydia emphasized the bond between competitors and their dogs, stating, 'They are our pets, they are spoilt rotten, they want for nothing—we have a great day out.' As Caitlyn prepares to break barriers at Crufts, her story underscores the blend of dedication, joy, and tradition that defines the event.
