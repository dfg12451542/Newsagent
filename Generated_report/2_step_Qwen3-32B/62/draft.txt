MasterChef presenter John Torode has confirmed he is the subject of an allegation involving the use of racist language, revealed as part of an inquiry into separate misconduct claims against co-host Gregg Wallace. In a statement posted on Instagram, Torode acknowledged the claim, which reportedly dates to 2018 or 2019, but stated he has 'no recollection' of the incident and 'do not believe that it happened.' He added that the individual he was speaking with at the time allegedly did not perceive the remarks as malicious and that he apologized immediately afterward.  Torode emphasized his stance against racial language, declaring, 'Any racial language is wholly unacceptable in any environment,' while expressing that he is 'shocked and saddened' by the allegation. The controversy emerges amid broader investigations into conduct by MasterChef personalities, with historical records noting two additional allegations involving racist language tied to other individuals.  The inquiry into Wallace, which initially prompted public scrutiny of the show, has now expanded to include Torode, who has remained on the program despite the ongoing review. Torode’s statement underscores the tension between accountability and personal denial, as he seeks to reconcile the allegation with his public persona as an advocate for inclusivity in culinary spaces.
