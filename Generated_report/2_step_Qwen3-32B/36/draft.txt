A 25-year-old man has been charged with murder following a street incident in Aberdeen, Scotland. Brandon Booth appeared at Aberdeen Sheriff Court on July 14, 2025, where he made no plea and was remanded in custody. The incident, which has sparked local concern, has been described by prosecutors as a tragic and violent altercation. Relatives of the victim, Dylan Geddes, expressed their grief, stating, 'The family is absolutely devastated by what has happened,' and 'Dylan was a much-loved son, grandson, brother and nephew.' They added, 'He will be sorely missed every day by all who knew him.'      Historical records indicate that Booth had previously faced charges of attempted murder and intentional strangulation, though details of those cases remain undisclosed. The current murder charge marks a significant escalation in the legal proceedings against him. The case has drawn attention to public safety in Aberdeen, with community members calling for clarity on the circumstances surrounding the incident.      The court hearing underscored the gravity of the situation, as Booth’s next appearance is expected to focus on pre-trial preparations. The Geddes family’s emotional statements highlighted the personal impact of the case, while prosecutors emphasized the need for a thorough investigation into the events leading to Dylan Geddes’ death.
