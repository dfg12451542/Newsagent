A devastating fire has caused major damage to the iconic Worm's Head Hotel in Rhossili, Gower, with the owners describing the destruction as 'heartbreaking and soul destroying.' The Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service responded to the blaze at 00:51 BST on Monday, which engulfed the accommodation block’s roof space, leading to a 25m (82ft) span collapse. Fortunately, no casualties were reported, and all occupants were accounted for, though the cause of the fire remains under investigation. The fire was extinguished by 05:30, with crews remaining on-site until 09:32 to monitor the area.  The owners, who have operated the hotel for 25 years, shared their anguish on social media, stating, 'It is particularly heartbreaking... to see 25 years of hard work destroyed in less than 30 mins.' They expressed uncertainty about the road to recovery but vowed to 'come back stronger.' Guests, including regular visitors like Sian Nichol and Beverly, reacted with shock and sadness. Nichol said, 'We used to come every summer. It’s absolutely shocking to see it like this,' while Beverly described feeling 'emotional' recalling New Year’s celebrations at the venue.  Hotel management confirmed the bar and hotel will remain closed pending further updates, assuring, 'Everyone’s safe. Thank you for your well wishes.' South Wales Police assisted at the scene. The incident echoes a 2025 fire at the Queens Hotel in Blaenau Ffestiniog, where a similar roof blaze left residents homeless, underscoring the vulnerability of historic properties to such disasters. As investigations continue, the community rallies around the landmark, hoping for a resilient resurgence.
