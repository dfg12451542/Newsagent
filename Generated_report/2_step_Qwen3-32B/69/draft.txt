Crews are battling a large fire at an industrial site in Lancashire, with thick plumes of smoke visible for miles. Firefighters have deployed eight fire engines to the scene, where the blaze has been described as 'giving off large volumes of smoke' by LFRS group manager Mark Warwick. A spokesperson for Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) confirmed crews are 'working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely,' though no injuries have been reported so far. The smoke, which has risen above the surrounding area, has prompted authorities to urge residents to keep windows and doors closed if they notice any fumes.      The fire has led to significant disruptions, including the closure of nearby roads such as part of the Bay Gateway. Warwick emphasized the scale of the smoke, warning the public to take precautions as crews work to contain the situation. Despite the intensity of the blaze, officials have not reported any casualties, and efforts remain focused on preventing the fire from spreading to adjacent properties.      This incident echoes a 2023 fire at an industrial estate in Leyton, London, which also produced extensive smoke and required a multi-engine response. While the Lancashire fire is currently under control efforts, the environmental and logistical challenges mirror those faced in similar industrial fires, highlighting ongoing concerns about safety protocols at such sites. Authorities have not yet released details on the cause of the blaze.
