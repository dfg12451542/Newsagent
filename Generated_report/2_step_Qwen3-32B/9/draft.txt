Toprak Razgatlioglu solidified his position at the forefront of the World Superbike Championship with a dominant performance at Donington Park, securing three victories over the weekend and moving into first place in the standings. The Turkish rider, representing BMW, clinched the second feature race on Sunday, building on his earlier triumphs in Saturday’s opening feature race and the Superpole sprint. His victories narrowed the gap to title rival Nicolo Bulega to just one point before Sunday’s final race, ultimately allowing Razgatlioglu to extend his lead by four points as Bulega finished second.      Alvaro Bautista rounded out the podium, with Danilo Petrucci and Andrea Locatelli following in third and fourth places, respectively. The results marked a strong resurgence for Razgatlioglu, who has consistently challenged for the championship title in recent seasons. Notably, six-time champion Jonathan Rea, competing in his 20th World Superbike season, secured the final point of the day by finishing 15th, improving slightly from his fifth-place finish in Saturday’s race—the best result of his 2025 campaign thus far.      Razgatlioglu’s three wins at Donington Park underscore his growing dominance in the series, particularly with BMW’s competitive machinery. His performance echoes his previous successes at the circuit, where he has long been a favorite. With the championship now tightly contested, the focus shifts to upcoming rounds as Bulega and Razgatlioglu aim to reclaim momentum. For Rea, the event highlighted both resilience and the challenges of adapting to a new team, as he continues his pursuit of a record-breaking seventh title.
