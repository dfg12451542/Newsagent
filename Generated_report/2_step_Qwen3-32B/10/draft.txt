St Mirren manager Stephen Robinson expressed disbelief and disappointment after his team's goalless draw with Arbroath in the Premier Sports Cup, followed by a penalty shootout defeat in a match that left both players and fans stunned. The Scottish Premiership side, facing League 1 champions Arbroath away from home, squandered a chance to secure a bonus point, with Robinson admitting he 'didn’t recognise' aspects of his team’s performance. 'I’m in a little bit of shock,' he told club media, acknowledging the disconnect between his side’s pre-season form and their display at Gayfield Park.  Robinson praised the efforts of 'maybe 13 of them' for matching the intensity of their pre-season work but criticized decision-making and passing errors that cost the team. 'We decided as a collective not to do that and got exactly what we deserved,' he added, while apologizing to supporters who traveled for the Saturday fixture. The defeat comes ahead of a crucial league clash against Forfar Athletic, where Robinson insists his squad must 'react' and return to fundamentals. 'We have to do simple things which we didn’t do,' he said, vowing not to dwell on the result.  The match marks a rare setback for St Mirren this pre-season, which had otherwise seen strong performances. Historical context shows the club’s recent focus on cup competitions, including a Scottish Cup tie against Hearts in February 2025. With the Premier Sports Cup offering early-season momentum, Robinson’s team now faces a critical test to bounce back and silence growing concerns ahead of the league campaign.
