The M11 near Stansted Airport was reopened on July 13, 2025, following a vehicle fire that caused significant disruption earlier in the day. Fire crews and emergency services attended the scene, with National Highways reporting delays of up to 60 minutes for motorists attempting to approach the area. The incident prompted temporary closures and congestion, though traffic has since resumed, albeit with lingering delays.      Nottinghamshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed its involvement, working alongside police to manage the scene and advising drivers to avoid the affected stretch of the M11 and nearby M1 where possible. A National Highways spokesperson noted that approximately 1.5 miles of the motorway remained congested, urging travelers to allow extra time for their journeys as delays gradually ease.      Images from the scene captured hundreds of vehicles at a standstill, with some drivers abandoning their cars and walking along the motorway. The fire, which occurred during a critical travel period, highlighted the vulnerability of major transport routes to sudden disruptions. Authorities have not yet released details about the cause of the blaze or whether anyone was injured.      This incident echoes similar disruptions in the region, underscoring the challenges of managing traffic and safety on high-traffic motorways. National Highways reiterated its commitment to improving incident response times and minimizing long-term impacts on commuters.
