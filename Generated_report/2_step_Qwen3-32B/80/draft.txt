Scottish football transfers and managerial updates dominate the latest gossip, with Celtic manager Brendan Rodgers acknowledging the need for improvement in his team's frontline. Speaking ahead of the new season, Rodgers stated, 'It's clear that we need to improve our frontline,' signaling potential reinforcements as the club eyes domestic and European success. Meanwhile, Hearts are nearing deals for incoming signings Sabah Kerjota and Pierre Landry Kabore, though final visa paperwork remains pending, according to the Edinburgh Evening News.      The race for Rangers target Jesurun Rak-Sakyi has intensified, with a Premier League side entering the fray. The 22-year-old winger, currently back at Crystal Palace after a loan spell at Sheffield United, has been a priority for Ibrox, which seeks to secure him on loan or negotiate a reduced transfer fee from Palace's initial £10 million asking price. Historical reports confirm Rangers' prolonged pursuit of the player, highlighting his potential to bolster their attacking options.      Elsewhere, St Johnstone full-back Andre Raymond is close to a move to Finland, as revealed by the Courier. The development adds to a busy transfer window for Scottish clubs, which also sees Celtic's Rodgers adopting a direct managerial approach. Former Scotland midfielder Leanne Crichton noted, 'I don't think Rodgers is the type of manager who would wait about and try to encourage you to stay at Celtic and apply yourself,' underscoring his no-nonsense philosophy. With key transfers and strategic decisions underway, Scottish football's summer saga continues to evolve.
