Nigeria and Algeria have secured their places in the quarter-finals of the 2024 Women's Africa Cup of Nations (Wafcon) following a goalless draw in their Group B clash, with the Super Falcons advancing as group winners to face Zambia. The match in Casablanca saw both teams unable to break the deadlock, but Nigeria's strong performance ensured they topped the standings, while Algeria progressed as runners-up to meet the second-placed team from Group C. The result sets up a crucial tie for the Super Falcons, who will aim to continue their dominance in the tournament. 'It’s a hard-fought result, but we’re focused on the next challenge,' said Nigeria’s coach, reflecting on the tightly contested game.  In another Group B encounter, Botswana staged a dramatic comeback to defeat Tunisia 2-1, with Gaonyadiwe Ontlametse scoring a stoppage-time winner to keep their hopes alive for a third-place playoff berth. Tunisia had taken the lead through Yesmin Khanchouch in the 12th minute, but Lesego Radiakanyo equalized for Botswana midway through the second half. The victory provided a boost for the Zebras of Botswana, who remain in contention for one of the two best third-placed spots.  Meanwhile, defending champions South Africa and Mali have already qualified for the quarter-finals from Group C ahead of their final match. Banyana Banyana and Mali will face off in Oujda, while Ghana and Tanzania clash in Berkane, with both sides needing wins to secure progression. The tournament, which has seen high expectations for Nigeria and Algeria as pre-tournament favorites, continues to deliver tense and competitive encounters as teams vie for a place in the last eight. With the quarter-finals set to begin, the stage is set for thrilling football in the race for continental glory.
