A car fire on the A30 in Cornwall escalated into a moorland blaze on July 13, 2025, prompting a swift response from emergency crews. According to the Cornwall Fire & Rescue Service, the incident began on the eastbound lane near Temple Tor on Bodmin Moor, where flames quickly spread from the vehicle to the surrounding dry vegetation. Firefighters worked to contain the blaze, which posed challenges due to the remote and rugged terrain of the area.      Crews from Launceston and Bodmin Fire Stations were deployed to tackle the fire, with reports indicating that the situation was brought under control after several hours of effort. Smoke from the burning vehicle and moorland was visible for miles, disrupting traffic on the A30 and prompting temporary closures. Authorities urged drivers to avoid the area and adhere to detour routes.      This incident highlights the heightened risk of wildfires in moorland regions during periods of dry weather. Emergency services emphasized the importance of vigilance, reminding the public to report any suspicious activity or signs of fire immediately. No injuries have been reported, but investigations into the cause of the car fire are ongoing.
