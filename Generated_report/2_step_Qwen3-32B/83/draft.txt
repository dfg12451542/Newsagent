This week on Teesside, a rescued puppy named Jager captured hearts after being found wandering the streets of Hartlepool. The eight-week-old Belgian Malinois was taken in by animal charity Stray Aid, with Durham Police confirming his rescue. Historical details from search records describe Jager as having tan fur with black around his eyes and nose, his ears partially flopped, and his head peeking out of a police dog handler vest. Stray Aid has since reached out to the Dog Support Unit to assess whether the pup has potential for future service.  Residents in Redcar also raised concerns after spotting a large fish floating on the surface of Locke Park’s lake. The Environment Agency investigated and concluded the incident was caused by 'natural factors,' though no further specifics were provided. Meanwhile, the Department for Transport confirmed new infrastructure work as part of a £92bn national package, marking a significant development for the region.  These stories highlight a mix of community efforts, environmental curiosity, and major infrastructure updates, underscoring the diverse happenings on Teesside this week.
