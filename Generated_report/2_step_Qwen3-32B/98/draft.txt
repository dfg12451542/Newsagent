Swansea made history on July 13, 2025, as the UK’s first-ever IRONMAN Pro Series event unfolded under sweltering conditions, with air temperatures exceeding 28°C and water temperatures surpassing 24°C. The professional swim was contested without wetsuits for the first time in the UK due to the heat, setting the stage for a fiercely competitive race. Defending champion Harry Palmer secured back-to-back victories in Swansea, claiming the overall Pro Series title with a time of 3:51:18, just 49 seconds ahead of his training partner, France’s Leon Chevalier. 'It was hot, and I do feel for all the age-groupers who are out there today,' Palmer told BBC Sport Wales, acknowledging the challenges faced by amateur athletes. 'The course is amazing... but it’s the people — it’s the Welsh. They just know how to put on an event,' he added, praising the local support.  Kat Matthews, the 2024 Ironman Pro Series champion, returned to Swansea to reclaim victory with a time of 4:20:37. 'Three years feels like a lifetime,' said Matthews, reflecting on her previous win in the city in 2022. The British athlete overcame a strong field, including compatriot Rebecca Anderbury and Germany’s Danila Kleiser, who finished second and third, respectively. Race Director Rebecca Sutherland hailed the event as a triumph, noting 'thousands of athletes' crossed the iconic red carpet finish line amid 'incredible' spectator support.  The race marked a milestone for the IRONMAN Pro Series, with Swansea’s 70-mile course drawing both elite athletes and age-group competitors, many of whom tackled their first IRONMAN 70.3. Organizers confirmed the event will return next summer for its fourth edition. Meanwhile, historical context highlights the broader Ironman landscape in the UK, including the grueling full-distance race in Leeds—a 112-mile bike ride, 2.4-mile swim, and marathon run—that has seen athletes like one competitor lose 19.5st (124kg) in preparation for the 17-hour challenge. Swansea’s success underscores the growing prominence of triathlon events across the country, blending elite competition with community spirit.
