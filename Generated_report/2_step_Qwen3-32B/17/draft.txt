A West Yorkshire Police officer has been charged with sexual assault by touching, the force confirmed on 14 July. PC Jack Blagbrough, 31, who serves in the Kirklees District, is set to appear at Barnsley Magistrates' Court on 16 July. The alleged offense, which occurred while Blagbrough was off-duty, involves a woman, and he has been suspended from duty since the investigation began. This case adds to a broader context of serious sexual assault investigations in the region. Between October 2021 and July 2022, West Yorkshire Police handled a high-profile case involving two charges of assault by penetration and four counts of sexual assault on a child under 13. While the current case is distinct, it underscores ongoing challenges in addressing such crimes, with the force emphasizing its commitment to thorough investigations and accountability. Historical records also note separate incidents, including a serious sexual assault near a Nottingham canal under investigation in recent years, though these are unrelated to the Kirklees case.
