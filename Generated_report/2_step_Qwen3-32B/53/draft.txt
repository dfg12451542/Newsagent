ArtHouse Jersey has announced the recipients of its second round of development grants, offering financial and creative support to local artists across diverse disciplines. Among the awardees is actor and writer Jack Evans-Rentsch, who is developing a comedy centered on remote island politics. 'There were plans to shoot the short film produced by Tiny Speck Productions on location in Jersey with a local cast and crew,' Evans-Rentsch noted, highlighting the project's community-focused approach. FlashMob band member Henry Matson also received funding for a second EP, which the rock band aims to record later this year. 'The support that ArtHouse Jersey provides to local artists is invaluable and ensures that grassroots artists are provided with opportunities they would not otherwise have,' Matson emphasized.  Other recipients include Hanni El Dardiry Osman, who will use the grant to undergo chainsaw operation training for timber processing to craft guitars, and filmmaker Sean Frost, whose grant will fund his cinematic short film *Memoriam*. Frost explained that the grant covers most of the film's budget, with production slated for Autumn in Jersey. 'ArtHouse Jersey's generous grant will provide the majority of the budget for the film *Memoriam*, an 8-10 minute short that will be shot in Jersey in the Autumn,' he stated.  ArtHouse Jersey's quarterly funding initiative, as outlined in its mission, aims to nurture new creative projects and empower individuals pursuing arts careers. By allocating resources to diverse endeavors—from music production to film and artisanal craftsmanship—the organization continues to strengthen Jersey's cultural landscape while fostering economic and artistic collaboration within the community.
