The 20th Island Games opened on July 13, 2025, in Orkney with a star-studded ceremony featuring Princess Anne, who met Channel Islands athletes in a celebrated moment described as a 'once in a lifetime' experience. Athletes from Alderney, including runner Benjamin Mutua and his coach Julie Longden, shared their excitement after conversing with the princess about their journey to the Games. 'The encounter was amazing,' said Longden. 'I have met royalty before, but because of her love of sport, it was so good to talk to her.' Princess Anne’s presence added prestige to the event, which sees competitors from islands across the UK and beyond showcase their talents until July 18.  Spectators, including Jersey residents Lucy and Nick Le Boutillier, traveled extensively to support family members competing in the Games. The couple, who queued for two and a half hours to secure entry, praised the 'glorious' weather and the 'great' opening ceremony. 'We’re her biggest supporters,' Lucy said, referring to her daughter Tilly, a gymnast. Meanwhile, Doreen Manson, an Orkney resident, performed at the ceremony with her exercise group, highlighting fitness for older adults. 'It’s just something we’ve never seen before,' Manson said, noting the energy brought by athletes from diverse nations.  Lorraine Kelly, a guest at the ceremony, expressed pride in the event’s organization and her fondness for Orkney. 'You know, I don’t know that the others [Channel Islands] need me, I think because they’re so good,' she remarked, adding that the smaller islands, like Sark, 'made more noise than anyone else.' The Games, running through July 18, have already captivated locals and visitors alike, blending athletic competition with cultural celebration under the banner of island unity.
