Channel Islands athletes described the experience of meeting Princess Anne as a 'once in a lifetime' opportunity at the opening ceremony of the 20th Island Games. The ceremony, held in Orkney, Scotland, was officially opened by Princess Anne, marking the commencement of the celebrated multi-sport event that gathers athletes from various islands worldwide.nnAmong the athletes, Alderney runner Benjamin Mutua accompanied by his coach, Julie Longden, had the special occasion to speak directly with Princess Anne about their journey to Orkney. Ms. Longden was quoted as saying that their encounter with the Princess was 'amazing,' emphasizing the shared connection through Anne's well-known passion for sports. 'I have met royalty before, but because of her love of sport it was so good to talk to her,' Ms. Longden remarked.nnThe vibrant opening was also a special moment for broadcaster Lorraine Kelly, who expressed her emotional connection to the Orkney Islands and her pride in the event's organization. Events like the Island Games have been instrumental in bringing attention and enthusiasm to the regions they are hosted in, with this year's edition demonstrating no exception. Spectator Doreen Manson, who performed alongside her exercise group during the ceremony, noted the excitement and unique atmosphere brought to the locale she has called home all her life.nnSpectators Nick and Lucy Le Boutillier made the journey from Jersey to cheer for their daughter, Tilly, who is competing in gymnastics. Despite long queues as people awaited entrance, Mr. Le Boutillier commented on the determination to secure a spot among the limited 3,000 available in attendance. The ardent support for the athletes and the community spirit encapsulated the essence of the Island Games, fostering camaraderie and sporting excellence.nnThe 2025 Island Games will continue until July 18, featuring a wide array of sporting events across Orkney, inviting both familiar faces and new talents to shine on the international stage.
