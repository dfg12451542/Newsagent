A heroic rescue unfolded at Guernsey's Port Grat Beach when a woman saved her husband's life after he collapsed in the sea. Jacky Meinke, 67, leapt into action when her husband, Chris Meinke, 77, found himself face down in the water on Friday afternoon. 'I heard a splash and carried on swimming, thinking he was behind me,' expressed Mrs. Meinke, recounting the moment she realized her husband was in distress. 'When I looked around, his head was face down in the water,' she said as she swiftly moved to rescue him.nnAccording to Mrs. Meinke, who is trained in first aid, she was able to roll her husband's head onto her shoulder and deliver a sharp blow to his back. This quick-thinking maneuver caused Mr. Meinke to cough and regain consciousness. Rosie Mclauchlan, a fellow swimmer, witnessed the event, describing Mr. Meinke's scenario succinctly: 'He was literally drowning.' Together, the two women managed to bring him ashore, where paramedics later attended to him. Fortunately, Mr. Meinke's condition stabilized, though the origin of his collapse remains unclear.nnWhile Mrs. Meinke expressed immense gratitude towards Ms. Mclauchlan and the bystanders who rushed to assist, Mr. Meinke reflected on the incident with a mixture of relief and uncertainty. 'The hospital thinks I had vertigo. I just didn't know which way was up,' he speculated. Despite the harrowing experience, both Mr. and Mrs. Meinke are now focused on uncovering the cause of the incident through further medical evaluations.nnThe incident highlights the importance of being prepared and having first aid knowledge, especially during such sudden, life-threatening situations. The principled actions of Mrs. Meinke, coupled with the courage of Ms. Mclauchlan and the supportive beachgoers, underline the power of quick thinking and community spirit in averting potential tragedy at sea.
