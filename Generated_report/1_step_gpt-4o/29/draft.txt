Petition to save the historic Victoria Marine Lake in St Helier has gained significant momentum, reaching 1,000 signatures as of July 14, 2025. The 128-year-old sea pool, a crucial part of Jersey's coastal heritage, is now set to prompt a response from the island's government due to the overwhelming public interest. Campaigners, including the newly-formed Save Our Pool Working Group, emphasize the lake's potential benefits for health, leisure, and tourism.nnPetition organiser Nina Zaech, referring to the pool as a 'vital piece of Jersey's heritage,' highlighted its importance not only historically but also for contemporary community use. However, a 2024 inspection report revealed that the pool required 'comprehensive work,' raising concerns about its future viability. Infrastructure Minister Andy Jehan recently noted that the cost of repairs might be uneconomical, especially with planned sea defence upgrades along the waterfront.nnThis critical situation draws attention to the fact that Victoria Marine Lake predates the more renowned Havre des Pas pool by eight years, yet remains unlisted and at risk. With a renewed push for restoration, the petition aims to ensure the lake's preservation for future generations. As public pressure mounts, local authorities will be called to reassess their stance on maintaining this historic site amid the tensions of modern infrastructure development and heritage conservation.
