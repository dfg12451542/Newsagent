A 25-year-old man, Brandon Booth, has been charged with murder following a grave incident on Pennan Road in Aberdeen. Booth made no plea and was remanded in custody after appearing at Aberdeen Sheriff Court. The case has sent ripples through the local community as people try to come to grips with the tragic event that left a young life cut short.nnThe victim, identified as Dylan Geddes, was found seriously injured at the scene and later succumbed to his injuries. His family has been left devastated by this tragic loss. 'The family is absolutely devastated by what has happened,' said relatives of Mr. Geddes. They described Dylan as a 'much-loved son, grandson, brother, and nephew' who will be 'sorely missed every day by all who knew him'.nnPolice Scotland continues to appeal for any information related to the incident as the investigation unfolds. Det Insp James Callander of the major investigation team expressed his sympathies to the Geddes family, stating that his thoughts are with them during this difficult time. Residents in the Northfield area of Aberdeen, where Mr. Geddes resided, are reeling from the news, and authorities are on high alert for any leads in this case.nnThe scene of the incident remains a point of gathering for those who wish to pay their respects and contribute any information that may assist the police investigations. An image of Dylan Geddes, showing him smiling against a backdrop of blue skies, has been widely shared as the community comes together to remember him and seek justice.
