The Open: Welcome to PortrushnnThe serene town of Portrush in Northern Ireland is the backdrop for an exhilarating week of golf as it hosts the 153rd Open Championship from July 17-20. The world’s greatest golfers have gathered to compete for the prestigious 'Champion Golfer of the Year' title, bringing with them a sense of anticipation and excitement to the storied links of Royal Portrush. As sports fans worldwide look forward to thrilling competition, the BBC promises comprehensive coverage of every major development throughout the tournament.nnDefending champion Xander Schauffele is returning to continue his Open campaign after his impressive victory last year at Royal Troon in Scotland. Meanwhile, the presence of Rory McIlroy adds an extra layer of intrigue for local fans, as the Northern Irish golf icon and Masters champion aims to secure yet another Grand Slam title on familiar grounds. Royal Portrush, which witnessed Shane Lowry's remarkable triumph in 2019, is set to offer another classic golfing spectacle where hometown support could prove to be a pivotal advantage.nnWith live radio and text broadcasts, along with press conferences featuring star players like McIlroy and Schauffele, BBC Sport is committed to delivering in-depth insights and expert analysis. Golf correspondent Iain Carter and reporters on the scene will provide viewers with a frontline perspective of all the action. To stay updated throughout the week, fans are encouraged to add the championship to their 'MySport' feed on the BBC Sport website or app.nnAs the coastal winds of Portrush prepare to test the mettle of the world’s best golfers, the history-rich fairways await new stories to unfold in golf's oldest championship. With excitement at an all-time high, the 153rd Open Championship promises to capture the imagination of sports enthusiasts and redefine golfing excellence over the coming days.
