Fire has engulfed fields near Guildford, Surrey, prompting a major response from local fire services. Some 10 fire crews were dispatched to tackle the large blaze in the Sandy Lane area, close to Piccards Farm House. This incident was reported to Surrey Fire & Rescue Service at approximately 16:20 BST on Monday.nnA spokesperson from the rescue service indicated that prompt action was critical in managing the situation: 'The fire broke out in fields adjacent to Piccards Farm House.' Thankfully, the swift response of the fire crews ensured that no properties were damaged during the incident. However, as a precaution, residents in the vicinity have been advised to keep windows and doors closed due to the presence of smoke.nnThis recent fire in Guildford follows a worrying pattern of fire-raising incidents in the wider region. Previously, properties in areas such as Provost Rust Drive, Cummings Park Crescent, and Birkhall Parade witnessed similar incidents of fire outbreak between Friday and Sunday. Given this context, the authorities are vigilant and taking measures to ensure public safety while investigating potential connections between these fires.nnAs efforts to control and eventually extinguish the fire continue, the local community is urged to stay informed through official channels and adhere to safety advice. As the situation develops, Surrey Fire & Rescue Service is expected to provide updates to keep the public apprised and safe.
