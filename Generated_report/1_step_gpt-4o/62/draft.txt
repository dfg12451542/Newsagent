MasterChef presenter John Torode has come forward regarding an allegation of using racist language, a charge part of a broader investigation into other allegations upheld against his co-host Gregg Wallace. In a candid Instagram post on Monday, Torode addressed the claims, which reportedly date back to 2018 or 2019, asserting that while he apologised immediately during the incident, he has no memory of it, stating, 'I do not believe that it happened.'nnTorode's statement mentions that while the person with whom he was speaking did not view the language as malicious, he is dismayed and shocked by the accusations. 'For the sake of transparency, I confirm that I am the individual who is alleged to have used racial language on one occasion,' Torode revealed, expressing his longstanding belief that racial language is 'wholly unacceptable in any environment.' He continued, 'I’m shocked and saddened by the allegation as I would never wish to cause anyone any offence.'nnThis revelation comes against the backdrop of a larger inquiry involving similar allegations of inappropriate conduct against multiple individuals, one of which also includes a case involving racist language.
