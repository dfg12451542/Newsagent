Crews from Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) are currently engaged in combating a significant fire at a commercial premises located in the White Lund Industrial Estate, Morecambe. The fire, which erupted at approximately 09:30 BST, has sent large plumes of smoke into the air, visible from miles away, causing concerns among local residents.nnThe LFRS has deployed eight fire engines to tackle the blaze. A spokesperson confirmed that the service is 'working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely', with no injuries reported at this time. However, the incident has led to the closure of nearby roads, including parts of the Bay Gateway, as safety measures are put in place.nnLFRS group manager Mark Warwick described the fire as 'giving off large volumes of smoke'. He urged residents, 'if you see or smell any smoke in the area, please keep windows and doors closed'. Smoke was described as billowing dramatically above Morecambe Bay, captured in an image depicting the hills in the background obscured by the cloud.nnThis incident recalls a similar situation from an earlier fire event recorded on Hoo Hill Lane, in the Layton area of the town, where LFRS also employed a considerable number of fire engines to manage the situation. As operations continue, residents are advised to stay informed through local news channels for updates on traffic conditions and safety advisories.
