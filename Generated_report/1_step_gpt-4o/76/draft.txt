The M11 highway near Stansted Airport has been reopened following a vehicle fire incident that caused major traffic disruptions on July 13, 2025. The event, which occurred earlier in the day, initially led to significant delays as emergency services rushed to the scene to manage the situation and ensure the safety of the public.nnAccording to a spokesperson from the National Highways, 'There is approximately 1.5 miles of congestion following this incident so please allow time for these delays to clear.' Fire crews quickly attended to the blaze, but motorists faced delays of up to 60 minutes as they approached the area. An image from the scene showed people getting out of their vehicles and walking on the motorway, highlighting the severity of the traffic disruption, with hundreds of cars at a standstill, some with doors open as drivers and passengers sought refuge from the heat.nnIncidents such as these highlight the ongoing challenges faced by highway authorities in maintaining the flow of traffic while prioritizing public safety. No injuries have been reported due to the incident, but travelers are advised to remain patient and plan for potential delays as congestion eases. National Highways will continue to monitor the situation to help ease congestion as quickly as possible, enabling the safe return to normal traffic conditions. This event underscores the importance of motorist awareness and preparedness for unforeseeable traffic disturbances.
