Evian, France - Lottie Woad of England gave a stellar performance at the Evian Championship, narrowly missing out on the major title as Australian golfer Grace Kim clinched her first major victory. Woad, who recently won the Irish Open, fired a remarkable seven-under 64 to set a challenging clubhouse target at 13 under par. However, she fell short of joining the play-off after a crucial missed birdie putt.nnThe championship concluded in dramatic fashion as Grace Kim secured her victory in France by holing a pivotal 12-foot eagle putt on the second extra playing of the 18th hole. This incredible feat emerged during a play-off against her close friend Atthaya Thitikul, who had the chance to claim her own major title but missed an eight-foot birdie putt.nnWoad watched the nail-biting finish unfold from the sidelines at the driving range. Reflecting on the excitement of the event, she added: 'I was trying to be in contention and winning this, so it will be the same there.' Despite the near-miss, Woad collected significant points towards her professional career, drawing nearer to LPGA aspirations. She mentioned the possibility of taking up or deferring a spot on the LPGA Tour, stating, 'It has always been in my dreams to play on the LPGA Tour and I'm grateful for the opportunities of the programme.'nnFor Kim, the triumph is a testament to her perseverance and skill on the course, highlighted by a spectacular finish where she eagled the 18th to draw even, forcing the playoff. Ultimately, Kim’s final putt concluded her journey at 14 under, adding a major victory to her burgeoning career and etching her name amongst the sport’s elite.
