Swansea Hosts UK's First Ever IRONMAN Pro Series Edition on a Sizzling Summer DaynnIn a landmark event for British triathlon, Swansea played host to the UK's inaugural IRONMAN Pro Series competition. The coastal city welcomed thousands of competitors and spectators despite soaring temperatures, marking a historical milestone in the UK's endurance sport calendar. As the mercury rose above 28°C on race day, with water temperatures exceeding 24°C, organizers declared the professional swim a non-wetsuit event, a first for elite athletes in the UK.nnHarry Palmer, Britain's own, proved unyielding in the face of tough conditions, securing back-to-back victories. Palmer completed the grueling course in an impressive 3:51:18, narrowly edging out training partner Leon Chevalier of France. In third place, fellow Brit Kieran Lindars completed the podium. Palmer expressed empathy for the age-group competitors, acknowledging the harsher conditions they faced throughout the day. 'It was hot, and I do feel for all the age-groupers who are out there today because they've got much harder conditions than we did, so fair play to everyone racing,' he remarked to BBC Sport Wales.nnIn the women’s division, Kat Matthews, who had not raced in Swansea since her triumph in 2022, embraced her evolution from past experiences, stating, 'Three years feels like a lifetime. A lot has happened since then.' Matthews emerged victorious with a finish time of 4:20:37, followed by Britain's Rebecca Anderbury and Danila Kleiser of Germany. Despite the intense competition, Matthews managed to pull ahead, describing the experience as feeling 'in control until the run when I felt a bit hot.'nnRace Director Rebecca Sutherland lauded the event, noting the exceptional crowd support and athlete participation. 'It's always great fun when we go racing in Swansea, and this year was no different,' Sutherland commented. The IRONMAN Pro Series will return to Swansea next year, carrying forward the vibrant camaraderie and competitive spirit that characterized this year's event. With the ever-welcoming Welsh community and enthusiastic participants, Swansea is positioned to reinforce its reputation as a premier destination for endurance sports.
