Island Games 2025: Jersey's women's football team commenced their campaign with a 3-1 defeat to defending champions Bermuda in the opener on Sunday. The match, reminiscent of the 2023 Island Games opener where Bermuda defeated Jersey 4-0, saw a similar outcome despite an early lead by the Jersey side.nnJersey's Anita Tavares delivered a promising start as she skillfully netted a left-footed shot in the sixth minute, putting Jersey ahead. The excitement was short-lived, however, as the Bermudans came close to equalizing before halftime, striking the bar and having penalty appeals denied along with their opponents. Jersey's hopes began to fade when Jaden Masters found the back of the net an hour into the match, leveling the score.nnThe critical juncture arrived 14 minutes later when Bermuda's Khyla Brangman scored a penalty, shifting the momentum decisively. Jya Rattaray Smith sealed the victory for Bermuda with a third goal in stoppage time after capitalizing on a rebound off the bar. Bermuda now leads Group B, while the loss places Jersey at the bottom, following a draw between Shetland and Isle of Wight.nnDespite the setback, Jersey looks to restore their tournament journey with upcoming group matches against Shetland Islands on Monday and Isle of Wight on Tuesday. Notably, Jersey was previously unable to secure a semi-final spot in 2023 due to a technicality, highlighting the importance of their upcoming performances. This initial setback underscores the challenging path ahead, as they seek redemption and advancement in this year's competition.
