Zoe Tucker from Sherborne has been crowned a world champion in the duathlon sprint category, after a remarkable victory in Spain last month. Taking up the sport just months ago, Tucker recently found herself atop the podium, an accolade she described as 'an absolute honour.' Her exceptional performance involved a 5km run, a 19.7km bike ride, followed by another 2.5km run, highlighted by Tucker becoming the fastest British woman across all age groups.nnThe journey to world champion began last October when Tucker participated in her first duathlon at Thruxton, Hampshire. She competed at the event alongside a few friends, where she discovered her latent talent for the combination of running and cycling. Reflecting on this unexpected trajectory, Tucker admitted, 'I had absolutely never in my wildest dreams expected to compete – let alone win – at the world championships.'nnThe intensity and scale of the world championships left a lasting impression. 'The scale of the event, the opening ceremony, the parade of nations, walking with GB kit and flag - it was an incredible experience,' Tucker recalled. With sights set on next year's world championships in Abu Dhabi, Tucker plans to train harder, aiming to incorporate an indoor training bike into her regimen to maintain peak competitive form.nnDespite the challenges, Tucker remains enthusiastic and intent on inspiring others. 'If an opportunity comes your way, just grab it and enjoy it along the way,' she advises aspiring athletes. Her victory serves as a testament to spontaneity and dedication, painting a picture of a sporting journey that has only just begun, with more thrilling chapters to come.
