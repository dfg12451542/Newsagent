Hibernian has reportedly shattered its transfer record to secure the signing of Togo international forward Thibault Klidje, bringing him in from Swiss club Luzern. The club announced the acquisition on July 13, noting that the 24-year-old has agreed to a three-year contract at Easter Road with an option for an additional year.nnThe significant investment in Klidje underscores Hibernian's ambition to strengthen their attacking options with the injection of extra pace and skill. Head coach David Gray highlighted the qualities that Klidje brings to the team. 'He adds extra pace and trickery to our front line and performed well for Luzern last season,' Gray stated, emphasizing the player's potential for further development.nnThis move marks a notable moment in Klidje's career, who, after showcasing his talents in Switzerland, is ready to face new challenges in the Scottish league. Hibernian is confident in their new signing's ability to elevate the team’s offensive capabilities. 'We want to help him progress, and look forward to working with him moving forward,' Gray expressed optimistically.nnHistorically, Hibernian has been cautious with its transfer spending, making this record fee for Klidje an indication of their commitment to competing at a higher level. The undisclosed fee reflects the competitive nature of today's transfer market and Hibernian's proactive approach to securing quality talent that aligns with their long-term vision.
