Stephen Robinson, manager of St Mirren, expressed his shock and disappointment following a goalless draw and subsequent penalty defeat at the hands of Arbroath in their Premier Sports Cup opener. The match, which took place at Gayfield Park, left Robinson and the fans both dejected and surprised by the team's performance. 'I'm in a little bit of shock,' Robinson stated post-match, acknowledging the lackluster display that was unforeseen given their promising pre-season.Robinson's sentiments reflected those of the supporters who made the trip to Arbroath, expecting a stronger showing. 'It's disappointing for us, disappointing for the people that travelled a long way,' he admitted, empathizing with fans who missed the comforts of a leisure Saturday to support the team. Despite this setback, Robinson remained optimistic about the squad's future performances, asserting that they 'have to be and will be better.'Historically, St Mirren have overcome tough challenges, famously winning the 2013 League Cup after defeating Celtic in the semi-final and clinching victory against Hearts at Hampden. This resilience is something Robinson aims to harness as he looks for a reaction from his team, particularly as they prepare for their upcoming fixture against League 2 Forfar Athletic on Tuesday. 'We have to react to it, we will be better,' Robinson affirmed, promising improvements in areas that faltered against Arbroath.
