Nutts Corner: Man Dies After Single Vehicle Crash in County AntrimnnA tragic single-vehicle crash occurred on the Ballyhill Road, County Antrim, resulting in the death of the driver of a white Volkswagen Golf. The incident happened at approximately 17:50 BST on Saturday. Police have confirmed that the driver succumbed to injuries at the scene despite the swift response of the ambulance service.nnAuthorities have reported that a passenger in the vehicle, a male, sustained injuries but is currently in a stable condition. 'Police added that a male passenger was taken to hospital with injuries, which are not believed to be life-threatening at this time,' an official statement from the police noted.nnThe Ballyhill Road was temporarily closed following the accident to facilitate investigations and ensure public safety but has since reopened. Witnesses described the area surrounding the crash site, noting floral tributes placed at a nearby wrought iron gate, symbolizing community grief and support. Investigation into the crash is ongoing, and the police have appealed for any witnesses or individuals with information to come forward.
