Kilmarnock fans are expressing their satisfaction and hope after the team's victory against Brora Rangers in their opening match under new manager Stuart Kettlewell. Kilmarnock secured a solid two-goal win over the Highland League champions, marking a promising start to Kettlewell's tenure. The match, held on a warm summer day, saw fans animated and optimistic about the future performances of their beloved team.nnA leading voice among the supporters, Gordon, shared his thoughts on the game, remarking, 'A very good, competitive game really enjoyed it. Not as easy as I thought it would be but the three points are the most important thing.' He looked forward with optimism to Kilmarnock's next fixture against Livingston, expressing confidence in gathering more points, 'Well done Killie and Kettlewell. Bring on Livingston on Wednesday hopefully another three points.'nnAnother fan, Amy, praised the team's young talent, highlighting Ben Brannan's performance as reminiscent of former Kilmarnock player Aaron Hickey. 'A long day but an enjoyable one,' she said. 'Young Ben Brannan reminds me of Aaron Hickey - can play RWB or LWB, is comfortable coming inside and taking men on and has a great engine. Hope to see more of him this season.'nnThe sentiment was echoed by George, who emphasized the importance of continuous improvement and credited the team's solid signings. 'Solid start, keep on improving the team's shape, and tactics will come together. Good solid signings. Come on the Killie,' George commented.nnThis victory marks a historic moment for Kilmarnock as they launch into a new era with managerial changes setting a positive tone. Fans now eagerly await the clash with Livingston, hopeful that their team will continue this momentum.
