Colchester is set to host a memorial running race on July 14 to honor the life of a beloved athlete, who tragically passed away in a road crash. The first anniversary event has been organized by Colchester Harriers AC, the running club of Mr. Arthur Whiston, and will commence at the garrison athletics track in the city at 09:30 BST on Sunday. This heartfelt event is expected to draw numerous participants who want to celebrate Mr. Whiston's inspiring legacy.nnLydia Cunningham, a close friend and fellow runner, remarked on Arthur's cherished presence in the community. 'Arthur was incredibly loved by the community,' she said, highlighting the deep connections he formed during his time with Colchester Harriers AC. 'He was a very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular,' Cunningham added, painting a picture of a man who touched the lives of many.nnIn memory of Mr. Whiston, the proceeds from this running event will be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity that he ardently supported. Participants are encouraged to register on the day of the event, allowing last-minute runners to join the memorial run. The race thus serves not only as a tribute to Mr. Whiston's contributions to sport but also continues his legacy of community and charity work, fostering connections and philanthropy reflecting his enduring spirit.
