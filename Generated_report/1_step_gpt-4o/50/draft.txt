Climber Suffers Head Injury in Fall at Buxton Quarry. On Friday, a dramatic rescue unfolded as Buxton Mountain Rescue Team was dispatched to Hoffman Quarry at Harper Hill, Buxton, after a climber was injured in a fall. The incident occurred around 11:22 BST, when the man slipped while climbing the quarry face. nnA spokesperson confirmed that the climber fell approximately 3 meters (9.8 feet), sustaining a head injury that caused him to briefly lose consciousness. The rescue team promptly arrived on the scene to provide necessary aid to the injured climber. nnTo expedite treatment, he was airlifted to a location where an ambulance was ready to transfer him to the hospital for further medical care. The air ambulance's swift response underscores the importance of rapid intervention in such cases to minimize potential complications. The incident serves as a reminder of the risks associated with quarry climbing and the critical role of emergency services in ensuring the safety of outdoor enthusiasts.
