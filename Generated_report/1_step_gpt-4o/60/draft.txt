Two women were tragically killed at the Richmond Road Baptist Church in Lexington, Kentucky, after a gunman opened fire following a confrontation with law enforcement. The incident began on Sunday when a state trooper pulled over 47-year-old Guy House's vehicle near Blue Grass Airport, a regional hub in Fayette County. According to Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers, the confrontation escalated when Mr. House shot and injured the trooper, leading to a chase that ultimately ended in the church's parking area.nnAfter wounding the trooper, the suspect car-jacked a vehicle, driving about 16 miles to the church with police in pursuit. Upon arrival, he began shooting at parishioners, claiming the lives of 72-year-old Beverly Gum and 32-year-old Christina Combs. Two male congregants were also shot, one critically and the other in stable condition, according to authorities. Chief Weathers noted that Mr. House was shot by responding officers and pronounced dead at the scene, with the intention to release further details pending next-of-kin notification.nnThe Richmond Road Baptist Church community was left in shock, describing their congregation as a 'very tight-knit group of people', where most attendees were closely connected. Investigators are working to establish the motive behind Mr. House's actions, with early indications suggesting a possible personal connection to individuals within the church. The Kentucky State Police and the department's Public Integrity Unit are overseeing the ongoing investigation. As the community comes to terms with this tragedy, police continue to gather evidence to understand the full circumstances that led to this heartbreaking event.
