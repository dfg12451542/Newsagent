More than £8,000 has been raised to support the family of a young boy, Daniel Drewitt, following his tragic death in Sutton Coldfield's Sutton Park lake. An online fundraising campaign, established in the aftermath of the accident, garnered significant public support, reaching this milestone within 48 hours. This financial aid aims to assist the grieving family during what organisers have described as a 'difficult time.'The GoFundMe page, created with the consent of Daniel's parents, initially set a target of £1,600. However, after quickly surpassing this amount, the campaign's organizers set a new goal of £11,000 to continue providing necessary support. Organizer Helena Swallow expressed gratitude on behalf of the family, saying, 'The family are so very grateful, thank you so much,' highlighting the meaningful impact of the donations and messages of support.This tragedy unfolded on Thursday evening when rescuers were alerted to a distress situation at Sutton Park just after 6 PM BST. Unfortunately, despite rapid response efforts, Daniel Drewitt could not be saved, and his body was recovered from the lake later that evening. The local community's heartfelt response through the fundraising page illustrates their shared grief and solidarity with the family in this harrowing time. In a follow-up post, Swallow mentioned that Daniel's mother was 'overwhelmed' by the support, underscoring the communal spirit rallying around the family.This incident adds to the historical concerns regarding water safety in recreational zones, bringing renewed attention to preventive measures and emergency response protocols. As the community mourns the loss of young Daniel, it exemplifies the collective support system that springs into action when faced with such unexpected and poignant circumstances.
