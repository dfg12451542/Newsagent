Ewan Henderson: Midfielder Signs for Wycombe from Belgian Side BeerschotnnIn a substantial boost to their squad, Wycombe Wanderers have announced the signing of midfielder Ewan Henderson, making him the club's eighth addition in the current transfer window. The 25-year-old, who has made a name for himself in the top-tier competitions in Scotland and Belgium, arrives at Adams Park from Belgian side Beerschot for an undisclosed fee.nnWycombe's head coach Mike Dodds expressed his enthusiasm about Henderson's arrival, noting the midfielder's dynamism and experience as valuable assets for the team. 'Ewan's a really energetic midfielder who's had a great start to his career in the top flights in Scotland and Belgium, and gives us another dimension in the middle of the park,' Dodds said. Highlighting Henderson's extensive playing time over the last three years, Dodds added, 'He's played a lot of football in the past three years, which stands him in good stead to come here and hopefully hit the ground running as he integrates into the squad in pre-season.'nnHenderson joins a string of newcomers at Wycombe, including Armando Junior Quitirna, Taylor Allen, Bradley Fink, Caolan Boyd-Munce, Dan Casey, James Tilley, and Mikki van Sas. His previous stint with Beerschot marked him as a versatile player, displaying significant prowess in his performances, notably during matches against teams like Sporting Charleroi. As Wycombe prepares for the upcoming season, Henderson's addition is seen as a strategic move to bolster the team's midfield capabilities.
