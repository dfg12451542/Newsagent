Salisbury, UK – In a remarkable feat of endurance and community spirit, Phyll Babb of Salisbury, Wiltshire, completed a 200-mile walking challenge on her 100th birthday, raising over £11,000 for the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless (STFH). This milestone was celebrated on Sunday as Ms. Babb crossed the finish line cheered on by family and supporters. 'It's hard to believe I've done 200 walks. It's gone like a dream,' said Ms. Babb at the conclusion of her challenge around the historic Salisbury Cathedral. Originally setting out to trek 100 miles, Ms. Babb doubled her target in January after realizing she was ahead of schedule. nnThe centenarian's journey began as a personal challenge, but quickly turned into a community event, inspiring many. 'Walking is good for you,' Ms. Babb explained, crediting her upbringing where walking was a part of daily life. 'I was brought up to walk, so as children we walked everywhere, and I still like walking.' Friends, family members, and even passers-by were eager to express admiration for her achievement. 'She is inspirational, definitely,' remarked Liz Huntley, a longtime admirer. Meanwhile, David Evans noted, 'To reach the age of 100 and to do it so fit, looking so well, being so articulate, that's the really amazing thing she's done.' nnA dedicated local accountant helped log each of Ms. Babb's walks, which were meticulously tracked on her mobile phone using Strava. Her family attended regularly to support each segment of her journey. 'Seeing my great aunt complete her 200th lap around the cathedral has been really special,' shared her great-niece, Ruth Simmons. The funds raised from this venture will assist STFH in its efforts to combat homelessness, aiding recent homeowner Gordon Pardy in furnishing a new housing project. 'We bought a house recently, and so the money that Phyll has raised will go towards furnishing the house,' said Pardy, emphasizing the substantial impact Ms. Babb’s endeavor will have on the community.nnAmidst jubilations, Ms. Babb extended heartfelt thanks to the supporters who contributed to her cause. 'People have been extremely generous,' she expressed with gratitude, acknowledging the wide-reaching support she had received throughout her monumental endeavor. Her story is not only one of personal achievement but a testament to the spirit of giving and community involvement that continues to resonate strongly in Salisbury.
