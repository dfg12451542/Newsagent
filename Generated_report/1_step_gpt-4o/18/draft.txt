Scottish Open: Rory McIlroy Happy & Ready for Portrush Despite Near MissnnIn a thrilling week at the Scottish Open 2025, Rory McIlroy concluded the tournament 13-under par, demonstrating exceptional form despite narrowly missing out on the trophy. McIlroy, who carded 19 birdies over the four rounds at The Renaissance Club in North Berwick, expressed satisfaction with his performance. 'No frustration; it's been a great week,' he remarked after completing his final round.nnMcIlroy highlighted his contentment with various aspects of his game, including his shot control and ball flight during the weekend. 'I'm really happy with where my game is; the way I played over the weekend; the shots that I hit, how I controlled my ball flight. Missing the trophy, that's about it,' he said. Despite challenging windy conditions which tested his proximity to the hole, McIlroy remains positive, noting the numerous opportunities he created.nnAs McIlroy heads to Royal Portrush, he is fueled by the anticipation of playing once again in Northern Ireland, where he previously clinched his first Green Jacket victory. His performance at the Scottish Open sets a formidable stage for his homecoming. 'I feel like I've gotten out of this week what I wanted. I'm looking forward to getting to Portrush tonight and getting out on to the golf course early tomorrow,' he added.nnThis return to Royal Portrush marks a significant moment for McIlroy as he competes in front of the home crowd for the first time since his career-defining triumph. This week will be a poignant reminder of his illustrious journey, as Darren Clarke, heralding the achievement, suggested it places McIlroy among Northern Ireland's greatest athletes. The golfing community eagerly awaits how this momentum from the Scottish Open will propel McIlroy into the heart of Portrush.
