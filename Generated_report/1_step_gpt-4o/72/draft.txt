Car fire ignites moorland blaze on A30 in CornwallnnFire crews have tackled a car fire which swiftly spread to nearby moorland in Cornwall, officials reported earlier today. The incident occurred on the eastbound lane of the A30 close to Temple Tor on Bodmin Moor, a historically significant and environmentally sensitive area. Smoke from the vehicle was seen billowing across the landscape, prompting swift action from local fire departments.nnFirefighters from Launceston and Bodmin were deployed to the scene to manage the conflagration. Their prompt response was crucial in preventing further spread across the moorland, known for its diverse ecosystems and archaeological relevance. 'The flames were intense, but our teams were able to contain it before it wrought more widespread damage,' said the spokesperson for Cornwall Fire & Rescue Service.nnHistorically, Bodmin Moor has faced threats from wildfires, which have endangered native flora and fauna and disrupted local communities. The A30 itself is a critical route, traversing Cornwall and linking it with the rest of England, often congested with both local traffic and tourists during summer months. This incident underscores ongoing concerns regarding fire safety on major roads and the importance of maintaining vigilance during dry seasons.
