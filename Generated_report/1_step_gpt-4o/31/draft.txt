Sean Bean criticises green belt housing plan in area of SheffieldnnActor Sean Bean, known for his roles in 'The Lord of the Rings' and 'Game of Thrones,' has voiced strong opposition to a controversial housing development planned for green belt land in Sheffield. Bean, who grew up in the area, expressed concerns that the proposal would 'practically obliterate the character and nature of our area.' His email of objection came just before the close of the public consultation period, underscoring his commitment to preserving the local environment.nnThe proposed development in the Handsworth area has stirred significant local resistance, with campaign group Save S13 Green Belt - The Sapphire McCarthy Campaign spearheading efforts to block the plan. Sadie Charlton, a leading member of the group, noted the morale boost resulting from Bean's support, stating, 'It's really boosted our morale, and everybody's so thankful for him to put his voice forward.' The campaign group was named in honor of Sapphire McCarthy, a prominent activist who played a critical role in organizing local opposition before her recent passing.nnSheffield is historically recognized for its green spaces, as evidenced by its rank as one of England's greenest cities with multiple parks and woodlands receiving Green Flag awards. This contextualizes the fight against the development as part of a broader effort to protect the city's prestigious environmental standing. Bean pointedly criticized city officials, suggesting they have targeted working-class communities while sparing more affluent areas from similar developments.nnDespite the end of the consultation period on July 11, there is optimism and determination within the community to continue the fight. Ms. Charlton emphasized the collective spirit and unity the campaign has fostered, stating, 'The community have really rallied around and come together, so this is just the beginning for us.' With Bean's high-profile support, the campaign aims to galvanize even more public participation in preserving Sheffield's cherished green belt lands.
