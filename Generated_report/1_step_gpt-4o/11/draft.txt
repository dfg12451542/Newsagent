Jens Berthel Askou, the manager of Motherwell, has called for his team to 'increase tempo' following a challenging draw against Clyde in the Premier Sports Cup. Drawing from the first-hand experience of the game, Askou described the match as an embodiment of how unpredictable football can be. 'It just shows how tricky football is in general,' he remarked to club media, highlighting the sport's unexpected nature, especially in cup tournaments.nnAskou noted the surprises inherent in football, emphasizing that not everything goes as planned. He reflected on the match by stating, 'We didn't get the result we wanted, obviously. A few other clubs, as far as I've heard, didn't do that either.' His comments underline the inherent unpredictability and the crucial need for teams to perform consistently each game.nnDespite the draw, Askou praised his team’s effort and structure but acknowledged areas for improvement, particularly against direct plays. He pointed out, 'Every time we tried to press, they put the ball in behind. I think we should have dealt better with some of their direct play.' Looking forward, he remains optimistic that Motherwell's home venue, Fir Park, will allow the team to play at a 'much higher tempo,' an essential factor for future matches.nnThe match against Clyde, held at New Douglas Park, serves as a learning experience for Motherwell as the team gears up for the rest of the Premier Sports Cup. Askou's focus on increasing intensity and dealing with opposition strategies will be pivotal as they aim to advance in the competition.
