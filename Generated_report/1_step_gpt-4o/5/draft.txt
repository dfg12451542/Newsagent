Trust in the police in Peterborough's Millfield area has seen a marked improvement, thanks to the Alliance project – an initiative aimed at curtailing organized crime. According to Peterborough City Council, the project, which has focused on the Lincoln Road vicinity since its inception, has resulted in a '700% increase in community intelligence reporting.' This development is seen as a significant boost to public confidence in local law enforcement efforts.nnThe Alliance project began in 2023 as part of the Home Office's Clear, Hold and Build strategy, a collaborative effort between the council and Cambridgeshire Police targeting the disruption of criminal organizations. It has also contributed to the seizure of 700,000 illegal cigarettes, 20,000 vapes, and £115,000 in cash, according to the council's recent report to a scrutiny committee. The placement of new drug needle bins further underscores efforts to enhance community safety in the area.nnWhile the project’s achievements are notable, its expansion to other parts of the city seems limited for now. Insp Sam Tucker of Cambridgeshire Police noted that while aspects of the initiative might be adopted elsewhere, full-scale implementation would not proceed immediately due to the project’s intense focus on sustainability and longevity. Despite these challenges, the agencies involved are committed to seeking additional funding to ensure continued success in Millfield.nnReflecting on the impact of the Alliance project, the council acknowledged some struggles in engaging with local groups, indicating room for improvement in community relations. Nonetheless, the substantial contributions of the project in enhancing public safety and trust present a promising outlook for its future developments within the region.
