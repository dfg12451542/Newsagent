The Open 2025 sees top golfers Xander Schauffele and Shane Lowry returning to the prestigious links of Royal Portrush this week. On Monday, Schauffele, the defending champion, took to the course in an attempt to reacquaint himself with the challenging layout and conditions unique to this part of Northern Ireland. Though he holds the title, Schauffele's recent form has been a point of concern. He is recovering from a rib injury sustained earlier this year, which, despite affecting his game, still saw him tying with Scottie Scheffler at nine-under par in last week's Scottish Open.nnAdding to the anticipation is Shane Lowry, the celebrated 2019 Open champion, who also took to the greens on Monday alongside Denmark's rising star, Rasmus Hojgaard. Lowry's triumph on these very grounds six years ago remains a memorable event, punctuated by the jubilant celebrations that erupted across the 18th green. As he makes another bid for the claret jug, Lowry brings both experience and local support to this year's competition.nnThe storied venue of Portrush, with its dramatic landscape and unforgiving weather, has long been a testament to a golfer's adaptability and skill. Both Schauffele and Lowry navigate the course with great familiarity but with different tails of fortune trailing them. As the tournament progresses, golf enthusiasts are eager to see if Schauffele can defend his title against not only the field of competitors but also his own physical challenges, and if Lowry can recreate his past glory on home soil. The opening encounters on Monday set the tone for an exciting contest that promises to captivate audiences around the world.
