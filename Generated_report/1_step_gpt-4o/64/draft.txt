Crystal Palace has unveiled their new home kit for the 2025-26 season, drawing significant attention as the club celebrates its participation in European competitions once again. The launch event showcased a range of players modeling the latest design, signaling both a fresh start aesthetically and a reaffirmation of the club's ambitions on the continental stage.nnAmong those spotlighted were Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, and new signing Maxence Lacroix, alongside stars from Palace Women, Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur. This move highlights Crystal Palace's commitment to promoting both their men's and women's teams on equal standings. The kit features the traditional red and blue palette with modern trims, while goalkeeper Walter Benitez marked his first appearance in the club's light green home jersey.nnIn a swift nod to its functionality, the Eagles wore their new attire in a pre-season match, recording a 1-0 victory over Millwall. The kit's debut not only thrilled the fans but also hinted at promising form as they gear up for the challenges of the forthcoming season, both domestically and in European ties. This sartorial shift is yet another step in Crystal Palace's ongoing evolution, aiming to fortify both their performance and brand image on a global scale.
