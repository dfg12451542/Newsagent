Hereford campervan family cheers Lionesses to victory at UEFA Women's Euros. nnA family from Hereford found themselves full of jubilation as they cheered England's Women's National Team to a resounding victory against Wales in the UEFA Women's Euros. Traveling 800 miles (1,280 km) in their campervan, the Morris family celebrated as the Lionesses secured a decisive 6-1 win at the St Gallen stadium in Switzerland, booking their place in the quarter-finals. nnKevin Morris, who attended the game with his wife Kelly, daughter Amelia, and son Ethan, described the atmosphere in the stadium as 'great'. He spoke to BBC Radio Hereford & Worcester about their football journey while the rest of the family caught up on sleep. 'As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,' said Mr. Morris.nnRecalling their late-night travel back to their campsite in Arbon, nestled on the side of Lake Constance, Mr. Morris noted the challenges they faced. 'We couldn't get on the train and were stranded for a while, but eventually, a bus turned up that took us back,' he said. Despite the inconvenience, Mr. Morris said the experience of watching the Lionesses triumph made it 'worth it' and expressed hope for 'getting through to see the semi-final'.nnThe Morris family's enthusiasm for the Women's Euros highlights a growing movement for women's football in Europe, following England's historic UEFA Women's Euro 2022 victory over Germany. This event, watched by a record-setting crowd, marked a turning point and helped catalyze a broader effort to professionalize the women's game, offering new generations of female athletes the possibility of pursuing football professionally.
