Businesses across Jersey and Guernsey are calling for increased collaboration between the Channel Islands to tackle shared economic challenges, according to the latest economic confidence survey released by the Institute of Directors (IoD) Jersey. More than 70 members participated, and the findings reveal a critical need for strategic alliances and coordinated policy efforts to spur economic growth.nnAlex Ruddy, Chair of IoD Jersey, emphasized the survey's role in providing a 'clear mandate for action.' Though there is confidence reported within individual organizations—56% of respondents were optimistic about their businesses—only 17% hold optimistic views about Jersey’s overall economic landscape. 'While our members are confident in their own organizations,' Ruddy stated, 'they are calling for bold, strategic investment and policy clarity to restore wider economic confidence.'nnEchoing the calls for collaboration, Richard Hemans, IoD Guernsey's lead on economics, underscored the similar economic, geographical, demographic, constitutional, and fiscal profiles between the islands as a basis for intensified cooperation. According to both the Jersey survey and the Guernsey survey released in late June, there exists a fervent recognition that the islands' overlapping challenges, such as fiscal deficits and the need for infrastructure investment, can be best met through a unified approach.nnTop priorities for boosting Jersey's economic growth include infrastructure investment, industry diversification, and housing affordability. The surveys pinpoint the need for fostering entrepreneurship and innovation through tax incentives, talent attraction, and skills development. Ruddy further stressed that 'Collaboration across the Channel Islands, support for innovation, and a focus on infrastructure and housing are essential to Jersey's future prosperity.'nnThe introduction of artificial intelligence is still nascent with 70% of companies offering training and one-third developing strategic approaches to its adoption. As the islands strive to align efforts and bolster economic resilience, the business communities of Jersey and Guernsey are unified in their call for proactive political action and policy alignment to ensure the future prosperity of the Channel Islands.
