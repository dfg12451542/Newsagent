More arrests have been made following a stabbing incident that took place outside a home in Leicester's Eyres Monsell neighborhood. The incident occurred shortly before 9:15 pm on Friday when a man in his 20s was stabbed on Sturdee Road, subsequently making his way to Lawford Road. The injured man was then taken to a hospital, where he remains in stable condition, according to a police spokesperson.nnLeicestershire Police have arrested a total of four more individuals in connection with the stabbing, bringing the investigation to a critical juncture. Initially, a 36-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of attempted murder. Following this, two men, aged 18 and 29, were also taken into custody under similar charges. Additionally, a 38-year-old woman faced arrest on suspicion of conspiracy to commit murder, and a 25-year-old woman was detained on suspicion of assisting an offender.nnThe 18-year-old male and the 25-year-old female suspects remain in police custody, while the other three have been released on bail as investigations continue. Police confirm that the parties involved were acquainted, and house-to-house inquiries as well as a comprehensive scene examination are underway. Detectives are seeking further assistance from the public. Det Sgt Adrian Davies commented, 'It was still light at that time and with the weather being very warm, there were probably quite a few people who were in the area or in their gardens. If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us.' The incident underscores ongoing concerns about knife crime in the region as law enforcement works diligently to resolve the case.
