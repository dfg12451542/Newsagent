The Open 2025 has commenced its festivities with a significant surge of spectators arriving at Royal Portrush on Monday morning, eager to witness Rory McIlroy's first practice round. The excitement is palpable as golf enthusiasts gather to see one of the sport's leading figures prepare for the prestigious tournament. The world number two, who previously claimed victory at The Open in 2014 at Hoylake, was met with an enthusiastic reception as he played through the course.nnMcIlroy did not disappoint the assembled crowd, successfully sinking a birdie putt on the 18th green. In a display of camaraderie, he spent 15 minutes thereafter engaging with fans, signing autographs and adding a personal touch to the morning's experience. As anticipation builds for the competition, McIlroy is scheduled to address the media Monday at 14:00 BST, with 2019 champion and fellow Irishman Shane Lowry following at 15:00 BST.nnThe historical significance of The Open's return to Royal Portrush cannot be overstated, as the event is expected to draw more than 275,000 spectators over the coming days. This influx would set a record as the largest sporting event ever hosted in Northern Ireland, underscoring the immense popularity and cultural importance of the tournament. Shane Lowry, who memorably claimed the title the last time The Open was held here in 2019, will also be one to watch as he seeks to replicate his success on home soil.nnWith both McIlroy and Lowry poised as fan favorites, Royal Portrush is set to become the epicenter of the golfing world this week. The combination of sporting excellence and supportive fans promises to create an electrifying atmosphere in the picturesque setting of Northern Ireland's coastline.
