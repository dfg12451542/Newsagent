A biker is in hospital with serious injuries following a crash on a rural road near Hawick, Scotland. The incident occurred on the B6399 at approximately 12:00 PM on Sunday, when emergency services were alerted to the crash involving a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike. The location of the accident was just south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm, a remote and frequently traversed area by bikers.nnThe 57-year-old motorcyclist was taken to the hospital for treatment of his injuries, which have been described as serious. In the wake of the accident, police have cordoned off the road, resulting in an eight-hour closure to facilitate investigations.
