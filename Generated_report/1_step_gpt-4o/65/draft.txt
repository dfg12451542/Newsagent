Chelsea F.C. clinched the Club World Cup title with a decisive 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain, showcasing their prowess in the final match held in New Jersey on Sunday. As the team celebrated their triumph, former U.S. President Donald Trump made an unexpected prolonged appearance on the stage during the trophy presentation. Typically, notable guests present the trophy and exit promptly, but Trump stayed alongside Chelsea captain Reece James and goalkeeper Robert Sanchez for the duration of the celebration.nnReece James expressed surprise at Trump's prolonged presence, mentioning, 'They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage... but he wanted to stay.' Fellow player Cole Palmer added, 'I knew he was going to be here but I didn't know he was going to be on the stand when we lifted the trophy.' The former president, who appeared to be enjoying himself, commented in an interview, 'It's great to watch - and this is a little bit of an upset that we're watching today, isn't it?'nnTrump, who has shown increased interest in sports during his second presidential term, even attending the Super Bowl and announcing Washington D.C. as the 2027 NFL Draft host, used the opportunity to praise FIFA president Gianni Infantino, describing him as a friend who has done a great job with the league and soccer. When quizzed about possibly renaming the sport from 'soccer' to 'football' in the United States, Trump playfully suggested that it might not be such an easy change to make, but hinted at the possibility with a smile.nnThis victory marks Chelsea as the undisputed Club World Cup champions, continuing their legacy of triumph on the global soccer stage. As Chelsea fans celebrated, the presence of a notable figure like Trump added an unexpected layer of international attention to the event, blending sports with political spectacle.
