A man has died following a single-vehicle crash in Nutts Corner, County Antrim on Saturday evening. The incident occurred on Ballyhill Road around 17:50 BST, involving a white Volkswagen Golf. Emergency services responded to the scene, however, the driver was pronounced dead at the scene.nnAccording to police reports, “Police said the man was the driver of a white Volkswagen Golf which was involved in a single-vehicle incident in Ballyhill Road about 17:50 BST on Saturday.” Investigations are currently underway to determine the cause of the crash.  The area was heavily affected, as depicted in images showing tributes and emergency markings near a shed.nnThe ambulance service also attended the scene, providing medical assistance. Sadly, their efforts to save the driver were unsuccessful.  Authorities have stated that a male passenger was also involved and sustained injuries.nnPolice confirmed, “Police added that a male passenger was taken to hospital with injuries, which are not believed to be life-threatening at this time.”  Ballyhill Road was temporarily closed following the incident to allow emergency services to work, but has since reopened to traffic.  Further updates will be provided as the investigation progresses.
