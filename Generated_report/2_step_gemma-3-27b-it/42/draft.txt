Casablanca, Morocco – Nigeria and Algeria have both advanced to the quarter-finals of the 2024 Women's Africa Cup of Nations (Wafcon), securing their spots after a goalless draw in their final Group B match. The Super Falcons topped the group, setting up a quarter-final clash against Zambia, while Algeria will face the runner-up from Group C. The match, played with intensity, saw both teams vying for control, but neither side could break the deadlock despite Nigeria’s late push for a winner, with Echegini’s attempt narrowly missing the target in the 89th minute.nnAlgeria’s progression marks a continued presence for the North African side in the latter stages of the tournament. The team entered the competition facing significant pressure as hosts and previous finalists, as noted by Sakina Ouzraoui who stated they knew “all the pressure is on us because the [Wafcon] is being played here and we were finalists at the last one.” Their ability to navigate a challenging group demonstrates their resilience and ambition.nnElsewhere in the tournament, Botswana secured a crucial victory over Tunisia, coming from behind to win 2-1. This result significantly boosts their chances of progressing as one of the best third-placed teams. Lesego Radiakanyo’s equaliser and a dramatic stoppage-time winner from Gaonyadiwe Ontlametse secured the win, preventing elimination for both teams had the match ended in a 1-1 draw.nnWith these results, defending champions South Africa and Mali have already confirmed their places in the quarter-finals, anticipating their match in Oujda. Meanwhile, Group C will culminate on Monday with Ghana facing Tanzania in Berkane, with both teams requiring a win to advance. Group C includes title holders South Africa, while Ghana and Mali are returning to the finals for the first time since 2018, and Tanzania is participating for only the second time since their debut in 2010, showcasing the evolving competitive landscape of the Women’s Africa Cup of Nations.
