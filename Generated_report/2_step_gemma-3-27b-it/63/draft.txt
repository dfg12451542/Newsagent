Rhossili, Gower – A major fire has caused significant damage to the landmark Worm’s Head Hotel in Rhossili, Gower, prompting a large response from Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service. Crews were called to the scene at 00:51 BST on Monday morning, battling a blaze that originated within the property’s accommodation block and rapidly spread to encompass the entire roof space. The fire was finally extinguished around 05:30, with crews remaining on site for several hours to monitor the area before fully departing at 09:32.nnThe extent of the damage is considerable, leaving a 25-meter span of the roof collapsed and exposing blackened beams and a chimney stack. While the cause of the fire remains under investigation, officials confirmed that no casualties were reported and all occupants were safely accounted for. South Wales Police were also present to assist with the incident and manage the surrounding area.nnThe Worm’s Head Hotel has been a popular destination for both tourists and locals for decades, known for its stunning location overlooking the iconic Worm’s Head peninsula. The loss is deeply felt within the community, as evidenced by the outpouring of support and shock from those who regularly frequented the establishment. “We used to come every summer. It’s absolutely shocking to see it like this,” commented Sian Nichol, a long-time visitor.nnThe owners of the hotel expressed their devastation in a social media post, stating, “It is particularly heartbreaking and soul destroying for me and family to see 25 years of hard work destroyed in less than 30 mins.” They acknowledged the uncertain future but pledged to rebuild, adding, “Not sure how we come back from this at all let alone in the near term future but we will strive to come back stronger.” Other visitors echoed this sentiment, highlighting the hotel’s role as a cherished fixture for special occasions. While the future of the Worm’s Head Hotel remains uncertain, the strong community spirit and determination to rebuild offer a glimmer of hope for its eventual return.
