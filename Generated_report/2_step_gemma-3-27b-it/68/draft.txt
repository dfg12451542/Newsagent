Sherborne resident Zoe Tucker has achieved remarkable success, winning gold at the duathlon world championships in Spain last month. The amateur athlete triumphed in the sprint category, completing a 5km run, 19.7km bike ride, and a final 2.5km run to claim the world title. Tucker was also the fastest British woman overall across all age groups at the competition, a significant feat for someone relatively new to the sport.nnDescribing the experience as “an absolute honour,” Tucker recounted the magnitude of the event. “The scale of the event, the opening ceremony, a parade of nations, walking with GB kit and flag - it was an incredible experience,” she said. The winning moment itself was particularly emotional, with Tucker stating, “Just coming round the track for the last lap, knowing I was probably going to get over that line first was incredible.”nnTucker emphasized the unexpected nature of her victory, confessing she “absolutely never in my wildest dreams” anticipated competing, let alone winning, at the world championships. Despite the personal triumph, she also expressed a desire to inspire others, noting, “I’m hopefully inspiring them a bit, but it’s been hard.” Looking ahead, Tucker plans to develop a more structured training regime, including investing in an indoor training bike, to maintain her competitive edge.nnThe championship event in Spain was the fourth of the World Triathlon Championship Series, marking the halfway point of the overall competition. The Series will culminate in finals scheduled for Zurich on August 27th and 28th, just weeks before the World Athletics Championships in Tokyo. Tucker encourages others to embrace opportunities, saying, “There are so many activities and opportunities and events - I just think if an opportunity comes your way, just grab it and enjoy it along the way.”
