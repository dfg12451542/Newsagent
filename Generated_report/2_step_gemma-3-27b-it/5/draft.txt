Trust in policing within the Millfield area of Peterborough has seen a significant boost following the implementation of a project targeting organized crime, according to local officials. The initiative, known as the Alliance project and centered on Lincoln Road, aims to disrupt criminal networks and foster safer public spaces through collaboration between Peterborough City Council and Cambridgeshire Police. Residents are reporting increased confidence in local law enforcement as a result.nnPeterborough City Council reports a dramatic “700% increase in community intelligence reporting” since the project’s inception. This surge in information from residents has been instrumental in disrupting criminal activity. To date, the project has yielded substantial seizures, including 700,000 illegal cigarettes, 20,000 vapes, and £115,000 in cash. New drug needle bins were also installed to address public health concerns related to the criminal activity.nnWhile the Alliance project has demonstrated initial success, its wider implementation faces challenges. A council report presented to a scrutiny committee on Thursday noted difficulties in fully engaging local groups. Despite these hurdles, authorities emphasize the long-term vision of the project. Insp Sam Tucker, from Cambridgeshire Police, acknowledged the value of the Alliance approach, stating, “We can definitely use some of the concepts in there but to use this initiative in its entirety is unlikely.”nnThe Alliance project underscores the importance of collaborative efforts between local government and police forces in addressing crime and building community trust. Officials are continuing to seek funding to sustain the project in Millfield, recognizing that its success hinges on “longevity and sustainability.” The council and police are carefully considering how to best adapt the proven strategies while navigating the complexities of widespread implementation.
