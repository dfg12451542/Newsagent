Arbroath, Scotland – St Mirren manager Stephen Robinson expressed his shock following his team’s goalless draw and subsequent penalty shootout defeat to League 1 champions Arbroath in the Premier Sports Cup opener on Saturday. The result has left Robinson apologizing to the St Mirren supporters who made the journey to Gayfield Park. “I’m in a little bit of shock,” Robinson told club media after the match, visibly frustrated with the performance.nnThe match itself ended without a goal after 90 minutes, forcing a penalty shootout which Arbroath ultimately won, securing them the bonus point.  Despite a strong pre-season campaign, Robinson was critical of his players’ display, stating he “didn’t recognise” the standard of play. St Mirren, who finished sixth in the Scottish Premiership last season, were expected to overcome a team from the league below.nnRobinson lamented the lack of execution from his squad, highlighting fundamental errors. He further stated that the side will be travelling to play League 2 team Forfar Athletic on Tuesday, and is demanding an immediate response.  “The performance of maybe 13 of them is something I’ve not seen all pre-season. We’ve had a fantastic pre-season,” he added, emphasizing the disconnect between their preparations and the on-field reality.nnRobinson didn’t shy away from taking responsibility, acknowledging that the team collectively failed to perform. “We decided as a collective not to do that and got exactly what we deserved,” he explained. He also offered a sincere apology to the travelling fans, acknowledging their dedication and disappointment.  He admitted, “We’re not going to feel sorry for ourselves... I bet they wish they had done” spent their Saturday elsewhere.nnDespite his disappointment, Robinson maintained confidence in his squad’s long-term capabilities and vowed they would learn from this setback.  “It’s a group of boys that have never let me down over a longer period of time,” he stated.  “We have to react to it, we will be better. We have to do simple things which we didn’t do.”  St Mirren will aim to bounce back quickly as they continue their Premier Sports Cup campaign.
