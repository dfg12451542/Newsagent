Six abandoned kittens have found new homes after being rescued from a rubbish dump in Eyemouth, Scotland, and nursed back to health by Borders Pet Rescue. The four-week-old kittens were discovered at the Eyemouth Recycling Centre on May 27th, suffering from severe malnutrition, flea and worm infestations, and painful eye infections. Centre manager Kim McNeill described the scene as “heartbreaking,” emphasizing the kittens’ frightened and weakened state.nnThe rescue organization provided extensive medical care, treating the kittens’ infections and parasites. Beyond medical attention, the animals required extensive rehabilitation. “These kittens needed more than medicine - they needed time, patience, and love,” McNeill explained. A dedicated foster carer provided the nurturing environment necessary for the kittens to regain their confidence and trust in humans, allowing them to slowly learn how to play and experience the comforts of a home.nnIn a heartwarming gesture, the kittens were affectionately named after the characters from the iconic 90s television show, *Friends*. “Inspired by their strong bond and the laughter they brought to us, we named them after characters from Friends - three girls and three boys,” McNeill stated. This playful naming convention resonated with the public, capturing hearts on social media and enabling Borders Pet Rescue to raise thousands of pounds for veterinary care and amplify their search for suitable owners.nnThanks to the dedication of Borders Pet Rescue and the generosity of the public, all six kittens have now been successfully adopted into loving homes. “We’re delighted to share that all six kittens have now been adopted into wonderful homes, each beginning a new chapter filled with love and joy,” McNeill confirmed, highlighting the positive outcome of this rescue story.
