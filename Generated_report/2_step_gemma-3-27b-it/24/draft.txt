A large blaze erupted in fields near Guildford, Surrey, on Monday afternoon, prompting a significant response from emergency services. Some ten fire crews are currently working to extinguish the fire, which began in the Sandy Lane area, according to Surrey Fire & Rescue Service, who were alerted to the incident at 16:20 BST. nnA spokesperson for the service confirmed the fire broke out in fields adjacent to Piccards Farm House.  Firefighters are battling the flames to prevent further spread, though the immediate threat to structures appears to be contained. nnFortunately, no properties have been damaged by the blaze, Surrey Fire & Rescue Service confirmed. However, local residents are being urged to take precautionary measures. nnResidents in the area have been advised to keep windows and doors closed due to the considerable smoke emanating from the fire. The cause of the blaze is currently unknown and under investigation by fire officials.
