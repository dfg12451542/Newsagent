Actor Sean Bean has publicly criticised plans to build homes on green belt land in Handsworth, Sheffield, the area where he grew up, stating the proposals would “practically obliterate the character and nature of our area.” Bean, known for roles in productions like *Lord of the Rings* and *Game of Thrones*, submitted an objection to the Sheffield City Council’s local plan shortly before the consultation period closed, expressing strong concerns about the impact on the community.nnIn a strongly worded email, seen by the BBC, Bean lamented what he perceives as inequitable treatment of working-class areas. “It seems the council sees us as second-class citizens who will just have to put up with it,” he wrote, suggesting that more affluent parts of Sheffield have been spared similar development proposals. This criticism highlights a growing tension regarding sustainable development and the preservation of green spaces within urban environments.nnLocal campaigners have welcomed Bean’s vocal support. Sadie Charlton, a member of the 'Save S13 Green Belt - The Sapphire McCarthy Campaign,' explained, “It’s really boosted our morale and everybody’s so thankful for him to take the time to put his voice forward because what he says in his objection is how we all feel.” The campaign itself is named in tribute to Sapphire McCarthy, a key activist who initially connected with Bean and whose recent passing has further galvanised the community.nnCharlton revealed that Bean’s involvement stemmed from a conversation with McCarthy, and that he’s remained engaged with the campaign. She stated, “He was really interested in keeping in touch about how the campaign is going because obviously he cares about Handsworth.” The community has demonstrably rallied in response to the proposed development, with Charlton adding, “The community have really rallied around and come together, so this is just the beginning for us.” Despite the consultation period closing on July 11th, the group intends to continue their fight to protect the green belt.
