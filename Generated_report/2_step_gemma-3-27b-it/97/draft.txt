The Wurzels were forced to cut short their performance at the Party at the Palace event in Wells, Somerset, on Saturday as band members succumbed to the intense summer heat. The iconic cider-drinking band had to abruptly end their set when singer Pete Budd, approaching his 85th birthday on July 18th, became unwell during the performance, with temperatures peaking at 31C.nnAccording to accordionist Tommy Banner, both he and Budd suffered from sun and heat exhaustion. “Both Pete and I appear to have fully recovered from sun and heat exhaustion. Up on stage there was no air moving and we were facing straight into the direct, unobstructed rays of a very strong sun,” Banner explained. He further recounted the moment he realized something was wrong. “I was about to announce the last song and Pete wasn’t besides me where he normally is. He was in a bad way.” Banner himself experienced dizziness after leaving the stage and was supported by medical staff alongside Budd.nnMedical personnel quickly responded to the situation assisting both musicians as they exited the stage. A nurse assessed both band members, providing Banner with exercises and advising him against driving. Fortunately, both appear to be recovering well.nnThe incident highlights the dangers of performing outdoors during periods of extreme heat. Experts advise that symptoms of heat exhaustion, such as dizziness and weakness, should be addressed immediately by removing unnecessary clothing, providing fluids, and cooling the skin with water. This wasn't the first time the band have suffered from heat exhaustion, and is a cautionary tale for all outdoor performers and attendees alike during the current heatwave.
