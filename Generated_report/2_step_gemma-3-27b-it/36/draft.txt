A 25-year-old man has been charged with murder following a street incident in Aberdeen on Sunday, July 13th. Brandon Booth appeared before Aberdeen Sheriff Court on Monday, making no plea and being remanded in custody, according to court officials. The charge relates to the death of 28-year-old Dylan Geddes, who was found injured on Union Street in the early hours of Sunday morning and later pronounced dead at the scene.nnThe incident has sent shockwaves through the Aberdeen community. Police investigations are ongoing, and a significant police presence remained in the area surrounding Union Street throughout Monday as forensic teams gathered evidence. Authorities have not yet released details regarding the nature of the incident, but have assured the public that they are treating the case with the utmost seriousness.nnFamily of the victim released a statement expressing their profound grief. “The family is absolutely devastated by what has happened,” the statement read. They described Dylan Geddes as “a much-loved son, grandson, brother and nephew,” adding, “He will be sorely missed every day by all who knew him.”nnWhile Aberdeen generally experiences low levels of violent crime, there have been isolated incidents of serious assault in the city center in recent years, prompting increased calls for enhanced security measures, particularly during late-night hours. This case is likely to reignite those discussions as local residents grapple with the loss and demand answers regarding maintaining safety within the city.nnThe investigation continues and police are appealing for anyone with information, particularly anyone who was in the Union Street area on Sunday morning, to come forward. Further updates are expected as the inquiry progresses and Booth prepares for his next court appearance.
