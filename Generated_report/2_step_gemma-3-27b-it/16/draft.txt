A biker is currently hospitalized with serious injuries following a single-vehicle crash on a rural road in the Scottish Borders. The incident occurred on the B6399 near Hawick on Sunday at approximately 12:00, prompting a significant emergency response and an extended road closure.nnEmergency services were alerted to the accident south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm. The motorcycle involved was identified as a white and blue Suzuki GSXR. Authorities confirmed that the road was shut for approximately eight hours to allow for investigation and recovery of the vehicle.nnThe injured man, a 57-year-old, was transported to a local hospital for treatment of serious, though currently unspecified, injuries. The extent of those injuries remains unknown at this time. nnPolice are actively investigating the circumstances of the crash and have issued a public appeal for any witnesses who may have been in the area at the time of the incident. Anyone with information is urged to come forward to assist with the ongoing investigation. Authorities have not yet released any further details regarding potential causes of the accident.
