Portrush, Northern Ireland – The world’s top golfers have converged on the picturesque coastal town of Portrush for the 153rd Open Championship, commencing today, July 14th, 2025. Royal Portrush Golf Club is set to host the prestigious tournament, drawing enthusiasts and competitors alike to the Antrim Coast. The event marks a highly anticipated return to Northern Ireland, a location steeped in golfing history and known for its challenging links course.nnThe Open Championship, often referred to simply as 'The Open,' is the oldest and most respected of the four major championships in professional golf.
