A car fire rapidly escalated into a significant moorland blaze along the A30 in Cornwall this Saturday, prompting a response from multiple fire crews. The incident occurred on the eastbound lane near Temple Tor on Bodmin Moor, quickly spreading from the vehicle to the surrounding vegetation. Firefighters worked swiftly to contain the flames and prevent further expansion of the fire.nnThe initial blaze, originating from a vehicle, presented immediate challenges due to the dry conditions of the moorland. According to reports, the fire consumed a substantial area of gorse and heather before crews brought it under control. The swift response was crucial in mitigating potential damage to the wider ecosystem and preventing the fire from encroaching on nearby properties.nnFire crews from Launceston and Bodmin were deployed to the scene, demonstrating a coordinated effort to combat the escalating situation. While the exact cause of the initial car fire remains under investigation, authorities are focusing on determining whether mechanical failure or another factor contributed to the incident.  No injuries have been reported at this time.nnThe Cornwall Fire & Rescue Service has confirmed the location and nature of the incident, highlighting the vulnerability of moorland areas to wildfires, particularly during periods of dry weather. This incident serves as a stark reminder of the importance of vehicle maintenance and responsible behavior in fragile upland environments.
