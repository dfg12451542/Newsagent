Crystal Palace has unveiled its new home kit for the 2025-26 season, coinciding with the excitement surrounding the club’s participation in European competition. The launch, announced this weekend, marks a fresh start for the Eagles as they prepare for a challenging and promising year ahead. The new kit design signals a continuation of the club’s iconic colours, aiming to resonate with both long-standing supporters and a growing fanbase.nnThe release of the kit comes as Crystal Palace gears up for its pre-season schedule. The team debuted the new strip in their 1-0 victory against Millwall on Saturday, offering fans the first glimpse of the kit in action. This fixture served as the initial test and showcase for the new design, allowing players to break in the kit before the competitive season begins.nnThe kit’s unveiling featured a diverse group of Palace stars. Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, Maxence Lacroix, Eddie Nketiah, Adam Wharton, Justin Devenny and Romain Esse all modeled the new attire, alongside representatives from Palace Women, including Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur. This inclusive approach highlights the club's commitment to both its men's and women's teams.nnAdding to the presentation of the new line-up, new signing Walter Benitez also showcased the club’s light green goalkeeper home jersey for the first time. This demonstrates the club's attention to detail and commitment to providing players with state-of-the-art equipment as they look to build momentum into the new season. The kit's release generates anticipation among fans as they look forward to seeing the team compete both domestically and in Europe.
