More than £8,000 has been raised in just 48 hours to support the family of a teenager who tragically died after going missing in a lake at Sutton Park in Sutton Coldfield. An online fundraising page, established with the permission of the boy's parents, quickly surpassed its initial target, demonstrating a remarkable outpouring of community support.nnThe GoFundMe campaign began with a goal of £1,600 but rapidly exceeded expectations, currently standing at over £8,000 and with a revised target of £11,000. Organisers stated the funds will assist the family through what they describe as an incredibly “difficult time” following the devastating loss.nnEmergency services were called to the scene on Thursday evening, just after 6:00 PM BST, but tragically, 18-year-old Daniel Drewitt was pronounced dead after his body was recovered from the water. The circumstances surrounding the incident are still under investigation, but the community has rallied around the grieving family.nn“The family are so very grateful, thank you so much,” said Helena Swallow, the fundraiser’s organiser, in a recent update. She also shared that Daniel's mother was “overwhelmed” by the generosity and support shown by donors. The public's quick and substantial response highlights the deep sense of loss felt within the local community and their desire to help during this heartbreaking period.nnThe fundraising page remains active for those wishing to contribute and offer support to the Drewitt family during their time of grief. It serves as a testament to the compassion and unity displayed by the Sutton Coldfield community in the wake of this tragic event.
