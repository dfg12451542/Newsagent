Royal Portrush, Northern Ireland – Preparations are underway for The Open Championship as players began familiarizing themselves with the challenging links course on Monday. Defending champion Xander Schauffele was among the first to take to the course, getting a feel for the conditions ahead of the tournament which begins later this week.nnAlso drawing attention on Monday was Shane Lowry, a player intimately familiar with Royal Portrush's demanding layout. Lowry knows what it takes to win on this historic course, having clinched the title the last time The Open was held here, in 2019. His return sparked considerable interest among spectators and fellow golfers alike.nnLowry was seen practicing alongside Denmark’s Rasmus Hojgaard, utilizing the opportunity to assess the course's nuances and refine his strategy. The 2019 Open champion’s presence is a poignant reminder of the course’s rich history and the pressure that comes with competing on such a prestigious stage.nnThe 2019 Open at Portrush marked a significant moment, being the first time the championship had been held in Northern Ireland since 1951. Lowry’s victory resonated deeply with local fans and underscored the enduring appeal of links golf. As Schauffele and Lowry, along with the rest of the field, continue their preparations, all eyes will be on Portrush as the competition heats up.
