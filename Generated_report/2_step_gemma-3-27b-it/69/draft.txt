A significant fire erupted at a commercial premises in Morecambe, Lancashire, this morning, sending large plumes of smoke visible for miles. Firefighters are currently battling the blaze at the White Lund Industrial Estate, which began at approximately 09:30 BST. Initial reports indicate no injuries have been sustained. nnThe incident has prompted a large emergency response, with eight fire engines dispatched to the scene. Witnesses report witnessing substantial smoke billowing from the site, causing concern for residents in nearby areas. Authorities have closed sections of the Bay Gateway to manage traffic and ensure the safety of responders and the public. nnLancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) confirmed they are “working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely.” No injuries have been reported at this time. An LFRS spokesperson stated that the focus remained on containing the fire and preventing its spread. nnLFRS group manager Mark Warwick described the fire as “giving off large volumes of smoke” and issued a public safety advisory. He urged residents “if you see or smell any smoke in the area, please keep windows and doors closed.” The extent of the damage to the industrial site is currently unknown. nnThe affected area, the White Lund Industrial Estate, is a hub for various local businesses. Nearby roads remain closed as crews continue to tackle the blaze, and updates will be provided by LFRS as the situation unfolds.
