Lord’s witnessed a dramatic fourth day of the Test match between England and India, with Brydon Carse’s crucial strikes revitalizing England’s hopes after a challenging batting performance. Carse claimed the key wickets of Karun Nair and Shubman Gill, swinging the momentum back in England’s favor after India had begun to assert dominance.  The day concluded with India reeling at 58-4, trailing by 135 runs, setting the stage for a tense final day. nnEarlier, England’s batting lineup struggled against a relentless Indian attack, led by Jasprit Bumrah and Washington Sundar. Harry Brook’s dismissal, after a promising start, and patient innings from Joe Root and Ben Stokes were not enough to build a substantial lead. Sundar, in particular, proved impactful, dismissing both past and present England captains, contributing to a record 12 wickets falling to spin for England in a home Test.  “We would have loved 250 and beyond but we would have taken 190 and our chance to bowl on that pitch,” noted England batting coach Marcus Trescothick, acknowledging the difficult conditions.nnCarse's intervention proved pivotal in shifting the balance of the match. Despite a challenging morning for England's batters, and questions surrounding some of their shot selection, Carse's late burst injected renewed energy into the home side's campaign. “It was absolutely gripping. Even World Cup finals don't quite generate that kind of madness,” commented BBC chief cricket commentator Jonathan Agnew, highlighting the intensity of the day’s play. The late wicket of nightwatchman Akash Deep by Ben Stokes further underscored England’s resurgence.nnIndia, however, remain confident of securing victory. Washington Sundar expressed unwavering optimism, stating, “We'd have loved to have about nine wickets [in hand]. It is what it is and we'll definitely win the game.” He further emphasized the importance of claiming all ten wickets, adding, “Every wicket was very very crucial.”  The stage is now set for a captivating final session on Monday morning at Lord’s, where the destiny of this thrilling Test match will be decided.
