Hibernian FC has announced the signing of Togo international striker Thibault Klidje from Swiss side Luzern, shattering the club’s transfer record in the process. The 24-year-old has agreed to a three-year deal with the Edinburgh club, with an option for a further year, marking a significant investment in the Hibs’ attacking options. The deal signifies the club's ambition to compete at the higher end of the Scottish Premiership.nnManager David Gray expressed his delight at securing Klidje’s signature, highlighting the player’s potential. “Klidje will add extra pace and trickery to Hibs’ front line,” Gray stated, emphasizing the qualities the striker will bring to the team. He further elaborated, stating, “He adds extra pace and trickery to our front line and performed well for Luzern last season, and at 24 has a lot of room for development.”nnKlidje’s career trajectory has seen him move from his native Togo to France as a teenager, joining Bordeaux in 2020. While opportunities in the Bordeaux first team were limited to five appearances, the move represented an important step in his development. He subsequently found more regular game time with Luzern, impressing with his performances which caught the attention of Hibernian.nnGray also touched upon the club’s commitment to nurturing Klidje’s talent. “We want to help him progress, and look forward to working with him moving forward,” he confirmed, suggesting that Hibernian sees Klidje as a long-term asset. The signing signals a focused effort to inject youthful dynamism and attacking flair into the Hibs squad as they prepare for the upcoming season.
