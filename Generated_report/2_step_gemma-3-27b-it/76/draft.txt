The M11 motorway near Stansted Airport has fully reopened following a vehicle fire that caused significant delays for drivers on Sunday, July 13th. The incident occurred on the southbound carriageway between junctions nine and eight, impacting access to both Great Chesterford and London Stansted Airport. nnFire crews responded to the scene and successfully extinguished the blaze, however, the aftermath led to substantial congestion. A National Highways spokesperson confirmed that delays of up to 60 minutes were experienced by motorists on approach. Images circulating online depicted a standstill with hundreds of cars halted, and numerous drivers opting to exit their vehicles and walk along the motorway.nn“There is approximately 1.5 miles congestion following this incident so please allow time for these delays to clear,” the National Highways spokesperson stated. Authorities worked to clear the affected area and assess any damage to the road surface. nnThe incident follows a period of congestion on the M11 southbound earlier on Sunday morning, highlighting the vulnerability of this key transport route, particularly during peak travel times. While the cause of the vehicle fire remains under investigation, the swift response of emergency services prevented further disruption. nnMotorists are advised to check traffic conditions before travelling, particularly around Stansted Airport, to avoid potential delays. Updates and further information can be found on the National Highways website and through traffic news services.
