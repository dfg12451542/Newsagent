A family from Hereford completed an 800-mile campervan journey to Switzerland to witness England’s emphatic 6-1 victory over Wales in the UEFA Women’s Euros on Sunday, booking the Lionesses’ place in the quarter-finals. The Morris family – Kevin, Kelly, and their children Amelia, 16, and Ethan, 13 – travelled to the St Gallen stadium to cheer on the team, declaring the atmosphere “great” and the performance “outstanding.” The win signifies a strong start for England in the tournament, which commenced on Wednesday, with the team’s initial match against France scheduled for Saturday.nnDriven by a passion for the Lionesses, the family’s adventure began in Hereford, leading them across Europe to witness the crucial group stage match. They are currently stationed in Arbon, near Lake Constance, enjoying the scenery as their journey continues in support of the national team. Their adventure hasn't been without its complications however, as post-match travel proved difficult. nn“As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,” stated Kevin Morris, reflecting on the team’s dominant display. However, the journey home presented unexpected challenges. According to Mr. Morris, “We got on to the one train station, we couldn’t get on to the train. The next one came in… and then just stopped in the middle of nowhere.” The family were forced to disembark and await a replacement bus, which itself took a circuitous route before finally returning them to Arbon.nnDespite the travel setbacks, the Morris family remain upbeat and determined to continue supporting the Lionesses. “We were there for about 20 or 30 minutes and we were told to get off and a bus would turn up… A bus did turn up but then it went everywhere, before we got back to where we’re staying in Arbon,” Mr. Morris explained. Looking ahead, the family is optimistic about England’s chances. “I think we can do it again”, said Morris. They are now preparing to travel back to Zurich to watch England face Sweden in the quarter-final on Thursday, a repeat of the Euro 2022 semi-final, hopeful they will be “lucky enough to get through to see the semi-final”.
