Chelsea Football Club secured a 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain in the Club World Cup final held in New Jersey on Sunday, but the post-match celebrations were overshadowed by an unexpected guest: former U.S. President Donald Trump. Trump presented the trophy to Chelsea captain Reece James, a customary gesture, but then defied expectations by remaining on stage during the trophy lift, standing alongside the players and FIFA President Gianni Infantino.nnFollowing the win, Trump was invited by Infantino to step aside, allowing the Chelsea players their moment with the trophy. However, as described by sources, Trump lingered, applauding as James raised the Club World Cup. “They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage, and I thought that he was going to exit the stage, but he wanted to stay,” recounted captain Reece James. The unusual scene sparked confusion among the players, with goalkeeper Robert Sanchez also standing near Trump during the prolonged celebration.nnPlayers expressed surprise at Trump’s extended presence. Cole Palmer admitted, “I knew he was going to be here but I didn't know he was going to be on the stand when we lifted the trophy… I was a bit confused, yes.” Trump himself appeared to be enjoying the event, telling Dazn at halftime that he was “having a really great time.”nnIn a later interview, Trump also weighed in on the ongoing debate surrounding the sport’s name, stating, “Gianni is a friend of mine, he’s done such a great job with the league and with soccer – or as they would call it ‘football’, but I guess we call it soccer. I’m not sure that change could be made very easily.” When playfully suggested he could issue an executive order changing the name to “football”, he responded with a smile, “I think we could do that. I think I could do that.”nnThe incident raises questions about the intersection of sports and politics, and the protocol surrounding trophy presentations at international events. While Trump’s attendance was known beforehand, his decision to remain center stage during the celebratory moment undoubtedly provided an unexpected and talked-about moment for Chelsea’s victory.
