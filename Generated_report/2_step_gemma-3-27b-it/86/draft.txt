A three-year-old girl from Powys, Wales, is set to become one of the youngest ever competitors at the prestigious Crufts dog show next year. Caitlyn, along with her eight-year-old Miniature Schnauzer, Zeeva, qualified for the event after a successful showing at the Three Counties Championship Show last month. The pair, from Groesffordd near Brecon, have captured attention not just for their achievement, but for Caitlyn’s exceptionally young age.nnCaitlyn’s mother, Lydia Mellor, expressed immense pride in her daughter’s accomplishment, describing it as “amazing.”  Caitlyn herself shared her enthusiasm, stating simply, “I like running around with her.” The qualification is particularly notable as Caitlyn is comfortable showing Zeeva independently in the ring, a testament to her confidence and connection with the dog.nnMellor emphasized Caitlyn’s exceptional sportsmanship, highlighting how she readily interacts with judges and fellow competitors. “She will shake hands with the judges, she shakes hands with the winners, she always congratulates everyone,” Mellor explained. “I am massively proud of the sportsmanship she is showing at this age.”  This positive attitude, according to Mellor, is pivotal for the future of the sport, stating, “It's going to keep our sport alive.” nnHistorically, Crufts has seen a wide age range of competitors, with the youngest typically around 14 years old.  While records are not meticulously kept for the youngest participants, Caitlyn is poised to significantly lower that benchmark. Helen Kerfoot, a representative from the show, remarked, “We are delighted to see a young competitor doing so well, having qualified for Crufts already – the result of a lot of hard work and dedication.”  Kerfoot also emphasized the accessibility of Crufts, adding, “Taking part in Crufts is a real achievement but it's also something that's accessible to all.”  Mellor also addressed misconceptions about the event, observing, “We knew it's often seen as a dog beauty pageant - but that's not the way the show world works. They are our pets, they are spoilt rotten, they want for nothing - we have a great day out.”
