Grace Kim of Australia secured her first major championship title at the 2025 Evian Championship in France, overcoming a fierce competition that included a dramatic playoff against Atthaya Thitikul.  Kim clinched the victory with a 12-foot eagle putt on the second replay of the 18th hole, showcasing remarkable composure under pressure. The tournament was a nail-biter, highlighting the growing talent in the women's professional golf scene.nnEngland’s Lottie Woad came incredibly close to forcing her way into the playoff, carding a stunning seven-under 64 to finish at 13 under par. A missed 10-foot birdie putt on the 18th ultimately kept her out, but not before she’d briefly set the clubhouse lead. Woad, recently crowned champion at the Irish Open, demonstrated her escalating skill and challenge to established players.  A moment of candid frustration was captured when, observing Thitikul’s position, Woad remarked to her caddie, “Is there even any point, she’s not making six.”nnThitikul herself had the opportunity to win outright, but a missed eight-foot birdie putt on the 18th sent the tournament into extra holes.  Kim further intensified the drama by eagling the 18th on both her initial and second playoff attempts.  The pair traded birdies on their first return to the 18th, with Kim demonstrating incredible finesse with a 30-yard chip. Ultimately, it was Kim’s clutch performance that secured the coveted title.nnWoad’s strong performance at Evian also carried significant implications for her future career. Finishing within the top 25 in a major earned Woad two points toward the LPGA’s Elite Amateur Pathway.  Reaching 20 points grants access to the LPGA Tour, a goal that now feels within reach for the rising star.  “It has always been in my dreams to play on the LPGA Tour and I'm grateful for the opportunities of the programme,” Woad stated, emphasizing her aspirations. She also expressed her intent to continue pursuing contention, stating, “I was trying to be in contention and winning this, so it will be the same there.”nnWoad confirmed she’ll be discussing her options with her family and coaches regarding her LPGA Tour prospects.  The Evian Championship underscored not only Kim’s triumph but also the emergence of Woad as a formidable competitor and a prospective future presence on the professional tour, adding increased depth to the LPGA’s future roster.
