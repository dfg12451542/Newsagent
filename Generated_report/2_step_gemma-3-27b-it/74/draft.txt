Newcastle United are reportedly pursuing a club-record deal for Eintracht Frankfurt striker Hugo Ekitike, signaling ambitious intentions for the upcoming season. The Magpies have initiated contact and are actively exploring the possibility of bringing the 23-year-old Frenchman to St. James' Park. nnWhile specific details are still emerging, sources indicate the potential transfer fee could exceed £70 million, making Ekitike the most expensive signing in Newcastle’s history. This move demonstrates the club's financial backing and determination to strengthen their attacking options after a promising previous campaign. Negotiations are expected to be complex, given Ekitike’s current status with Frankfurt and the potential for competing offers.nnInterest from other top European clubs, including Liverpool, has also surfaced, though any move for Ekitike by the Merseyside club is contingent on the approval of Darwin Nunez’s potential transfer. This adds another layer of complexity to the situation, and it remains to be seen whether Newcastle can secure their target amidst competition. nnNotably, amidst speculation linking Alexander Isak with a move away from Newcastle, BBC Sport understands the club have no intention of selling their top scorer from last season. This commitment to retaining key players suggests a strategic approach to squad building, focusing on both additions and maintaining a strong core.  The pursuit of Ekitike appears to be alongside, not as a replacement for, Isak. nnThe developments signify Newcastle’s intent to build upon recent successes and establish themselves as a consistent force in both domestic and European competitions. The signing of Ekitike, should it materialize, would undoubtedly represent a significant statement of intent for the club.
