Wales’ new Sustainable Farming Scheme (SFS) is “absolutely the right scheme,” asserts Agriculture Minister Huw Irranca-Davies, despite previous iterations facing strong opposition from farming unions and resulting in widespread protests. The revised policy, set to be unveiled next week, aims to balance food production with environmental sustainability, a crucial consideration given that 80% of Wales’ land is managed by farmers and their vital role in addressing climate change and ecosystem decline. nnIrranca-Davies expressed optimism about the scheme’s reception, acknowledging that while “there will be the odd person” who disagrees, the final version is “the result of more than 12 months of intense work” involving various stakeholders.  He emphasized the scheme’s uniqueness, stating it will be “unlike anything else that’s been delivered in the UK,” adopting a “whole farm approach and a whole nation approach.” Though specific details remain under wraps, the minister highlighted a focus on delivering for the people of Wales by ensuring farming produces “good food to high animal welfare standards and also does the right thing for the environment.”nnConcerns regarding the scheme’s economic impact remain. Irranca-Davies declined to publish a full impact assessment, stating that work is still ongoing, but expressed confidence that the new iteration will be beneficial for both “viable farm businesses and environmental benefits as well.” He assured that mitigations will be in place to support farmers during the transition to a greener economy, promising “real subtleties” within the scheme. nnHowever, skepticism persists among opposition parties. Plaid Cymru’s Delyth Jewell MS voiced frustrations that farmers have felt unheard throughout the process, emphasizing the need to ensure the sustainability of those who “sustain our lives.” Conservative Senedd leader Darren Millar MS called for a parliamentary vote on the policy to ensure “democratic legitimacy” and stressed the importance of food security, advocating it be “at the heart” of the new scheme.
