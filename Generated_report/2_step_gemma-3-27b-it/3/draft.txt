Jersey’s women footballers commenced their 2025 Island Games journey with a 3-1 defeat against the reigning champions, Bermuda, on Sunday. The match, marking the start of their campaign, saw early promise from Jersey quickly overshadowed by Bermuda’s determined comeback. nnJersey initially took the lead in the sixth minute thanks to a well-placed left-footed shot from Anita Tavares, finding the corner of the net. This early goal ignited hopes for an upset against the formidable Bermudan side. nnThe first half saw a closely contested battle, with both teams pressing for further goals. Bermuda came close to levelling the score, striking the Jersey crossbar, and both sides had penalty appeals dismissed by the referee. The game remained balanced, setting the stage for a tense second half. nnBermuda demonstrated their championship pedigree in the second half, equalising through Jaden Masters in the 60th minute. They then took the lead in the 74th minute when Khyla Brangman expertly converted a penalty.  Adding to Jersey's woes, Jya Rattaray Smith secured a third goal for Bermuda in stoppage time, capitalizing on a rebound off the bar to solidify their position at the top of Group B. nnJersey will seek to bounce back from this opening defeat, facing Shetland Islands on Monday and Isle of Wight on Tuesday in their remaining group matches. They missed out on a semi-final place two years ago due to an administrative error and will be keen to avoid a similar outcome this year after finding themselves at the bottom of their group following a 2-2 draw between Shetland and the Isle of Wight earlier in the day.
