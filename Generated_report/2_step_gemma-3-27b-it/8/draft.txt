Corby, Northamptonshire – A local dog named Jimmy has been rescued after spending 11 days trapped down a well on Stephenson Way, sparking a widespread community search effort. The dramatic rescue unfolded on July 2nd, bringing relief to his owner, Dorota Gruszczynska, who had feared the worst after Jimmy went missing on June 21st. nnMs. Gruszczynska described the agonizing search, stating, “We searched the area so many times and nothing, we created posters, put them online and sent them around the local area.” She initially believed Jimmy may have been hit by a car, saying, “We thought he had been hit by a car and we would never see him again.” A breakthrough came when Northamptonshire Fire and Rescue Service, responding to a separate fire nearby, heard barking emanating from the well. nnAccording to the fire service, a Corby crew discovered Jimmy while attending to a nearby incident. Crews utilized a reach pole and lines to carefully remove cylinders obstructing the well, enabling them to safely lift the canine to the surface. They contacted Ms. Gruszczynska after identifying the dog through local missing pet pages. “Firefighters had been called to the area, and had heard him barking, they asked if he had a green harness on and told me he was alive,” Ms. Gruszczynska recounted, overjoyed at the news. nn“It was near where we had lost him... We couldn't believe it was him,” she added, reflecting on the moment of reunion.  Ms. Gruszczynska expressed profound gratitude to all those involved in the rescue, including the firefighters and a local drone company who dedicated significant effort to the search.  “We’re so grateful for everyone who helped... They did a huge amount of work, purely out of their heart,” she said, further emphasizing the power of the community response.  Jimmy was taken to a vet and found to be hungry but remarkably well, with Ms. Gruszczynska stating, “He’s back on his feet, he's recovered surprisingly well, we don't know how he survived, there must have been water in the well,”
