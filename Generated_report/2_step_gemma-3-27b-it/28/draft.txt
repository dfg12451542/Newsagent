Wales secured an elusive victory against Japan in Kobe on Saturday, defeating the hosts 31-22 in a hard-fought match that fly-half Dan Edwards hailed as a testament to the team’s character. The win provides a much-needed boost for Welsh rugby, which has faced a period of struggle and criticism.  Edwards, who scored a vital late try, emphasized the significance of the result, particularly after recent setbacks. nnThe match saw Wales demonstrate resilience, particularly in the second half with impactful contributions from replacements like Taine Plumtree. Plumtree’s audacious reverse pass, setting up Edwards for the try, became a defining moment of the game.  “It was about getting a win, I’m absolutely chuffed,” Edwards stated, clearly pleased with the team’s performance. The victory was especially sweet given the doubts surrounding the team following their previous performance. nnEdwards continued, “Especially with everyone saying we didn’t deserve to win last week and were writing us off, people have been writing us off for however long. We’ve come back and shown everyone why we’re here. It shows the character we’ve got. That was a real statement that when it gets tough, we can pull through.” The win is seen by many as a turning point, offering a much-needed lift to Welsh rugby morale. nnThe final play, engineered by Plumtree, perfectly encapsulated the team’s newfound bravery, according to interim head coach Matt Sherratt. “That last play summarises it for me,” said Sherratt. “It would have been easy to go to a safe lineout at the front. We have thrown to the back, run a more risky play. And Taine Plumtree throws a reverse pass. I didn’t intend for him to be that brave, but it worked!”  nnFor players like Keelan Giles, who returned to the Welsh squad after a nine-year absence, the victory held particular significance.  “It’s a massive day for me and my family,” Giles said. “They made the trip over, which made it extra special. It’s unbelievable.”  The win marks a step forward for Wales, signalling a renewed determination and a willingness to take risks under a new sense of optimism.
