Donegal manager Jim McGuinness believes his team has “deserved the right” to compete for the All-Ireland title, following their decisive 3-26 to 0-15 victory over Meath in the semi-final. The win secures Donegal’s place in the final against Kerry, a familiar opponent and last year’s defeated contender. A dominant second-half performance, fueled by goals from Oisin Gallen, Ciaran Moore, and Conor O’Donnell, propelled Donegal to a comfortable victory at Croke Park.nnThe Ulster champions held a five-point lead at halftime, but it was the trio of second-half goals that ultimately sealed the win and ensured their progression to the final on July 27th. McGuinness praised his team's performance, stating, “Full credit to the boys, they have done a brilliant job, and we got over the line today.” The team displayed an attacking intent, successfully converting opportunities as planned.nnLooking ahead to the final, McGuinness acknowledged the challenge posed by Kerry, noting, “We know what we will get with Kerry, they are a team who know their way around Croke Park like the back of their hand.” Kerry’s experience and pedigree as All-Ireland winners will present a significant hurdle for Donegal. nnDespite the looming challenge, McGuinness remains confident in his team’s ability, stating, “They know how to win an All-Ireland so it’s a massive, massive challenge but we’ve deserved the right to play that game and I’m delighted they’ve got that opportunity.” He urged the team to stay focused and deliver their best performance, adding, “Please god, now they can take it.” Donegal previously faced Kerry in the 2012 final, ultimately falling to defeat, and will be eager to avenge that loss.nnMcGuinness also stressed the importance of balancing the excitement of the upcoming final with dedicated preparation. “We have a job to do,” he said, emphasizing the need to remain focused on the task at hand and “bring the highest level possible to our performance”. The two-week build-up will be crucial as Donegal prepares to challenge for the coveted All-Ireland title.
