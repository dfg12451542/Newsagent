MasterChef presenter John Torode has confirmed he is the subject of an allegation of using racist language, an issue that emerged during an inquiry into separate claims against his co-host, Gregg Wallace. Torode addressed the allegation in a statement posted on Instagram Monday, stating the incident purportedly occurred in either 2018 or 2019. The revelation comes as the popular cooking competition prepares for another season, casting a shadow over the show’s typically lighthearted atmosphere.nnThe chef acknowledged the allegation, stating, “For the sake of transparency, I confirm that I am the individual who is alleged to have used racial language on one occasion.” He further clarified that the person with whom he was speaking reportedly did not perceive the language as malicious and that he issued an immediate apology. However, Torode also expressed confusion, admitting, “I have absolutely no recollection of any of this, and I do not believe that it happened.”nnDespite his lack of memory regarding the specific incident, Torode was emphatic in his denunciation of racial language. “However, I want to be clear that I’ve always had the view that any racial language is wholly unacceptable in any environment,” he stated. “I’m shocked and saddened by the allegation as I would never wish to cause anyone any offence.” The acknowledgement and subsequent clarification represent a complex response to the accusation, attempting to balance transparency with a denial of intent.nnThe inquiry into the allegations against both Torode and Wallace has not released specific details concerning Wallace's claims. The BBC, the broadcaster of MasterChef, has not yet released a statement regarding Torode’s acknowledgement. The situation raises questions about workplace conduct and the standards expected of high-profile television personalities, at a time when sensitivity surrounding issues of race and inclusivity is paramount.
