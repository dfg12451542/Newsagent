A 71-year-old motorcyclist was killed in a six-vehicle collision near Loch Lomond on Sunday, July 13th. The incident occurred on a busy stretch of road, prompting a six-hour closure for police investigation. While the drivers of the other vehicles involved escaped injury, the motorcyclist died at the scene, according to Police Scotland.nnEmergency services responded to the crash and immediately began work at the scene. Authorities have yet to release details regarding the cause of the collision, but are actively pursuing all lines of inquiry. Sergeant Kenny Malaney of Police Scotland stated, “Our inquiries are continuing to establish the full circumstances of what happened.”nnSgt. Malaney also expressed condolences to the victim’s family and friends, saying, “Sadly, the male motorcyclist died and our thoughts are with his family and friends.” The area around Loch Lomond is a popular tourist destination, especially during the summer months, and witnesses are being sought to help piece together the events leading to the tragedy.nnPolice are specifically requesting assistance from anyone who may have been traveling in the vicinity at the time of the crash, or who possesses dashcam footage. “The road would have been busy at the time and I would ask anyone who may have witnessed the crash, or who may have dashcam footage to contact us,” Sgt. Malaney added. Anyone with information is urged to come forward to aid in the ongoing investigation.
