Motherwell manager Jens Berthel Askou has urged his team to “increase tempo” following a frustrating draw against Clyde in the Premier Sports Cup, acknowledging the unpredictable nature of the game. The result, while not ideal, comes early in the season and offers lessons for improvement, according to the manager.nnAskou emphasized the inherent difficulties in football, stating that results are rarely guaranteed. The draw against Clyde served as a clear demonstration of this, with several other clubs also experiencing unexpected outcomes in the cup competition.  “It just shows how tricky football is in general,” Askou told club media, reflecting on the match's outcome.nnWhile acknowledging areas for growth, Askou was quick to praise his team’s effort and structural approach during the game. He noted their intensity in pressing, but highlighted a vulnerability to Clyde’s direct play.  “In terms of effort and structure, we were good… We didn’t get the result we wanted, obviously. A few other clubs, as far as I’ve heard, didn’t do that either,” he explained. “Every time we tried to press, they put the ball in behind. I think we should have dealt better with some of their direct play.”nnLooking ahead, Askou believes a key focus will be raising the team’s overall pace of play. He is confident that playing at Fir Park will provide the environment needed to achieve this. “That’s something to improve on, definitely,” Askou stated, “and then it’s about getting the tempo up, but I’m sure that Fir Park will give us the possibility to play at a much higher tempo.”
