Kilmarnock supporters expressed delight and cautious optimism following their team’s opening pre-season victory over Brora Rangers, marking the start of the Derek McInnes era. We asked fans for their views after the match, and responses indicated a positive mood tempered with an understanding of the work ahead. nnMany fans appreciated the competitive nature of the game. “A very good, competitive game really enjoyed it,” said one supporter, Gordon, reflecting a sentiment echoed by others present. The match provided a first glimpse of the team under new management and an opportunity for fans to assess early progress. nnWhile acknowledging the challenge posed by Brora Rangers, the focus remained firmly on securing the win. Gordon further commented, “Not as easy as I thought it would be but the three points are the most important thing.” This pragmatic approach resonated with other fans, highlighting the value of building momentum throughout pre-season.nnLooking ahead, supporters voiced hopes for continued success.  “Well done Killie and Kettlewell. Bring on Livingston on Wednesday hopefully another three points,” Gordon added, already setting sights on the next fixture. The upcoming match against Livingston presents an opportunity to build on the positive start and refine the team's performance.nnBeyond the result, individual performances also drew attention, with young Ben Brannan particularly impressing. “A long day but an enjoyable one. Young Ben Brannan reminds me of Aaron Hickey - can play RWB or LWB, is comfortable coming inside and taking men on and has a great engine. Hope to see more of him this season,” observed Amy.  Another fan, George, added, “Solid start, keep on improving the team's shape and tactics will come together. Good solid signings. Come on the Killie.”  These comments suggest a growing sense of expectation and belief in the team’s potential for the season ahead.
