Salisbury resident Phyll Babb celebrated her 100th birthday with a remarkable achievement: completing a 200-mile walking challenge and raising over £11,000 for the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless (STFH). Ms. Babb reached the finish line on Sunday, circling Salisbury Cathedral to mark the culmination of her incredible feat. What began as a goal to walk 100 miles quickly evolved as she discovered she was ahead of schedule, ultimately doubling her target.nnMs. Babb’s journey resonated deeply within her community and beyond. She originally intended to walk 100 miles (161km) but surpassed her goal in January, propelled by her consistent effort. The support she received was evident during the final mile, surrounded by cheering family members and supporters. This was no spur-of-the-moment decision; the lifelong walker explained, “Walking is good for you,” noting she grew up walking everywhere as a child and continues to enjoy it immensely.nn“It's hard to believe I've done 200 walks. It's gone like a dream,” Ms. Babb shared, reflecting on the accomplishment. Her dedication was meticulously tracked by a local accountant, who helped confirm each of her 200 walks recorded on the Strava app. The funds raised will provide vital support to the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless, assisting with furnishing a new house for those in need.nnFamily members expressed immense pride in Ms. Babb's accomplishment. Her great-niece, Ruth Simmons, described watching her complete the 200th lap as “really special.” David Evans, a friend, lauded her fitness and articulation at 100 years old as “the really amazing thing she’s done.” Liz Huntley simply stated, “She is inspirational, definitely. She’s been inspirational my whole life, to be honest.”nnGordon Pardy, linked to the beneficiary charity, highlighted the impact of Ms. Babb’s efforts, noting that the donation will directly contribute to providing shelter and support. “To do this in her 100th year was so impressive,” he added. Ms. Babb expressed her gratitude for the “extremely generous” donations, offering a heartfelt “thank you all very, very much” to her supporters.
