A memorial running race is set to take place this Sunday in Colchester to honour the life of Arthur Whiston, a beloved local athlete who tragically died in a road crash. The event, organised by Colchester Harriers AC – Mr Whiston’s running club – will begin at 09:30 BST at the garrison athletics track. Organisers hope the race will serve as a lasting tribute to a man who was deeply cherished by the community.nnThe anniversary event aims to celebrate Mr Whiston's passion for running and his positive impact on those around him. Participants can choose between a 5k or 10k route. While pre-registration has closed, runners are still welcome to turn up on the day and register to take part, ensuring inclusivity for all who wish to honour Arthur’s memory.nn“Arthur was incredibly loved by the community,” said Lydia Cunningham, a close friend and fellow runner. The race is not only a commemoration of his life but also a demonstration of the strong bonds he forged within Colchester’s running circles. nnCunningham further described Mr Whiston as a “very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular,” highlighting his generous spirit and the respect he garnered from those who knew him. This sentiment reflects the widespread grief felt following his untimely death earlier this year.nnThe funds raised from the event will be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity that Mr Whiston passionately supported. This final act of generosity, chosen by his loved ones, reflects his commitment to the local environment and his desire to leave a positive legacy beyond the running track.
