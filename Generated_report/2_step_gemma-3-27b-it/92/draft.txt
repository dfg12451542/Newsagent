Wycombe Wanderers have bolstered their midfield options with the signing of Ewan Henderson from Belgian side Beerschot, marking the club’s eighth addition of the summer transfer window. The 25-year-old Scotland youth international arrives at Adams Park for an undisclosed fee, as the Chairboys prepare for the upcoming season.nnHenderson brings with him experience from top-flight football in both Scotland and Belgium, having previously played in the Scottish Premiership. He joins a growing list of new faces in Buckinghamshire, including Armando Junior Quitirna, Taylor Allen, Bradley Fink, Caolan Boyd-Munce, Dan Casey, James Tilley and Mikki van Sas, signalling a period of significant squad rebuilding.nnWycombe head coach Mike Dodds expressed his enthusiasm about the new signing, highlighting Henderson’s energetic style of play. “Ewan’s a really energetic midfielder who’s had a great start to his career in the top flights in Scotland and Belgium, and gives us another dimension in the middle of the park,” Dodds explained. The manager is keen to integrate Henderson quickly into the team.nnDodds also noted Henderson's recent playing time as a positive factor, anticipating a relatively seamless transition. “He’s played a lot of football in the past three years which stands him in good stead to come here and hopefully hit the ground running as he integrates into the squad in pre-season,” Dodds added.  Fans will hope Henderson's arrival provides a spark in midfield as Wycombe look to improve upon their previous season’s performance.
