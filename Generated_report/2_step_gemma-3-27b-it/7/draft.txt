Leicestershire Police have made four additional arrests in connection with a stabbing that occurred in the Eyres Monsell area of Leicester on Friday evening. The arrests, consisting of two men aged 18 and 29, and two women aged 25 and 38, bring the total number of suspects in custody, or released on bail, to five. The initial arrest of a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted murder prompted the subsequent investigation, which is now focusing on a network of individuals known to the victim.nnThe incident took place shortly after 9:15 PM BST on Sturdee Road, with the injured man, in his 20s, managing to reach Lawford Road before being transported to hospital, where he remains in a stable condition. According to Detective Sergeant Adrian Davies, “We believe the stabbing happened shortly before 9.15pm,” adding that the warm weather and lingering daylight likely meant there were potential witnesses in the area.  Police have confirmed the suspects and the victim are known to one another, indicating the incident was not a random attack.nnAuthorities are actively pursuing leads and gathering information from the community.  House-to-house inquiries are underway and the scene has undergone a “thorough examination,” according to police reports.  The 18-year-old man and the 25-year-old woman are currently being held in police custody, while the remaining three suspects have been released on police bail pending further investigation. nnDetectives are urging anyone with information, no matter how small, to come forward. Det Sgt Davies emphasized the importance of public assistance, stating, “If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us.”  Anyone with information is encouraged to contact Leicestershire Police to aid in the ongoing investigation and bring those responsible to justice.
