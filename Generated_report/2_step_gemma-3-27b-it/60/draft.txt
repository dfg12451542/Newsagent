Two women were killed and two men injured after a gunman opened fire at the Richmond Road Baptist Church in Lexington, Kentucky, on Sunday, following a violent chain of events that began with the shooting of a state trooper. The suspect, identified as 47-year-old Guy House, was subsequently shot and killed by responding law enforcement at the scene, according to officials. The incident has left the tight-knit community reeling.nnThe ordeal began when a state trooper pulled over House’s car near Blue Grass Airport after an alert triggered by a license plate reader, Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers explained at a news conference. “A state trooper pulled over Mr House's car on Terminal Drive after receiving a registration plate reader alert,” Chief Weathers stated. The suspect then shot the trooper, inflicting non-life threatening injuries, before carjacking a vehicle and driving approximately 16 miles to the church, with police in pursuit. nnUpon arriving at the church, House “fired his weapon at individuals on church property,” Chief Weathers said, resulting in the deaths of Beverly Gum, 72, and Christina Combs, 32. Two male parishioners were also wounded, one critically, and transported to a local hospital. Investigators believe House may have had a connection to individuals at the church, though the specifics of that connection remain under investigation. nnChief Weathers confirmed that “the suspect was shot by responding law enforcement and was pronounced deceased at the scene,” adding that the gunman’s identity would be released once next-of-kin had been notified. A county coroner official noted that the church was small, with attendees largely comprised of related individuals or close friends, underscoring the profound impact of the tragedy on the community. nnThe Kentucky State Police and the department’s Public Integrity Unit are currently investigating the incident. Further details regarding the suspect’s motives and the nature of his connection to the church are expected to be released as the investigation progresses.
