Rory McIlroy has expressed his excitement ahead of the 2025 Open Championship at Royal Portrush, saying that a win on home soil would be 'just as emotional' as his Masters triumph. The 36-year-old, who won the Masters in 2014, told BBC Sport NI that a Portrush win would be 'amazing' and that he is grateful for the support he receives from home. 'It would be absolutely incredible,' he said. 'I'm so grateful and appreciative of the support I get from home and they really make me feel that out there.' McIlroy, the 2014 Open champion, has had a successful season so far, and is encouraged by his recent performances. 'I'm certainly encouraged by how I've played the last two starts, especially in Scotland,' he said. He also mentioned that the two weeks off after the Travelers to reset and get over to the Open has been 'really nice.' The Northern Irish golfer is looking to make the most of the opportunity to play in front of his home crowd. 'There's pressure internally to put on a show for everyone but I know that if I just go out and play my game, I'm playing well enough to give myself a great chance,' he said. McIlroy also reflected on his previous experience at the Open in 2019, saying that he is a better player now and manages his game better. 'I didn't have the week I wanted in 2019,' he said. 'I'm a better player, manage my game better, and even if I have an off day, it's not going to be a 79 like it was on the first day in 2019.'McIlroy also spoke about embracing the attention that comes with playing in front of his home crowd. 'I think I probably tried to isolate, and I think it's better for everyone if I embrace it,' he said. 'It just makes for a better interaction.' With his game in good form, McIlroy is confident that he has a great chance of winning the Open Championship on home soil. The Open Championship returns to Royal Portrush for the first time since 2019, and McIlroy is eager to make the most of the opportunity. 'I'm so happy it's back so soon because I want to have another crack at it,' he said.
