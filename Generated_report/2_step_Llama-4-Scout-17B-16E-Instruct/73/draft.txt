Callie Cooke, star of the upcoming film F1: The Movie, revealed that she got to perform a real pit stop during last year's British Grand Prix. In an interview, Cooke described the experience as 'mad' and 'one of the best experiences of my life.' She spent the day at the Silverstone circuit, changing tires, oil, and even the wing of a car, as she immersed herself in the world of a garage. Cooke's connection to Northamptonshire and Silverstone made the experience even more special. 'Because of the Northamptonshire-Silverstone connection, it feels so special and so real, it's just been the coolest thing,' she said. The film's cast and crew, including star Brad Pitt, spent at least a year working in and around Northamptonshire and Silverstone. Cooke noted that Pitt 'loved working in Northamptonshire and Silverstone' and had a great rapport with the crew, appreciating their British sense of humor.To prepare for her role, Cooke trained with a real pit crew, where she was the only actor among mechanics, engineers, and motorsport professionals. She recalled, 'I think it did kind of help growing up in Northampton and not far from Silverstone because I do know a lot of people that work with cars, and I just got stuck in that garage.' Her experience and determination likely contributed to landing her role, as she noted, 'I knew that if I was going to play a woman in this male-dominated industry, I was just going to have to hold my own and get stuck in, and I think maybe that's how I got the job.'The film's production team took their commitment to authenticity to the next level by performing a real pit stop between Mercedes and Ferrari teams during the British Grand Prix. Cooke proudly stated, 'We did a real pit stop... and we did it as quick as the other teams.' The experience was a 'once-in-a-lifetime' opportunity for the cast and crew, allowing them to work closely with real F1 teams and experience the high-stakes world of Formula 1 racing.
