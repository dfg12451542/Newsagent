The Open: Welcome to PortrushThe best golfers in the world have descended on the Northern Irish town of Portrush for the 153rd Open Championship. This prestigious event has brought top talent to the region, promising an exciting competition for the coveted title of 'Champion Golfer of the Year'.As the tournament unfolds, BBC Sport will provide comprehensive coverage, taking viewers through every twist and turn of the championship. The broadcaster will offer live radio and text updates throughout the week, supplemented by press conferences featuring prominent golfers such as Rory McIlroy and defending champion Xander Schauffele. Additionally, expert analysis from golf correspondent Iain Carter and on-course reporters will offer valuable insights into the competition.For those looking to stay up-to-date with the latest developments, simply hit the 'follow' button on the website or app to add it to your 'MySport' feed. This dedicated resource will ensure that fans don't miss a moment of the action as the world's top golfers vie for the championship title at Royal Portrush.
