A recent survey conducted by the Institute of Directors (IoD) Jersey has revealed that businesses in the Channel Islands are calling for greater collaboration between the islands to drive economic growth. The survey, which gathered input from 70 IoD Jersey members, found that while 56% of respondents were optimistic about their own organizations, only 17% were optimistic about Jersey's overall economy. The survey's findings suggest that businesses are looking for bold, strategic investment and policy clarity to restore wider economic confidence. According to Alex Ruddy, IoD Jersey Chair, 'While our members are confident in their own organisations, they are calling for bold, strategic investment and policy clarity to restore wider economic confidence.' The top priorities for growth in Jersey included infrastructure investment, attracting new industries, and housing affordability.The survey also found that both Jersey and Guernsey face similar economic challenges, with respondents urging political leaders to work together to address these issues. Richard Hemans, IoD Guernsey's lead on economics, noted that 'there is clear recognition from the business community in both islands that - given the same challenges apply to both islands and our economic, geographical, demographic, constitutional and fiscal profile is so similar - the best way to confront our mutual challenges is to work together more closely.'The survey results also highlighted areas of potential growth, including entrepreneurship and innovation, which could be supported through tax incentives, youth talent attraction, and skills development. Additionally, the survey found that AI adoption is in its early stages, with 70% of companies offering training and 33% having a strategy in development. Overall, the survey provided a 'clear mandate for action,' according to Alex Ruddy, with businesses calling for collaboration across the Channel Islands, support for innovation, and a focus on infrastructure and housing to ensure Jersey's future prosperity.
