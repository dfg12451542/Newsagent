A 15-year-old boy was taken to hospital with non-life-threatening injuries after being stabbed at Sunnydale Park on Saturday. Derbyshire Police reported that they were called to the scene at about 16:20 BST following reports of the incident. The victim's condition is stable and not believed to be life-threatening. A 14-year-old boy, who cannot be named for legal reasons, has been charged with wounding with intent and possession of a bladed article in a public place. He has been remanded in custody to appear at Southern Derbyshire Magistrates' Court on Monday. In connection with the stabbing, four other teenagers were arrested and have been released on bail as investigations continue. According to Derbyshire Police, the 14-year-old charged is currently being held in custody. The police are continuing their investigation into the incident.
