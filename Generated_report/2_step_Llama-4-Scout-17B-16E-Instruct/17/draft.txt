A West Yorkshire Police officer has been charged with sexual assault by touching, the force has confirmed. PC Jack Blagbrough, 31, who works in the Kirklees District, is due to appear at Barnsley Magistrates' Court on July 16. The charge relates to an alleged offence against a woman while the officer was off-duty. West Yorkshire Police suspended PC Blagbrough from duty at the start of the investigation. The force has not released further details, but the officer's appearance at court will likely bring further scrutiny to the case.
