Motherwell manager Jens Berthel Askou emphasized the need for his team to 'increase tempo' following a challenging start to their Premier Sports Cup campaign. The team's draw with Clyde was a prime example of the unpredictable nature of football, Askou told club media. 'It just shows how tricky football is in general,' he said. 'There'll always be surprises in football, especially in the cup tournaments, there will be things that don't go as expected.' Askou acknowledged that his team failed to secure the desired result, but took some positives from the match. 'I think in terms of effort and structure, we were good,' he noted. 'In terms of intensity, I think we pressed them with intensity.' However, he identified areas for improvement, particularly in dealing with Clyde's direct play. 'Every time we tried to press, they put the ball in behind. I think we should have dealt better with some of their direct play.'The manager is optimistic that his team can elevate their performance, particularly at home. 'That's something to improve on, definitely and then it's about getting the tempo up, but I'm sure that Fir Park will give us the possibility to play at a much higher tempo.' Askou's comments reflect his pragmatic approach to the team's early-season struggles, and his focus on building momentum in the competition.
