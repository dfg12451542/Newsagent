Chris Gotterup secured his second PGA Tour title with a thrilling two-shot victory over Rory McIlroy at the Scottish Open. The world number 158 held his nerve to card a closing 66, finishing on 15 under at the Renaissance Club. 'I just hung in there tight and tried to keep it together,' Gotterup said. 'It's amazing. It's all hitting me. It's just so cool. I played really well this week and I knew today was going to be tough.' The 25-year-old American's win also earned him a debut appearance in The Open at Royal Portrush next week, along with a £1.2m prize. McIlroy, who began the final round tied for the lead with Gotterup, faltered on the back nine to share second place with England's Marco Penge. 'It was pretty windy and hard to get the ball super close. I gave myself plenty of chances coming down the stretch. Chris played a great round of golf. He was so solid,' McIlroy said. Despite the disappointment, McIlroy remained upbeat, adding, 'It's been a great week. I'm really happy with where my game is. No frustration, really.' Gotterup's victory was all the more impressive given that it was only his second competitive outing on a links course. He overcame a shaky start, recovering strongly to pick up birdies on three, seven and eight, before seizing a two-shot advantage with birdies on two of the ensuing three holes. His lead was briefly halved, but he regained control with a 10-foot birdie on the par-five 16th. Gotterup's win marked a significant upset, with McIlroy being the star attraction in the leading group. However, the American's solid play and composure under pressure ultimately earned him the top spot.
