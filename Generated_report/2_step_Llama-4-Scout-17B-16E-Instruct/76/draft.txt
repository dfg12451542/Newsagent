The M11 near Stansted Airport has reopened after a vehicle fire caused significant disruptions. On July 13, 2025, fire crews attended the scene, and people faced delays of up to 60 minutes on approach, according to a National Highways spokesperson. The spokesperson warned of approximately 1.5 miles of congestion following the incident, advising drivers to allow extra time to clear the delays. Nottinghamshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed they were at the scene, assisting police with the incident, and advised drivers to avoid the area and the M1 where possible. The incident led to hundreds of cars being stuck, with some drivers leaving their vehicles and walking on the motorway.A National Highways spokesperson stated, 'There is approximately 1.5 miles congestion following this incident so please allow time for these delays to clear.' The spokesperson's warning came as a relief to many drivers who were stuck in traffic. The M11 has since reopened, but not before causing considerable delays to travelers. The incident serves as a reminder to check traffic updates before embarking on journeys and to plan for potential delays.
