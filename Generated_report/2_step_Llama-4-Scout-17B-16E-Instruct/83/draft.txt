Five Teesside stories you might have missed this week include a heartwarming tale of a lost puppy and a concerning incident of pollution in a local lake. In Hartlepool, an eight-week-old Belgian Malinois named Jager was found running around the streets before being taken in by animal charity Stray Aid. Durham Police reported that the puppy was discovered last week and was subsequently assessed by the force's Dog Support Unit, which saw potential in the young dog and decided to offer him a role. In another part of Teesside, residents of Redcar were concerned when they spotted a large fish floating on the surface of the lake at Locke Park. The Environment Agency (EA) was called to investigate and later determined that the incident had been caused by 'natural factors'. In transportation news, the Department for Transport (DfT) has confirmed a significant investment in the region as part of a £92bn national package announced on Tuesday. These stories and more may have flown under the radar, but they highlight the diverse range of events and issues affecting communities across Teesside. From animal welfare to environmental concerns and infrastructure development, there is always something happening in the region.
