Donegal manager Jim McGuinness believes his team have 'deserved the right' to face Kerry in the All-Ireland final. McGuinness' side dominated their semi-final match against Meath, winning 3-26 to 0-15 at Croke Park. The team's impressive performance was highlighted by second-half goals from Oisin Gallen, Ciaran Moore, and Conor O'Donnell, who was named man-of-the-match. McGuinness praised his team's performance, saying, 'Full credit to the boys, they have done a brilliant job, and we got over the line today.' He also acknowledged the challenge that lies ahead, stating, 'We know what we will get with Kerry, they are a team who know their way around Croke Park like the back of their hand. They know how to win an All-Ireland so it's a massive, massive challenge.'The Donegal manager emphasized that his team have earned their place in the final, saying, 'We've deserved the right to play that game and I'm delighted they've got that opportunity.' McGuinness also expressed his hopes for the final, adding, 'Please god, now they can take it.' As the team prepares for the final against Kerry on July 27, McGuinness stressed the importance of staying focused, saying, 'We have to keep on the job now, prepare as best we can and stay focused on the things that are important.' He also highlighted the need to balance the excitement of the two-week build-up with the demands of the final, stating, 'We have to bring the highest level possible to our performance.'
