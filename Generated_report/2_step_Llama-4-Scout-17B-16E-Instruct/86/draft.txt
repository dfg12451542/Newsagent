A three-year-old girl from Powys is set to become one of the youngest competitors ever at the Crufts dog show. Caitlyn, from Groesffordd near Brecon, qualified with her eight-year-old Miniature Schnauzer, Zeeva, after being placed in the Three Counties Championship Show last month. Caitlyn's mum, Lydia Mellor, expressed her pride, saying, 'She is so confident generally and she works so well with the dogs. She loves the dogs.' The pair's achievement is a testament to their hard work and dedication, with Helen Kerfoot, an organizer, stating, 'We are delighted to see a young competitor doing so well, having qualified for Crufts already – the result of a lot of hard work and dedication.' Caitlyn, who loves 'winning' and 'getting rosettes' with Zeeva, will be a role model for young enthusiasts, showcasing the sportsmanship and passion required for the show. Lydia Mellor added, 'She will shake hands with the judges, she shakes hands with the winners, she always congratulates everyone. I am massively proud of the sportsmanship she is showing at this age.' The family's achievement also highlights the accessibility of the sport, with Helen Kerfoot saying, 'Taking part in Crufts is a real achievement but it's also something that's accessible to all.'
