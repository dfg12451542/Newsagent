A 71-year-old motorcyclist was killed in a six-vehicle crash near Loch Lomond on July 13, 2025. According to Police Scotland, the man died at the scene. The drivers of the cars involved were not injured. The road was closed for over six hours as police conducted their investigations. Sgt Kenny Malaney of Police Scotland stated, 'Sadly, the male motorcyclist died and our thoughts are with his family and friends.' He added that inquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances of the incident and appealed for witnesses or anyone with dashcam footage to come forward. 'The road would have been busy at the time and I would ask anyone who may have witnessed the crash, or who may have dashcam footage to contact us,' he said.
