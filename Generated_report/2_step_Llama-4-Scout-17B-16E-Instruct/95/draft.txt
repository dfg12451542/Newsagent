Are Hull City fans falling out of love with Acun Ilicali?Hull City fans are growing increasingly restless with owner Acun Ilicali, citing concerns over the club's financial struggles and on-field performance. 'It's such a tricky time, but it's not like we haven't seen it before,' said Daniel Mawer, a Hull City fan. 'But usually, it felt a bit more of a fun rollercoaster to be on over the past four years. This one feels more like we're on the steady decline of the rollercoaster.'When Ilicali purchased the club, he was welcomed with open arms, particularly after introducing cheaper tickets. 'He came in [and] he got the crowd's backing straight away with cheaper tickets,' said Joe Collins, another fan. However, after a disastrous season and growing financial concerns, some fans are now calling for Ilicali's exit. 'You've got some fans who are 'Acun out', and there is definitely merit to that point of view,' Collins added.Despite the concerns, not all fans have lost faith in Ilicali. 'Being Hull City, nothing surprises me anymore,' said Charlotte Berry. 'I feel like it's a blip in the road. Acun's got my full backing. It just looks like admin errors.' Mawer also believes that Ilicali 'does care' and 'wants it to work.' However, with the club facing a transfer restriction and struggling to keep up with day-to-day payments to suppliers, it's clear that significant changes are needed. As Collins noted, 'massively change the way we do things from now forward, because if this transfer restriction stays, we can't carry on spending as we were.'
