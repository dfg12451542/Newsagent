A runner participating in a 55-mile ultramarathon in the Lake District was airlifted to safety on Saturday after collapsing from heat exhaustion. The Patterdale Mountain Rescue Team (PMRT) was called to Grisedale Valley at approximately 16:00 BST after the runner became pale, vomited, and lost consciousness. The North West Ambulance Service contacted the mountain rescue team and the coastguard, which dispatched a helicopter to winch the runner to safety. He was then taken to the King George playing field in Patterdale, where he was checked over by a team doctor at Patterdale Base and later transferred to hospital for further checks.The rescue operation, which lasted two and a half hours, involved 11 team members from PMRT and support from the coastguard.
