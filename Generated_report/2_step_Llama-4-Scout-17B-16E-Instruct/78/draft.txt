More than £8,000 has been raised in just 48 hours for the family of a teenager who tragically died after going missing in a lake in Sutton Coldfield. An online fundraising page, set up with permission from the family, was initially aimed at raising £1,600 but quickly surpassed its target. The GoFundMe page now has a revised goal of £11,000. Organiser Helena Swallow said the family are 'so very grateful' for the outpouring of support. The fundraiser aims to help support the family during this difficult time.
