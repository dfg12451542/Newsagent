Chelsea won the Club World Cup after a 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain in New Jersey on Sunday. The team's captain, Reece James, was presented with the trophy by former US President Donald Trump. However, in an unexpected move, Trump chose to stay on stage with the players for the trophy lift, despite initial plans for him to exit the stage. Reece James revealed that he was surprised by Trump's decision, saying, 'They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage, and I thought that he was going to exit the stage, but he wanted to stay.' James added that Trump congratulated him and the team, telling them to 'enjoy the moment.' Cole Palmer, a Chelsea player, also expressed his surprise at Trump's presence on stage, saying, 'I knew he was going to be here but I didn't know he was going to be on the stand when we lifted the trophy.' Palmer added that he was 'a bit confused' by the situation. Trump, who was watching the game alongside Fifa president Gianni Infantino, said he was 'having a really great time.' He praised Infantino, saying, 'Gianni is a friend of mine, he's done such a great job with the league and with soccer - or as they would call it 'football', but I guess we call it soccer.' When asked if he could make an executive order to refer to the sport as 'football,' Trump smiled and said, 'I think we could do that. I think I could do that.' This isn't Chelsea's first time winning the Club World Cup, as they have previously lifted the trophy in 2021.
