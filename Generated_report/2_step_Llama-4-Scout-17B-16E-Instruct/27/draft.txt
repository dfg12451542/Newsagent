A man died at the scene of a single-vehicle crash in County Antrim on Saturday, July 12, 2025. According to police, the incident occurred in Ballyhill Road, Nutts Corner, at approximately 17:50 BST. The driver, a man, was involved in a collision in a white Volkswagen Golf. The ambulance service attended the scene, but unfortunately, the driver succumbed to his injuries and was pronounced dead at the scene. A male passenger was taken to hospital with injuries that are not believed to be life-threatening at this time. The Ballyhill Road was closed following the crash but has since reopened.
