A petition to save the historic Victoria Marine Lake in St Helier, Jersey, has reached 1,000 signatures, prompting a response from the island's government. The 128-year-old sea pool, a popular spot for swimming and leisure, has been facing an uncertain future. A 2024 inspection found that the pool required 'comprehensive work' or 'extensive repairs,' sparking concerns about its maintenance.The petition, launched by the Save Our Pool Working Group, calls for urgent restoration of the marine lake, which campaigners describe as a 'vital piece of Jersey's heritage.' Nina Zaech, the petition organiser, said that the pool could support health, leisure, and tourism initiatives. 'It's a vital piece of Jersey's heritage that could support health, leisure, and tourism,' she said.The level of interest in the petition means that Jersey's government will now respond to the campaign. Last month, Infrastructure Minister Andy Jehan was asked about the site's maintenance and said that repairs would be 'uneconomical' due to planned sea defence upgrades along the waterfront. However, campaigners remain hopeful that the petition will lead to a change in the government's stance.The Victoria Marine Lake has been a staple of St Helier's coastline for over a century, and its restoration could have significant benefits for the local community. As the petition continues to gather support, it remains to be seen how the government will respond to the campaign to save this historic sea pool.
