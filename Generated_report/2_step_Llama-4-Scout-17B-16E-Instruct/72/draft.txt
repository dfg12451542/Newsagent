A car fire on the A30 in Cornwall sparked a moorland blaze, prompting a rapid response from local fire crews. The incident occurred on July 13, 2025, and involved a vehicle fire that spread to nearby moorland. Fire crews from Launceston and Bodmin were deployed to tackle the blaze. Fortunately, there were no reported casualties or injuries. The cause of the car fire is currently unknown and may be subject to further investigation. The fire was successfully brought under control, but not before smoke was seen billowing from the scene.
