Actor Sean Bean has spoken out against a plan to build homes on green belt land in the Sheffield area where he grew up. In an email of objection, Bean said the proposal would 'practically obliterate the character and nature of our area.' He also expressed concerns that the council seems to view the local community as 'second-class citizens who will just have to put up with it.' Bean's comments have boosted the morale of a local campaign group, Save S13 Green Belt - The Sapphire McCarthy Campaign, who are fighting the development plans. Sadie Charlton, a member of the group, said Bean's involvement has been a significant boost, and his objection echoes the feelings of many in the community. The campaign group was formed following the death of Sapphire McCarthy, a local campaigner who had previously been in touch with Bean. Charlton credited McCarthy with galvanizing the community's efforts, saying 'without her, none of this would have happened.' Bean's objection was submitted just before the consultation period closed on July 11, and the campaign group plans to continue rallying against the plans.Bean's objection also highlighted what he sees as an imbalance in the council's approach, stating 'it seems like this working-class community and its surrounding areas has been specifically targeted, while other more affluent, genteel areas on the other side of Sheffield have escaped lightly.' The campaign group aims to keep the momentum going, with Charlton saying 'the community have really rallied around and come together, so this is just the beginning for us.'
