Kilmarnock fans react to win over Brora RangersKilmarnock kicked off the Kettlewell era with a hard-fought victory over Brora Rangers. We asked you for your views on the match, and fans were thrilled with the outcome. 'A very good, competitive game really enjoyed it,' said Gordon, encapsulating the sentiments of many supporters.While some fans were expecting an easier win, Gordon noted that 'Not as easy as I thought it would be but the three points are the most important thing.' The victory marked a positive start for Kilmarnock under new management, and fans are already looking ahead to the next match. 'Well done Killie and Kettlewell. Bring on Livingston on Wednesday hopefully another three points,' Gordon added.The match also provided a chance for fans to assess some of Kilmarnock's new players.
