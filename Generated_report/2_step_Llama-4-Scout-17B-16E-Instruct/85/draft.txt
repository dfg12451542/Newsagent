A devastating Israeli strike in the Gaza Strip has killed a young Palestinian boy, Abdullah, who was waiting in line for water with his family. His father, Mahmoud Abdul Rahman Ahmed, described the tragic event, saying his son was 'searching for a sip' of water when he was killed. The strike occurred on Sunday morning in the urban Nuseirat camp in central Gaza, where displaced people and those exhausted by the war had gathered. Mahmoud recounted the scene, saying, 'The children, Abdullah among them, stood in a queue with empty stomachs, empty jerrycans, and thirsty lips.' He claimed that minutes after the children and thirsty people gathered, Israeli warplanes bombed the water distribution point without prior notice. Mahmoud believes that Israel intended to convey a message: 'it won't allow people to drink even the drinking water that they crave.'The incident resulted in the deaths of 10 people, including six children, and injured 16 others, according to Al-Awda hospital in Nuseirat. The Israeli military acknowledged the strike, stating that they had targeted a Palestinian Islamic Jihad 'terrorist' but that a technical error led to the munition falling short of its target, harming uninvolved civilians. The incident is under review.This tragic event is not an isolated incident. Last Thursday, 10 children and three women were killed as they waited for nutritional supplements outside a clinic in the nearby town of Deir al-Balah. The UN children's agency (Unicef) and other international organizations have condemned the incidents, calling for an urgent review of the rules of engagement and full compliance with international humanitarian law. The Gaza Strip has faced significant escalation of Israeli airstrikes, resulting in numerous civilian casualties. The international community continues to pressure both parties to end the conflict and protect civilians.
