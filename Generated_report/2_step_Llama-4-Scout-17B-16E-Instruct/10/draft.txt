St Mirren manager Stephen Robinson expressed his shock and disappointment after his team's goalless draw with Arbroath in the Premier Sports Cup, which ultimately led to a defeat in the penalty shootout. Robinson, speaking to club media, said, 'I'm in a little bit of shock.' He attributed the team's underwhelming performance to poor decision-making, passing, and a lack of execution on simple tasks. 'The performance of maybe 13 of them is something I've not seen all pre-season,' he noted, highlighting that the team's display was far from their usual standard. The manager took responsibility for the outcome, stating, 'We decided as a collective not to do that and got exactly what we deserved.' Robinson also acknowledged the disappointment this result would cause for the fans who traveled to watch the match, saying, 'We're not going to feel sorry for ourselves... it's disappointing for us, disappointing for the people that travelled a long way on a Saturday.' Despite the setback, Robinson remained optimistic about his team's ability to bounce back, emphasizing, 'We have to react to it, we will be better. We have to do simple things which we didn't do.' St Mirren will look to regroup and improve as they face Forfar Athletic in their next match on Tuesday.
