Hibernian has announced the signing of Togo international striker Thibault Klidje from Luzern for a record fee. The 24-year-old has signed a three-year deal at Easter Road, with Hibs having the option to add another year. Klidje is expected to bring 'extra pace and trickery' to Hibs' front line, according to manager David Gray. 'He adds extra pace and trickery to our front line and performed well for Luzern last season, and at 24 has a lot of room for development,' Gray said. The club aims to help Klidje progress and look forward to working with him moving forward.
