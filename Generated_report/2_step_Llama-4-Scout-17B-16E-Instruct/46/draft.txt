Production has begun on the highly anticipated TV adaptation of Harry Potter, with the release of the first image of lead actor Dominic McLaughlin in costume as the iconic wizard. The 11-year-old McLaughlin was pictured in a photo released on Monday, marking a significant milestone in the production of the new series. The new adaptation has also announced several new cast members, including Rory Wilmot as Neville Longbottom, Amos Kitson as Dudley Dursley, Louise Brealey as Madam Rolanda Hooch, and Anton Lesser as Garrick Ollivander. They join an already star-studded cast, including John Lithgow as Dumbledore, Nick Frost as Hagrid, Janet McTeer as Minerva McGonagall, and Paapa Essiedu as Severus Snape.The production team also boasts several notable additions, including director of photography Adriano Goldman and hair and makeup designer Cate Hall, both of whom have previously worked on The Crown. With production now underway, fans of the beloved book series can expect the first series to launch in 2027. The Harry Potter franchise, which follows the magical adventures of young wizard Harry Potter and his friends at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, has captivated audiences worldwide since the release of the first book in 1997. The new TV adaptation is expected to bring a fresh take on the classic story, with the new cast and production team promising to deliver a faithful and enchanting retelling of J.K. Rowling's beloved novels.
