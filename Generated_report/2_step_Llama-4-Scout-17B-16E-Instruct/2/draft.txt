Six abandoned kittens, dubbed the 'Smelly cats' after the popular TV show Friends, have been rehomed after being found in a rubbish dump in the south of Scotland. The kittens, four weeks old, were discovered at Eyemouth Recycling Centre on May 27 in a 'heartbreaking scene' by a member of the public. Centre manager Kim McNeill said the kittens were suffering from severe malnutrition, flea and worm infestations, and painful eye infections. 'They were frightened, weak, and understandably wary of human contact,' she said. Borders Pet Rescue stepped in to nurse the kittens back to health, providing medical treatment for their eyes and parasites. The vet team also gave them the time, patience, and love they needed to recover. One of the rescue's foster carers took in the kittens, giving them a safe and nurturing environment where they could begin to trust again. The kittens, named after characters from Friends - three girls and three boys - slowly gained confidence, learned how to play, and experienced the warmth of a home for the first time. Inspired by their strong bond and the laughter they brought to the rescue team, the names were chosen to reflect their unique personalities. After about a month in foster care, the kittens were 'healthy and full of personality,' and Borders Pet Rescue began to look for their 'forever homes.' The 'Kitten Friends' captured hearts on social media, allowing the rescue to raise thousands of pounds for their veterinary care and spread the word about their search for owners. Kim McNeill said, 'We're delighted to share that all six kittens have now been adopted into wonderful homes, each beginning a new chapter filled with love and joy.' The kittens' story echoes the theme of a famous song from the 90s TV show Friends, 'Smelly Cat,' performed by character Phoebe, which revolves around an overlooked pet.
