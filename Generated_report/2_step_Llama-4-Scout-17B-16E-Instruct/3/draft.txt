Jersey's women's football team began their 2025 Island Games campaign with a 3-1 loss to reigning champions Bermuda. The game got off to a promising start for Jersey, with Anita Tavares scoring a sixth-minute goal, shooting left-footed into the corner. However, Bermuda hit back, with Jaden Masters striking from close range after an hour to equalize. Bermuda took the lead 14 minutes later when Khyla Brangman converted from the penalty spot. The 2023 gold medalists sealed their win with a third goal in stoppage time, courtesy of Jya Rattaray Smith, who reacted quickest after a shot came back off the bar. This result leaves Jersey bottom of Group B, after Shetland and Isle of Wight drew 2-2 earlier on Sunday. Jersey will face Shetland Islands on Monday and Isle of Wight in their final group match on Tuesday, looking to bounce back from the loss and a missed semi-final place in the previous edition due to an administrative error.
