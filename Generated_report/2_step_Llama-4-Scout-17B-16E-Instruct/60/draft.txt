A gunman opened fire at a church in Kentucky, killing two women and injuring two men, after a car chase that began with the suspect shooting and injuring a police officer. The incident occurred on Sunday at the Richmond Road Baptist Church in Lexington, where the gunman, identified as 47-year-old Guy House, opened fire at parishioners. According to Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers, a state trooper had pulled over House's car on Terminal Drive after receiving a registration plate reader alert, but House shot the trooper, inflicting non-life threatening injuries. The suspect then car-jacked a vehicle and drove to the church, where he fired his weapon at individuals on church property, Chief Weathers said. The gunman was shot by responding law enforcement and pronounced deceased at the scene. The two women killed were identified as Beverly Gum, 72, and Christina Combs, 32. Investigators believe the suspect had a connection to individuals at the church, which is a small, tight-knit community where most attendees are either related or close friends. The incident is being investigated by the Kentucky State Police and the department's Public Integrity Unit.
