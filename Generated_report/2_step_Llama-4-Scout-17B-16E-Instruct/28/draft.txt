Wales secured a hard-fought 22-31 victory over Japan in Kobe, with fly-half Dan Edwards praising his team's character after the elusive win. The tourists' triumph came after a crucial late try from Edwards, set up by a remarkable reverse pass from replacement flanker Taine Plumtree. Edwards, 22, expressed his delight with the win, saying, 'It was about getting a win, I'm absolutely chuffed.' He added that the victory was a statement of intent, especially after people had written off Wales following their previous performance. 'We've come back and shown everyone why we're here,' Edwards said. 'It shows the character we've got. That was a real statement that when it gets tough, we can pull through.'The win is a significant boost for Welsh rugby, which has faced criticism in recent times. Edwards acknowledged this, saying, 'It's also just that little bit of a boost that Welsh rugby needed.' The victory comes against a Japan team currently ranked 14th in the world, while Wales has moved back up to 12th. The match was marked by a stunning display from Plumtree, who came on as a replacement and made a decisive impact. 'Fair play to Taine, he was brave doing that,' Edwards said, praising his teammate's role in the winning try. Plumtree's reverse pass set up Edwards for the crucial score, and the fly-half was grateful to finish it off. The win is also a personal triumph for Keelan Giles, who came on as a half-time replacement and played a key role in the victory. 'It's a massive day for me and my family,' Giles said, with his family making the trip to Kobe to watch him play. Giles added that the win was a long time coming and a significant moment for Welsh rugby, providing a boost ahead of the autumn and future matches.
