Liverpool have signed 20-year-old Scotland midfielder Kirsty Maclean from Rangers. The Rangers academy product, who made over 100 appearances for the Glasgow club, said it was the 'right time for a new challenge and to take the next step'. Maclean expressed her excitement about joining the club, stating, 'I think the club speaks for itself. It's obviously a massive club with a lot of history and to be able to represent the badge is a really proud moment for me.' As a midfielder, Maclean described her playing style as someone who likes to be on the ball and tries to make things happen, hoping to link up with her new teammates and contribute to scoring goals this season. 'I'm a midfielder who likes to be on the ball and tries to make things happen so hopefully I can link up with the players in front of me and we can try to score lots of goals this season,' she said.
