West Lindsey's popular 'big bin hire' scheme set to be extended. A scheme that hires out large bins in West Lindsey, similar to those used by restaurants or shops, could become a permanent fixture following positive feedback from residents. The trial scheme, which provides residents with access to big bins on wheels, has seen hundreds of bins hired out since its inception.     According to a report by the authority, 95 per cent of customers left four or five-star reviews, indicating a high level of satisfaction with the service. The large black bins, which feature a sticker on the side highlighting prohibited items, have proven to be a hit with residents.     Based on the success of the trial, the report recommended that the scheme becomes permanent. A decision on the proposal will be made on Tuesday. If approved, the 'big bin hire' scheme will continue to provide residents with a convenient and efficient way to dispose of large quantities of waste.
