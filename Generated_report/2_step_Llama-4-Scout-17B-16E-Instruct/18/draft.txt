Rory McIlroy expressed satisfaction with his performance at the Scottish Open, despite a near miss at securing the trophy. McIlroy finished the four rounds with an impressive 13-under par, thanks to 19 birdies throughout the week. 'No frustration; it's been a great week,' he said, reflecting on his game. McIlroy attributed his near miss to a combination of factors, including windy conditions and bumpy greens. 'It was pretty windy and was hard to get the ball super close, but I gave myself plenty of chances coming down the stretch,' the Northern Irishman explained. He also mentioned that some of his putts did not go in, citing mis-reads or poor speed. Despite the setback, McIlroy is optimistic about his game and is looking forward to the upcoming event at Portrush. 'I'm really happy with where my game is; the way I played over the weekend; the shots that I hit, how I controlled my ball flight. Missing the trophy, that's about it,' he said. McIlroy plans to make the most of his momentum, stating, 'I feel like I've gotten out of this week what I wanted. I'm looking forward to getting to Portrush tonight and getting out on to the golf course early tomorrow.'
