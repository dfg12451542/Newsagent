A 57-year-old biker was seriously injured in a single-vehicle crash on a rural road near Hawick on Sunday. The accident occurred on the B6399, just south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm, involving a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike. Emergency services were called to the scene at about 12:00, and the biker was taken to hospital for treatment of serious injuries. The road was shut for approximately eight hours as police attended the scene and appealed for witnesses. The incident is the latest in a series of road accidents in the Borders region, highlighting concerns about rural road safety.
