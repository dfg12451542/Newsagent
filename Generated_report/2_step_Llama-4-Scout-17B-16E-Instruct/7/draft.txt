More arrests have been made in connection with a stabbing incident outside a house in Leicester's Eyres Monsell area. Police have arrested four additional people, bringing the total number of suspects to five, in relation to the stabbing of a man in his 20s on Sturdee Road on Friday evening. According to Det Sgt Adrian Davies, the stabbing occurred shortly before 9:15 pm, when it was still light and warm in the area. 'It was still light at that time and with the weather being very warm there were probably quite a few people who were in the area or in their gardens,' he said. The arrested individuals include a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted murder, two men aged 18 and 29 on suspicion of the same offence, a 38-year-old woman on suspicion of conspiracy to commit murder, and a 25-year-old woman on suspicion of assisting an offender. The 18-year-old man and the 25-year-old woman remain in police custody, while the other three suspects have been released on police bail.The injured man made his way to Lawford Road following the stabbing and was taken to hospital, where he remains in a stable condition. Police believe the suspects and the victim were known to each other. Det Sgt Davies appealed to the public for any information, stating, 'If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us.' The police are conducting house-to-house inquiries and a thorough examination of the scene has been carried out.
