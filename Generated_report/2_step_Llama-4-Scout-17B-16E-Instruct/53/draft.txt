ArtHouse Jersey has announced the successful artists in the second round of its development grants. The Jersey arts charity aims to support local artists in their creative endeavors. Among the selected artists is actor and writer Jack Evans-Rentsch, who is working on a new comedy about remote island politics. He plans to shoot a short film produced by Tiny Speck Productions on location in Jersey with a local cast and crew. The grants have also been awarded to FlashMob band member Henry Matson, who said: 'The support that ArtHouse Jersey provides to local artists is invaluable and ensures that grassroots artists are provided with opportunities they would not otherwise have.' The four-piece rock band aims to record a second EP by the end of the year.Other recipients of the grant include Hanni El Dardiry Osman, who will use the funding to undertake skills training to operate a chainsaw to process timber to make guitars. Filmmaker Sean Frost also received a grant, which will help him build on his skills and create an immersive, cinematic short film called Memoriam. Mr. Frost said: 'ArtHouse Jersey's generous grant will provide the majority of the budget for the film Memoriam, an 8-10 minute short that will be shot in Jersey in the Autumn.' The grants demonstrate ArtHouse Jersey's commitment to nurturing local artistic talent.
