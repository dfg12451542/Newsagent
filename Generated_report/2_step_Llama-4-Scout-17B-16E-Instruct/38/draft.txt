A memorial bench in Whitby dedicated to Sophie Lancaster, a 20-year-old Goth community member who was tragically murdered in 2007, was temporarily relocated to make way for a giant sightseeing wheel. The Sophie Lancaster Foundation, which was established in her memory, expressed shock and disappointment at the move, as they were not consulted or informed about the decision. The bench, located on Whitby's West Cliff, has been a place of reflection and remembrance for many who knew Sophie and the Goth community. According to a spokesperson for the Sophie Lancaster Foundation, 'For Sylvia, Sophie's mother, the bench was a tangible connection to Sophie.' The charity described the move as 'a shock' and 'painful' for those who visit the bench to remember Sophie.North Yorkshire Council, which oversaw the installation of the big wheel, described it as 'a respectful, exciting addition to the town.' Councillor Mark Crane stated that the location was chosen for its stunning views across the harbour and towards the town's famous abbey, and that initial feedback from local businesses has been overwhelmingly positive. The memorial bench will be restored to its original location on August 4. In the meantime, the Sophie Lancaster Foundation will hold a small ceremony to rededicate the bench at Tomorrow's Ghosts Festival on November 1. The charity expressed their gratitude for the community's response and looks forward to the bench's return to its home on West Cliff. Whitby mayor, councillor Sandra Turner, described the incident as 'a serious oversight' and confirmed that North Yorkshire Council has apologized and assured that it will not happen again.
