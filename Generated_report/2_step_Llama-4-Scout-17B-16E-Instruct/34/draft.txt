The Open 2025: Huge crowds descend on Royal Portrush for Rory McIlroy's practice round. Golf fans descended in great numbers on Monday morning to catch Rory McIlroy's first practice round at Royal Portrush ahead of this week's Open. The world number two, who won his Open title at Hoylake in 2014, delighted the sizeable galleries on Monday morning, holing a birdie putt on the 18th green before signing autographs for 15 minutes. McIlroy is due to speak to the media at 14:00 BST on Monday, followed by 2019 champion Shane Lowry at 15:00 BST.
