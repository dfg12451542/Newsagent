MasterChef presenter John Torode has confirmed that he is the subject of an allegation of using racist language, as part of an inquiry into separate allegations against co-host Gregg Wallace. In an Instagram post on Monday, Torode stated that the allegation claims he made the remarks in 2018 or 2019 and that he had apologized immediately afterwards. However, Torode expressed that he has 'absolutely no recollection' of the incident and 'do not believe that it happened.' Torode emphasized that he has always considered racial language to be 'wholly unacceptable in any environment.' He added, 'I'm shocked and saddened by the allegation as I would never wish to cause anyone any offence.' According to Torode, the person he was speaking with did not believe the remark was intended in a malicious way and acknowledged his immediate apology.This allegation is part of a broader inquiry that confirmed two separate allegations involving other individuals, one of which also involved the use of racist language. Torode's statement comes as a response to the allegations, showcasing his commitment to transparency and his stance against racial language.
