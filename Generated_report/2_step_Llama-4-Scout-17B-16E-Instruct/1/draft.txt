A memorial running race is set to take place in Colchester to honour the life of beloved athlete Arthur Whiston, who died in a road crash last year. On July 5, 2024, Mr. Whiston, aged 73, was cycling home when he was involved in an accident on Argents Lane close to West Bergholt in Essex. To celebrate his life, Colchester Harriers AC, Mr. Whiston's running club, has organised a memorial event which is due to start at the garrison athletics track in the city at 09:30 BST on Sunday. Lydia Cunningham, a friend of Mr. Whiston, remembered him as a 'very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular.' She said, 'Arthur was incredibly loved by the community.' The event aims to bring the community together to pay tribute to his life and legacy.The money raised from the event is set to be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity which Mr. Whiston supported. Runners can still turn up on the day of the event and register to take part. The event is expected to be a fitting tribute to Mr. Whiston's life and passion for running.
