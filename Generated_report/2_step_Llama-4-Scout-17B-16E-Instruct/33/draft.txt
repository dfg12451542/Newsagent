A dramatic rescue unfolded at Guernsey's Port Grat beach on Friday afternoon, as a 77-year-old man was saved from drowning by his wife and a fellow swimmer. Chris Meinke, 77, had been swimming with his wife, Jacky, 67, when he suddenly collapsed in the water. In a swift response, Jacky turned around to find her husband floating face down, 'effectively dead,' she later described. Without hesitation, Jacky, who is first-aid trained, grabbed her husband's head out of the water and began administering back blows, which helped him splutter and regain consciousness. Fellow swimmer Rosie Mclauchlan assisted in pulling Mr. Meinke back to the beach, where they put him in the recovery position until paramedics arrived.Mr. Meinke was taken to the hospital for further treatment and spent the night there. Although the exact cause of his collapse remains a mystery, his wife believes it may have been related to vertigo. 'It was very scary, and I'm not a panicky person, but I'm just relieved he's OK,' Jacky Meinke said. The couple has expressed gratitude to Rosie Mclauchlan and passers-by who helped during the ordeal, including those who fetched a defibrillator and provided a warm blanket.The incident serves as a stark reminder of the importance of water safety, particularly during the summer months when drowning incidents tend to peak. According to the Water Incident Database, the majority of drownings over the last three years have occurred in July, predominantly involving young males. Fortunately, thanks to the quick thinking and actions of Jacky Meinke and Rosie Mclauchlan, Chris Meinke is now on the road to recovery.
