A dramatic rescue effort has paid off in Corby, Northamptonshire, where a dog named Jimmy was saved after being stuck down a well for 11 days. Dorota Gruszczynska, Jimmy's owner, had been searching for her pet extensively, creating posters, posting online, and distributing them throughout the local area. 'We searched the area so many times and nothing, we created posters, put them online and sent them around the local area,' she said. The ordeal began when Jimmy went missing, and Gruszczynska feared the worst, assuming he had been hit by a car. 'We thought he had been hit by a car and we would never see him again,' she added.However, on July 2, Gruszczynska received a message from a firefighter saying they thought they had found her dog. Northamptonshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed that a Corby crew was attending a nearby fire when they heard the sound of a dog barking coming from a well on Stephenson Way. Firefighters used a reach pole and lines to lift cylinders off Jimmy, allowing him to be lifted to safety. Gruszczynska was overjoyed to be reunited with her pet, expressing her gratitude to everyone involved in the rescue, including a drone company that did extensive work pro bono. 'We're so grateful for everyone who helped, including the drone company. They did a huge amount of work, purely out of their heart,' she said.Miraculously, Jimmy recovered surprisingly well from his ordeal, with Gruszczynska attributing his survival to the presence of water in the well. 'He's back on his feet, he's recovered surprisingly well, we don't know how he survived, there must have been water in the well,' she said. After being taken to the vet, Jimmy was found to be hungry but not dehydrated. The successful rescue is a testament to the power of community support and the dedication of emergency services.
