Crystal Palace has unveiled its new home kit for the 2025-26 season, amid a busy weekend for the Eagles. The team released the new jersey on Monday, July 14, 2025, and it was worn during their pre-season fixtures, which kicked off with a 1-0 win against Millwall on Saturday. The launch featured several players, including Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, Maxence Lacroix, Eddie Nketiah, Adam Wharton, Justin Devenny, and Romain Esse, as well as Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur from Palace Women. New signing Walter Benitez also modelled the club's light green goalkeeper home jersey for the first time. The kit's release comes as the team prepares for the upcoming season, both domestically and in European competitions.
