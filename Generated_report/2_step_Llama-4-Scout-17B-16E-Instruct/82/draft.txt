The Scottish League 2 transfer window is in full swing, with several clubs making significant moves in the summer of 2025. Here is a club-by-club guide to the ins and outs in Scotland's fourth tier.Annan Athletic have made some key signings, including James Hilton, midfielder from Dumbarton, Kyle Fleming, midfielder from Annan Athletic, and Sam Campbell, defender from East Stirlingshire. On the other hand, Martin Rennie, forward, has left for Forfar Athletic, while Lee Hamilton, defender, has joined Stirling Albion. Dom Docherty, midfielder, has also left for Dumbarton.Dumbarton have brought in Zach Balfour, goalkeeper from Queen's Park, Joel Mumbongo, forward from Dumbarton, Euan Deveney, defender from Stirling Albion, and Gio Clarke, goalkeeper from The Modern Day GK Academy. However, Mackenzie Strachan, midfielder, has left for Cove Rangers, and Kyle Fleming, midfielder, has joined Annan Athletic.East Kilbride have signed Jannik Wanner, forward from SKU Amstetten, Leighton McIntosh, forward from Al Ahli, Scott Honeyman, forward from Falkirk, and Shay Kelly, goalkeeper from St Mirren on a permanent deal. They have also brought in Morgyn Neill, defender from Alloa Athletic, Chris Johnston, forward from Stranraer, and Ally Roy, forward from Stirling Albion. Other notable transfers include Clyde's signing of Dom Docherty, midfielder from Annan Athletic, and the departure of Michael Ruth, forward, to Queen's Park. Elgin City have signed Lewis Martin, defender from Brechin City, and Chris Scott from Jeanfield Swifts.Stirling Albion have signed Lee Hamilton, defender from Annan Athletic, and Russell McLean, forward from Forfar Athletic, while Stranraer have brought in Josh O'Connor, forward from Crusaders, and James Lyon, midfielder from Kelty Hearts.
