The 22nd Island Games opened in the Orkney Islands with a grand ceremony, attended by Princess Anne, who officially opened the event. Channel Island athletes described meeting the Princess Royal as a 'once in a lifetime' experience. Alderney runner Benjamin Mutua and his coach, Julie Longden, had the opportunity to speak with Princess Anne about their involvement in the games. Ms. Longden described the encounter as 'amazing,' noting that Princess Anne's love of sport made for a great conversation. 'I have met royalty before but because of her love of sport it was so good to talk to her,' Ms. Longden said. The ceremony was hosted by broadcaster Lorraine Kelly, who expressed her pride in the way the event had been organized and her fondness for the Orkney Islands. Kelly also jokingly expressed her support for the small team from Sark, saying 'Beautiful Sark, amazing and fantastic, I love there's only two of them, and they made more noise than anyone else, it's just great so I'm rooting for them.' Spectators, including Lucy and Nick Le Boutillier, who traveled from Jersey to support their daughter Tilly in the gymnastics, were thrilled to be part of the event. The Le Boutilliers described the experience as wonderful, with Mrs. Le Boutillier saying 'We're her biggest supporters so we're standing firmly on the sidelines to cheer on team Jersey and Tilly.' The games have brought a great buzz to the Orkney Islands, with local resident Doreen Manson, who performed at the opening ceremony with her exercise group, expressing her excitement about the event. 'It's just something that we've never seen before, and all the different athletes coming in from different countries, it's just great, I think, really exciting,' she said.
