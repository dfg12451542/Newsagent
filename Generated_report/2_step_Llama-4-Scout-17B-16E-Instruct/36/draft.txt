A 25-year-old man, Brandon Booth, has been charged with murder over a street incident in Aberdeen. Booth appeared before Aberdeen Sheriff Court, where he made no plea and was remanded in custody. The incident has left a family devastated, with relatives of the victim, Dylan Geddes, describing him as a 'much-loved son, grandson, brother and nephew.' 'The family is absolutely devastated by what has happened,' they said. 'He will be sorely missed every day by all who knew him.' The community is in shock, and the court proceedings will continue as the case unfolds.
