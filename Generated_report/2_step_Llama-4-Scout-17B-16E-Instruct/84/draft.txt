A family from Hereford who travelled 800 miles in their campervan to cheer on England's Lionesses in the UEFA Women's Euros are thrilled after the team's 6-1 victory over Wales. The Morris family, consisting of Kevin, Kelly, and their children Amelia and Ethan, drove to Switzerland to watch the match at the St Gallen stadium, where they witnessed an outstanding performance by the Lionesses. 'As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,' said Kevin Morris, who was in the stadium with his family. The family, who are staying in their campervan at Arbon near Lake Constance, said the atmosphere was 'great' and that it was 'worth it' despite a late night getting to and from the stadium. England's Lionesses are the reigning European champions, having won the title in 2022, and the Morris family is hopeful they will repeat their success. 'I think we can do it again,' said Mr Morris, who is looking forward to watching the team take on Sweden in the quarter-final on Thursday. The family will drive their campervan back to Zurich for the match, which is a repeat of the Euro 2022 semi-final. However, their journey back was not without its challenges, as they faced delays and had to change trains. 'It was just busy coming out of the stadium... We changed for our last train and then just stopped in the middle of nowhere,' said Mr Morris. A bus eventually arrived to take them to their destination, but not before a lengthy delay. Despite this, the family's spirits remain high, and they are excited to see what the rest of the tournament holds for the Lionesses.
