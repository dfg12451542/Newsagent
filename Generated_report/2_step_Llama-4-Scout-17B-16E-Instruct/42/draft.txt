Wafcon 2024: Nigeria and Algeria reach quarter-finals after draw. Nigeria's Super Falcons secured a quarter-final spot against Zambia at the 2024 Women's Africa Cup of Nations after a 0-0 draw with Algeria, clinching top spot in Group B. The result also saw Algeria progress to the last eight, where they will face the second-placed finisher in Group C. In other matches, Botswana boosted their chances of advancing as one of the two best-ranked third-placed sides with a 2-1 comeback victory over Tunisia. Yesmin Khanchouch put Tunisia ahead in the 12th minute, but Lesego Radiakanyo equalized for Botswana midway through the second half. The match ended dramatically with Botswana scoring a winner in the fifth minute of stoppage time through Gaonyadiwe Ontlametse.The results have assured defending champions South Africa and Mali of a place in the quarter-finals before Group C concludes on Monday. South Africa will face Mali in Oujda, while Ghana takes on Tanzania in Berkane, with both needing a win to progress. Nigeria's match against Algeria was marked by a late surge for a winner, with Echegini's curling effort going just wide in the 89th minute.
