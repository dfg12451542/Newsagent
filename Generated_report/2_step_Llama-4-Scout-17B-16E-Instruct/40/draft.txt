Trainer Dave Coldwell has expressed his disapproval of Manny Pacquiao's upcoming comeback fight against Barrios, stating he won't be watching the highly anticipated bout. 'I won't be watching Manny Pacquiao's comeback fight on Saturday,' Coldwell said. 'It feels like a shortcut. A nod to nostalgia rather than merit, and that's unfair to fighters who are working their way up properly.' Pacquiao, an eight-division world champion and global icon, is set to return to the ring after a period of retirement. However, Coldwell believes that Pacquiao's direct entry into a high-profile fight sets a strange precedent and undermines the hard work of other fighters. 'I realise that might sound harsh given he is one of the sport's all-time greats,' Coldwell added. 'I have immense respect for what he achieved in his prime. He's a global icon, a true legend.'The news of Pacquiao's return shocked many in the boxing community, particularly given that he is skipping the usual tune-up fights and going straight into a high-stakes match. 'That's the part that really shocked me when I heard of Pacquiao's return,' Coldwell said. 'Usually, a fighter coming back from retirement eases into the sport with tune-up fights.' Coldwell also mentioned a brief exchange with Mauricio Sulaiman, the WBC president, on social media, where he expressed his doubts about Pacquiao's comeback fight.
