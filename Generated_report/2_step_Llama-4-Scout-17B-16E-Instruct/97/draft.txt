The Wurzels were forced to end their gig abruptly at the Party at the Palace stage in Wells, Somerset, on Saturday, as band members Pete Budd and Tommy Banner fell ill due to sun and heat exhaustion. The incident occurred as temperatures peaked at 31C, with the band performing under the direct, unobstructed rays of a strong sun. 'Both Pete and I appear to have fully recovered from sun and heat exhaustion,' said Tommy Banner, the band's accordionist. 'Up on stage there was no air moving and we were facing straight into the direct, unobstructed rays of a very strong sun.' Pete Budd, who turns 85 on July 18, had to leave the stage before the end of the set, with Banner noticing his absence and concern for his well-being. 'I was about to announce the last song and Pete wasn’t beside me where he normally is. He was in a bad way,' Banner recalled. Both band members were supported by medical staff when they left the stage, with a nurse arriving to check them over. Banner was advised not to drive and given exercises to do.The Wurzels, known for their hit song 'I Am a Cider Drinker,' have been a staple of British music for decades. While the band has experienced a range of performances over the years, this incident highlights the challenges of performing in extreme weather conditions. Fortunately, both Budd and Banner appear to have made a full recovery and will likely continue to entertain fans with their unique blend of music.
