Zoe Tucker, an amateur running enthusiast from Sherborne in Dorset, has won a gold medal in the sprint category at the duathlon world championships. The competition, which took place last month in Spain, saw Ms Tucker claim victory in a stunning upset, just months after taking up the sport. In the event, Ms Tucker became world champion in the sprint distance, completing a 5km run, a 19.7km bike ride, and a 2.5km run to finish. She not only won her category but also emerged as the fastest British woman overall in all age groups. Describing her experience as 'an absolute honour,' Ms Tucker said she had 'absolutely never in my wildest dreams' expected to compete - let alone win - at the world championships. The scale of the event, including the opening ceremony and parade of nations, was an 'incredible experience,' she added. As she crossed the finish line, Ms Tucker recalled feeling an overwhelming sense of excitement, saying 'just coming round the track for the last lap, knowing I was probably going to get over that line first was incredible.' With the next world championships set to take place in Abu Dhabi next year, Ms Tucker aims to develop a training plan and secure an indoor training bike to sustain her competing at the highest level. Ms Tucker's achievement serves as an inspiration to others, and she hopes to encourage people to grab opportunities as they come, saying 'there are so many activities and opportunities and events - I just think if an opportunity comes your way, just grab it and enjoy it along the way.'
