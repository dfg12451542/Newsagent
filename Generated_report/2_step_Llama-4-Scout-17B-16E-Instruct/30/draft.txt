England's Lottie Woad came close to winning her second consecutive title at the Evian Championship, but ultimately finished as runner-up as Australian Grace Kim claimed her first major title. Kim triumphed in a play-off against Atthaya Thitikul, holing a 12-foot eagle putt on the second extra playing of the 18th hole to secure the win. Woad, who won last week's Irish Open, posted a sensational seven-under 64 to finish on 13 under par and set the clubhouse target. However, she narrowly missed out on a chance to force a play-off with Thitikul, racing a 10-foot birdie putt past the hole. When Thitikul birdied the 17th to get to 14 under, Woad joked with her caddie: 'Is there even any point, she's not making six.'Despite missing out on the win, Woad's performance earned her two points for finishing in the top 25 in a major, taking her total to 20 points on the LPGA's Elite Amateur Pathway. This achievement could earn her a spot on the LPGA Tour, although she can also defer. 'It has always been in my dreams to play on the LPGA Tour and I'm grateful for the opportunities of the programme,' Woad said. She will discuss her options with her family and coaches.Kim's win marked a significant milestone in her career, claiming her first major title. She also eagled the 18th to get into the play-off after Thitikul missed an eight-foot birdie putt that would have seen her win a maiden major. The two players had birdies when they first replayed the 18th, before Kim again got down in three shots to take the title when they played it again.
