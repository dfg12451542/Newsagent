A devastating fire has caused major damage to the landmark Worm's Head Hotel in Rhossili, Gower. The blaze broke out on Monday morning at 00:51 BST, prompting a response from the Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service. The fire, which has not yet been fully investigated, significantly damaged the property, particularly within the accommodation block and its entire roof space.The hotel's owners expressed their heartbreak and devastation in a social media post, stating, 'It is particularly heartbreaking and soul destroying for me and family to see 25 years of hard work destroyed in less than 30 mins.' They vowed to strive to come back stronger, despite the immense challenge ahead.Fortunately, no casualties were reported, and all occupants were accounted for. The fire service successfully extinguished the blaze by around 05:30, although crews continued to monitor the scene until 09:32. A 25m span of the roof collapsed, leaving blackened beams and an exposed chimney stack visible.The hotel's regular visitors were left shocked and emotional by the sight of the damaged hotel. Sian Nichol, who has visited the hotel every summer, described it as 'absolutely shocking.' Beverly, another regular, said she felt emotional when she saw the hotel's state, having spent many New Year's celebrations there. The hotel management confirmed that the bar and hotel would remain closed pending future updates, expressing gratitude for the well-wishes and confirming everyone's safety.
