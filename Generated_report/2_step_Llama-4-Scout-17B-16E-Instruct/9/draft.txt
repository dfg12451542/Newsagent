Toprak Razgatlioglu has taken the lead of the World Superbike Championship after securing a treble win at Donington Park. The BMW rider dominated the weekend, claiming victories in Saturday's opening feature race, the Superpole sprint race, and Sunday's second feature race. Razgatlioglu had entered the weekend just one point behind title rival Nicolo Bulega, but his strong performance has seen him move four points clear at the top of the standings. In Sunday's feature race, Razgatlioglu crossed the line ahead of Ducati rider Bulega, with Alvaro Bautista completing the podium. The Turkish rider's impressive weekend has solidified his position as a top contender for the championship. Meanwhile, six-time champion Jonathan Rea, from Northern Ireland, managed to secure a point in 15th place, after previously finishing fifth in Saturday's race - his best finish of the season so far.
