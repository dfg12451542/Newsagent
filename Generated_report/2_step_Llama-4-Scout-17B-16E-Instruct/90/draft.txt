A fire that destroyed facilities at a west Belfast GAA club was started deliberately, police have said. The blaze occurred at around 18:20 BST on Saturday, causing extensive damage to the club. 'It's absolutely unacceptable that a community organisation should be targeted in this way,' said Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) Inspector Dalton. The fire has been described as a 'blatant act of vandalism' by Belfast city councillor Arder Carson, who added that the club was 'in the heart' of preparing for its children's summer camps and the start of a new championship season. 'It definitely fills you with despair and some anger that a club that does so much for the local community... has been subject to such an attack,' Carson said. The incident has sparked outrage, with Carson also condemning the reckless nature of the fire, which was set outdoors during a period of dry weather. Police are appealing for anyone with information to contact them.
