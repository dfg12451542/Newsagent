A car fire rapidly escalated into a significant moorland blaze on the A30 in Cornwall today, prompting a response from multiple fire crews. The incident occurred on the eastbound lane near Temple Tor on Bodmin Moor, quickly spreading from the vehicle to the surrounding vegetation. Firefighters worked diligently to contain the flames and prevent further expansion of the fire across the moorland.nnInitial reports indicate the fire originated within a vehicle, but the cause remains under investigation. While there have been no reports of injuries, the blaze caused disruption to traffic on the A30 as emergency services worked at the scene. Smoke could be seen for miles, raising concerns about air quality in the surrounding areas.nnFire crews from Launceston and Bodmin were dispatched to tackle the blaze, employing various techniques to extinguish the flames and create fire breaks to isolate the burning areas.  The challenging terrain of Bodmin Moor presented additional difficulties for firefighters.nnCornwall Fire & Rescue Service confirmed the location of the incident and mobilized resources accordingly. Further updates will be provided as the situation develops and the investigation progresses. Authorities are advising motorists to exercise caution when travelling on the A30 in the vicinity of Bodmin Moor and to be aware of potential smoke hazards.
