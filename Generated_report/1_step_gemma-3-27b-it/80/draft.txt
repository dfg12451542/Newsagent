Scottish football clubs are actively reshaping their squads as the summer transfer window heats up, with Celtic, Rangers, Hearts, and St Johnstone all featuring prominently in the latest gossip. Competition for key targets is intensifying, and several deals are nearing completion, though bureaucratic hurdles remain for some. nnRangers’ pursuit of Crystal Palace winger Jesurun Rak-Sakyi has taken a new twist, with a Premier League club now entering the fray, according to the Scottish Sun. Rak-Sakyi, valued around £10 million by Palace, has also drawn interest from Sheffield United and Southampton, signaling a potentially competitive bidding war for the promising young player. nnCeltic manager Brendan Rodgers has publicly acknowledged the need for attacking reinforcements, stating it's “clear that we need to improve our frontline.” This declaration hints at further activity in the transfer market for the Hoops as they prepare for their title defense and Champions League commitments. nnMeanwhile, Hearts are finalizing moves for two new signings, Sabah Kerjota and Pierre Landry Kabore, but are awaiting the completion of visa paperwork before officially announcing the deals. Kerjota joins from Italian side Sambendetese, while Kabore is arriving from Narva Trans, adding international flair to the Tynecastle Park squad. nnAt the other end of the spectrum, St Johnstone are set to lose full-back Andre Raymond, who is reportedly on the verge of completing a transfer to a club in Finland, as reported by The Courier. These developments highlight a busy period of player movement across Scottish football, as clubs strive to strengthen their rosters ahead of the new season.
