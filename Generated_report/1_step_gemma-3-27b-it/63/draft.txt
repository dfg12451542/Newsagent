Rhossili, Wales – A devastating fire ripped through the Worm’s Head Hotel in the early hours of Monday morning, causing major damage to the landmark establishment. Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service responded to the blaze at 00:51 BST, finding the property already significantly affected. nnThe fire originated within the hotel’s accommodation block, rapidly spreading to encompass the entire roof space, according to the fire service. While the cause of the fire remains under investigation, crews worked diligently to extinguish the flames, finally succeeding around 05:30. Monitoring continued for several hours afterward, with crews fully departing the scene at 09:32. Thankfully, no casualties were reported, and all occupants were accounted for.nnThe extent of the damage is considerable, with a 25-meter span of the roof collapsing, leaving blackened beams and an exposed chimney stack.  The impact on the local community is palpable, as the Worm’s Head Hotel has long been a cherished destination. “It is particularly heartbreaking and soul destroying for me and family to see 25 years of hard work destroyed in less than 30 mins,” the owners shared in a social media post, reflecting the immense personal loss. nnWitnesses expressed shock and sadness at the scene. “We used to come every summer. It’s absolutely shocking to see it like this,” said Sian Nichol, a frequent visitor. Beverly, who has celebrated New Year’s Eve at the hotel for several years, added, “We’ve been coming here a few years, spent many a fantastic new year here, so to see it like this, I felt quite emotional when we turned the corner.” Even those hoping to enjoy the hotel’s amenities were left disappointed, with Gareth commenting, “The vision I had today was to sit outside there with a nice meal and lager together.”nnHotel management issued a statement confirming everyone’s safety and expressing gratitude for the outpouring of well wishes.  However, the hotel and its bar will remain closed indefinitely “pending future updates.”  The owners concluded with a message of resilience, stating, “Not sure how we come back from this at all let alone in the near term future but we will strive to come back stronger.” South Wales Police also attended the incident, assisting with securing the area.
