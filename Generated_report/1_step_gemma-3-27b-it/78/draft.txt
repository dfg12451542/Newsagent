More than £8,000 has been raised in just 48 hours for the family of 16-year-old Daniel Drewitt, who died after going missing in a lake at Sutton Park, Sutton Coldfield, on Thursday evening. An online fundraising page, established with the permission of Daniel's parents, quickly surpassed its original goal and continues to gather donations as the community rallies to offer support.nnThe GoFundMe campaign was initially aiming to raise £1,600 to assist the family during what organisers described as a “difficult time.” However, fuelled by an outpouring of grief and generosity, the total swiftly exceeded expectations, reaching over £8,000 by Sunday. Organisers have since revised the target to £11,000 to provide further assistance.nnDaniel was discovered in a pool at Sutton Park after emergency services were called to the scene shortly after 6 PM on Thursday. Paramedics confirmed that Daniel had died before his body could be recovered from the water. Colmers School and Sixth Form College, where Daniel was a student, described him as a “confident sportsman” deeply loved by both staff and pupils.nnAccording to updates posted on the fundraising page, Daniel’s mother is “overwhelmed” by the volume of support and donations received from the public. Organiser Helena Swallow expressed the family’s gratitude, stating, “The family are so very grateful, thank you so much.”nnThe fundraising effort underscores the profound impact of Daniel’s death on the Sutton Coldfield community, offering a measure of comfort to his grieving family as they navigate this tragic loss. Donations can still be made through the GoFundMe page as the community continues to demonstrate its solidarity.
