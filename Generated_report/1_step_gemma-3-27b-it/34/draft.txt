Huge crowds have descended on Royal Portrush, Northern Ireland, on Monday morning as Rory McIlroy began his practice rounds ahead of The Open Championship, which commences on July 17th. Golf enthusiasts flocked to the course to witness the world number two prepare for the tournament, eager to support their hometown hero.nnMcIlroy, who secured his Open title at Hoylake in 2014, treated the large galleries to a promising display, including a successful birdie putt on the 18th green. Following his practice session, he generously spent 15 minutes signing autographs for delighted fans, demonstrating his appreciation for the strong local support. This marks McIlroy’s first appearance at Royal Portrush since achieving a career milestone – winning the Green Jacket – a victory heralded by many as cementing his status as Northern Ireland’s greatest ever sportsperson.nnThe Open Championship holds significant history, with Royal Portrush previously hosting the event in 1951, 1964, 1978, 1989, 1999 and 2019. The 2019 championship was particularly noteworthy, as it marked the return of The Open to Northern Ireland after a 68-year absence, generating considerable excitement and boosting local tourism.nnWith anticipation building for the tournament, McIlroy is scheduled to address the media at 14:00 BST on Monday. He will be followed by the 2019 Open champion, Shane Lowry, who is slated to speak at 15:00 BST. Both players are expected to share insights into their preparations and outlook for the competition.nnThe Open Championship at Royal Portrush promises to be a captivating event, drawing international attention and showcasing the beauty of Northern Ireland's coastline. As the tournament nears, all eyes will be on McIlroy as he aims to secure his second Open title in front of a supportive home crowd.
