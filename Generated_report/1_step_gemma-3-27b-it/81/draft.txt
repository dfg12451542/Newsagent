Orkney, Scotland – The 20th Island Games officially commenced on Saturday with an opening ceremony presided over by Princess Anne, drawing athletes from 24 island communities. The event, hosted by Orkney for the first time, was met with enthusiastic support from both participants and spectators, with many describing encounters with the Princess Royal as a highlight. Princess Anne, a lifelong athlete herself with a history in hockey, tennis, and various track and field events, took time to engage with competitors, demonstrating a keen interest in their experiences.nnChannel Island athletes expressed their delight at meeting Princess Anne, recognizing the significance of the encounter. “The encounter was “amazing”,” said Ms. Longden, a coach accompanying Alderney runner Benjamin Mutua. Ms. Longden further noted, “I have met royalty before but because of her love of sport it was so good to talk to her.” The arrival of the games has generated excitement across the Orkney Islands. While a long-time resident Doreen Manson commented on the atmosphere, stating, “It’s just something that we’ve never seen before, and all the different athletes coming in from different countries, it’s just great, I think, really exciting.”nnSpectators also shared their positive experiences, highlighting the community spirit surrounding the games. Lucy and Nick Le Boutillier, who travelled from Jersey to cheer on their daughter Tilly, a gymnast competing in the event, recounted their dedication to securing a spot at the opening ceremony, waiting in line for two and a half hours.  Their commitment reflects the strong support system surrounding the athletes. Lorraine Kelly, also in attendance, spoke of her emotional connection to Orkney and pride in the event's organization.nnKelly also expressed strong support for the smaller islands’ teams. “Beautiful Sark, amazing and fantastic, I love there’s only two of them, and they made more noise than anyone else, it’s just great so I’m rooting for them,” she said. As the week-long competition unfolds, organizers and residents alike express hope that the Island Games will leave a lasting positive impact on Orkney, fostering a sense of community and celebrating athletic achievement.
