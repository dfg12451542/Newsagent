Sherborne resident Zoe Tucker has achieved global success, winning gold at the duathlon world championships in Spain last month. The amateur athlete, who only recently took up the sport, secured victory in the sprint category, completing a challenging course consisting of a 5km run, a 19.7km bike ride, and a final 2.5km run. Tucker’s win marks a remarkable achievement for the Dorset athlete, establishing her as the fastest British woman overall across all age groups.nnDescribing the experience as “an absolute honour,” Tucker spoke of the overwhelming atmosphere of the championships. “The scale of the event, the opening ceremony, a parade of nations, walking with GB kit and flag - it was an incredible experience,” she said. Her triumph culminated in an emotional final lap. “Just coming round the track for the last lap, knowing I was probably going to get over that line first was incredible,” Tucker recounted. nnTucker admitted her success was beyond her expectations. “I’d absolutely never in my wildest dreams” anticipated competing, let alone winning, at a world championship level. She hopes her story will inspire others to embrace new opportunities. “I’m hopefully inspiring them a bit, but it’s been hard,” she stated, acknowledging the difficulties of balancing training with other commitments.nnLooking ahead, Tucker has her sights set on the next world championships in Abu Dhabi. She plans to develop a comprehensive training plan, including investing in an indoor training bike to maintain peak performance. Her advice to aspiring athletes and those considering taking up a new challenge is simple: “There are so many activities and opportunities and events - I just think if an opportunity comes your way, just grab it and enjoy it along the way.”
