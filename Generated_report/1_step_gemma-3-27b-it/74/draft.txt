Newcastle United is reportedly pursuing a record-breaking deal for Eintracht Frankfurt striker Hugo Ekitike, signaling their ambition ahead of their return to the Champions League. The club has made an initial move for the French forward, aiming to strengthen their attacking lineup following a fifth-place finish in the Premier League last season.nnEkitike, who has established himself among Europe’s top young goalscorers, netting 10 or more goals in top five leagues, presents an exciting prospect for Newcastle manager Eddie Howe.  He joins an elite group of under-23 talents including Benjamin Sesko and Bradley Barcola, demonstrating his rising prominence in European football.nnSources indicate that securing Ekitike’s signature could exceed £70 million, making it a potentially club-record transfer. The substantial investment reflects Newcastle’s intent to compete at the highest level, bolstering their squad with proven talent capable of making an immediate impact in both domestic and European competitions.nnDespite the strong interest in Ekitike, Newcastle have firmly dismissed any speculation surrounding the potential sale of their current top scorer, Alexander Isak.  The Swedish striker has attracted attention from several European clubs, including Liverpool, but BBC Sport reports the club has no intention of letting him leave, highlighting his importance to their future plans. This suggests Newcastle are preparing to build a potent attacking force, rather than dismantle their existing one.
