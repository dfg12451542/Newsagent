A biker is currently hospitalised with serious injuries following a single-vehicle crash on a rural road in the Scottish Borders. The incident occurred on the B6399 near Hawick at approximately 12:00 on Sunday, prompting a significant emergency response and an eight-hour road closure.nnEmergency services were alerted to the crash and swiftly attended the scene. The accident took place just south of the access road leading to the Pines Burn wind farm. Initial investigations indicate the motorbike involved was a white and blue Suzuki GSXR. The B6399 is known locally as a popular route for motorcyclists, particularly during the warmer months, due to its winding stretches and scenic views.nnPolice confirmed that a 57-year-old man was transported to hospital for treatment of serious injuries sustained in the crash. Authorities have launched an investigation to determine the cause of the accident and are appealing for any witnesses who may have been travelling in the area at the time to come forward with information.nnThe road was fully reopened to traffic around 8:00 PM on Sunday evening, following a thorough investigation by accident reconstruction teams. While the Borders region generally experiences lower traffic volumes on rural roads, this incident underscores the vulnerability of motorcyclists and the importance of road safety, particularly on challenging bends and undulating terrain.
