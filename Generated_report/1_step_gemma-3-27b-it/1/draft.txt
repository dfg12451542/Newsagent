A memorial running race is set to take place in Colchester this Sunday to honour the life of Arthur Whiston, a beloved local athlete who tragically died in a road crash on July 5, 2024. The event, organised by Colchester Harriers AC – Mr Whiston’s running club – will commence at 09:30 BST at the garrison athletics track. Organisers hope the race will serve as a fitting tribute to a man deeply valued by the community.nnThe anniversary event aims to celebrate Whiston’s life and passion for running. Participants of all abilities are welcome to join, and runners can still register on the day. The route has been designed to be accessible, encouraging broad participation while reflecting the distances Mr. Whiston frequently ran himself. The event is expected to draw a large crowd, with many eager to pay their respects and share memories of the popular runner.nn“Arthur was incredibly loved by the community,” said Lydia Cunningham, a friend and fellow runner. His passing has left a significant void, not only within the Colchester Harriers but also amongst those who regularly saw him out running and cycling in the local area. nnCunningham further described Mr. Whiston as a “very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular”.  He was known for his encouragement of other runners and his unwavering positive spirit.  Beyond his athletic pursuits, Whiston was a dedicated supporter of the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity that will benefit from the funds raised during the memorial race.nnThe money collected from the event will be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, a charity Mr. Whiston actively supported. The Trust conducts important work in conservation, including the annual Somerset State Nature report which analyses Essex’s wildlife.  This act of giving reflects Whiston’s commitment to both his personal passions and the preservation of the natural world around him.
