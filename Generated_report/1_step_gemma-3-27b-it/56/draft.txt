An ultramarathon runner was airlifted to safety after collapsing from heat exhaustion during a grueling 55-mile mountain race in the Lake District on Saturday. The incident occurred in Grisedale Valley, prompting a large-scale rescue operation involving Patterdale Mountain Rescue Team (PMRT), North West Ambulance Service, and the coastguard. nnPatterdale Mountain Rescue Team received the call around 16:00 BST after the runner exhibited symptoms of severe heat distress, including paleness, vomiting, and loss of consciousness. Recognizing the challenging terrain and the runner’s condition, authorities swiftly mobilized a coordinated response. nnThe coastguard dispatched a helicopter to winch the man to safety, transporting him to the King George playing field in Patterdale.  There, he was immediately assessed by a team doctor before being transferred to a hospital for further medical evaluation. The operation highlighted the risks associated with endurance events in challenging environments.nnThe entire rescue operation took approximately two and a half hours and required the efforts of 11 PMRT members, alongside the coastguard personnel.  While the runner’s current condition remains undisclosed, the swift and efficient response undoubtedly played a crucial role in ensuring his safe evacuation and access to medical attention.
