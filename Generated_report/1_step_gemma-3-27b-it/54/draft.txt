A 71-year-old motorcyclist died in a six-vehicle collision near Loch Lomond on Saturday, July 13th, 2025. The crash, involving multiple cars, occurred at approximately 11:30 BST and resulted in the immediate closure of the affected stretch of road for over six hours while police conducted investigations.nnPolice Scotland confirmed the victim died at the scene. According to authorities, the drivers of the other vehicles involved escaped without injury. The incident involved a complex scene, with several vehicles requiring recovery.  The closure caused significant disruption, and authorities initially estimated the road would not reopen until at least 02:30 BST.nnSergeant Kenny Malaney of Police Scotland stated, “Sadly, the male motorcyclist died and our thoughts are with his family and friends.”  He further added, “Our inquiries are continuing to establish the full circumstances of what happened.”nnPolice are appealing for information and dashcam footage from anyone who may have witnessed the accident or was traveling in the area at the time.  “The road would have been busy at the time and I would ask anyone who may have witnessed the crash, or who may have dashcam footage to contact us,” Sgt. Malaney urged. Authorities are hoping to piece together a complete picture of events through witness testimony and available video evidence.
