Six abandoned kittens have found loving homes after being rescued from a rubbish dump in Eyemouth, Scotland, and nursed back to health by Borders Pet Rescue. The four-week-old kittens were discovered at the Eyemouth Recycling Centre on May 27th, suffering from severe malnutrition, flea and worm infestations, and painful eye infections, according to centre manager Kim McNeill. The heartbreaking scene prompted a rescue effort that would ultimately lead to a heartwarming conclusion.nnBorders Pet Rescue took the kittens in and provided the necessary medical attention. “All were suffering from severe malnutrition, flea and worm infestations, and painful eye infections,” McNeill explained.  Initially frightened and wary of humans, the kittens required extensive care and patience.  A dedicated foster carer stepped in, offering a safe and nurturing environment where they could begin to rebuild their trust and learn to play.nnIn a nod to the strong bond the kittens shared and the joy they brought to the rescue team, each kitten was given a name inspired by the popular 1990s sitcom *Friends* – Joey, Ross, Chandler, Phoebe, Monica, and Rachel.  “Inspired by their strong bond and the laughter they brought to us, we named them after characters from Friends - three girls and three boys,” said McNeill. The move proved popular, capturing hearts on social media and raising thousands of pounds towards their veterinary care.nnThe *Friends* connection resonated with the public, allowing Borders Pet Rescue to spread awareness about the kittens’ plight and ultimately, find them permanent homes. “We’re delighted to share that all six kittens have now been adopted into wonderful homes, each beginning a new chapter filled with love and joy,” McNeill confirmed.  The remarkable recovery of the ‘Kitten Friends’ serves as a testament to the dedication of the rescue organization and the kindness of their supporters.
