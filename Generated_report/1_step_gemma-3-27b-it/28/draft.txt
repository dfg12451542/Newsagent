Wales defeated Japan 31-22 in Kobe on Saturday, ending a challenging run of form that saw them endure an 18-Test losing streak. The victory, which levels the two-match series at 1-1, was lauded by fly-half Dan Edwards as a testament to the team's character. “It was about getting a win, I’m absolutely chuffed,” Edwards said, reflecting the relief felt throughout the squad.nnThe match saw a spirited comeback from Wales, particularly in the second half, fueled by impactful replacements. A pivotal moment came with a stunning play orchestrated by flanker Taine Plumtree, who delivered a remarkable reverse pass that led to a crucial try. Edwards further emphasized the team's resilience, stating, “Especially with everyone saying we didn’t deserve to win last week and were writing us off, people have been writing us off for however long.”nnEdwards highlighted the significance of the win, not just for the team, but for Welsh rugby as a whole. “We’ve come back and shown everyone why we’re here. It shows the character we’ve got. That was a real statement that when it gets tough, we can pull through,” he explained. He also added, “It’s also just that little bit of a boost that Welsh rugby needed.”nnFor Wales, this win marks a significant turning point after a prolonged period of struggle. The victory in Kobe offers a much-needed morale boost and a foundation to build upon as they look towards future challenges. Keelan Giles, who came on as a half-time replacement, spoke to the emotional importance of the win, saying, “It’s a massive day for me and my family…To get that win, it’s almost the monkey off our backs.” The team now hopes to capitalize on this momentum and move forward.nnThe daring play-making exemplified by Plumtree’s assist also drew praise. Interim head coach Matt Sherratt commented on the decisive play, saying, “That last play summarises it for me…It would have been easy to go to a safe lineout. We have thrown to the back, run a more risky play.” He lauded Plumtree’s boldness saying, “And Taine Plumtree throws a reverse pass. I didn’t intend for him to be that brave, but it worked!”
