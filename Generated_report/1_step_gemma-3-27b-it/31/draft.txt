Actor Sean Bean has publicly criticised plans to build housing on green belt land in his hometown of Sheffield, describing the proposals as a potential devastation to the local character and natural environment. The Bafta-winning star, known for roles in productions like 'Game of Thrones' and 'Lord of the Rings', voiced his opposition in a detailed email objection to the Sheffield City Council, arguing the area is being unfairly targeted. nnBean’s email, seen by the BBC, expresses frustration with the council's approach, stating: “It seems the council sees us as second-class citizens who will just have to put up with it.” This sentiment reflects a growing concern amongst residents in the Handsworth area, a working-class community facing the prospect of significant development on previously protected land. The proposed housing plan aims to address the city’s housing needs, but locals fear the loss of valuable green space and the impact on the area’s identity.nnCampaigners fighting the plan have received a significant boost from Bean’s support. Sadie Charlton, a member of the ‘Save S13 Green Belt - The Sapphire McCarthy Campaign’, explained: “It’s really boosted our morale and everybody’s so thankful for him to take the time to put his voice forward because what he says in his objection is how we all feel.” The group is named in tribute to Sapphire McCarthy, a key campaigner who tragically passed away and initially connected Bean to the issue.nnCharlton revealed Bean’s involvement began with a phone call to McCarthy while she was shopping. She added: “He was really interested in keeping in touch about how the campaign is going because obviously he cares about Handsworth.” Bean submitted his objection just hours before the consultation period closed, after meeting McCarthy, and highlighted that the area felt “specifically targeted” compared to more affluent parts of Sheffield. The campaign is now galvanised, with residents determined to continue fighting to protect their green spaces, viewing Bean’s support as a pivotal moment in their efforts.
