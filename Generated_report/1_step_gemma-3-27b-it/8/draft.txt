A Corby, Northamptonshire dog named Jimmy has been rescued after spending 11 days trapped down a well, sparking a widespread community effort. The dramatic rescue unfolded on Stephenson Way after firefighters, responding to a separate incident, heard barking emanating from the abandoned well. Jimmy, a beloved family pet, had been missing since June 22nd, leading his owners to fear the worst.nnOwner Dorota Gruszczynska detailed the agonizing search, explaining, “We searched the area so many times and nothing, we created posters, put them online and sent them around the local area.”  Ms. Gruszczynska recounted the despair felt by the family, stating, “We thought he had been hit by a car and we would never see him again.”  Hope was reignited when a firefighter contacted her, having heard the animal and recognizing the description of a green harness.  “Firefighters had been called to the area, and had heard him barking, they asked if he had a green harness on and told me he was alive,” Ms. Gruszczynska said.nnNorthamptonshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed that a Corby crew discovered Jimmy while attending a nearby fire. Crews skillfully utilized a reach pole and lines to carefully remove cylinders that were obstructing the dog, ultimately lifting him to safety.  After locating Jimmy, firefighters contacted missing-dog pages online and quickly connected with Ms. Gruszczynska.  “It was near where we had lost him, we had called him so much in the area, but never heard him barking. We couldn't believe it was him,” she explained.nnMs. Gruszczynska expressed profound gratitude for the outpouring of support, highlighting the crucial role of the drone company involved in the searches and the power of social media. “We’re so grateful for everyone who helped, including the drone company. They did a huge amount of work, purely out of their heart,” she stated, adding, “So many people shared the cause, and it helped as the firefighters knew to contact me.”  Remarkably, Jimmy is recovering well despite his ordeal. “He's back on his feet, he's recovered surprisingly well, we don't know how he survived, there must have been water in the well,” Ms. Gruszczynska shared, relieved and overjoyed to have her companion home.
