Royal Portrush, Northern Ireland – Rory McIlroy has expressed that a victory at The Open Championship 2025, held at Royal Portrush, would be a profoundly emotional experience, comparable to, or even exceeding, the significance of his 2024 Masters triumph. The 36-year-old Northern Irishman is relishing the opportunity to compete on home soil once more, having previously played the Open at Portrush in 2019. nnThe 2019 Open at Royal Portrush marked a historic occasion, being the first time the championship had returned to the Northern Irish coast in 68 years. While McIlroy didn’t contend for the title that year, the enthusiastic support of local fans left a lasting impression. Reflecting on the prospect of another chance, McIlroy stated, “I’m so happy it’s back so soon because I want to have another crack at it.” nnMcIlroy acknowledges the weight of expectation that comes with playing in front of a home crowd, but views it as a motivating factor. “It would be amazing,” McIlroy told BBC Sport NI.  He added, “I think it would be just as emotional, if not more emotional than Augusta – and everyone saw the mess I was after that.”  The player intends to embrace the attention and the energy of the fans this week. nnHaving secured a major championship title earlier this year at the Masters, McIlroy feels confident and well-prepared. He attributes much of this readiness to a recent period of rest and focused preparation. “The two weeks off… to reset… has been really nice,” McIlroy said. He also believes he has evolved as a player since his last appearance at Portrush, stating, “I didn’t have the week I wanted in 2019. I’m a better player, manage my game better…”nnUltimately, McIlroy is eager to deliver a performance that will resonate with the passionate fans and cement his legacy as one of golf’s greatest players. “I’m excited with where my game’s at,” he affirmed, “and I … want to embrace that this week.” He hopes to capitalize on the encouraging results from recent starts, particularly his performance in Scotland, and build momentum towards a memorable Open Championship run.
