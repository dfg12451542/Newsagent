A quick-thinking Guernsey woman, Jacky Meinke, saved her husband’s life after he collapsed while swimming at Port Grat beach on Friday afternoon. The 77-year-old, identified as Chris Meinke, was found face down in the water by his wife, who immediately sprang into action. The dramatic rescue unfolded around 4:30 PM BST, highlighting the potential dangers even in popular bathing spots.nnMrs. Meinke initially thought her husband was swimming behind her, but quickly realized the severity of the situation. “I heard a splash and carried on swimming, thinking he was behind me,” she recounted. “When I looked round his head was face down in the water.” She immediately began screaming his name and pulled him from the water, describing him as “completely white” and unresponsive.  “I put his head on my shoulder, walloped his back and he spluttered and came to,” she said, explaining she was able to revive him with first aid.nnFellow swimmer Rosie Mclauchlan also assisted in the rescue, noting the speed at which the situation deteriorated. “He was literally drowning,” Ms. Mclauchlan stated. “I was amazed how quickly someone can get into difficulty.”  The pair managed to bring Mr. Meinke back to the beach, where they placed him in the recovery position until paramedics arrived.nnMr. Meinke was transported to the hospital and spent the night under observation. While the cause of his collapse remains a mystery, he believes he may have experienced vertigo. “I was effectively dead,” he said, adding, “The hospital thinks I had vertigo, I just didn’t know which way was up.” Scans are currently being conducted to provide a definitive diagnosis. Both Mr. and Mrs. Meinke expressed immense gratitude to Ms. Mclauchlan and the bystanders who assisted, including those who retrieved a defibrillator and provided a blanket.
