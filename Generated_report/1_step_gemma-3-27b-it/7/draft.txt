Leicestershire Police have made four additional arrests in connection with a stabbing that occurred in Eyres Monsell, Leicester, on Friday evening. The incident, which took place shortly after 9:15 PM BST on Sturdee Road, left a man in his 20s hospitalized in stable condition. This brings the total number of individuals arrested to five, following the initial arrest of a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted murder. nnThe newly arrested individuals include two men, aged 18 and 29, also held on suspicion of attempted murder. Additionally, a 38-year-old woman faces suspicion of conspiracy to commit murder, and a 25-year-old woman is being questioned on suspicion of assisting an offender. While the 18-year-old man and the 25-year-old woman remain in custody, the other three suspects have been released on police bail pending further investigation. nnAccording to Detective Sergeant Adrian Davies, “We believe the stabbing happened shortly before 9.15pm,” and emphasized the importance of potential witnesses. “It was still light at that time and with the weather being very warm there were probably quite a few people who were in the area or in their gardens,” Davies stated, appealing to the public for information. Police confirmed all involved parties were known to each other. nnAuthorities are currently conducting house-to-house inquiries and a thorough examination of the crime scene. Davies urged anyone with information, however small, to come forward, stating, “If you saw something or heard something that might help with our investigation, please contact us.” This latest development continues an investigation that began after the injured man sought help on Lawford Road following the attack.
