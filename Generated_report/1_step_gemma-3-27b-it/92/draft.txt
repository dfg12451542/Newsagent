Wycombe Wanderers have bolstered their midfield options with the signing of Ewan Henderson from Belgian side Beerschot, marking the club’s eighth addition of the summer transfer window. The 25-year-old Scotland youth international joins Adams Park for an undisclosed fee as manager Mike Dodds continues to reshape his squad ahead of the new season.nnHenderson arrives with experience in both Scottish and Belgian top flights, having previously played in those leagues.  He is an experienced player, having played for Beerschot in recent years. Wycombe have already confirmed the signings of Armando Junior Quitirna, Taylor Allen, Bradley Fink, Caolan Boyd-Munce, Dan Casey, James Tilley and Mikki van Sas this window, demonstrating an active approach to strengthening the team.nnSpeaking about the new acquisition, Wycombe head coach Mike Dodds highlighted Henderson’s energetic style of play and recent match fitness. “Ewan's a really energetic midfielder who's had a great start to his career in the top flights in Scotland and Belgium, and gives us another dimension in the middle of the park,” Dodds stated.  He added that Henderson’s consistent playing time over the last three years would be invaluable as he quickly integrates into the team.nnDodds expects Henderson to “hit the ground running,” with pre-season training underway. “He's played a lot of football in the past three years which stands him in good stead to come here and hopefully hit the ground running as he integrates into the squad in pre-season.” Henderson’s international experience, having represented Scotland at under-17, under-19, and under-21 levels, adds another layer of quality to Wycombe’s squad as they prepare for the challenges ahead.
