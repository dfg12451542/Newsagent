The Wurzels were forced to cut short their performance at Party at the Palace in Wells, Somerset on Saturday as band members succumbed to the heat. The iconic West Country cider-themed band had to abruptly end their set when singer Pete Budd, approaching his 85th birthday on July 18th, became unwell during the peak of temperatures reaching 31C. nnPete Budd was seen leaving the stage before the set’s completion, prompting concern amongst fans. Accordionist Tommy Banner also experienced symptoms, describing feeling sunstroke and dizziness after exiting the stage. “Both Pete and I appear to have fully recovered from sun and heat exhaustion,” Banner explained, adding, “Up on stage there was no air moving and we were facing straight into the direct, unobstructed rays of a very strong sun.” Both musicians received support from medical staff as they left the performance area.nnAccording to Banner, the incident unfolded quickly. “I was about to announce the last song and Pete wasn’t besides me where he normally is. He was in a bad way,” he stated. A nurse on-site assessed both Budd and Banner, guiding Banner through exercises and advising him against driving. nnThis incident occurred during a heatwave currently gripping Somerset, with temperatures exceeding 31C this week. While the band members are now reportedly recovering, the incident highlights the dangers of performing outdoors in extreme heat, even for seasoned performers like The Wurzels, who have been entertaining audiences for decades. The band have not yet commented on whether any future performances will be affected.
