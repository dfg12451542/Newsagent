Portrush, Northern Ireland is bracing for a week of world-class golf as the 153rd Open Championship gets underway today, July 14th. The best golfers from across the globe have descended upon Royal Portrush, setting the stage for a thrilling competition to crown the 2025 'Champion Golfer of the Year'. The tournament runs through July 20th, promising a full schedule of play and related coverage.nnThis year's Open carries particular significance for local fans, with Northern Ireland's own Rory McIlroy competing. McIlroy, a Masters champion and one of golf's biggest stars, will undoubtedly draw large crowds and heightened excitement. The venue itself holds special memories, as it was at Royal Portrush in 2019 that Irishman Shane Lowry secured a stunning victory, captivating audiences worldwide.nnBBC Sport will serve as a dedicated resource for fans throughout the tournament, providing comprehensive coverage of every moment. Live radio and text updates will be available throughout the week, alongside press conferences featuring top contenders like McIlroy and defending champion Xander Schauffele, who claimed the title at Royal Troon in Scotland last year.
