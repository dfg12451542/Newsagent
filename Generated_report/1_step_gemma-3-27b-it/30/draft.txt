Grace Kim of Australia clinched her first major title at the 2025 Evian Championship in France, overcoming a thrilling playoff against Atthaya Thitikul. Kim sealed the victory with a 12-foot eagle putt on the second replay of the 18th hole, demonstrating remarkable composure under pressure. The competition was fierce, with both players showcasing exceptional skill throughout the tournament and a dramatic finish that required multiple extra holes.nnEngland’s Lottie Woad came incredibly close to joining the playoff, carding a sensational seven-under 64 to finish at 13 under par. A missed 10-foot birdie putt on the 18th ultimately dashed her hopes, leaving her to watch as Kim and Thitikul battled it out. Reflecting on the situation, Woad wryly remarked to her caddie, “Is there even any point, she’s not making six,” demonstrating a realistic assessment of the difficulty of the competition.nnThe climactic moments saw Kim eagle the 18th to force the playoff after Thitikul narrowly missed a birdie putt that would have secured her own maiden major. Both players then birdied the first replay of the 18th, setting the stage for Kim’s dramatic winning putt. The victory marks a significant milestone in Kim’s career, solidifying her position as a rising star in the world of women’s golf.nnWoad’s strong performance at the Evian Championship also yielded positive results beyond the near-miss. Earning two points for finishing in the top 25 of a major, Woad reached the 20-point threshold on the LPGA’s Elite Amateur Pathway, granting her a coveted spot on the LPGA Tour – an opportunity she expressed gratitude for, stating, “It has always been in my dreams to play on the LPGA Tour and I'm grateful for the opportunities of the programme.” She intends to discuss next steps with her family and coaches. “I’m going to discuss it with my family and coaches,” Woad said, while also showing ambition, stating, “I was trying to be in contention and winning this, so it will be the same there.”
