A climber suffered a head injury after a fall at Hoffman Quarry in Buxton, Derbyshire, on Friday morning, prompting a rescue operation involving Buxton Mountain Rescue Team and an air ambulance. The incident occurred at Harper Hill around 11:22 BST, with emergency services swiftly responding to the scene. nnThe climber reportedly fell approximately 3 meters (9.8ft), resulting in a head injury and a brief loss of consciousness, according to a spokesperson.  “The climber fell about 3m (9.8ft) and briefly lost consciousness due to a head injury,” the spokesperson stated.  Emergency responders secured the individual and prepared for evacuation.nnDue to the quarry's location and the nature of the injury, the climber was airlifted to a designated landing zone where a waiting ambulance transported him to the hospital for further treatment.  This mirrored a similar incident in the past where a climber required emergency surgery and a nearly two-week hospital stay after a fall, highlighting the potential dangers of climbing in such environments.nnThe condition of the climber is currently unknown. Authorities have not released further details about the individual or the circumstances surrounding the fall. Hoffman Quarry, while popular with climbers, is known for its challenging terrain, and this incident serves as a reminder of the importance of safety precautions when engaging in outdoor activities.
