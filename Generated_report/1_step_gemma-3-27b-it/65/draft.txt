Chelsea Football Club secured their place in history on Sunday, defeating Paris St-Germain 3-0 in the Club World Cup final held in New Jersey. However, the on-field triumph was overshadowed by an unexpected post-match moment involving former U.S. President Donald Trump. Trump presented the trophy to Chelsea captain Reece James, but then lingered on stage, remaining alongside the players as James lifted the coveted prize.nnFollowing the presentation, Trump’s continued presence drew attention. According to reports, Fifa president Gianni Infantino attempted to move Trump aside, inviting him to exit the stage. However, Trump remained alongside James and goalkeeper Robert Sanchez, applauding as the trophy was raised – staying in position for several seconds before departing with Infantino. The unusual scene sparked confusion amongst the Chelsea players.nnChelsea players expressed surprise at Trump's extended stay. “They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage, and I thought that he was going to exit the stage, but he wanted to stay,” explained captain Reece James. Midfielder Cole Palmer echoed this sentiment, stating, “I knew he was going to be here but I didn't know he was going to be on the stand when we lifted the trophy. I was a bit confused, yes.”nnDuring a halftime interview with DAZN, Trump was effusive in his praise of the event, stating he was “having a really great time.” He also discussed his relationship with Fifa President Gianni Infantino, saying, “Gianni is a friend of mine, he’s done such a great job with the league and with soccer – or as they would call it ‘football’, but I guess we call it soccer. I’m not sure that change could be made very easily.”  When playfully suggested he could issue an executive order to officially rename the sport “football” in the US, Trump responded with a smile, “I think we could do that. I think I could do that.”nnThe incident, while seemingly lighthearted, highlights the unusual intersection of sports and politics, and quickly became a talking point following Chelsea's victory. This is not the first time Trump has been involved in a high profile sporting event, but his decision to remain center-stage during the trophy lift added a uniquely unexpected element to Chelsea’s historic win.
