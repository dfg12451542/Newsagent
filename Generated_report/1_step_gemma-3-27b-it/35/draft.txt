Kilmarnock supporters expressed delight following their team’s victory over Brora Rangers on Saturday, marking a positive start to the Stuart Kettlewell era. The match, a 2-0 win for Kilmarnock, saw the team face a competitive challenge from the Highland League champions, signalling a good test for the new manager. We asked fans for their initial reactions to the game and the team's performance.nnMany fans, like Gordon, praised the competitive nature of the match. “A very good, competitive game really enjoyed it,” he said, highlighting the engaging atmosphere. The win wasn’t necessarily expected to be straightforward, according to Gordon, who also added, “Not as easy as I thought it would be but the three points are the most important thing.” This sentiment underscored the value of securing a win in the opening match of the season.nnOptimism for the future was also prevalent amongst the fanbase. Gordon continued, “Well done Killie and Kettlewell. Bring on Livingston on Wednesday hopefully another three points.”  The focus is already shifting to the next challenge, with supporters eager to see continued success under the new managerial guidance. nnBeyond the overall result, individual player performances also garnered attention. Fan Amy specifically highlighted young Ben Brannan, noting his potential and versatility. “A long day but an enjoyable one. Young Ben Brannan reminds me of Aaron Hickey - can play RWB or LWB, is comfortable coming inside and taking men on and has a great engine. Hope to see more of him this season,” she commented. This enthusiastic response to a promising young player further fuelled positive feelings amongst the supporters. nnOverall, the mood amongst Kilmarnock fans was buoyant.  George summed up the general feeling, stating, “Solid start, keep on improving the team's shape and tactics will come together. Good solid signings. Come on the Killie.”  The victory comes as Kilmarnock transitions after Derek McInnes moved to Heart of Midlothian, and supporters seem confident in Kettlewell’s ability to build on the foundations laid and guide the team forward.
