Salisbury resident Phyll Babb celebrated her 100th birthday in remarkable fashion, completing a 200-mile walking challenge and raising over £11,000 for the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless (STFH). The centenarian, who initially set out to walk 100 miles, doubled her goal earlier this year after quickly surpassing her initial target. Reaching the finish line on Sunday with a final mile circumnavigating Salisbury Cathedral, Ms. Babb expressed her astonishment, stating, “It’s hard to believe I’ve done 200 walks. It’s gone like a dream.”nnBabb's impressive feat was met with widespread admiration from family and supporters. She was cheered on by loved ones who lauded her as a source of inspiration. The challenge began with a simple goal, but quickly gained momentum as Babb continued to surpass expectations. Originally intending to walk 100 miles, her dedication drove her to reach an astounding 200.nn“Walking is good for you,” Babb shared, reflecting on a lifelong habit. “I was brought up to walk, so as children we walked everywhere, and I still like walking.”  Her dedication was meticulously tracked by a local accountant, who confirmed each of her 200 walks, initially recorded on the Strava app. The funds raised will go towards furnishing a new home, according to her son-in-law, Gordon Pardy.  “To do this in her 100th year was so impressive,” he added.nnFamily members underscored the inspirational nature of Babb’s accomplishment. Her great-niece, Ruth Simmons, described the scene as “really special,” while Liz Huntley affirmed, “She is inspirational, definitely. She’s been inspirational my whole life, to be honest.” David Evans, another supporter, highlighted the significance of her vitality at such an advanced age, calling her achievement “the really amazing thing she’s done.”  Ms. Babb herself expressed gratitude for the overwhelming support, saying, “People had been extremely generous…thank you all very, very much.”
