A man died following a single-vehicle crash in Nutts Corner, County Antrim on Saturday evening. The incident occurred on Ballyhill Road at approximately 17:50 BST, involving a white Volkswagen Golf. Emergency services attended the scene, but the driver was pronounced dead. nnAccording to police reports, the driver was the sole occupant of the vehicle at the time of the crash. “Police said the man was the driver of a white Volkswagen Golf which was involved in a single-vehicle incident in Ballyhill Road about 17:50 BST on Saturday,” a police spokesperson stated. The incident prompted a temporary closure of Ballyhill Road while authorities investigated. nnThe ambulance service also responded, however, they were unable to save the driver's life. Details regarding the victim’s identity have not yet been released by authorities.  Investigations into the cause of the collision are ongoing. nn A male passenger was also present in the Golf and sustained injuries in the crash, though police have indicated these are not believed to be life-threatening. “Police added that a male passenger was taken to hospital with injuries, which are not believed to be life-threatening at this time,” police confirmed.  Ballyhill Road has since reopened to traffic. nnThis incident echoes past collisions in the area involving Volkswagen Golfs, with previous cases documented in local court records regarding driving offences and subsequent arrests. Authorities are appealing for any witnesses to the collision to come forward and assist with their enquiries.
