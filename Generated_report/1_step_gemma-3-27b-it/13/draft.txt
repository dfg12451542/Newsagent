Royal Portrush, Northern Ireland – Defending champion Xander Schauffele was among the first players to grace the famed links course on Monday, getting an early feel for conditions ahead of The Open Championship this week. Schauffele, who secured his first major victory at Royal Troon in Scotland last year, is looking to become the first back-to-back champion since Padraig Harrington in 2008.nnAlso taking advantage of the practice day was Shane Lowry, a familiar face at Royal Portrush and a player intimately acquainted with the challenges the course presents. Lowry famously captured The Open title at Portrush in 2019, delivering a memorable performance that culminated in jubilant scenes on the 18th green and marking a historic win for Irish golf.nnThe 2019 Open champion was paired with Denmark’s Rasmus Hojgaard for Monday’s practice round, navigating the undulating fairways and testing the notoriously tricky greens. The Open Championship always draws a strong international field, with 156 players all vying for the coveted Claret Jug and the title of 'Champion Golfer of the Year.'nnWith Schauffele aiming to repeat his success and Lowry eager to recapture the magic of his 2019 victory on home soil, the stage is set for a compelling tournament. Royal Portrush, steeped in history, promises a stern test of skill and temperament for all competitors as the championship gets underway later this week.
