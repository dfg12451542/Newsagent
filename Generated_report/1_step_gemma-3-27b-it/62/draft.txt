MasterChef presenter John Torode is facing an allegation of using racist language, an accusation that emerged during an inquiry into separate claims against his co-host, Gregg Wallace. Torode addressed the allegation in a statement posted on Instagram Monday, confirming he is the individual involved and stating the incident reportedly occurred in either 2018 or 2019. He claims he immediately apologized at the time. nnAccording to Torode's statement, the person he was speaking with did not perceive the language as malicious, and he offered an immediate apology. However, the TV chef expressed confusion, stating, “I have absolutely no recollection of any of this, and I do not believe that it happened.” This conflicting account comes to light amidst a wider push for anti-racist practices within the television industry.nnThis allegation is the latest in a series of incidents prompting increased scrutiny of conduct within television production. The inquiry, it has been reported, also resulted in two independent allegations against other individuals being sustained, one of which also involved the use of racist language. These findings are prompting calls for systemic change, including the implementation of anti-racist frameworks and comprehensive staff training.nnDespite his lack of recollection, Torode was clear in denouncing racist language, stating, “I want to be clear that I’ve always had the view that any racial language is wholly unacceptable in any environment.” He expressed shock and sadness at the allegation, emphasizing his desire to never cause offense. The incident highlights the continuing challenges in addressing and combating racism within the entertainment sector, even as organizations pledge to foster more inclusive environments.
