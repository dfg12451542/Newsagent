Silverstone, Northamptonshire – Callie Cooke, a star of the new film *F1: The Movie*, revealed she participated in a genuine pit stop during the 2024 British Grand Prix, describing the experience as “mad” and “one of the best experiences of my life”. Cooke and the film’s crew operated a fully functional garage within the Silverstone pit lane throughout both the 2023 and 2024 Grand Prix events, immersing themselves in the high-pressure world of Formula 1 to ensure authenticity for the upcoming release.nnCooke, who plays a key role in the film, explained the extent of her involvement went beyond simply being present. “I spent the day there and I was changing the tyres and I was changing the oil, changing the wing of the car, and just seeing if I could exist in the world of a garage,” she said. She noted that she was the only actor amongst a crew comprised of seasoned mechanics, engineers and motorsport professionals. Cooke's local connection to Northamptonshire and Silverstone, where a significant portion of filming took place, proved beneficial. “Because of the Northamptonshire-Silverstone connection, it feels so special and so real,” she stated.nnThe dedication to realism extended to a fully functional pit stop simulation, successfully executed between the Mercedes and Ferrari teams.  “We did a real pit stop…and we did it as quick as the other teams,” Cooke recounted, seemingly still amazed. “It was just mad. I don't know how they ever let us get away with it, to be honest.” Brad Pitt, a producer and star of the film, also enjoyed the immersive environment. According to Cooke, “Pitt loved working in Northamptonshire and Silverstone…It was our home for at least a year”.nnCooke emphasized the importance of understanding the demanding nature of the sport, particularly for women in a traditionally male-dominated field. “I knew that if I was going to play a woman in this male-dominated industry, I was just going to have to hold my own and get stuck in, and I think maybe that's how I got the job,” she said. She added that growing up in Northampton and being surrounded by people working with cars helped prepare her for the challenge.nn*F1: The Movie*, directed by Joseph Kosinski, aims to provide an insider’s look into the world of Formula 1, and Cooke’s story highlights the lengths the production team went to achieve accuracy and authenticity. The film’s presence during actual Grand Prix events, underscored by the successful pit stop, demonstrates a commitment to capturing the intensity and precision of the sport for audiences worldwide.
