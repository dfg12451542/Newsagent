Crystal Palace has unveiled its new home kit for the 2025-26 season, launching the design amidst growing excitement surrounding the club’s participation in European competition. The release provides a fresh look for the Eagles as they prepare for a challenging and anticipated season ahead. nnThe new kit was revealed over the weekend and showcased by a host of first-team players, including Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, and recent signing Adam Wharton. Representing the full scope of the club, stars from Palace Women, Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur, also modeled the new strip. nnWhile the club focuses on preparing for their European campaign, transfer activity continues to be a key aspect of the pre-season. Recent reports have indicated interest in Italian defender Davide Calabria, a free agent after leaving AC Milan, as well as speculation regarding potential offers for forward Jean-Philippe Mateta from the Saudi Pro League. nnPlayers including Mateta, Guehi, Maxence Lacroix, Eddie Nketiah and Romain Esse were all pictured sporting the new kit, alongside new goalkeeper Walter Benitez in the light green goalkeeper jersey. The team debuted the kit in a pre-season friendly against Millwall on Saturday, securing a 1-0 victory. nnThe kit launch marks a significant moment for Crystal Palace, setting the stage for a season of both domestic and European challenges. With new players arriving and continued attention from clubs in other leagues, the Eagles are positioning themselves for a competitive 2025-26 campaign.
