Aberdeen, Scotland – A 25-year-old man has been charged with murder following an incident on a street in Aberdeen that left one man dead. Brandon Booth appeared before Aberdeen Sheriff Court on Monday, making no plea and being remanded in custody, according to court officials. The charge comes after police responded to reports of a seriously injured man near an intersection in the Penn North area of the city on Thursday morning.nnThe victim has been identified as Dylan Geddes. News of the charge comes amidst profound grief from Mr. Geddes’ family. The sentimental value of his life was further emphasized with, “He will be sorely missed every day by all who knew him.”nnPolice are continuing to investigate the circumstances surrounding the incident.
