A Hereford family’s unwavering support for the Lionesses has taken them 800 miles across Europe, culminating in jubilant celebrations after England’s decisive 6-1 victory over Wales in the UEFA Women's Euros. The Morris family – Kevin, Kelly, and their children Amelia, 16, and Ethan, 13 – travelled by campervan to the St Gallen stadium in Switzerland to witness England secure their place in the quarter-finals.  The family’s dedication underscores the growing enthusiasm surrounding women’s football, reminiscent of the dedicated fans who supported the early growth of the sport with trips to Wales from London, attracting large crowds and attention.nnDriven by the desire to cheer on the Lionesses in person, the Morris family embarked on a European adventure. “As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,” said Kevin Morris, describing the atmosphere at the stadium as “great.”  Their journey isn’t simply about attending matches; it’s a testament to the family’s passion for the squad and a willingness to go the distance to support them.nnThe trip home, however, proved unexpectedly challenging.  “It was just busy coming out of the stadium,” Mr. Morris explained. “We couldn’t get on the first train, and after changing trains, we ended up stranded in the middle of nowhere for nearly half an hour before a replacement bus finally arrived.” The bus route itself was circuitous, adding further delays before they eventually reached their campervan site in Arbon, near Lake Constance.nnDespite the travel woes, Mr Morris insists “it was worth it”. The family is now setting their sights on the quarter-final match against Sweden, a rematch of the Euro 2022 semi-final.  “I think we can do it again,” he stated, expressing optimism about England’s chances.  They plan to drive back to Zurich on Tuesday, hoping to “be lucky enough to get through to see the semi-final,” before beginning their journey back to Hereford on July 24th, echoing the positive atmosphere described by fans like Nikki Brewer who commented on a “fantastic” experience.
