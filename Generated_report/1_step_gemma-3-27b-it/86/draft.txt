A three-year-old girl from Powys, Wales, is set to become one of the youngest ever competitors at the prestigious Crufts dog show after qualifying with her Miniature Schnauzer, Zeeva. Caitlyn, from Groesffordd near Brecon, secured her spot at the 2025 event following a successful showing at the Three Counties Championship Show last month.  “I like running around with her,” Caitlyn shared, expressing her enjoyment of competing with her eight-year-old companion.nnThe pair's remarkable achievement is a testament to Caitlyn’s natural aptitude and confidence around dogs, according to her mother, Lydia Mellor. “She is so confident generally and she works so well with the dogs. She loves the dogs,” Mellor stated.  Beyond her skill in the ring, Caitlyn has already demonstrated exceptional sportsmanship, readily congratulating fellow competitors and even shaking hands with judges and winners.  “She is already an incredibly good sport,” Mellor added, noting her daughter’s respectful demeanor. nnThis qualification is particularly noteworthy as the current youngest confirmed competitor at Crufts is 14 years old, with the oldest participant being 81.  Mellor believes Caitlyn’s participation holds broader significance. “I am massively proud of the sportsmanship she is showing at this age… It's going to keep our sport alive,” she explained. She also emphasized that Crufts is more than just a “dog beauty pageant,” highlighting the deep bond owners share with their pets – “They are our pets, they are spoilt rotten, they want for nothing - we have a great day out.”nnThe Kennel Club, organizers of Crufts, echoed the sentiment, with spokesperson Helen Kerfoot stating: “We are delighted to see a young competitor doing so well, having qualified for Crufts already – the result of a lot of hard work and dedication.” Kerfoot also emphasized the show’s accessibility, adding, “Taking part in Crufts is a real achievement but it’s also something that’s accessible to all.”  Caitlyn and Zeeva’s story is a heartwarming example of dedication, passion, and the enduring connection between humans and their canine companions.
