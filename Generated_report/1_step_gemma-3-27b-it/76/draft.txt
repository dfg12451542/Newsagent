The M11 motorway near Stansted Airport has fully reopened following a vehicle fire that caused significant traffic delays on Sunday, July 13th. The incident, which occurred between junctions eight and nine, brought traffic to a standstill, with motorists experiencing delays of up to 60 minutes. Authorities are still investigating the cause of the fire, though initial reports suggest a flare or other flammable item may be responsible. nnFire crews swiftly attended the scene to extinguish the blaze, preventing any injuries – a fortunate outcome given the potential for harm in a motorway environment. However, the fire did result in extensive damage to property in the immediate vicinity. The congestion stretched back approximately 1.5 miles, leading to a considerable backlog of vehicles.nnAccording to a National Highways spokesperson, “There is approximately 1.5 miles congestion following this incident so please allow time for these delays to clear.” Images circulating online depicted hundreds of cars halted on the motorway, with frustrated drivers leaving their vehicles to stretch their legs while awaiting the road to reopen. nnThis incident follows a period of already congested traffic on the M11 southbound on Sunday morning. While the specific cause of the initial congestion remains separate from the vehicle fire, it contributed to the overall disruption experienced by travelers. Travelers are advised to check traffic conditions before commencing their journeys. nnWith the motorway now clear, traffic is expected to return to normal, although residual delays may persist as vehicles clear the backlog. Authorities continue to urge caution and safe driving practices for all motorists using the M11.
