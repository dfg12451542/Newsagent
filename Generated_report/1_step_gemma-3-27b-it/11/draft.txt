Motherwell manager Jens Berthel Askou has urged his team to ‘increase tempo’ following a challenging start to the Premier Sports Cup campaign, culminating in a draw against Clyde on Saturday. The result, while not ideal, underscored the unpredictable nature of the sport, according to Askou. The match at New Douglas Park marked Askou's first competitive fixture in charge of the Scottish Premiership club.nnDescribing the Clyde match as a demonstration of how “tricky” football can be, Askou acknowledged the team’s effort but pinpointed areas requiring improvement. While praising the team’s pressing intensity and structural organisation, Askou highlighted their vulnerability to direct play, with opponents consistently finding space in behind the Motherwell defence.nn“It just shows how tricky football is in general,” Askou told club media. He further elaborated, “There’ll always be surprises in football, especially in the cup tournaments, there will be things that don’t go as expected.” He noted that other clubs also experienced unexpected results, adding, “You have to be there on the day.” The manager also saw positives, stating, “I think in terms of effort and structure, we were good… in terms of intensity, I think we pressed them with intensity.” However, he stressed the need to better handle opposing attacks and raise the overall pace of play.nnLooking ahead, Askou believes the team’s home ground, Fir Park, will provide the ideal platform to elevate their performance. “That’s something to improve on, definitely and then it’s about getting the tempo up, but I’m sure that Fir Park will give us the possibility to play at a much higher tempo,” he explained. The club will be hoping to translate these improvements into a more decisive result as the cup competition progresses.
