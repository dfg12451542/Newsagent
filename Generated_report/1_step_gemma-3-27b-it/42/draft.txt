Casablanca, Morocco – Nigeria and Algeria have both secured their places in the quarter-finals of the 2024 Women’s Africa Cup of Nations (Wafcon) following a 0-0 draw between the two nations. The result in Group B saw Nigeria finish top, setting up a quarter-final showdown with Zambia. Meanwhile, Algeria will advance to the last eight as one of the best runners-up, where they will face the second-placed finisher in Group C.nnThe Super Falcons’ top spot was confirmed despite a hard-fought contest against a determined Algerian side. The match, played in Casablanca, lacked clear-cut chances, with both teams prioritizing defensive solidity.  Nigeria relentlessly pressed for a winning goal, exemplified by Echegini's curling effort that narrowly missed the target in the 89th minute.  nnElsewhere in the tournament, Botswana pulled off a dramatic victory against Tunisia, winning 2-1.  After falling behind to Yesmin Khanchouch’s early goal, Botswana battled back with Lesego Radiakanyo equalizing in the second half. A stoppage-time winner from Gaonyadiwe Ontlametse secured a vital three points and significantly boosted their hopes of progressing as one of the two best-ranked third-placed teams. nnThe results on Sunday also confirmed the qualification of defending champions South Africa and Mali to the quarter-finals. Group C will conclude on Monday with crucial matches between Banyana Banyana and Mali, and Ghana versus Tanzania, respectively. Both of those matches carry high stakes as both Ghana and Tanzania need wins to progress.  Ahead of the potential clash with Algeria, historical analysis suggests Nigeria, nine-time Wafcon champions, will be strong contenders for the title should they avoid defeat in this Sunday's match.
