Two women were killed and two men injured Sunday at the Richmond Road Baptist Church in Lexington, Kentucky, after a shooting spree that began with the suspect wounding a state trooper during a traffic stop. Authorities have identified the gunman as 47-year-old Guy House, who was shot and killed by responding law enforcement at the scene. The incident unfolded after a state trooper pulled over House’s vehicle due to a registration plate reader alert, according to Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers. nnFollowing the initial traffic stop, House allegedly shot the trooper, inflicting non-life threatening injuries, before car-jacking a vehicle and fleeing towards the church, approximately 16 miles away, with police in pursuit. Chief Weathers stated, “The suspect fired his weapon at individuals on church property,” before being neutralized by officers. The car-jacking occurred without further injury to the vehicle’s occupant. Investigators believe House had a connection to individuals at the church, though the nature of that connection remains under investigation.nnThe victims have been identified as Beverly Gum, 72, and Christina Combs, 32, both pronounced dead at the scene.  Two male parishioners were also shot; one is in critical condition, while the other is stable.  A county coroner official noted the close-knit nature of the congregation, stating, “It’s a very tight-knit group of people.” nnChief Weathers confirmed the suspect was shot by law enforcement and pronounced dead at the scene, adding that his identity would be released after notification of next of kin.  The shooting is being investigated by the Kentucky State Police and the department’s Public Integrity Unit. The initial shooting near the Blue Grass Airport occurred at 11:36 local time (15:36 GMT) on Sunday, quickly escalating into a tragic event that has shaken the Lexington community.
