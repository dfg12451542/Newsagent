Liverpool have announced the signing of 20-year-old Scotland midfielder Kirsty Maclean from Rangers, bolstering their squad ahead of the upcoming season. The move sees a promising young talent join one of the Women’s Super League’s top clubs, signalling Liverpool’s intent to compete at the highest level. The transfer fee was undisclosed.nnMaclean, a product of the Rangers academy, departs the Glasgow club having made over 100 appearances. The midfielder expressed her enthusiasm for the move, stating it was the “right time for a new challenge and to take the next step” in her career.
