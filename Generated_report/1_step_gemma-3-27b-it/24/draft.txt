A large blaze is currently being tackled by ten fire crews in fields near Guildford, Surrey. The fire, which broke out on Monday afternoon, prompted a swift response from Surrey Fire & Rescue Service, who were alerted to the situation at 16:20 BST in the Sandy Lane area. Emergency services are working diligently to contain the fire and prevent further spread.nnA spokesperson for the service confirmed the fire originated in fields adjacent to Piccards Farm House. While the intensity of the blaze is significant, authorities have confirmed that no properties have been directly damaged.
