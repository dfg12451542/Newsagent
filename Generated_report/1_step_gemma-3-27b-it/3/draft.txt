Jersey’s women footballers have begun their 2025 Island Games campaign with a 3-1 loss to reigning champions Bermuda on Sunday. The defeat places Jersey at the bottom of Group B after the opening matches, mirroring a previous encounter between the two islands at the 2023 Games held in Guernsey, where Bermuda won 4-0.nnJersey enjoyed an early lead with a sixth-minute goal from Anita Tavares, expertly shooting left-footed into the corner. However, Bermuda responded strongly, nearly scoring before halftime when their shot struck the Jersey bar, and both teams saw penalty appeals denied during a competitive first half. nnBermuda equalized after an hour with a goal from Jaden Masters, quickly followed by a penalty converted by Khyla Brangman fourteen minutes later, shifting the momentum of the match. The 2023 gold medalists sealed their victory in stoppage time when Jya Rattaray Smith capitalized on a rebound off the bar to secure a 3-1 win and take the top spot in Group B.nnThis year’s Island Games mark a return to Scotland for the first time since 2005, when Shetland hosted the event. Jersey, who unfortunately missed out on a semi-final place two years ago due to an administrative error, will need to rebound quickly. They face Shetland Islands on Monday and the Isle of Wight on Tuesday in their remaining group matches. With Shetland and the Isle of Wight drawing 2-2 earlier on Sunday, the group remains competitive, and Jersey’s path to qualification will require strong performances in their upcoming fixtures.
