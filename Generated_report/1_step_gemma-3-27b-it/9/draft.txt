Donington Park, UK – Toprak Razgatlioglu surged to the top of the World Superbike Championship standings this weekend, securing a remarkable treble of victories at Donington Park and establishing a four-point lead over rival Nicolo Bulega. The BMW rider’s dominant performance included wins in both feature races and the Superpole sprint, marking a significant shift in the championship battle.nnRazgatlioglu’s charge began with a victory in the Superpole sprint race, immediately closing the gap to Bulega, the Ducati rider, to within a single point. This set the stage for a thrilling Sunday, where the reigning champion demonstrated his prowess and secured a commanding win in the main feature race. The Turkish rider proved untouchable, underlining his continued dominance in the series. nnSunday’s feature race victory was particularly decisive, extending Razgatlioglu’s advantage to four points over Bulega, who finished second. The BMW rider’s consistent performance throughout the weekend has positioned him perfectly as the championship heads into its next phase.  This marks the third victory of the weekend for Razgatlioglu, after his Saturday feature race triumph and Superpole win.nnCompleting the podium were Alvaro Bautista in third, followed by Danilo Petrucci and Andrea Locatelli. Meanwhile, six-time World Superbike champion Jonathan Rea, after showing promising form with a fifth-place finish on Saturday, secured a point-scoring 15th place in the final race. nnThe weekend’s results highlight Razgatlioglu's increasing momentum and potentially signals a turning point in the championship. His consistent victories at Donington Park demonstrate both his individual skill and the strength of the BMW team, setting the stage for a fierce rivalry with Bulega as the season progresses.
