Hibernian FC has broken its transfer record to secure the signing of Togo international striker Thibault Klidje from Swiss side Luzern. The 24-year-old has agreed to a three-year deal with the Scottish Premiership club, with an option for an additional year, marking a significant investment in the team’s attacking options. The undisclosed fee signifies Hibernian’s ambition as they prepare for the upcoming season.nnManager David Gray expressed his excitement about Klidje’s arrival, highlighting the qualities he will bring to the Easter Road side. “Klidje will add extra pace and trickery to Hibs’ front line,” Gray stated. He further elaborated, noting Klidje “performed well for Luzern last season, and at 24 has a lot of room for development.”nnKlidje’s move to Hibernian follows a period playing in Switzerland after previously competing in the French top division during the 2021-22 season. The forward's versatility and potential for growth are key factors in Hibernian’s decision to bring him to Edinburgh. nnGray emphasized the club’s commitment to nurturing Klidje’s talent, stating, “We want to help him progress, and look forward to working with him moving forward.” The signing signals a clear intent from Hibernian to bolster their attacking prowess and compete at a higher level in the Scottish Premiership.
