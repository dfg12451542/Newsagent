As Live Aid marks its 40th anniversary, the spotlight is turning to the unsung heroes behind the historic 1985 concert. While iconic performances by artists like Queen and David Bowie are vividly remembered, the contributions of individuals like Maurice Jones, a rock promoter from the West Midlands, are only now receiving due recognition. Jones's name was prominently featured on the event’s ticket, a testament to his crucial role, yet he remained a quietly essential figure in the concert's immense undertaking.nnAccording to his daughter, Nicola Jones, Maurice was instrumental in numerous logistical aspects of Live Aid, including venue organization, backstage catering, and band bookings. “He was very modest. His take on it was that he was there to do a job, he wasn’t there to be in the limelight. He did play an important part,” Nicola shared with BBC WM. She explained that his industry connections led him to be approached to assist with the concert, operating largely behind the scenes.nnNicola recounted her own experience attending the Wembley concert with her 15-year-old father, offering a glimpse into the electrifying atmosphere. She described being seated in the royal box, just rows behind Princess Diana and Prince Charles, and witnessing the star-studded lineup firsthand, including Paul McCartney and Elton John.  “I was very lucky,” she said, reminiscing about the legendary performance by Freddie Mercury and Queen, and witnessing the audience's overwhelming reaction. “Watching the reaction of the audience was really spectacular.”nnBeyond the celebrity encounters, Nicola emphasized the overall sense of unity and excitement that permeated both the audience and backstage areas. She recalled being initially unfamiliar with Queen, but instantly recognizing their magnitude during Mercury’s captivating performance.  Her father, she noted, deliberately avoided self-promotion. “I think, because of my dad’s nature, he didn’t want to promote himself even though he was very good at promoting bands and acts.”nnNow, Nicola hopes her father's contribution will be remembered and acknowledged. “It will be nice if he is remembered in a small way,” she said, expressing her pride in Maurice Jones and his pivotal behind-the-scenes work at one of the most significant events in music history. The rediscovery of Jones’ role serves as a potent reminder that monumental achievements often rely on the dedication of countless individuals working tirelessly outside the glare of the spotlight.
