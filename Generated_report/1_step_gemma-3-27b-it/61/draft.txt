West Lindsey District Council is poised to make its popular 'big bin hire' scheme permanent following overwhelmingly positive feedback from residents. The initiative, launched as a trial, allows residents to rent large capacity bins for substantial clear-outs and spring cleaning – a service proving highly valuable across the district.nnThe scheme has demonstrably filled a need within the community. According to council reports, hundreds of the larger bins were hired out during the trial period, demonstrably assisting residents with larger disposal tasks. These bins, similar in size to those used by commercial establishments, hold up to six times the volume of a standard wheelie bin, catering to major decluttering projects.nnA recent authority report highlighted the scheme’s success, noting that 95 per cent of customers awarded the service four or five-star reviews. The initial trial saw the hiring of 437 large bins and 27 smaller ones, as reported by the Local Democracy Reporting Service, clearly indicating a strong demand for the service. The positive response underlines the potential of the scheme to continue benefitting local households.nnThe report’s authors have recommended extending the trial into a permanent offering, and a formal decision is scheduled for Tuesday. If approved, residents will be able to continue accessing the larger bins, offering a convenient and efficient solution for larger waste disposal needs and solidifying the Council's commitment to responsive public services.
