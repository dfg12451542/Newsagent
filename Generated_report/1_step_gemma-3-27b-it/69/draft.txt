A significant fire erupted at a commercial premises within the White Lund Industrial Estate in Morecambe, Lancashire, this morning, causing large plumes of smoke visible for miles. Firefighters have been working to contain the blaze since approximately 09:30 BST, prompting road closures and public safety warnings. nnInitial reports indicate that eight fire engines were dispatched to the scene to battle the fire. Fortunately, no injuries have been reported. The extent of the damage to the industrial site remains unknown, but the visible smoke has caused considerable concern among local residents. nn“Working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely,” stated a Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) spokesperson. The LFRS is managing the incident and coordinating efforts to bring the fire under control. Smoke continues to billow from the site, impacting air quality in the surrounding areas. nnLFRS group manager Mark Warwick confirmed the fire is “giving off large volumes of smoke” and urged the public to take precautions. He advised, “if you see or smell any smoke in the area, please keep windows and doors closed.” Residents downwind of the industrial estate are particularly advised to heed this warning. nnNearby roads, including a section of the Bay Gateway, have been closed to facilitate firefighting operations and ensure public safety. Authorities are continuing to monitor the situation and provide updates as they become available, anticipating potential disruption while crews continue to tackle the blaze and assess the damage.
