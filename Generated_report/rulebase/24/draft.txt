Some 10 fire crews are attempting to put out a large blaze in fields near a Surrey town.
Surrey Fire & Rescue Service was called at 16:20 BST on Monday to the blaze in the Sandy Lane area of Guildford.
No properties were damaged in the blaze, the service confirmed.
Residents in the area have been advised to keep windows and doors closed because of smoke.
