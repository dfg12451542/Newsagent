Police have arrested four more people over a stabbing outside a house in Leicester.
Officers were called to Lawford Road, in Eyres Monsell, shortly after 21:15 BST on Friday after a man in his 20s was stabbed on Sturdee Road.
Following the arrest of a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted murder, Leicestershire Police said it had arrested two men aged 18 and 29 on suspicion of the same offence.
A 38-year-old woman was also arrested on suspicion of conspiracy to commit murder, and a third woman, aged 25, on suspicion of assisting an offender.
The 18-year-old man and the 25-year-old woman remain in police custody while the other three suspects have been released on police bail while investigations continue.
A spokesperson for the police said the injured man had made his way to Lawford Road following the stabbing and was taken to hospital, where he remained in a stable condition.
Police said the suspects and the man were known to each other.
The force added house-to-house inquiries were under way and a "thorough examination" of the scene had been carried out.
