A biker is in hospital with serious injuries after a crash on a rural road in the Borders.
Emergency services were called to the single-vehicle crash on the B6399 near Hawick at about 12:00 on Sunday.
The accident happened just south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm and involved a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike.
A 57-year-old man was taken to hospital for treatment to serious injuries and the road was shut for about eight hours. Police have appealed for witnesses.
