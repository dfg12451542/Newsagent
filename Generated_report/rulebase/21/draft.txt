The Institute of Directors (IoD) Jersey released findings from its second economic confidence survey following input from 70 members.
The findings suggest 56% of respondents were optimistic about their own organisations but only 17% were optimistic about Jersey's overall economy. Top priorities for growth in Jersey included infrastructure investment, attracting new industries and housing affordability.
Responses found both islands faced similar economic challenges following the findings of Guernsey IOD's own survey published at the end of June, and urged political leaders to work together.
Jersey respondents said they wanted to work with Guernsey for its fiscal deficit strategies by reducing public spending and driving economic growth.
Survey results found entrepreneurship and innovation could be supported through tax incentives, youth talent attraction and skills development.
It also found AI adoption was in its early stages with 70% of companies offering training and 33% having a strategy in development.
