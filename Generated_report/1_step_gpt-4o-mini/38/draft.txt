A memorial bench dedicated to Sophie Lancaster, a victim of a violent hate crime, has been temporarily relocated to accommodate the installation of a giant sightseeing wheel in Whitby. This decision has prompted backlash from the Sophie Lancaster Foundation and local officials, who emphasize the bench's significant emotional connection for many visitors. 'We were shocked to find the bench had been temporarily relocated and the charity had not been consulted on the move,' said a spokesperson for the foundation. The bench, revealed in 2008, serves as a special place of remembrance for Lancaster, whose tragic story continues to resonate with the community.nnNorth Yorkshire Council defended the decision by stating the big wheel was intended as a 'respectful, exciting addition to the town.' They assured that the memorial bench would be restored to its original location by August 4. In light of the situation, Whitby mayor Sandra Turner described the relocation as 'a serious oversight' and expressed her concern over the lack of communication from the council. 'This has been a serious hiccup in the relationship between local authorities,' she remarked.nnLocal councillor Mark Crane explained that the wheel is part of a broader tourism strategy designed to enhance the aesthetics and foot traffic in Whitby. 'The location was chosen for its stunning views across the harbour and towards the town's famous abbey,' he stated, noting that initial feedback from local businesses has been positive.nnIn response to the incident, the Sophie Lancaster Foundation will hold a rededication ceremony for the bench during the Tomorrow's Ghosts Festival on November 1, underscoring the community's enduring bond with Lancaster's memory. A foundation spokesperson expressed gratitude for the support from the alternative community, stating, 'Their love for Sophie and affection for the foundation set up in her memory is truly heart-warming.' As the town navigates this conflict between tribute and tourism, the return of the memorial bench remains a pivotal point in honoring Sophie's legacy.
