Jersey’s women footballers faced a challenging start to their 2025 Island Games campaign, suffering a 3-1 defeat to reigning champions Bermuda in their opening match on July 13. The game, which took place against the backdrop of the prestigious multi-sport event, saw Jersey aim to shift the narrative after missing out on a semi-final spot two years ago due to an administrative error.nnThe match began promisingly for Jersey as Anita Tavares found the back of the net in the sixth minute, delivering a confident left-footed shot into the corner. However, the momentum shifted as Bermuda responded aggressively, with Jaden Masters equalizing from close range after an hour of play. Just 14 minutes later, Bermuda took the lead through Khyla Brangman’s penalty, following a foul in the box.nnThe Islanders continued to exert pressure and secured a third goal in stoppage time when Jya Rattaray Smith capitalized on a rebound after a shot hit the bar, reinforcing Bermuda's dominance in Group B. This result places Jersey at the bottom of their group, particularly after Shetland Islands and Isle of Wight ended their earlier match in a 2-2 draw.nnHistorically, this match echoed the opening game between the two teams during the 2023 Island Games in Guernsey, where Bermuda claimed a resounding 4-0 victory. Jersey will now look to regroup and prepare for their upcoming matches against Shetland Islands on Monday and Isle of Wight on Tuesday, hoping to turn their fortunes around in the tournament.
