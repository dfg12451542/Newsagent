Colchester is set to host a memorial running event this Sunday to honor the life of popular local athlete Arthur Whiston, who tragically passed away in a road accident. The race, organized by the Colchester Harriers AC—Whiston's running club—will begin at 09:30 BST at the garrison athletics track. A heartfelt tribute to a man who was 'incredibly loved by the community,' this event aims to celebrate Whiston’s contributions to local athletics and the joy he brought to those around him. According to Lydia Cunningham, a close friend, 'He was a very kind man who was very highly thought of and popular.' nnThe memorial race not only serves as a fitting tribute, but it also aims to give back to a cause that was close to Whiston's heart. Funds raised from the event will be donated to the Essex Wildlife Trust, reflecting Whiston's passion for supporting local charities and environmental causes. nnParticipants who have not pre-registered can still join on the day, making it an inclusive event for runners of all levels who wish to pay their respects. This gathering highlights the strong sense of community present in Colchester, where local events often draw significant participation from residents. James Young, Colchester Events operations manager, expressed the importance of hosting events on familiar grounds, further fostering community spirit and connection.nnAs the community gathers to remember Arthur Whiston, the memorial run stands as a testament to his legacy and the impact he had on those who knew him.
