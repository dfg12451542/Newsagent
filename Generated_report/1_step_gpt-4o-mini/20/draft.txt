The 153rd Open Championship has officially kicked off in the picturesque Northern Irish town of Portrush, welcoming the best golfers from around the globe. Taking place from July 13 to July 20, 2025, this prestigious event promises to deliver exceptional golfing moments and thrilling competition as players vie for the title of 'Champion Golfer of the Year.' The excitement is palpable as fans flock to Royal Portrush, a historic course that has previously hosted remarkable golf tournaments.nnThis year's coverage, spearheaded by BBC Sport, aims to provide exhaustive updates and live coverage throughout the championship week. Golf enthusiasts will have access to live radio broadcasts, real-time text updates, and press conferences featuring star players such as Rory McIlroy and defending champion Xander Schauffele. Viewers can engage with the content by simply hitting the 'follow' button on the BBC Sport website or app to add the event to their 'MySport' feed.nnDefending champion Xander Schauffele, despite battling a rib injury that has impacted his form this season, hopes to reclaim his title amidst fierce competition. Schauffele recently finished tied with Scottie Scheffler at nine-under par in the Scottish Open, providing him with a boost in confidence as he progresses into the week ahead. Golf analyst and correspondent Iain Carter has noted that while Schauffele faces challenges, the field is wide open, with many players ready to seize opportunities in the upcoming rounds.nnAs day one unfolds, all eyes are on the players as they navigate the renowned links of Royal Portrush. With the backdrop of breathtaking scenery and the atmosphere of a lively crowd, the 153rd Open Championship is set to be a memorable event in the world of golf.
