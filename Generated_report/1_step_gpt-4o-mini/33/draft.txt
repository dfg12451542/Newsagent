In a dramatic rescue at Port Grat beach in Guernsey on Friday afternoon, local woman Jacky Meinke, 67, saved her husband Chris Meinke, 77, from drowning after he collapsed in the water. Witnesses reported the scene unfolding around 16:30 BST as Jacky heard a splash and turned to find Chris floating face down. 'When I looked round his head was face down in the water,' she recalled. In a moments' panic, she started screaming his name and quickly pulled him to safety, recalling, 'He was completely white.' nnThanks to Jacky's quick actions, which included putting his head over her shoulder and performing back slaps, Chris regained consciousness. 'He was coughing, coughing, coughing,' Jacky recounted, grateful for the assistance from fellow swimmer Rosie Mclauchlan, who alerted beachgoers to call for help and fetch a defibrillator. Chris was later attended to by paramedics and spent the night in the hospital, although the cause of his collapse remains a mystery. 'It was very scary... something happened to disorientate me,' Chris said, reflecting on the incident.nnSince 2007, Guernsey has seen the establishment of safety schemes operating on its beaches during the summer months to promote water safety. Local officials stress the importance of beachgoers being aware of conditions and seeking assistance when needed. Jake Butt, a representative from the safety scheme, highlighted to the community, 'We'd urge anyone hoping to enjoy the beach or spend time on or in the water to take note of the conditions and key safety advice.' nnBoth Jacky and Chris expressed their gratitude to those who helped during the emergency. 'I am so grateful to them all,' said Jacky, who emphasized her relief that Chris is now safe. This incident serves as a reminder of the importance of vigilance near the water and the impact of swift action in lifesaving situations.
