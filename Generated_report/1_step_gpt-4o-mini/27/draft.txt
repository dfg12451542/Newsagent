A tragic incident occurred on Saturday, July 13, 2025, in County Antrim, where a man lost his life in a single-vehicle crash. According to police reports, the accident took place on Ballyhill Road around 17:50 BST. The man was driving a white Volkswagen Golf when the vehicle overturned, resulting in his immediate death at the scene despite the prompt response of the ambulance service.nnIn addition to the driver, police confirmed that a male passenger was also involved in the crash and was subsequently taken to the hospital. Fortunately, his injuries are not believed to be life-threatening at this time. The Ballyhill Road was closed following the incident while emergency services managed the scene but has since reopened to the public.nnThis incident adds to the ongoing concerns regarding road safety in the region, reflecting the challenging conditions that drivers often face. Authorities are continuing to investigate the cause of the crash and have urged witnesses to come forward with any information that may assist in their inquiries.
