A significant fire has erupted in fields near Guildford, prompting a swift response from emergency services. Surrey Fire & Rescue Service was alerted to the incident at 16:20 BST on Monday, and approximately ten fire crews are currently working to extinguish the blaze, which is located adjacent to Piccards Farm House in the Sandy Lane area. Fortunately, authorities have confirmed that no properties have been damaged as a result of the fire. However, local residents are being advised to keep their windows and doors closed due to the smoke in the vicinity.nnThis fire is part of a troubling pattern observed in the region, as 2025 has already seen 16 grass fires requiring extensive fire engine deployment. These incidents follow one of the driest springs on record in the UK, which has exacerbated conditions conducive to wildfires. Fire services are on high alert as dry weather continues to pose a significant risk, and the community's safety remains a top priority.nnAs efforts to control the fire continue, officials and local residents remain concerned about the potential for further blazes in the area. With dry conditions persisting, the Surrey Fire & Rescue Service urges all community members to stay vigilant and adhere to safety recommendations.
