Wales' Agriculture Minister Huw Irranca-Davies asserted that the new Sustainable Farming Scheme (SFS) is 'absolutely the right scheme' ahead of its upcoming launch. In a statement highlighting the importance of the scheme, Irranca-Davies expressed optimism about its reception, though he acknowledged that 'there will be the odd person' who may prefer a different approach. The second iteration of the SFS aims to address the concerns raised by farmers and unions after the original plan was met with widespread protest, emphasizing that the upcoming announcement will detail payment rates for participating farmers.nnThe concept of the SFS comes at a critical time, as approximately 80% of Wales' landscape is managed by farmers, underscoring their essential role in combating climate change and preserving natural ecosystems. Over the past year, the Welsh government has engaged in discussions with farming unions, environmental groups, and stakeholders from the meat, livestock, and dairy sectors to create a balanced scheme that reflects the needs of both farmers and the environment. 'It will be a whole farm approach and a whole nation approach that delivers for the people of Wales,' Irranca-Davies stated.nnDespite his enthusiasm for the plan, Irranca-Davies refrained from detailing specific elements of the SFS, emphasizing that it will be unlike any other scheme delivered in the UK. Concerns have been raised about ensuring food security and sustainability for farmers, with Conservative Senedd leader Darren Millar calling for the scheme to be voted on for 'democratic legitimacy'. Collaboration among stakeholders continues to be vital, as observed by Plaid Cymru's Delyth Jewell MS, who noted the long-standing frustrations among farmers regarding their voices being heard in the policymaking process.nnAs Wales moves towards a greener economy, the Sustainable Farming Scheme represents a crucial effort to integrate environmental benefits into agricultural practices while maintaining the economic viability of farming. The Welsh government maintains that the SFS will support the vital £9.3 billion food and farming supply chain in Wales, providing a sustainable future for its farmers.
