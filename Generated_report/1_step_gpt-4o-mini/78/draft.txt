In a heartbreaking incident at a lake in Sutton Coldfield, more than £8,000 has been raised for the family of Daniel Drewitt, a young boy who tragically drowned after going missing in the water. An online fundraising initiative, launched with the permission of Daniel's parents, rapidly gained support, surpassing its original goal of £1,600 within just 48 hours. The appeal is now aiming to reach £11,000 to help support the family during this incredibly difficult time.nnOrganiser Helena Swallow expressed gratitude for the overwhelming response, stating, 'The family are so very grateful, thank you so much.' An update shared by Swallow mentioned that Daniel's mother was 'overwhelmed' by the community's love and contributions, highlighting the emotional toll of the tragedy on the family.nnRescuers were dispatched to Sutton Park shortly after 6:00 PM BST last Thursday, but sadly, Daniel had already lost his life by the time ambulance crews arrived. Following the incident, the local community has come together to offer support to the devastated family. The fundraiser, which has now raised over £9,000, serves as a reflection of the deep sorrow felt by many in the community following the shocking news of Daniel's death.
