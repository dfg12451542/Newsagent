St Mirren Manager Stephen Robinson expressed his dismay after his team's disappointing performance in their Premier Sports Cup opener against Arbroath, which ended in a goalless draw followed by a defeat in a penalty shootout. A visibly 'shocked' Robinson apologized to the fans who traveled to Gayfield Park, acknowledging that the team's play was far from the level expected after a strong pre-season. 'I'm in a little bit of shock,' he said, reflecting on the stark contrast between their pre-season preparations and the match's outcome. 'The performance of maybe 13 of them is something I've not seen all pre-season.'nnRobinson criticized the decision-making and passing displayed during the match, noting, 'I didn't recognise some of the decision-making, some of the passing, some of the simple things we’ve continually done through the pre-season.' He emphasized the need for his players to learn from the experience instead of wallowing in disappointment, stating, 'We’re not going to feel sorry for ourselves. It’s disappointing for us, disappointing for the people that travelled a long way on a Saturday when they could have been sitting in the garden – I bet they wish they had done.'nnThe coach's concerns come as St Mirren, competing in the Scottish Premiership, faced the League 1 champions Arbroath and must now regroup swiftly. With their next match against League 2 Forfar Athletic looming, Robinson is demanding a response from his squad, reiterating, 'We have to react to it; we will be better. We have to do simple things which we didn’t do.' The team's subpar performance and the subsequent penalty shootout loss resulted in them forfeiting the bonus point, a setback that Robinson hopes to quickly turn around as the season progresses.
