A devastating fire at the Worm's Head Hotel in Gower has caused significant damage to the iconic landmark, leaving owners and patrons reeling from the aftermath. The Mid and West Wales Fire and Rescue Service received an emergency call at 00:51 BST on Monday morning, responding swiftly to the blaze that erupted within the property's accommodation block and ultimately spread to the entire roof space. Fortunately, all occupants were accounted for and no casualties were reported, allowing guests and staff to escape the flames safely.nnThe fire, which was extinguished around 05:30, resulted in the collapse of a 25-meter span of the hotel’s roof, exposing blackened beams and creating a harrowing scene for those familiar with the beloved establishment. 'It is particularly heartbreaking and soul-destroying for me and my family to see 25 years of hard work destroyed in less than 30 minutes,' the hotel's owners lamented through a social media post, expressing their devastation at the loss.nnLocal residents and frequent visitors echoed the owners' sentiments. Sian Nichol, a loyal summer guest, remarked, 'It's absolutely shocking to see it like this,' while Beverly recalled her years of celebrating New Year at the hotel, stating, 'To see it like this, I felt quite emotional when we turned the corner.' Gareth, another disappointed patron, added, 'The vision I had today was to sit outside there with a nice meal and lager together.'nnThe cause of the fire is still under investigation, although initial indications suggest it may have originated from a faulty washing machine in a box van. During the fire service's operation, South Wales Police also attended the scene, ensuring that safety protocols were followed. In a statement, hotel management confirmed, 'Everyone's safe. Thank you for your well wishes. The bar and hotel will be closed pending future updates,' indicating that while they remain optimistic about recovery, the path ahead is fraught with uncertainty for the cherished establishment.
