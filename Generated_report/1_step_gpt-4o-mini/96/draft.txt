Great Britain's Oliver Rowland has clinched the Formula E championship title for the first time after delivering a solid performance with a fourth-place finish at the Berlin race, held this past Sunday. Rowland's journey to the championship was marked by consistent performance and remarkable determination throughout the season, securing victories in crucial races in Mexico, Jeddah, Monaco, and Tokyo.nnRowland won the title with two races remaining, showcasing his dominance in the series as he became the 10th different champion in 11 seasons. 'Honestly, I was just thinking before the race not to have too much damage going into [the final round in] London,' Rowland reflected on his strategy leading into the race. The Nissan driver concluded the weekend with a total of 184 points, comfortably ahead of his nearest rival, Porsche's Pascal Wehrlein, who fell to 16th place after starting on pole in Berlin, accumulating just 125 points.nnDespite pressure from aggressive competitors, Rowland stated, 'I can't believe it, I have no words. It’s incredible. I was trying to play it safe but everybody was so aggressive, so at some point I was all in. Thankfully I stayed out of trouble.' This winning mentality has characterized Rowland's season, as he navigated various challenges to emerge as the season’s champion.nnThe race outcomes were also significant elsewhere, with Jaguar's Nick Cassidy taking the win, followed by Jake Dennis from Andretti and Jean-Eric Vergne of DS Penske finishing second and third, respectively. With the finale set to take place in London on July 26-27, Rowland's historic victory in Germany not only solidifies his place in Formula E history but also sets an exciting stage for the concluding races of the season.
