A growing movement to save the historic Victoria Marine Lake in St Helier, Jersey, has reached a significant milestone as a petition calling for its restoration has gained 1,000 signatures. This 128-year-old sea pool, known for its unique design and cultural importance, is being championed by local campaigners, particularly the Save Our Pool Working Group, which is urging for immediate restoration efforts. Petition organizer Nina Zaech described the pool as a 'vital piece of Jersey's heritage' that could enhance health, leisure, and tourism in the area.nnThe response to the petition has prompted Jersey's government to take note, indicating a possible commitment to address the concerns raised by the community. A recent inspection conducted last year identified the need for 'comprehensive work' on the pool, highlighting the urgency of the situation and the potential loss of a cherished local landmark.nnDespite the growing support for preservation, there are challenges ahead. Infrastructure Minister Andy Jehan has previously stated that necessary repairs may be 'uneconomical' due to planned sea defense upgrades along the waterfront, which could complicate restoration efforts. As campaigners continue their advocacy, the future of the Victoria Marine Lake hangs in the balance, with the local community rallying behind a cause that echoes their longstanding connection to this historic site.
