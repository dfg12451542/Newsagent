A corgi cross named Jimmy has been rescued after spending 11 harrowing days trapped in a well in Corby, Northamptonshire. Jimmy went missing on June 21 during a walk with his owner, Dorota Gruszczynska, who desperately searched for him, creating posters and utilizing social media to spread the word about her beloved pet. 'We searched the area so many times and nothing, we created posters, put them online and sent them around the local area,' Gruszczynska recounted. After days of despair, she feared the worst: 'We thought he had been hit by a car and we would never see him again.'nnOn July 2, hope emerged when Gruszczynska received a message from a firefighter who believed they had found Jimmy. Firefighters from Northamptonshire Fire and Rescue Service, attending a nearby fire, heard a dog barking and, upon investigation, discovered the trapped dog's location on Stephenson Way. 'Firefighters had been called to the area and had heard him barking; they asked if he had a green harness on and told me he was alive,' Gruszczynska stated. It was confirmed to be Jimmy, who had been missing from a site close to where he was found.nnActions by the firefighters included employing a reach pole and lines to lift cylinders that were obstructing Jimmy's exit from the well. Gruszczynska expressed her gratitude to all those who aided in Jimmy’s rescue, particularly the drone company that assisted throughout the intensive search effort. She mentioned that 'so many people shared the cause, and it helped as the firefighters knew to contact me.'nnUpon his rescue, Jimmy was taken to the vet for a check-up and was found to be hungry but surprisingly not dehydrated. 'He's back on his feet, he's recovered surprisingly well; we don’t know how he survived—there must have been water in the well,' said an elated Gruszczynska, marking a joyful reunion that many feared might not happen. Jimmy’s story highlights the lengths to which communities will go to help reunite pets with their owners, fostering resilience and hope in difficult situations.
