In a worrying escalation of violence, Leicester police have arrested four additional suspects in connection with a stabbing incident that occurred on Sturdee Road in Eyres Monsell. The incident took place shortly before 9:15 PM on Friday, when a man in his 20s was attacked. According to Detective Sergeant Adrian Davies, 'It was still light at that time and with the weather being very warm there were probably quite a few people who were in the area or in their gardens.' He urged anyone who might have seen or heard anything that could contribute to the investigation to come forward.nnAuthorities initially apprehended a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted murder. Following this, two additional arrests were made: an 18-year-old and a 29-year-old man, both also on suspicion of the same charge. In a further twist, a 38-year-old woman was taken in on suspicion of conspiracy to commit murder, while a 25-year-old woman was arrested for allegedly assisting an offender. The 18-year-old and the 25-year-old are currently in police custody, whereas the other three have been released on bail as police investigations continue.nnA police spokesperson confirmed that the injured man managed to make his way to Lawford Road following the stabbing. He was then transported to the hospital, where he remains in stable condition. Investigations have revealed that the suspects and the victim were known to each other. In an effort to gather more information, the police are carrying out house-to-house inquiries and have completed a thorough examination of the crime scene, hoping to uncover additional leads in this troubling case.
