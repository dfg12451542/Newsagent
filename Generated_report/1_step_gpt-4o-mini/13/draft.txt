As the excitement builds for The Open Championship at Royal Portrush, defending champion Xander Schauffele was seen on the course Monday, working to adapt to the challenging links. Although he secured a tie with Scottie Scheffler at nine-under par in the Scottish Open just last week, Schauffele has acknowledged that his season has been marred by a rib injury sustained earlier this year. 'I hope to regain my form in time for the tournament,' he stated, alluding to the pressures of defending his title amidst ongoing physical challenges.nnAlongside Schauffele is Shane Lowry, the 2019 Open champion, who knows the intricacies of the course well. Having also achieved success earlier in the season at the Abu Dhabi HSBC Championship, Lowry is a player to watch as he navigates the familiar surroundings. 'It's special to be back at Portrush,' said Lowry while preparing for his round with Danish golfer Rasmus Hojgaard, emphasizing the unique challenges that come with competing at home.nnThe upcoming championship is set to feature a formidable field of 156 players competing for the Claret Jug and the coveted title of 'Champion Golfer of the Year.' The last time The Open was held at Portrush, Lowry emerged victorious, and anticipation is building for whether he can replicate that success. As both Schauffele and Lowry prepare mentally and physically for the event, golf fans can expect an intense competition over the next few days.
