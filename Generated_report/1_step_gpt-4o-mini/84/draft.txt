The Morris family from Hereford celebrated in style after their lengthy journey to St Gallen, Switzerland, paid off with England's impressive 6-1 victory over Wales in the UEFA Women's Euro 2025. Having traveled 800 miles (1,280 km) in their campervan, Kevin Morris, along with his wife Kelly and their children, Amelia, 16, and Ethan, 13, rejoiced in the atmosphere of the match as the Lionesses secured their place in the quarter-finals.nn'As soon as the game started, they put on another great performance and blew away Wales,' Kevin Morris reflected on the triumph. The jubilance of the stadium contrasted sharply with their struggle navigating public transport afterwards. 'It was just busy coming out of the stadium… We couldn’t get on to the train,' he narrated, explaining how they ended up waiting for a bus to complete their journey back to their accommodation near Lake Constance.nnTheir journey did not stop at the match; it included stops in Paris, Courgenay, and Zurich. Yet, the Morris family hopes to see England face off against Sweden again in the quarter-finals, echoing their previous encounter in the Euro 2022 semi-final where the Lionesses triumphed. 'I think we can do it again,' Kevin expressed confidence as they prepared for the next leg of their adventure.nnThe Lionesses, reigning champions, entered Euro 2025 on the back of a historic win at Euro 2022, where they captured their first major title against Germany in a thrilling match crowned by a record-setting crowd. As the competition progresses, fans like the Morris family remain enthusiastic about their team's chances, setting out on memorable journeys filled with hope and pride.
