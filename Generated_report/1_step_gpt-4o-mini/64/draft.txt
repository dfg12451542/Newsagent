Crystal Palace Football Club has unveiled its new home kit for the 2025-26 season, coinciding with the excitement surrounding the team's participation in European competition. The launch took place over the weekend, drawing attention as the Eagles prepare for a thrilling season ahead.nnProminent players including Jean-Philippe Mateta, Marc Guehi, and Eddie Nketiah showcased the new kit, along with Palace Women athletes Issy Sibley and Chloe Arthur. The design features a fresh look for the club, igniting enthusiasm among fans who eagerly anticipate the upcoming season.nnIn a notable highlight, new signing Walter Benitez made his debut appearance in the club’s light green goalkeeper jersey. The excitement didn't stop there, as Palace defended their new ensemble in their preseason opener, securing a 1-0 victory against Millwall on Saturday. This match not only marked the debut of the new kit but also set a positive tone for the season ahead as they compete on multiple fronts, including their return to European competitions.
