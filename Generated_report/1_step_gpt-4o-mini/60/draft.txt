Two women were tragically killed in a shooting at the Richmond Road Baptist Church in Lexington, Kentucky, on Sunday, July 13, following a series of violent events that began with gunfire aimed at law enforcement. The incident commenced when 47-year-old Guy House shot a state trooper during a traffic stop, inflicting non-life-threatening injuries, according to Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers. After the shooting, House carjacked a vehicle and made his way to the church, approximately 16 miles from the initial incident, where he opened fire on parishioners.nnDuring the attack, House shot four individuals, resulting in the deaths of Beverly Gum, 72, and Christina Combs, 32, who were pronounced dead at the scene. The police reported that two male parishioners were injured, with one in critical condition and the other stable. Chief Weathers noted that it appeared the suspect may have had connections to individuals at the church, raising further questions about the motive behind the attack.nnResponding law enforcement engaged with House, ultimately shooting him dead at the church. Chief Weathers confirmed that the investigations into the incident are being handled by the Kentucky State Police and the Public Integrity Unit. This shooting has devastated the local community, with a county coroner official describing the church congregation as a tight-knit group where most attendees are close friends or family.nnAs this story develops, local authorities are urging anyone with information related to the tragic events to come forward. The Lexington community is mourning the loss of life in this senseless act of violence that disrupted a Sunday service, an event that should have been a peaceful gathering.
