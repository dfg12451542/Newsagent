A dramatic car fire on the A30 in Cornwall has sparked a significant blaze on nearby moorland, prompting swift action from local fire crews. According to the Cornwall Fire & Rescue Service, the fire erupted on the eastbound lane near Temple Tor on Bodmin Moor, rapidly spreading to the surrounding area. Fire crews from both Launceston and Bodmin were dispatched to the scene, where they worked diligently to contain the flames and prevent further damage to the moorland ecosystem.nnIn response to the emergency, the fire service received multiple 999 calls alerting them to the situation. Witnesses reported seeing smoke billowing from the burning vehicle, as firefighters battled the fire amidst challenging conditions. The rapid spread of flames is indicative of a troubling trend this year, as reported by the Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue service, which has noted an increase in outdoor fires compared to previous years.nnThe incident has raised concerns regarding fire safety, especially in vulnerable natural areas like Bodmin Moor. The recent uptick in outdoor fires highlights the need for vigilance and responsible behavior in the countryside, particularly during dry spells when the risk of wildfire is heightened. As firefighters continue to manage the aftermath of the incident, local authorities are urging residents and visitors to exercise caution and adhere to fire regulations.
