In a thrilling finale to the 2025 Club World Cup, Chelsea clinched the title with a commanding 3-0 victory over Paris St-Germain at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey. The celebrations took an unexpected turn as former President Donald Trump, who was initially expected to present the trophy and exit, decided to stay on stage alongside the players during the trophy lift. Chelsea captain Reece James remarked, 'They told me that he was going to present the trophy and then exit the stage, but he wanted to stay. He just congratulated me and the team for lifting the trophy and told us to enjoy the moment.' nnTrump's presence added an unusual flair to the post-match proceedings. After handing the trophy to James, Trump stayed to applaud as the team celebrated their hard-earned victory. He expressed his enjoyment of the event, stating in an interview, 'I’m having a really great time,' and he praised FIFA president Gianni Infantino for his contributions to the sport, saying, 'Gianni is a friend of mine, he's done such a great job with the league and with soccer - or as they would call it 'football'.' nnAs the fireworks lit up the East Rutherford sky, players and staff were caught up in the jubilant atmosphere. Cole Palmer, another key player for Chelsea, shared his surprise at Trump's decision to remain on stage during the trophy lift, commenting, 'I knew he was going to be here but I didn't know he was going to be on the stand when we lifted the trophy.' nnThe moment marked a significant chapter in Chelsea's history, reflecting their prowess on the global stage. With Trump standing in the midst of the jubilant Chelsea squad, the players celebrated their remarkable achievement nearly three hours after kick-off, a victory that not only added to their accolades but also showcased the cultural intersections of sport and politics at a high-profile event.
