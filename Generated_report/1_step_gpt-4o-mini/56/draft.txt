An ultramarathon runner was airlifted to safety during the grueling 55-mile (88.5km) race in the scenic Lake District on Saturday, July 14, 2025. The incident unfolded in Grisedale Valley around 16:00 BST when the runner collapsed due to heat exhaustion, becoming pale, vomiting, and ultimately losing consciousness. Emergency services were alerted immediately, marking a swift response to the precarious situation.nnThe Patterdale Mountain Rescue Team (PMRT) quickly mobilized, responding to the urgent call for assistance. North West Ambulance Service coordinated with mountain rescue teams and the coastguard, which dispatched a helicopter to reach the runner. Rescuers managed to winch him to safety before transporting him to the King George playing field in Patterdale.nnAfter the challenging two-and-a-half-hour rescue operation, which involved 11 PMRT members and coastguard personnel, the runner was assessed by a team doctor at Patterdale Base. Following an initial examination, he was transferred to the hospital for further evaluation and treatment.nnThe swift actions of the PMRT and emergency services ensured that the runner received timely medical attention, underscoring the importance of preparedness and response in such demanding events. As the popularity of ultra-distance races grows, so too does the need for comprehensive safety measures in extreme outdoor events like this one.
