Rory McIlroy is feeling upbeat and poised as he prepares for the upcoming Royal Portrush tournament, despite a near miss at the Scottish Open, where he finished strong at 13-under par. 'No frustration; it's been a great week,' McIlroy stated, reflecting on his performance that included 19 birdies over the four rounds. The Irish golfer expressed satisfaction with his play, particularly over the weekend, saying, 'I’m really happy with where my game is; the way I played over the weekend; the shots that I hit, how I controlled my ball flight.' nnAlthough missing the trophy was disappointing, McIlroy noted the challenging weather conditions. 'It was pretty windy and was hard to get the ball super close, but I gave myself plenty of chances coming down the stretch,' he explained. He also mentioned the slight difficulty with the greens, stating, 'I feel like the greens got a little bumpy. I had some good putts that just didn’t go in, whether they were mis-reads or poor speed.' nnLooking ahead, McIlroy is eager to play at Portrush, a significant venue for him, especially since it will be the first time he competes there in front of a home crowd after winning the Masters earlier. McIlroy seeks to reclaim the Scottish Open title he won in 2023, hoping to build momentum from his recent performance as he said, 'But I feel like I’ve gotten out of this week what I wanted. I’m looking forward to getting to Portrush tonight and getting out on to the golf course early tomorrow.' nnWith fluctuating form in recent tournaments after securing his Green Jacket, McIlroy aims to capitalize on his home advantage and deliver a stellar performance as he vies to solidify his status as one of Northern Ireland's greatest athletes.
