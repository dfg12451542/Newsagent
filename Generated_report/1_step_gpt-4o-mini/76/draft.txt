The M11 motorway near Stansted Airport has reopened following a significant vehicle fire that caused major disruptions earlier today. Delays of up to 60 minutes were reported as fire crews worked to extinguish the blaze and ensure the safety of motorists. A spokesperson for National Highways confirmed that there is approximately 1.5 miles of congestion in the area, urging drivers to allow extra time to navigate through the delays.nnWitnesses described the scene as chaotic, with many drivers exiting their vehicles in response to the standstill, some of whom were seen walking along the motorway. Fire crews were deployed swiftly on Thursday to manage the incident, which also led to warnings about plumes of smoke affecting visibility on the M11 and the adjoining M1.nnThis incident follows another bout of traffic delays earlier this week on Sunday morning, where motorists experienced similar slowdowns between junctions nine for Great Chesterford and eight for London Stansted Airport in Essex. The repeated issues highlight ongoing traffic challenges for travelers in the area, especially during peak times.nnAs the situation progresses, authorities encourage all commuters to pay attention to local updates and drive safely, as the clear-up continues and the traffic flow stabilizes.
