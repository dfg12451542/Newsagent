Wycombe Wanderers have announced the signing of midfielder Ewan Henderson from Belgian side Beerschot, marking the club's eighth acquisition this summer. The 25-year-old Scotland youth international joins Wycombe for an undisclosed fee, bringing a wealth of experience from his recent stints in Scotland and Belgium. 'Ewan's a really energetic midfielder who's had a great start to his career in the top flights in Scotland and Belgium, and gives us another dimension in the middle of the park,' said Wycombe head coach Mike Dodds, emphasizing the positive impact Henderson is expected to have on the team's dynamics.nnHenderson, who has been a pivotal player in the midfield over the past three years, is seen as a valuable asset as he integrates into the squad during pre-season preparations. Dodds noted, 'He's played a lot of football which stands him in good stead to come here and hopefully hit the ground running.' His addition is expected to bolster Wycombe's midfield as they gear up for the upcoming campaign.nnPrior to his move to Wycombe, Henderson had a significant career trajectory, starting at Hibernian in 2022. After a successful loan spell at second-tier Oostende, he secured a permanent transfer to Beerschot last summer. At Wycombe, he joins a growing list of new signings, including Armando Junior Quitirna, Taylor Allen, and Bradley Fink, all of whom are aiming to contribute to the team's ambitions in the upcoming season at Adams Park.
