'It just shows how tricky football is in general,' Askou remarked, highlighting the unpredictability inherent in cup tournaments. The match at New Douglas Park did not go as planned for Motherwell, with Askou acknowledging, 'We didn't get the result we wanted, obviously. A few other clubs, as far as I've heard, didn't do that either.' He emphasized the importance of being prepared for unexpected outcomes in football, stating, 'That's just football in general. You have to be there on the day.' nnDespite the disappointing match, Askou noted that the team performed well in terms of effort and structure.
