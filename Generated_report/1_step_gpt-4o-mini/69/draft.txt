Firefighters are battling a significant blaze at the White Lund Industrial Estate in Morecambe, Lancashire, which erupted around 09:30 BST this morning. The fire has created large plumes of smoke visible from miles away, prompting concerns among local residents. A spokesperson from the Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service (LFRS) stated that crews are 'working as fast as they can to extinguish the fire safely.' As firefighting efforts continue, nearby roads, including part of the Bay Gateway, have been closed to ensure public safety.nnLFRS group manager Mark Warwick confirmed that the fire is 'giving off large volumes of smoke' and advised residents in proximity to keep windows and doors closed to limit exposure. There have been no reported injuries stemming from the incident, but the billowing smoke remains a serious concern as emergency services work on site.nnEfforts to bring the situation under control have mobilized eight fire engines to the scene. Local authorities continue to monitor the situation closely while crews tackle the blaze, aiming to minimize any potential risks to the surrounding community amid the ongoing emergency.
