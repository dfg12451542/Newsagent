Phyll Babb, a remarkable woman from Salisbury, has completed a 200-mile walking challenge on her 100th birthday, demonstrating that age is no barrier to achieving extraordinary feats. On Sunday, July 13, 2025, she crossed the finish line surrounded by family and supporters, raising over £11,000 for the Salisbury Trust for the Homeless (STFH). Initially committed to walking 100 miles, Babb doubled her target after realizing she was ahead of schedule earlier this year. 'It’s hard to believe I’ve done 200 walks. It’s gone like a dream,' she expressed with joy upon completing her final mile around the iconic Salisbury Cathedral.nnBabb's passion for walking is deeply rooted in her upbringing. 'Walking is good for you,' she stated. 'I was brought up to walk, so as children we walked everywhere, and I still like walking.' Her dedication has inspired those around her, with many describing her as an inspiration. 'To reach the age of 100 and to do it so fit, looking so well, being so articulate, that’s the really amazing thing she’s done,' said family friend David Evans.nnHer accomplishments do not go unnoticed by her family, who rallied to support her throughout the challenge. 'Seeing my great aunt complete her 200th lap around the cathedral has been really special,' shared Ruth Simmons. With logistical support from local accountant Gordon Pardy, who recorded her walks, Babb documented her journey using the Strava app. Pardy noted, 'To do this in her 100th year was so impressive.'nnConcluding her remarkable challenge, Babb expressed gratitude towards her supporters, stating that people had been 'extremely generous' and thanked everyone for their contributions to the cause. Her story serves not only as a testament to her indomitable spirit but also as a call to give back to the community, highlighting the enduring power of kindness and resilience at any age.
