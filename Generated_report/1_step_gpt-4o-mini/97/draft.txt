The Wurzels abruptly ended their performance at the Party at the Palace festival in Wells, Somerset, on Saturday, July 13, as rising temperatures and severe heat took a toll on band members. The band’s singer, Pete Budd, who is set to celebrate his 85th birthday this upcoming week, had to leave the stage before concluding the set, sparking concern among fans. Accordionist Tommy Banner noted that both he and Budd experienced symptoms typical of sun and heat exhaustion, with temperatures peaking at a steamy 31 degrees Celsius. nnDescribing the situation, Banner stated, 'I was about to announce the last song and Pete wasn’t besides me where he normally is. He was in a bad way.' He added in a follow-up comment that he, too, felt sunstroke and dizziness after stepping off the stage, with medical personnel swiftly coming to offer assistance. nnWitnesses to the event noted the lack of airflow on stage and the direct sunlight that the artists faced, making the conditions increasingly challenging. Health professionals at the festival quickly assessed both Banner and Budd’s conditions, instructing Banner to refrain from driving while providing guidance on recovery exercises. As of now, both musicians have reportedly recovered from their ordeal and continue to engage with their dedicated fan base. nnDespite the unfortunate end to their set, the incident has sparked conversations about artist welfare in extreme weather conditions during outdoor performances, emphasizing the need for proper care and preventive measures at such events.
