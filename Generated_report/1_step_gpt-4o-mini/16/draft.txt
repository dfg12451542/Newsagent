A biker suffered serious injuries following a crash on a rural road in the Borders near Hawick on Sunday, July 14. Emergency services responded to the incident involving a white and blue Suzuki GSXR motorbike, which occurred around noon on the B6399, just south of the access road to the Pines Burn wind farm.nnThe 57-year-old man was subsequently transported to a hospital for urgent medical treatment. Authorities have indicated that the road was closed for approximately eight hours to facilitate emergency operations and investigation at the scene.nnPolice are now appealing for witnesses to the accident to come forward, as they continue to gather information surrounding the circumstances of the crash. Local residents and other road users are urged to assist in understanding the events that led to this unfortunate incident.
