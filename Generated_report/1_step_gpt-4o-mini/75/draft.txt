Chris Gotterup secured a stunning victory at the Scottish Open, holding off world number two Rory McIlroy to win by two shots at the Renaissance Club. The 25-year-old displayed remarkable composure throughout the final round, which saw him card an impressive closing round of 66 to finish at 15 under par. 'I just hung in there tight and tried to keep it together,' Gotterup said, reflecting on his performance. This victory marks Gotterup's second PGA Tour title, following his triumph at last year's Canadian Open.nnStarting the final round tied for the lead with McIlroy, Gotterup initially faced a shaky beginning with a bogey on the first hole but quickly rallied, picking up birdies on the third, seventh, and eighth holes. As the round progressed, he solidified his lead with two more birdies on the back nine, effectively distancing himself from a faltering McIlroy, who failed to secure a birdie in his last ten holes. Despite his struggle, McIlroy remained optimistic, stating, 'Chris played a great round of golf. He was so solid.'nnThis victory also comes at a pivotal moment for Gotterup, who prior to the Scottish Open had faced a challenging year, missing cuts in eight of his first twelve events and entering the tournament ranked just 88th in the FedEx Cup standings. 'It's amazing. It's all hitting me. It's just so cool. I played really well this week,' he expressed in disbelief at his achievements. With this win, Gotterup has not only claimed £1.2 million but has also secured a debut appearance at The Open next week at Royal Portrush.nnMeanwhile, McIlroy, who was vying to reclaim the title he won in 2023, will head to the Open looking to capitalize on this week's performance despite his disappointment. He shared the second-place finish with England's Marco Penge, who also had a strong showing throughout the tournament. The final leaderboard also showcased notable performances from players like Matt Fitzpatrick and Justin Rose, who remained in contention but ultimately fell short of the leading scores. Gotterup’s unexpected triumph serves as a significant milestone in his young career, illustrating the unpredictable and thrilling nature of professional golf.
