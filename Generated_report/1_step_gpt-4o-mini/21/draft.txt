A recent survey conducted by the Institute of Directors (IoD) Jersey has revealed a significant call for collaboration between businesses across the Channel Islands. The survey, which gathered insights from 70 IoD members, highlighted that while 56% of respondents felt optimistic about their own organizations, only 17% exhibited confidence in Jersey's overall economy. Alex Ruddy, IoD Jersey Chair, described the survey results as providing a 'clear mandate for action.' He emphasized the necessity for bold investment and policy clarity to enhance economic confidence, stating, 'Collaboration across the Channel Islands, support for innovation, and a focus on infrastructure and housing are essential to Jersey's future prosperity.' nnRichard Hemans, who leads economics for IoD Guernsey, echoed Ruddy’s sentiments, urging that the similar challenges faced by both islands – related to economic, geographical, demographic, and fiscal factors – can best be addressed through closer cooperation. The findings also suggest prioritizing infrastructure investment, attracting new industries, and addressing housing affordability as critical steps for Jersey's growth. nnAmidst these findings, both islands appeared to embrace a collective approach towards overcoming these challenges. Jersey respondents specifically expressed a desire to learn from Guernsey's strategies in reducing public spending and stimulating economic growth. Furthermore, the survey indicated that supporting entrepreneurship and innovation through tax incentives, skills development, and youth talent attraction could foster a more robust economic environment. nnHistorical trends point to the importance of such collaboration, with previous studies suggesting that enhanced cooperation could lead to a significant increase in tax revenues exceeding £150 million annually for both islands. This aligns with the urgent need to adapt to evolving economic landscapes and demographic shifts, particularly as the islands face a growing elderly population. As businesses call for strategic partnerships, the forward momentum could not only strengthen economic resilience but also enhance the quality of life for islanders.
