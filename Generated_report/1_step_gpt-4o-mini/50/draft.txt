A climber suffered a head injury after falling approximately 3 meters (9.8 feet) at Hoffman Quarry in Buxton on Friday. The incident occurred around 11:22 BST when emergency services were alerted to the scene. A spokesperson for the Buxton Mountain Rescue Team reported that the climber briefly lost consciousness due to the impact before rescuers arrived.nnUpon reaching the site, the mountain rescue team worked swiftly to stabilize the injured climber and prepare him for transport. He was subsequently airlifted to a nearby location where an ambulance was awaiting to take him to a hospital for further evaluation and treatment.nnThis incident underscores the ongoing risks associated with rock climbing and outdoor activities, prompting reminders for climbers to prioritize safety measures and take necessary precautions while ascending challenging terrains.
