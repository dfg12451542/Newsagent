In a solemn tribute, Liverpool FC marked the first match since the untimely death of their forward, Diogo Jota, during a pre-season friendly at Deepdale on July 13, 2025. Fans and players alike came together to honor Jota, a cherished figure in the club's recent history, remembered not just for his prowess on the pitch but also for his persona off it. A visibly emotional atmosphere enveloped the stadium, with thousands of supporters donning custom T-shirts and holding scarves emblazoned with Jota's number 20, symbolizing their collective grief and solidarity.nnDuring the match, a poignant moment arose when a minute's silence was impeccably observed before kickoff, followed by an emotional rendition of Jota's supporters’ chant, which echoed throughout the stadium. The away end, filled with Liverpool fans, united in song as the players walked out, reinforcing the deep connection between the club and its community. ‘I feel very emotional,’ remarked Jane, a Liverpool fan, who expressed pride in her team's efforts to celebrate Jota's life. Her sentiments were echoed by fellow fans, including Mathew, who described the difficult conversations he had to navigate with his young son about Jota’s passing.nnLiverpool head coach Arne Slot, in his first remarks since the death of Jota, reflected on the emotional journey the team is undertaking. 'If we want to laugh, we laugh; if we want to cry, we’re going to cry,' Slot stated, emphasizing the importance of embracing their feelings during this challenging time. The match also featured several tributes from players, with Conor Bradley and Darwin Nunez both recognizing Jota’s legacy through their celebrations, further highlighting how integral he was to the team.nnOn a historic note, Jota’s passing has led Liverpool to retire his number 20 shirt across all levels of the club, an act announced at 20:20 BST just days before the match. This gesture cements Jota's legacy, as he was not only a skillful player but also a devoted family man, described by many as a loving husband and father. As fans filled the Bill Shankly Kop, the emotional tributes showcased that although he is no longer on the pitch, Jota's spirit will forever remain in the hearts of those who loved him and supported him.nnThe game itself, while ultimately a sporting event, transformed into a celebration of Jota's life and impact. The profound sense of camaraderie among fans and between the players reflects the tight-knit family that Liverpool has become, standing together to console each other in their shared grief and everlasting love for Diogo Jota.
