Jim McGuinness, manager of the Donegal Gaelic football team, expressed his confidence in his squad following their dominant performance against Meath, securing a place in the All-Ireland final against Kerry. 'We know what we will get with Kerry, they are a team who know their way around Croke Park like the back of their hand,' McGuinness told BBC Sport, highlighting the experience and expertise of their opponents.nnDonegal displayed a powerful offensive in their semi-final match, with Oisin Gallen, Ciaran Moore and Conor O'Donnell netting second-half goals, leading to a convincing victory with a score of 3-26 to 0-15. 'Full credit to the boys, they have done a brilliant job, and we got over the line today,' McGuinness added, acknowledging the hard work of his team that culminated in their advancement to the final, which is scheduled for July 27.nnWith the Ulster champions holding a five-point lead at halftime, they solidified their dominance after the break with a flurry of goals. McGuinness noted the preparations leading up to the final are crucial, stating, 'We have a job to do' while emphasizing the balance between enjoying the build-up and maintaining focus on performance. 'We have to bring the highest level possible to our performance,' he asserted.nnHistorically, this final marks a significant moment for Donegal, as they aim to prove themselves at Croke Park—a venue steeped in tradition and success for Kerry, who recently dispatched Tyrone with ease. The upcoming clash also underscores Donegal's remarkable journey to the decider, notably after Meath's giant-killing exploits earlier in the season, having knocked out top teams like Dublin and Galway. As the countdown begins, McGuinness conveys a mix of anticipation and determination for a successful outing against the storied Kerry side.
