John Torode, the well-known presenter of MasterChef, has acknowledged an allegation of using racist language during a recent inquiry into various claims against his co-host, Gregg Wallace. In a statement shared on social media, Torode revealed that the allegation concerns remarks purportedly made in 2018 or 2019. He stated, 'For the sake of transparency, I confirm that I am the individual who is alleged to have used racial language on one occasion.' However, he also expressed his disbelief regarding the incident, claiming, 'I have absolutely no recollection of any of this, and I do not believe that it happened.'nnThe inquiry, conducted by an independent law firm at the behest of production company Banijay, followed a BBC News investigation into allegations against Wallace concerning inappropriate sexual remarks spanning nearly two decades. The report revealed two substantiated allegations against those involved in the show, focusing on inappropriate language, including swearing and racist comments, not directly tied to Wallace. Torode's remark that 'the person I was speaking with did not believe that it was intended in a malicious way' highlights a complex dynamic surrounding the accusation, suggesting a lack of intent.nnIn his defense, Torode stressed his commitment to upholding respectful communication, saying, 'I want to be clear that I've always had the view that any racial language is wholly unacceptable in any environment. I'm shocked and saddened by the allegation as I would never wish to cause anyone any offence.' The ongoing review into these matters reflects broader concerns regarding workplace behavior in the entertainment industry and the repercussions of past actions as exemplified by this high-profile case. nnAs the investigation continues, the production team and audience alike await further clarifications regarding the allegations and the future of both Torode and Wallace on the MasterChef show.
