A three-year-old girl from Groesffordd near Brecon, Powys, is making waves in the dog show world after qualifying with her Miniature Schnauzer, Zeeva, for next year's Crufts. Caitlyn, who expressed her excitement about 'winning' and 'getting rosettes,' is set to become one of the youngest competitors in the prestigious show’s history. The duo secured their spot by being placed at the recent Three Counties Championship Show last month, marking a significant milestone in Caitlyn's early sporting journey.nnCaitlyn's mother, Lydia Mellor, was beaming with pride, stating, 'It’s amazing,' as she reflected on her daughter's accomplishments. Lydia has been involved in dog shows since she was just ten years old, and she remarked on Caitlyn's remarkable confidence and sportsmanship, saying, 'She is so confident generally and she works so well with the dogs. She loves the dogs.' According to Lydia, Caitlyn's maturity belies her age; she happily shows Zeeva in the ring alone and congratulates fellow competitors, indicating a spirit of camaraderie and competition that is commendable.nnHelen Kerfoot, a representative of the Crufts show, echoed this sentiment, expressing delight at seeing such a young competitor qualifying and emphasizing that 'taking part in Crufts is a real achievement but it's also something that's accessible to all.' With a history of dog shows in the family, it’s clear that Caitlyn's journey is shaped by a blend of enthusiasm and dedication, which mirrors her mother's experience in the dog show world. As events unfold leading up to the 2026 Crufts, Caitlyn’s story serves as an inspiration for young competitors, showcasing that dog shows are about more than just beauty—they are about the joy of companionship and community.
